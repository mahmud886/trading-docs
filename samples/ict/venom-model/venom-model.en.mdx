## What Is the Venom Model?

The **Venom** model is an aggressive entry right after a **liquidity sweep**. Once price sweeps a pool of liquidity in a kill zone and reverses with a strong **displacement**, you enter on the **first retracement** into the [FVG](/en/ict/fvg) that displacement left, with the stop just beyond the sweep.

The idea is speed: you don't wait for a second retest or a higher-timeframe confirmation. That's also its risk.

<Callout type="info" title="About the name">
  "Venom" is a name used in ICT-derived teaching for this entry style rather than a separately defined ICT pattern. The
  mechanics are a [liquidity sweep](/en/ict/liquidity-sweep) plus a first-touch FVG entry.
</Callout>

---

## The Sequence

1. **Liquidity target:** obvious buy-side or sell-side, such as equal highs/lows or the previous day's high/low.
2. **Sweep:** price takes it during a [kill zone](/en/ict/ict-kill-zones).
3. **Displacement:** one or a few strong candles reverse immediately and leave an FVG.
4. **Entry:** a limit at the FVG's CE (50%), filled on the **first** retracement.
5. **Stop:** just beyond the sweep extreme.
6. **Target:** the opposite liquidity pool.

If the first retracement goes through the whole FVG and back to the sweep, the setup has failed. Don't re-enter.

## Worked Example — Bullish Venom, Gold (5m, US summer)

1. **Liquidity:** equal lows at **2,020**.
2. **Sweep:** at **1:15 PM BD** (London kill zone) price drops to **2,016**.
3. **Displacement:** at 1:20 PM BD a 14.6-point candle reverses to 2,032 and leaves an FVG at **2,022 – 2,028**.
4. **Entry:** the first pullback reaches the CE **2,025** at 1:40 PM BD. Buy.

| Level   | Price | Note                       |
| ------- | ----- | -------------------------- |
| Entry   | 2,025 | CE of the FVG, first touch |
| Stop    | 2,015 | Below the 2,016 sweep      |
| Target  | 2,060 | Buy-side liquidity above   |
| **R:R** | **1 : 3.5** | Risk $10, reward $35 |

### Bearish Venom — EUR/USD (no chart)

The previous day's high at **1.0940** is buy-side. At about **6:00 PM BD** (NY AM, US summer) price spikes to **1.0948**, then three bearish candles leave an FVG at 1.0928 – 1.0935. Sell at the CE **1.0932**, stop **1.0950**, target **1.0870**: risk 18 pips, reward 62, **R:R 1 : 3.4** (planned).

## Venom Compared With Other Models

| Model             | Key feature                          | When you enter                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Venom**         | Sweep + displacement FVG             | First retracement only         |
| **[Unicorn](/en/ict/unicorn-model)** | Breaker and FVG overlap | Usually a later, deeper retrace |
| **[Silver Bullet](/en/ict/silver-bullet)** | FVG inside a fixed hour | The first FVG in the window   |
| **[Order block](/en/smc/order-blocks)** | Last opposite candle before displacement | Any valid retest |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                         | Fix                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Entering during the sweep       | Wait for the displacement that follows it                  |
| Chasing the displacement        | Use a limit at the CE                                      |
| Re-entering after a failed first touch | One shot; if it fails, it's done                    |
| Outside a kill zone             | Sweeps outside the sessions are less reliable              |
| A stop that needs to be huge    | If the sweep is far from the FVG, it's not a Venom setup   |

## Practice

1. Mark equal highs/lows before London and NY for 20 sessions.
2. When one is swept, check whether a displacement FVG followed, and whether the first touch of the CE held.
3. Record the planned and actual R:R in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Element     | Rule                                         |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Trigger** | Kill-zone sweep of obvious liquidity         |
| **Proof**   | Immediate displacement leaving an FVG        |
| **Entry**   | Limit at the CE, first touch only            |
| **Stop**    | Just beyond the sweep                        |
| **Target**  | The opposite liquidity pool                  |
