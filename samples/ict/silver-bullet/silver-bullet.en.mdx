## What Is the Silver Bullet?

The **Silver Bullet** is a time-based entry model. Inside a fixed one-hour window you wait for a **displacement** in the direction of your bias, mark the [FVG](/en/ict/fvg) it leaves, and enter on the retrace into it.

Its strength is discipline: one hour, one setup, a clear "no trade" when nothing forms.

<Callout type="info" title="Why it can work">
  The original credited "the algorithm" creating moves at these hours. A more checkable reason: these windows follow
  the London open, the US data releases and the US stock open, when volume and displacement are most likely. Test the
  windows on your own pair.
</Callout>

## The Three Windows (Bangladesh Time)

| Window            | New York time   | BD — US summer   | BD — US winter   | Usual context                       |
| ----------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **London SB**     | 3 – 4 AM        | 1 – 2 PM         | 2 – 3 PM         | After the London open's first move  |
| **NY AM SB**      | 10 – 11 AM      | **8 – 9 PM**     | **9 – 10 PM**    | After US data and the stock open    |
| **NY PM SB**      | 2 – 3 PM        | 12 – 1 AM        | 1 – 2 AM         | Afternoon continuation              |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: EST vs summer time">
  The original labelled the New York times "EST" but gave BD times that only match **US summer time (EDT)**. From early
  November to mid-March, add one hour to every BD time above.
</Callout>

---

## The Rules

1. **Bias first.** Know the [daily bias](/en/ict/daily-bias) before the window opens.
2. **Displacement inside the window**, in the bias direction, that leaves an FVG. The FVG must form in the window.
3. **Entry:** a limit order at the FVG, often at its CE (50%).
4. **Stop:** beyond the swing point the displacement started from.
5. **Target:** the next liquidity in the bias direction.
6. **One per window:** the first valid FVG. If none forms, no trade.

### London Silver Bullet — Gold sell (no chart)

Bias bearish (premium, sell-side draw at 2,020). At **1:20 PM BD** (US summer) a bearish 5m displacement leaves an FVG at 2,038 – 2,044. Sell limit at the CE **2,041**, stop **2,048**, target **2,020**: risk $7, reward $21, **R:R 1 : 3** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                          | Fix                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| No daily bias                    | Without it, it's random FVG trading                        |
| FVG formed before the window     | A 9:30 AM NY FVG isn't a NY AM Silver Bullet               |
| Taking several per window        | The first valid one only                                   |
| Forcing a trade                  | No FVG in the window → no trade                            |
| Weak "displacement"              | It must be a strong candle, clearly bigger than the ones before |
| Summer times in winter           | Add one hour from early November to mid-March              |

## Practice

1. For 20 days, screenshot the NY AM window and mark the first FVG in the bias direction, if any.
2. Record whether the CE was reached and whether the target or stop came first.
3. Repeat for the London window. Log results in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
