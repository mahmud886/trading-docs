## What Was the 2022 Mentorship?

In 2022 ICT published a free mentorship series on YouTube. Its focus was a **single, repeatable entry model** built from ideas covered elsewhere on this site: daily bias, kill zones, liquidity sweeps, market structure shifts with displacement, and fair value gaps.

It's popular because it's compact: one model, practised over and over, instead of dozens of tools.

<Callout type="info" title="What this page does and doesn't claim">
  The original described the 2022 series as "the definitive version" that "corrects misinterpretations" and listed a
  table of changes from earlier teachings, plus a named "Institutional Order Entry Drill (IOED)". Those claims weren't
  sourced and are left out. This page sticks to the model itself, which you can test on any chart.
</Callout>

## The 2022 Entry Model

| Step | What                                    | Lesson                                      |
| ---- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| ①    | Higher-timeframe bias: weekly → daily → 4H | [Daily Bias](/en/ict/daily-bias)          |
| ②    | Pick a session: London or NY AM kill zone | [Kill Zones](/en/ict/ict-kill-zones)      |
| ③    | Wait for the **sweep** against the bias (the Judas swing) | [Judas Swing](/en/ict/judas-swing) |
| ④    | **MSS with displacement** on 5m–15m     | [MSS](/en/ict/mss)                          |
| ⑤    | Mark the **FVG** the displacement left (ideally in OTE) | [FVG](/en/ict/fvg), [OTE](/en/ict/ote) |
| ⑥    | **Enter** with a limit at the CE        |                                             |
| ⑦    | **Stop** beyond the sweep               |                                             |
| ⑧    | **Target** the opposite liquidity       | [Liquidity](/en/ict/liquidity)              |

| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ① Bias     | Weekly in discount; yesterday a bullish PO3 candle; 4H higher lows |

## Supporting Ideas From the Series

| Idea                         | In short                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Liquidity → imbalance → liquidity** | Price runs liquidity, leaves an imbalance, then runs the other side |
| **PO3 on every timeframe**   | The same open–manipulation–close shape on daily, 4H, 1H candles. See [Power of 3](/en/ict/power-of-3). |
| **IPDA ranges**              | The 20/40/60-day highs and lows as targets. See [Monthly Profiles](/en/ict/monthly-profiles). |
| **Macros**                   | Short windows inside the kill zones. See [Time and Price](/en/ict/time-and-price-theory). |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: macro times">
  The original listed macros in "EST", including a 1:50 – 2:10 PM PM macro. The commonly cited list and its BD times for
  both seasons are in [Time and Price Theory](/en/ict/time-and-price-theory).
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                           | Fix                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skipping step ①                   | Without a bias, a sweep in either direction looks like a setup |
| Treating any gap as the FVG       | It must come from the displacement that made the MSS       |
| Entering before the MSS           | The sweep alone isn't a signal                             |
| Treating the series as gospel     | Test it on your own pair and sessions                      |
| Not practising                    | Replay 50 sessions and mark every step before trading live |

## Practice

1. Replay 50 London or NY sessions and mark steps ①–⑧ wherever the model appears.
2. Record how often the CE was reached and which came first, target or stop.
3. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Element    | Rule                                    |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Bias**   | Higher timeframe first                  |
| **Time**   | London or NY AM kill zone               |
| **Trigger** | Sweep against the bias, then MSS with displacement |
| **Entry**  | CE of the FVG, ideally in OTE           |
| **Stop / target** | Beyond the sweep / opposite liquidity |
