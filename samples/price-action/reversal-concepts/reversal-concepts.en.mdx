## What Is a Reversal?

A **reversal** is when a trend ends and price starts trending the other way. Catching one early gives a large move, but it's harder than trading with the trend: most counter-trend signals are only pullbacks.

So reversals need **more** evidence, not less: exhaustion, a key level, and a structural shift.

---

## Signs of Exhaustion

| Type           | Sign                                                       |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Structure**  | A failed new high (uptrend) or low (downtrend); a lower high or higher low |
| **Momentum**   | Smaller impulse candles; deeper pullbacks                  |
| **Candles**    | Long rejection wicks, engulfing candles, dojis at the level |
| **Location**   | Price at a major higher-timeframe level                    |

## The Double Top

1. **Exhaustion:** the uptrend makes a high at **1.1000**, pulls back to **1.0950**, and the next push fails at **1.0998**: no new high.
2. **Neckline:** the **1.0950** low between the tops is the last higher low.
3. **Shift:** a candle **closes below 1.0950**: the bearish CHOCH. The uptrend's structure is broken.
4. **Retest and trade:**

| Level   | Price  | Note                                           |
| ------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0945 | Retest of the neckline from below              |
| Stop    | 1.0962 | Above the retest                               |
| Target  | 1.0900 | Neckline − pattern height (1.0950 − 50 pips)   |
| **R:R** | **1 : 2.6** | Risk 17 pips, reward 45 pips              |

A more conservative stop goes above the second top (about 1.1005). It's safer against a deep retest but drops the R:R below 1 : 1 for this target, so many traders skip the trade or wait for a lower-timeframe entry instead.

## Where Reversals Happen

1. **Major higher-timeframe levels:** weekly/daily support and resistance, previous highs and lows.
2. **Liquidity pools:** equal highs or lows that get swept.
3. **Deep Fibonacci retracements:** the 61.8–78.6% zone of the prior move.
4. **Round numbers:** 1.1000, 150.00, 2,400.

---

## Three Entry Models

| Model                     | Steps                                                         | Stop                  | Target                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Sweep + CHOCH**         | Price sweeps a key high/low, closes back, then breaks structure the other way | Beyond the sweep | The opposite liquidity |
| **Double top / bottom**   | Two failed tests, a neckline break, a retest                   | Above the retest (or the tops) | Neckline ± height |
| **Key-level rejection**   | A strong rejection candle at a major level, then confirmation  | Beyond the wick       | The next key level        |

See [Manipulation](/en/smc/manipulation) for the sweep model in detail.

## Reversal or Pullback?

| Clue                     | Reversal                              | Pullback                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Structure**            | The last HL (or LH) breaks — CHOCH    | Holds above the last HL            |
| **Depth**                | Beyond 78.6% of the last impulse      | 38–61.8%                           |
| **Higher timeframe**     | At a major level, or trend already weak | Mid-trend, away from levels      |
| **Follow-through**       | A BOS in the new direction            | The old trend resumes              |

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. GBP/USD double bottom

GBP/USD tests **1.2600** twice (1.2598, 1.2602) after a decline. The neckline between them is **1.2650**. A 4H candle closes above it, then retests from above. Buy **1.2655**, stop **1.2635**, target **1.2700** (neckline + 50): risk 20 pips, reward 45 pips, **R:R 1 : 2.3** (planned).

### 2. Gold sweep + CHOCH

Gold sweeps the equal highs at **2,450** to **2,458** and closes back below. On 1H, it breaks the last higher low at **2,438**. Sell the retrace at **2,442**, stop **2,461**, target the **2,400** support: risk $19, reward $42, **R:R 1 : 2.2** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Problem                               | Fix                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Calling tops in strong trends        | Fighting momentum                     | Wait for the CHOCH                    |
| Entering on the first rejection      | Most rejections are pullbacks          | Wait for a close beyond structure     |
| Ignoring the higher timeframe        | Reversing into the HTF trend           | Prefer reversals at HTF levels        |
| Stop too tight                       | A normal retest hits it                | Beyond the retest or structure        |
| Targets too ambitious                | New trends start slowly                | First target: the nearest level       |

## Summary

| Aspect      | Key point                                           |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Need**    | Exhaustion + a key level + a structure break        |
| **Trigger** | A close beyond the last HL/LH (CHOCH)               |
| **Entry**   | On the retest of the broken level                   |
| **Target**  | Pattern height, or the next level                   |
| **Caution** | Most counter-trend signals are pullbacks            |
