## What Is a Fakeout?

A **fakeout** (false breakout) is a move beyond a level that **fails**: price breaks out, draws traders in, then returns inside the level. The traders who bought the breakout (or sold the breakdown) are trapped, and their exits push price the other way.

---

## Why Fakeouts Happen

| Reason                 | What happens                                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stops beyond levels** | The break triggers the stops sitting just beyond the level, then runs out of new orders |
| **No follow-through**  | Not enough new buyers (or sellers) join after the first push          |
| **Big orders on the other side** | Large opposite orders absorb the breakout                   |
| **News spikes**        | A fast spike on a release reverses once the first reaction is over    |

<Callout type="info" title="Reframed: 'institutional liquidity collection'">
  The original explained fakeouts as institutions deliberately collecting liquidity. That's one possible explanation,
  but intent can't be seen on a chart. What you can see is the pattern: a break, no follow-through, and a close back
  inside. That pattern is what this lesson trades.
</Callout>

## The Two-Candle Trap

![EUR/USD 1H: a range 1.0875–1.0931, two candles close above resistance, one large candle closes back inside and engulfs both; sell the retrace at 1.0938, stop 1.0968, target 1.0870](/images/price-action/fakeouts/fakeout-trap-dark.png)

<Callout type="info" title="About this chart">
  The candles are **synthetic**, built around the levels in this lesson. They are not real prices. R:R is the planned
  risk-to-reward, not a result.
</Callout>

1. **The range:** EUR/USD holds between **1.0875 and 1.0931**.
2. **The bait:** two candles **close above** resistance, the second wicking to **1.0960**. Breakout buyers enter.
3. **The trap:** one large bearish candle closes back inside, below 1.0931, and **engulfs both** breakout bodies. Everyone who bought the breakout is now losing.
4. **The trade:**

| Level   | Price  | Note                                   |
| ------- | ------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0938 | On the retrace to the old level        |
| Stop    | 1.0968 | Above the 1.0960 high                  |
| Target  | 1.0870 | Below the range bottom                 |
| **R:R** | **1 : 2.3** | Risk 30 pips, reward 68 pips      |

A single candle that wicks above and closes back inside (a pin bar) is the one-candle version; see the [swing failure pattern](/en/price-action/swing-high-low).

## Types of Fakeouts

| Type                  | Shape                                                | Where it's common               |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Single-candle**     | A pin bar: wick beyond, close back inside             | Any key level                   |
| **Multi-candle trap** | One or more closes beyond, then a close back inside  | Range edges                     |
| **Stop hunt**         | A sharp run past equal highs/lows, then a reversal   | Obvious swing points            |
| **Session**           | London or New York takes the Asian range high or low first | Session opens             |
| **News**              | A spike on a release that reverses                    | NFP, CPI, rate decisions        |

## Fakeout or Real Breakout?

| Clue                | Fakeout                                  | Real breakout                    |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Close**           | Back inside the level                    | Holds beyond the level           |
| **Next candles**    | Return into the range                    | Continue away                    |
| **Wick**            | Long wick beyond the level               | Small wicks, large body          |
| **Trend**           | Against the higher-timeframe trend       | With it                          |
| **Retest**          | Fails from the other side                | Holds                            |

## Three Strategies

### 1. Pin bar at S/R

Wait for a pin bar whose wick pokes beyond a key level and closes back inside. Enter after it closes; stop beyond the wick plus 5–10 pips; target the opposite level or the range middle.

### 2. Close back inside (trap)

Let the breakout candles close beyond the level. Enter only after a candle closes back inside, ideally engulfing them. Stop beyond the fakeout's extreme; target the other side of the range.

### 3. Session sweep (BD time)

1. Mark the **Asian range** (about **6 AM – 12 PM BD**).
2. At the **London open** (about **1 PM BD** in summer, 2 PM in winter), watch for a run beyond the Asian high or low.
3. Wait for a close back inside and a lower-timeframe shift.
4. Stop beyond the sweep; target the opposite side of the Asian range and beyond.

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: Asian range time">
  The original gave the Asian range as "2:00 AM – 10:00 AM BD". Tokyo opens at **6:00 AM BD**, so the Asian range runs
  from about 6 AM to 12 PM BD. See [Session Liquidity](/en/smc/session-liquidity).
</Callout>

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. Gold stop hunt

Gold has equal highs at **2,400**. It runs to **2,410**, triggers the buy stops, and closes back below 2,400. Sell **2,390**, stop **2,415**, target **2,350**: risk $25, reward $40, **R:R 1 : 1.6** (planned).

### 2. NAS100 session fakeout (BD time)

The Asian range is **18,200 – 18,280**. Around **1:00 PM BD**, London breaks below 18,200 to **18,175**, then closes back inside. Buy **18,235**, stop **18,170**, target **18,350**: risk 65 points, reward 115 points, **R:R 1 : 1.8** (planned).

### 3. USD/JPY news fakeout

On NFP (**6:30 PM BD** in summer), USD/JPY spikes down to **149.20** and closes the 15m candle back above **149.50** support. Buy **149.55**, stop **149.15**, target **150.30**: risk 40 pips, reward 75 pips, **R:R 1 : 1.9** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                   | Fix                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Calling a fakeout before the close        | Wait for the close back inside                          |
| Fading every breakout                     | Fade only breaks against the trend, at key levels       |
| Stop just beyond the level                | Beyond the fakeout's extreme                            |
| Trading the first seconds of news         | Wait for the candle to close                            |
| Ignoring a breakout that holds its retest | That's a real breakout; switch sides or stand aside     |

## Summary

| Aspect      | Key point                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Fakeout** | A break that fails and closes back inside              |
| **Trap**    | Closes beyond, then one candle back inside engulfing them |
| **Entry**   | After the close back inside, or on the retrace         |
| **Stop**    | Beyond the fakeout's extreme                           |
| **Target**  | The other side of the range                            |
