## What Is Trend Analysis?

**Trend analysis** answers three questions before every trade:

1. **Which way** is price trending: up, down, or sideways?
2. **How strong** is the trend?
3. **Is it ending?**

Trading in the direction of a strong trend means pullbacks are your entries and the trend does part of the work.

<Callout type="info" title="Removed: '70% of profitable trades'">
  The original said "over 70% of profitable trades come from trading with the trend". There's no source for that
  figure. Trading with the trend is a sound default, but its value has to show in your own journal.
</Callout>

## Three States

| State          | Swings                       | What to do                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Uptrend**    | Higher highs, higher lows    | Buy pullbacks               |
| **Downtrend**  | Lower highs, lower lows      | Sell rallies                |
| **Sideways**   | Highs and lows at similar levels | Trade the edges or wait  |

---

## Ways to Identify the Trend

| Method                 | How                                              | Strength                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| **Swing points**       | Label HH/HL or LH/LL (see [Market Structure](/en/price-action/market-structure)) | Most reliable    |
| **200 EMA filter**     | Price above: bullish bias; below: bearish; crossing back and forth: no trend | Quick, but lags |
| **Trendline**          | Connect two or more higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs (downtrend) | Shows the trend's pace |
| **Channel**            | A parallel line through the highs (uptrend) or lows (downtrend) | Shows where the trend is stretched |

<Callout type="info" title="Removed: the '45-degree rule'">
  The original suggested judging trend health by a 45° angle. The angle on screen changes with how you zoom or scale
  the chart, so it isn't a consistent measure. Pullback depth and swing structure are.
</Callout>

## Strong vs Weak Trends

| Sign               | Strong trend                       | Weak / exhausting trend               |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Pullback depth** | 38–50% of the previous impulse     | 78% or more                           |
| **Impulses**       | Large candles, fast moves          | Smaller, overlapping candles          |
| **20 EMA**         | Price stays on one side            | Price crosses back and forth          |
| **Trendline**      | Holds                              | Breaks                                |
| **New highs (uptrend)** | Clearly beyond the last one   | Barely beyond, or a lower high        |

### Signs a trend is ending

1. A **deep pullback** (78%+ of the last impulse).
2. A **trendline break** with a close beyond it.
3. A **lower high** in an uptrend (or a higher low in a downtrend).
4. A **close beyond the last higher low** (uptrend) or **lower high** (downtrend): the CHOCH.

One sign is a warning; several together mean stop buying the dips.

## Multi-Timeframe Alignment

| Timeframe | Role                    | Example                               |
| --------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Daily     | The main trend          | Uptrend: HH/HL, above the 200 EMA     |
| 4H        | The pullback            | Price pulls back to an HL or the 50 EMA |
| 1H / 15m  | The entry trigger       | A bullish CHOCH or engulfing at the level |

Take trades where all three point the same way.

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. GBP/USD, uptrend pullback

Daily and 4H uptrend. Price pulls back 45% to the last 4H higher low and a bullish engulfing forms. Buy **1.2665**, stop **1.2630**, target **1.2780**: risk 35 pips, reward 115 pips, **R:R 1 : 3.3** (planned).

### 2. GBP/USD, downtrend continuation

4H downtrend. Price rallies to **1.2490**, the last lower-high zone near the 50 EMA, and rejects it. Sell **1.2480**, stop **1.2510**, target **1.2380**: risk 30 pips, reward 100 pips, **R:R 1 : 3.3** (planned).

### 3. Gold, trend exhaustion

After a long rally, gold makes a lower high, then closes below its last higher low. It retests the broken level from below. Sell **2,445**, stop **2,465**, target **2,390**: risk $20, reward $55, **R:R 1 : 2.75** (planned).

### 4. NAS100, intraday trend (BD time)

In the NY session (from **7:30 PM BD** in US summer), NAS100 makes higher highs and lows on the 15m. A pullback to the last higher low holds. Buy **18,285** at about **8:30 PM BD**, stop **18,255**, target a new high at **18,350**: risk 30 points, reward 65 points, **R:R 1 : 2.2** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                                |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Calling the top of a strong trend          | Wait for several ending signs, including a CHOCH   |
| Buying deep pullbacks in a tired trend     | 78%+ pullbacks are a warning, not a discount       |
| Using the 200 EMA alone                    | Confirm with swing structure                       |
| Forcing a trendline through every wick     | Use clear swing lows (or highs); allow small wicks |
| Ignoring the higher timeframe              | The daily trend outranks the 15m                   |

## Practice

1. On three daily charts, label the trend, draw the trendline, and measure each pullback's depth.
2. Mark every sign of exhaustion you can find and when the trend actually ended.
3. Check how many signs appeared before the CHOCH.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Direction**   | Swing points first, the 200 EMA as a filter        |
| **Strength**    | Shallow pullbacks, holds the trendline and 20 EMA  |
| **Exhaustion**  | Deep pullback, trendline break, lower high, CHOCH  |
| **Alignment**   | Daily trend + 4H pullback + 1H trigger             |
