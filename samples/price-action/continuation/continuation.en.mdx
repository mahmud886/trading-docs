## What Are Continuation Patterns?

**Continuation patterns** are pauses inside a trend that usually end with the trend carrying on. They let you join a trend after it has proved itself, with a clear stop beyond the pause.

### Why trends pause

After a fast move, some traders take profit and others wait for a better price. Price moves sideways or slightly against the trend while orders rebalance. If no strong opposite side appears, the trend resumes.

## Types of Continuation

| Pattern              | Shape                                                    | Rules of thumb                         |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Flag**             | A small channel sloping against the trend                | Retraces 23.6–50% of the pole          |
| **Pennant**          | A small symmetrical triangle after a pole                | Short; breaks in the pole's direction  |
| **Inside bar**       | A candle entirely within the previous candle's range     | Enter on a break of the mother bar's high (uptrend); stop below its low |
| **Order-block pullback** | A pullback to the last opposite candle before the impulse | Stop beyond the OB; target the next liquidity |

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: how deep a flag can be">
  The original allowed flags up to 61.8% in one place and 50% in another. A pullback that deep is usually called a
  pullback rather than a flag. This lesson uses **23.6–50%** for flags; deeper pauses are covered in
  [Pullbacks](/en/price-action/pullbacks).
</Callout>

## BOS as Confirmation

A continuation is confirmed when price **breaks the last swing in the trend's direction** (a BOS):

- **Bullish:** a close above the last higher high after the pause.
- **Bearish:** a close below the last lower low after the pause.

Until the BOS, the pause could still turn into a reversal.

## Three Entry Strategies

| Strategy              | Entry                                         | Stop                         | Target                    |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Flag breakout**     | Close beyond the flag line                    | Beyond the flag's extreme    | Flagpole height projected |
| **BOS + OB**          | After a BOS, on the pullback into the order block | Beyond the OB             | The next swing or liquidity |
| **FVG retest**        | On the retrace into the FVG the impulse left  | Beyond the FVG               | The next liquidity        |

## Continuation or Reversal?

| Clue               | Continuation                   | Reversal                       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Pullback depth** | 23.6–61.8%                     | Beyond 78.6%                   |
| **Candles in the pause** | Small, overlapping       | Large, against the trend       |
| **Structure**      | Holds the last higher low      | Breaks it (CHOCH)              |
| **Breakout**       | In the trend's direction       | Against it                     |

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. EUR/USD bear flag

EUR/USD drops 90 pips from **1.0950 to 1.0860**, then drifts up in a small channel to **1.0890** (33%). A 1H candle closes below the channel at **1.0878**. Sell **1.0878**, stop **1.0895** above the flag, target **1.0788** (entry − 90-pip pole): risk 17 pips, reward 90 pips, **R:R 1 : 5.3** (planned). A target at the next support, if closer, is more conservative.

### 2. Gold inside bar

In a 4H uptrend, a candle forms entirely inside the previous candle (high **2,418**, low **2,402**). Buy on a break of **2,418**, stop **2,400** below the mother bar, target the last swing high at **2,460**: risk $18, reward $42, **R:R 1 : 2.3** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                | Fix                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Calling a deep retracement a flag      | Flags retrace 23.6–50%                             |
| Entering before the breakout close     | Wait for the close beyond the flag line            |
| Trading flags against the higher timeframe | Only with the trend                           |
| Ignoring a large counter-trend candle  | It's a warning of reversal, not a flag             |
| Using the pole target into a major level | Target the level if it comes first              |
