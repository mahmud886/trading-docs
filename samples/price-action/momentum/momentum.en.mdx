## What Is Momentum?

**Momentum** is how fast and how hard price is moving in one direction. You don't need an oscillator to see it: the candles show it directly.

---

## Reading Momentum From Candles

| Sign                  | Strong momentum                        | Weak momentum                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Body size**         | Large compared with recent candles     | Small                                |
| **Body-to-range**     | Body is more than ~70% of the range    | Wicks are larger than the body       |
| **Close position**    | Near the high (up) or low (down)       | Mid-candle                           |
| **Overlap**           | Each candle opens near the last close and moves on | Candles overlap heavily  |
| **Follow-through**    | Several in a row                       | One candle, then nothing             |

## Impulse vs Correction

| Impulse                                | Correction                               |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Moves with the trend                   | Moves against it                         |
| Large bodies, few wicks                | Small, overlapping candles               |
| Fast                                   | Slow, choppy                             |
| Breaks structure                       | Stays inside the previous leg            |

In a healthy trend, impulses are clearly stronger than corrections. When corrections start looking like impulses, the trend is in trouble.

---

## Spotting a Momentum Shift

1. **Pushes fade:** each impulse in the trend direction has smaller bodies.
2. **New extremes on little effort:** a new high (or low) made with small candles.
3. **The opposite side gets bigger:** the first counter-move has larger bodies than the last impulse.
4. **Structure confirms:** a close beyond the last higher low (or lower high) — see [Market Structure](/en/price-action/market-structure).

**Trading the shift:** wait for the first counter-impulse, then enter on the weak pullback that follows. Stop beyond the pullback; target the start of the last strong impulse or the next level.

---

## Rules of Thumb

1. Trade in the direction of the **stronger** side.
2. Don't fade strong momentum; wait for it to fade first.
3. Don't chase after three or more large candles.
4. Enter at the **end of corrections** for the best R:R.
5. Treat a new high on small candles as a warning, not a signal to buy.

## Momentum and Timeframes

| Timeframe | Use                          |
| --------- | ---------------------------- |
| Daily     | The dominant momentum        |
| 4H / 1H   | Setup momentum: impulses and corrections |
| 15m       | Entry momentum               |

Trade lower-timeframe momentum only when it agrees with the higher timeframe.

### Example (no chart) — gold, fading rally

On 4H, gold makes three pushes up to **2,450**, with average bodies of $14, $9 and $4. Then two bearish candles of about $12 each close below the last higher low at **2,436**. Sell the pullback at **2,440**, stop **2,452**, target **2,410**: risk $12, reward $30, **R:R 1 : 2.5** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                  | Fix                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Buying a new high made on tiny candles   | Compare its bodies with the earlier impulses          |
| Shorting a single large red candle       | Wait for follow-through and structure                 |
| Entering in the middle of an impulse     | Wait for the correction                               |
| Reading momentum on one timeframe only   | Check the higher timeframe first                      |

## Practice

1. On a 1H chart, mark five trends and measure the average body of each impulse.
2. Note where the bodies started shrinking and when structure broke.
3. Compare the size of the first counter-move with the last impulse.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Concept       | Key point                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Momentum**  | Speed and strength, seen in bodies and closes     |
| **Strong**    | Large bodies, closes at the extreme, little overlap |
| **Fading**    | Shrinking bodies, new extremes on little effort   |
| **Shift**     | Counter-move bodies larger than the last impulse  |
| **Trade**     | Enter on the weak pullback after the shift        |
