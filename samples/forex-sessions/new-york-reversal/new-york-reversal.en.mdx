## What Is the New York Reversal?

A **New York reversal** is when the New York session turns London's direction around: London trends one way, and after New York opens — often around US data — price takes out London's extreme, fails, and runs the other way.

Most days New York **continues** London's move or ranges. A reversal is the exception, and it needs evidence.

<Callout type="warning" title="Clarified">
  The original example had London making the low, New York dipping below it and then rallying — which continues
  London's direction rather than reversing it. This lesson uses the plain meaning: New York reverses London's trend.
</Callout>

## Reversal or Continuation?

| Leans toward a reversal                          | Leans toward a continuation                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| London's move is already large for the day        | London's move has only just started           |
| London ran into a daily/weekly level             | Open space ahead                              |
| US data surprises against London's direction     | No data, or data in London's direction        |
| Daily bias is opposite to London's move           | Daily bias matches London                     |
| **A sweep of London's extreme + a CHOCH**        | Pullbacks hold above London's higher lows     |

The last row is the trigger; the others are context. Without a sweep and a CHOCH, treat New York as a continuation.

## Rules

1. Before 6:00 PM BD (7:00 PM winter), note London's direction, extreme and last swing.
2. Wait for New York to take London's extreme — often with the 6:30 PM BD data (7:30 PM winter).
3. Require a close back inside and a **CHOCH** against London's trend.
4. Enter on the retest; stop beyond the swept extreme.
5. Targets: London's last swing back, then London's start or 50% of London's range.
6. If nothing has happened by about 10:00 PM BD (London's close), stand aside.

### Example (no chart) — a bullish New York reversal on EUR/USD

London sells EUR/USD from 1.0890 to **1.0842** by 6:00 PM BD. US data at 6:30 PM drops it to **1.0834**, then a 15m candle closes back at **1.0846**. At 7:00 PM a close above London's last lower high **1.0852** confirms the CHOCH. Buy the retest at **1.0850**, stop **1.0831**, target **1.0880**: risk 19 pips, reward 30, **1 : 1.6** planned.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                       | Fix                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Fading London just because New York opened    | Wait for a sweep and a CHOCH                          |
| Selling the data spike itself                 | Wait for the close back inside                        |
| Stop inside the sweep                         | Stop beyond the extreme                               |
| Expecting a reversal every day                | Most days continue or range                           |

## Practice
