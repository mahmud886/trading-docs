## One Job per Block

Each session has a different job for you. Asia's job is **preparation**, London's is **finding the day's first setup**, the 6:00 PM hour's is **deciding** whether New York continues or reverses, and 10:00 PM's is **stopping**. Using one strategy for all of them is how traders end up trading the quiet hours.

<Callout type="warning" title="Removed">
  The original said the right strategy "dramatically improves your win rate" and called London's the "highest success
  rate for SMC setups". Neither has a source. Sessions change **how often** setups appear and **how far** price moves —
  they don't change whether a given setup works.
</Callout>

| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |

## The Strategies

### Asia — prepare, don't trade

Mark the high and low of 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM BD and write your daily bias. Range trading in Asia is possible on JPY and AUD pairs but needs small targets and watching the spread — see [Tokyo Session](/en/forex-sessions/tokyo).

### London — sweep reversal or opening-range break

| Setup                  | Condition                                                   | Reference                                              |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sweep reversal**     | Asian high/low taken, close back inside, CHOCH               | [Asian Range](/en/forex-sessions/asian-range)          |
| **Opening-range break** | 1:00 – 1:30 PM range; a close outside it; a retest holds   | [London Session](/en/forex-sessions/london)            |

### New York — decide at 6:00 PM

| London's move is…                               | Prefer                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fresh, in open space, with the bias             | Continuation: buy/sell the pullback with London            |
| Stretched, at a big level, against the bias     | Reversal — only after New York sweeps London's extreme and a CHOCH forms ([New York Reversal](/en/forex-sessions/new-york-reversal)) |
| Unclear                                         | Skip, or trade smaller                                     |

### Stop — 10:00 PM

London closes at 10:00 PM BD (11:00 PM in winter). After that, ranges often shrink; manage open trades and stop opening new ones.

## A Worked Day

EUR/USD, summer. The daily bias (written 11:30 AM): **long** on pullbacks, invalidation below the daily low.

1. **12:00 PM:** Asian range **1.0852 – 1.0872**.
2. **1:00 – 1:30 PM:** London dips to 1.0846 — watch only.
3. **1:45 PM:** a candle closes back inside; a 5m CHOCH follows. Buy the retest at **1.0860**, stop **1.0842**, target **1.0892**: risk 18 pips, reward 32, **1 : 1.8** planned.
4. **6:00 PM decision:** London has moved 40 pips with the bias and sits in open space → **continuation**, not reversal.
5. **7:10 PM:** pullback to London's last higher low; buy **1.0884**, stop **1.0868**, target **1.0915**: risk 16, reward 31, **1 : 1.9** planned.
6. **10:00 PM:** London closes — manage or close any open trade; no new entries.

The day had two planned setups, each with a defined risk. Many days have none.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                   | Fix                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trading Asia's range like London's        | Smaller targets; or skip                               |
| Deciding continuation vs reversal after the fact | Decide at 6:00 PM with the criteria above       |
| Reversal trades without a sweep and CHOCH | They need both                                         |
| Using summer times in winter              | Add one hour to London and New York                    |
| No stop time                              | Choose one (e.g. 10:00 PM BD) and keep it              |

## Practice

1. Write your own playbook with the blocks you can actually attend, in BD time for both seasons.
2. For 15 days, note which block gave a setup that met your rules.
3. Count the setups you took outside your playbook and their results.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
