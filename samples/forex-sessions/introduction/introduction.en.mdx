## Forex Trading Sessions Overview

The forex market trades **24 hours a day, five days a week**, passing from one financial centre to the next: Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York. Activity is not even across those hours — it rises when big centres open and peaks when two of them are open together.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bangladesh traders 🇧🇩">
  All times are **Bangladesh time (UTC+6)**. Bangladesh doesn't use daylight saving, but the UK, US and Australia do —
  so London and New York hours **shift by one hour** in BD time twice a year. "BST" can mean Bangladesh Standard Time
  or British Summer Time; this site writes "BD time" to avoid the mix-up.
</Callout>

## The Four Major Sessions

| Session         | Local hours          | **BD time (US/UK summer)** | **BD time (winter)** | UTC (summer)       |
| --------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| 🇦🇺 **Sydney**   | 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM    | 3:00 AM – 12:00 PM         | 2:00 AM – 11:00 AM   | 21:00 – 06:00      |
| 🇯🇵 **Tokyo**    | 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM    | 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM          | 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM    | 00:00 – 09:00      |
| 🇬🇧 **London**   | 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM    | 1:00 PM – 10:00 PM         | 2:00 PM – 11:00 PM   | 07:00 – 16:00      |
| 🇺🇸 **New York** | 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM    | 6:00 PM – 3:00 AM          | 7:00 PM – 4:00 AM    | 12:00 – 21:00      |

Sydney's own summer is the northern winter, so its BD hours move the **other way** (earlier) when the US and UK are on winter time.

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected">
  The original table put Tokyo at 5:00 AM – 2:00 PM BD and gave no winter hours. Tokyo is 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM BD all
  year (Japan has no daylight saving); London and New York are one hour later in winter.
</Callout>

## Key Overlaps

| Overlap                  | BD time (summer)    | BD time (winter)    | What to expect                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Tokyo–London**         | 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM   | 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM   | London takes over from Asia; the Asian range is often tested |
| **London–New York**      | 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM  | 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM  | Usually the most active hours; US data at 6:30 PM (summer) |

<Callout type="warning" title="Removed: &quot;70%+ of daily volume&quot;">
  The original said more than 70% of daily volume happens in the London–New York overlap. We couldn't find a source for
  that figure. What's well documented is that London is the largest FX trading centre and that the overlap is usually
  the busiest part of the day — without a fixed percentage.
</Callout>

## Kill Zones in Bangladesh Time

Kill zones are the windows ICT-style traders watch most closely. In US/UK **summer**:

| Window             | BD time (summer)     | New York time        | Purpose                                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Asian range        | 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM   | 8:00 PM – 2:00 AM    | The range London often tests                |
| London kill zone   | 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM   | 2:00 AM – 5:00 AM    | First directional move of the day           |
| London silver bullet | 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM  | 3:00 AM – 4:00 AM    | One-hour window inside London               |
| New York kill zone | 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM    | 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM   | US open and data; continuation or reversal  |
| NY AM silver bullet | 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM   | 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM  | One-hour window after the data              |
| London close       | 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM   | 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM  | Profit-taking; possible reversals           |
| NY PM silver bullet | 12:00 AM – 1:00 AM  | 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM    | Afternoon window                            |

Add **one hour** to every BD time in winter. See [Kill Zones (BD Time)](/en/forex-sessions/kill-zones).

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected">
  The original put the New York kill zone at 7:00 – 10:00 PM BD and an "AM silver bullet" at 9:00 – 10:00 AM BD. The NY
  kill zone (7 – 10 AM New York) is 5:00 – 8:00 PM BD in summer, and the AM silver bullet (10 – 11 AM New York) is
  8:00 – 9:00 PM BD.
</Callout>

## Which Hours Should You Trade?

| If you can trade…        | Consider                                     | Why                                          |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Afternoons               | London open, 1:00 – 4:00 PM BD               | First directional move; Asian range as reference |
| Evenings                 | London–NY overlap, 6:00 – 10:00 PM BD         | Most active hours; US data                    |
| Mornings only            | Tokyo, 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM BD                  | Quieter; JPY and AUD pairs; smaller ranges    |
| Two hours a day          | 6:30 – 8:30 PM BD (summer)                    | US data and the NY kill zone                  |

Pick **one** window and trade it consistently; results from a fixed window are easier to review.
