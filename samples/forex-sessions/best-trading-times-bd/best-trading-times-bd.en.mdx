## There's No Single "Best" Time

The most active hours for forex are London and the London–New York overlap, which fall in the Bangladesh afternoon and evening — convenient. But the best window for **you** is the one you can trade **consistently, attentively and with a plan**. A tired 11 PM trader in a "good" window usually does worse than a rested 3 PM trader in a quieter one.

## Choosing One Window

1. Write down the hours you can give **full attention** (not "I can glance at the phone").
2. Match them to the table. If two windows fit, start with the one that suits your bias-setting time.
3. Pick **one** window for the next 20 trading days. Journal every trade in it.
4. Review: spreads, how many valid setups appeared, and your results.
5. Only then add or change windows.

### Example (no chart) — an evening trader

A trader who finishes work at 5:30 PM BD picks **6:30 – 9:30 PM**. At **12:00 PM** (lunch break) they mark the Asian range and write the daily bias. At 6:00 PM they check London's move and the 6:30 PM data (7:30 PM in winter). They trade only a pullback with London or a sweep + CHOCH reversal, risk 0.5% per trade with a maximum of two trades, and stop at 9:30 PM. In winter the same plan runs one hour later.

## Risks to Plan For

| Risk                      | When (BD)                                           | What to do                                       |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Weekend close             | Friday 5:00 PM New York = Saturday 3:00 AM BD (4:00 AM winter) | Close or protect positions before the weekend; gaps happen at the Monday open |
| Scheduled data            | See [Economic Releases](/en/forex-sessions/economic-releases)  | Stand aside or reduce size around the release    |
| Holidays                  | US/UK/Japan holidays                                | Check the calendar; thin sessions behave differently |
| Fatigue                   | Late-night windows                                  | Fixed stop time; max trades per day              |
| Clock changes             | March and October/November                          | Re-check every window after the change           |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                       | Fix                                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Trading every window "to catch more"          | One window for 20 days, then review                |
| Trading tired in a "better" window            | Choose the window you can attend alert             |
| Not re-checking after clock changes           | Add a calendar reminder for March and October      |
| Holding over the weekend without a plan       | Decide on Thursday what happens before Friday's close |

---

## Practice

1. Write your own windows in BD time for both seasons, and set phone alarms for the start and the stop.
2. For 20 days, trade only inside the window; note spreads and valid setups.
3. Compare your results in the window with any trades outside it.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
