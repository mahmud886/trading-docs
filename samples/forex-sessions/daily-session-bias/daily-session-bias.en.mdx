## What Is Daily Session Bias?

**Daily bias** is the direction you're prepared to trade today — decided **before** London opens, from the daily and 4-hour charts — plus the level that would prove you wrong. It's a **filter**: it tells you which setups to take and which to skip. It is not a forecast.

## When to Do It (BD time)

| Moment                            | BD time (summer)        | BD time (winter)        |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Daily candle closes (5 PM New York) | 3:00 AM                | 4:00 AM                 |
| **Write today's bias**            | Before ~12:00 PM        | Before ~1:00 PM         |
| London opens                      | 1:00 PM                 | 2:00 PM                 |

Do it once, write it down, and don't rewrite it during the day unless the invalidation level is hit.

| 2   | Where is price in the dealing range?    | Range **1.2570 – 1.2712**, 50% = **1.2641**; price ~1.2615 | Discount |

| 4   | Where is the draw on liquidity?         | Unswept high at **1.2712**                               | Target above |

**Bias: long, on pullbacks only.** Target area **1.2712**. **Invalidation:** a daily close below the last higher low **1.2580**. All four agree here; if two or more disagree, there's no bias — skip the day or trade smaller and shorter.

## Applying Bias to the Sessions

| Bias    | London                                           | New York                                         |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Long    | Prefer a sweep of the Asian low followed by a close back inside | Buy pullbacks into London's move       |
| Short   | Prefer a sweep of the Asian high followed by a close back inside | Sell rallies into London's move       |
| None    | Let London show direction; trade smaller         | Follow what London and the data confirm          |

A counter-bias setup isn't forbidden — it needs more evidence (a sweep, a CHOCH and a big level) and a smaller size.

### Example (no chart) — a short bias on EUR/USD

Daily structure lower highs and lower lows; yesterday closed down; price is in premium of the 1.0780 – 1.0940 range (50% = 1.0860, price 1.0905); an unswept low sits at **1.0780**. Bias: **short, on rallies**. Invalidation: a daily close above **1.0940**. At 1:30 PM BD London sweeps the Asian high and closes back inside; a CHOCH follows. Sell the retest at **1.0898**, stop **1.0918**, target **1.0850**: risk 20 pips, reward 48, **1 : 2.4** planned.

## Rules

1. Write the bias **before** London opens — not after the first move.
2. Include an **invalidation level** and a **target**.
3. Two or more disagreeing checks = no bias.
4. Prefer setups in the bias direction; require more evidence and smaller size against it.
5. If the invalidation level is hit, drop the bias; don't argue with the chart.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                   | Fix                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bias from yesterday's candle colour alone | Use the full checklist                                  |
| Deciding after seeing London's first move | Write it before the open                                |
| No invalidation level                     | State the level that cancels it                         |
| Treating bias as a prediction             | It's a filter; losses still happen on biased trades     |

## Practice

1. For 15 days, write the four-step bias before 12:00 PM BD, with target and invalidation.
2. After the day, mark: bias right, wrong, or invalidated.
3. Compare days where all four checks agreed with days where they didn't.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
