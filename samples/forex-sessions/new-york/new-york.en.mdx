## New York Session Overview

New York is the second-largest FX centre and the home of the most market-moving data. For Bangladesh it's the **evening and late night** — and its first four hours overlap with London.

| Detail              | Info                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🕐 **BD time**      | **6:00 PM – 3:00 AM** (US summer) · **7:00 PM – 4:00 AM** (winter)        |
| 🌐 **Local / UTC**  | 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM New York · 12:00 – 21:00 UTC (summer)                  |
| 📊 **Activity**     | High during the London overlap; much lower after London closes            |
| 💱 **Main markets** | EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, gold, US indices                     |

## US Events in BD Time

| Event                                 | New York time | **BD (summer)** | **BD (winter)** |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| NFP, CPI, GDP, retail sales, jobless claims | 8:30 AM | **6:30 PM**    | **7:30 PM**     |
| US stock market open (NYSE, Nasdaq)   | 9:30 AM       | 7:30 PM         | 8:30 PM         |
| ISM, consumer confidence              | 10:00 AM      | 8:00 PM         | 9:00 PM         |
| London closes                         | 11:00 AM*     | 10:00 PM        | 11:00 PM        |
| FOMC decision                         | 2:00 PM       | 12:00 AM        | 1:00 AM         |
| NY session end / daily close          | 5:00 PM       | 3:00 AM         | 4:00 AM         |

\*London closes at 4:00 PM London time.

| Window                     | New York time      | **BD (summer)**     | **BD (winter)**     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| **NY kill zone**           | 7:00 – 10:00 AM    | **5:00 – 8:00 PM**  | 6:00 – 9:00 PM      |
| NY AM silver bullet        | 10:00 – 11:00 AM   | 8:00 – 9:00 PM      | 9:00 – 10:00 PM     |
| London close window        | 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 8:00 – 10:00 PM    | 9:00 – 11:00 PM     |
| NY PM silver bullet        | 2:00 – 3:00 PM     | 12:00 – 1:00 AM     | 1:00 – 2:00 AM      |

## How a New York Evening Often Unfolds

| Time (BD, summer) | What often happens                                         |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6:00 – 6:30 PM    | Positioning before data                                    |
| 6:30 – 7:30 PM    | Data reaction; spreads widen briefly                       |
| 7:30 – 10:00 PM   | Stocks open; London–NY overlap — usually the busiest hours |
| 10:00 PM          | London closes; London's move may continue, pause or reverse — see [New York Reversal](/en/forex-sessions/new-york-reversal) |
| 10:00 PM – 3:00 AM | Quieter; FOMC at 12:00 AM on decision days               |

### Example (no chart) — USD/CAD on a quiet data day

No major US data. USD/CAD pulls back in the London–NY overlap to the London session's breakout level **1.3720** and a 15m bullish candle closes at **1.3726** at 8:15 PM BD. Buy **1.3726**, stop **1.3712**, target **1.3760**: risk 14 pips, reward 34, **1 : 2.4** planned; close by 11:00 PM BD if not at target.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                     | Fix                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Using summer times in winter                | US data is 7:30 PM BD in winter                      |
| Entering right before 6:30 PM data          | Wait for the release, then the pullback              |
| Trading after midnight out of boredom       | Liquidity is thinner; set a stop time                |
| Holding through FOMC without a plan         | Close, reduce or accept the risk in advance          |
