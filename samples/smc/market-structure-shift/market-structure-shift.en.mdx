## What Is a Market Structure Shift?

A **market structure shift (MSS)** is a break of the last swing **against** the trend, made with **displacement**: one or more large, decisive candles that usually leave a [fair value gap](/en/smc/fair-value-gap).

Many traders use MSS and [CHOCH](/en/smc/choch) interchangeably. The useful distinction: **a CHOCH is any first close against the trend; an MSS is a CHOCH with displacement.** The displacement is what makes it more convincing.

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: 'the algorithm's intent'">
  The original said an MSS shows the "market-making algorithm" changing its intent. That's ICT's framing, not a fact.
  What the chart shows is simpler: the side that was winning lost control decisively.
</Callout>

## BOS vs CHOCH vs MSS

| Concept   | What it is                                   | Direction     |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **BOS**   | Close beyond the last swing, with the trend   | Continuation  |
| **CHOCH** | First close beyond the last swing, against the trend | Possible reversal |
| **MSS**   | A CHOCH with displacement                    | Stronger reversal signal |

## The Sweep → Displacement → Retrace Model

![Bullish MSS: a sweep of 1.0805 to 1.0800, three strong candles close above the 1.0830 lower high, the higher low retraces into the FVG](/images/smc/market-structure-shift/bullish-mss-dark.png)

<Callout type="info" title="About this example">
  The candles are **synthetic**, built to show the model. They are not real prices or a real trade. R:R is the planned
  risk-to-reward.
</Callout>

1. **Downtrend:** lower highs and lower lows.
2. **Sweep:** price drops to **1.0800**, under the 1.0805 low, and closes back above. Sell-side stops are taken.
3. **MSS:** three strong bullish candles (31 pips together) close above the **1.0830** lower high and leave an FVG at **1.0821 – 1.0825**.
4. **Retrace:** the first pullback forms a higher low at **1.0825**, in the FVG. Buy.
5. **Confirmation:** a BOS above 1.0862 later confirms the new uptrend.

| Level   | Price  | Note                           |
| ------- | ------ | ------------------------------ |
| Entry   | 1.0825 | Top of the FVG                 |
| Stop    | 1.0798 | Below the 1.0800 sweep         |
| Target  | 1.0880 | Buy-side liquidity above       |
| **R:R** | **1 : 2.0** | Risk 27 pips, reward 55 pips |

## Identifying an MSS

| Bullish MSS                                   | Bearish MSS                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Downtrend: lower highs, lower lows            | Uptrend: higher highs, higher lows              |
| Sell-side sweep under a key low               | Buy-side sweep over a key high                  |
| Displacement closes **above** the last lower high | Displacement closes **below** the last higher low |
| FVG/OB left below                             | FVG/OB left above                               |

## Entry Models

| Model          | Entry                                        | Stop                    | Notes                         |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **MSS + FVG**  | Limit in the FVG (top, or its 50%)           | Beyond the sweep        | Deeper entry = better R:R, more misses |
| **MSS + OB**   | The last opposite candle before the displacement | Beyond the sweep or the OB | Can confirm with a lower-timeframe CHOCH in the OB |

Target the next liquidity in the new direction: the last lower high's highs, equal highs, or a higher-timeframe level.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Fix                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Calling a slow drift through a swing an MSS | No displacement = a CHOCH at most                 |
| Entering on the displacement candle  | Wait for the retrace into the FVG/OB                   |
| Ignoring the sweep                   | An MSS without a sweep fails more often                |
| Tight stop inside the sweep          | The stop goes beyond the sweep extreme                 |

## Practice

1. Find 10 MSS on 1H charts. Mark the sweep, the displacement candles, the FVG and the retrace.
2. Measure how deep each retrace went into the FVG (top, 50%, bottom, through).
3. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Step             | Rule                                          |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Sweep**        | Liquidity taken beyond the last swing         |
| **Displacement** | Strong candles break the opposite swing       |
| **Retrace**      | Enter in the FVG/OB the displacement left     |
| **Stop / target** | Beyond the sweep / the next liquidity        |
