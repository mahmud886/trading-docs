## What Are Smart Money Concepts?

**Smart Money Concepts (SMC)** is a way of reading price through the footprints that large orders leave: where stops cluster (**liquidity**), how trends are built and broken (**market structure**), and the zones that strong moves start from (**order blocks** and **fair value gaps**).

"Smart money" means the large participants, such as banks, funds and dealers, whose orders are big enough to move price. SMC doesn't claim to see their orders. It reads the traces those orders leave on an ordinary price chart.

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: unsupported numbers">
  The original said institutions are "over 90% of daily volume" and that they trade from "dark pools" and order flow
  retail can't see. Those figures had no source and vary a lot by market. The useful point stands without them: big
  orders need liquidity, and the chart shows where that liquidity sits.
</Callout>

## The Cycle Behind It

| Phase            | What happens                                         |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Accumulation** | Price ranges while positions are built               |
| **Manipulation** | A false move takes liquidity on one side             |
| **Expansion**    | The real move, with displacement                      |
| **Distribution** | Price ranges again near the opposite extreme         |

This comes from Wyckoff's work; see [Accumulation](/en/smc/accumulation), [Manipulation](/en/smc/manipulation) and [Wyckoff Relation](/en/smc/wyckoff-relation).

## SMC and ICT

| Feature        | SMC                                   | ICT                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Origin**     | Taught by many educators, built largely on ICT's ideas | Michael Huddleston's own teaching |
| **Focus**      | Structure, liquidity, zones           | Same, plus a strong focus on time      |
| **Names**      | BOS, CHOCH, OB, FVG                   | MSS, PD arrays, OTE, kill zones, PO3   |

The ideas overlap a lot. This site's [ICT section](/en/ict/introduction) covers time, sessions and the named ICT models.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake                         | Fix                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trading every order block       | Only zones with context: structure, premium/discount, liquidity taken |
| Ignoring the higher timeframe   | Trade in its direction                                     |
| Entering before the setup completes | Wait for sweep → CHOCH → retrace                        |
| Marking everything              | Keep only the levels that matter on your timeframe         |
| Mixing up BOS and CHOCH         | BOS continues the trend; CHOCH is the first sign it may end |

## Summary

| Concept              | Purpose                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Market structure** | Direction                               |
| **BOS / CHOCH**      | Continuation / first sign of reversal   |
| **Liquidity**        | Where stops sit and where price goes    |
| **Premium/discount** | Where to sell and where to buy          |
| **OB / FVG**         | Where to enter                          |
