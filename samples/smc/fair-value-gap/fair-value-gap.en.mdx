## What Is a Fair Value Gap?

A **fair value gap (FVG)** is a three-candle pattern where the middle candle moves so far that the **first and third candles don't overlap**. The price range between them was traded only in one direction, quickly. Price often comes back to it later.

| Candle       | Role                                            |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Candle 1** | Its **high** (bullish FVG) or **low** (bearish FVG) is one edge |
| **Candle 2** | The displacement candle: large body, little overlap |
| **Candle 3** | Its **low** (bullish) or **high** (bearish) is the other edge |

- **Bullish FVG:** candle 3's low is **above** candle 1's high.
- **Bearish FVG:** candle 3's high is **below** candle 1's low.

The midpoint is the **consequent encroachment (CE)**, the 50% level.

1. **London** drops to **1.0850**, then three candles rally to **1.0920**.
2. Candle 1's high is **1.0870**, candle 3's low is **1.0885**: a bullish FVG at **1.0870 – 1.0885**, CE **1.0878**.
3. Price drifts back over the next few hours, touches the top of the gap, then reaches the CE at **5 PM BD**, the start of the NY session.
4. Buy **1.0878**.

| Level   | Price  | Note                          |
| ------- | ------ | ----------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0878 | CE of the FVG                 |
| Stop    | 1.0866 | Below the FVG                 |
| Target  | 1.0950 | Above the 1.0920 high         |
| **R:R** | **1 : 6.0** | Risk 12 pips, reward 72 pips |

## How Price Reacts to an FVG

| Reaction            | What happens                                   | Reading                              |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Edge rejection**  | Touches the near edge and turns                | Strong; you may miss a CE entry      |
| **50% (CE)**        | Fills to the midpoint and turns                | The classic entry                    |
| **Full fill**       | Trades through the whole gap, then turns       | Still valid if it closes back        |
| **Invalidation**    | **Closes** beyond the far edge                 | The FVG has failed; it may become an [inversion FVG](/en/ict/fvg) |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: 'price will return'">
  The original called FVGs "unfinished business" that price "has a natural tendency" to fill, and said institutions
  have orders there. Many FVGs are revisited; many in strong trends are not, for days or weeks. And nobody can see whose
  orders are in the gap. Treat an FVG as a likely reaction zone in the direction of the trend, not a promise.
</Callout>

## Entry Models

| Model                        | How                                                          | Trade-off                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| **1. Limit at the CE**       | Limit order at the 50%; stop beyond the FVG                  | Best price; misses edge rejections |
| **2. Reaction entry**        | Wait for a rejection or lower-timeframe CHOCH inside the FVG | More confirmation; worse price |
| **3. FVG + OB confluence**   | Enter where the FVG overlaps the [order block](/en/smc/order-blocks) | Fewer setups, cleaner zones |

**Stop:** beyond the far edge of the FVG, or beyond the swing it came from. **Target:** the next liquidity.

## FVGs With Other Concepts

| Combination                   | Why it helps                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sweep → FVG**               | The FVG comes from the reversal after liquidity was taken |
| **MSS → FVG**                 | The FVG is in the displacement that broke structure. See [MSS](/en/smc/market-structure-shift). |
| **FVG in discount / premium** | Buy FVGs in discount, sell FVGs in premium. See [Premium & Discount](/en/smc/premium-discount). |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Fix                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trading every FVG                    | Only FVGs from displacement, in the trend direction    |
| Counter-trend FVGs in strong trends  | They often don't hold                                  |
| Stop inside the FVG                  | Beyond the far edge                                    |
| FVGs in dead sessions                | Gaps from thin markets mean less                       |
| Treating a wick through as failure   | Only a close beyond the far edge invalidates it        |

## Summary

| Element        | Rule                                                 |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **FVG**        | Candle 1 and candle 3 don't overlap                  |
| **CE**         | The 50% level, the classic entry                     |
| **Valid**      | Until a candle closes beyond the far edge            |
| **Best**       | From displacement, after a sweep/MSS, in the trend direction |
