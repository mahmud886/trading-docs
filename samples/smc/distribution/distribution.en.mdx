## What Is Distribution?

**Distribution** is a **range at the end of a rally** where selling gradually absorbs buying. Price goes sideways near the highs, often looks strong, and then breaks down into a **markdown**. It's the mirror image of [accumulation](/en/smc/accumulation).

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: who is doing what">
  The original described institutions "offloading" while "retail traders are buying". You can't see who is on each
  side. What you can see is the pattern: rallies that stop making progress, a false break above the range, and a break
  below it on rising volume.
</Callout>

## What Volume Does

| Stage           | Volume                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Buying climax   | **Highest**                                     |
| Later rallies   | **Weaker**: buyers are running out              |
| Sign of weakness | **Rising** on the down-moves                   |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: volume">
  The original said volume is "increasing on pushes UP (supply entering)". In Wyckoff's model, after the climax the
  **rallies** come on falling volume and the **breakdown** comes on rising volume.
</Callout>

## Trading Distribution

1. Find a **range after a rally**, at a higher-timeframe resistance or in [premium](/en/smc/premium-discount).
2. Wait for the **UTAD**: a sweep above the range high that closes back inside.
3. Look for a **bearish CHOCH** on a lower timeframe.
4. Sell the **test** or the OB/FVG from the reversal, or wait for the SOW and sell the **LPSY**.
5. **Stop** above the UTAD high.
6. **Target** the range bottom first, then the next higher-timeframe level.

| Level   | Price  | Note                         |
| ------- | ------ | ---------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0942 | On the failed test after the UTAD |
| Stop    | 1.0972 | Above the 1.0968 UTAD        |
| Target  | 1.0800 | Next higher-timeframe level  |
| **R:R** | **1 : 4.7** | Risk 30 pips, reward 142 pips |

## Distribution vs Accumulation

| Factor          | Distribution             | Accumulation             |
| --------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Location        | After a rally, premium   | After a decline, discount |
| False break     | UTAD above the highs     | Spring below the lows    |
| Volume          | Climax at the high, rallies weaken | Climax at the low, dips weaken |
| Breaks          | Down (markdown)          | Up (markup)              |

A range near the highs that breaks **up** and holds was **re-accumulation**, not distribution. See [Reaccumulation](/en/smc/reaccumulation).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Fix                                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Shorting the middle of the range     | Sell the UTAD/test or the LPSY                         |
| Calling every push above the range a UTAD | It must close back inside                         |
| Stop just above the range high       | Above the UTAD high                                    |
| Ignoring a breakout that holds       | That's not distribution                                |

## Practice

1. Find five ranges after rallies on daily charts.
2. Label BC, AR, ST, UTAD (if any), SOW and LPSY.
3. Note which broke down and which broke up. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
