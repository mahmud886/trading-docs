## Fresh vs Tested

- A **fresh** zone hasn't been revisited since price left it.
- A **tested** zone has been revisited at least once.

The first return to a zone usually reacts best. Each later touch tends to react less, and eventually the zone breaks.

---

## Why Touches Weaken a Zone

1. **Orders get used up.** If the reaction comes from orders left in the base, each touch fills some of them. Fewer remain for the next touch.
2. **Liquidity builds beyond it.** Traders buying each touch put their stops just below the zone. Every touch adds to that pool, which makes it a better target for a sweep or a break.
3. **The lows creep closer to the distal line.** Each touch that goes a little deeper shows buyers stepping in later.

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: 'after 3 tests'">
  The original said to "consider the zone consumed" after three tests. That's a common rule of thumb, not a measured
  limit. Some zones break on the second touch, and some hold for more. Watch how each reaction compares with the last:
  shrinking reactions matter more than the count.
</Callout>

## How to Trade Each Touch

| Touch           | Approach                                                        |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First (fresh)** | Your main setup: limit at the proximal line, or a lower-timeframe CHOCH in the zone |
| **Second**      | Only with extra confluence (HTF trend, discount, a CHOCH); reduce size |
| **Third or later** | Usually skip. Consider the break instead: the stops below are a target |
| **Close beyond distal** | The zone is finished; remove it from the chart          |

**First-touch trade in the chart:** buy **1.2614**, stop **1.2595** (below the distal), target the **1.2665** swing high: risk 19 pips, reward 51 pips, **R:R 1 : 2.7** (planned).

### Second example (no chart) — a tested supply zone becomes the target

EUR/USD has a 4H supply zone at **1.0950 – 1.0962** that has been touched three times, with highs at 1.0952, 1.0956 and 1.0959. Instead of selling a fourth touch, a trader with a bullish bias buys a 1H demand zone at **1.0905**, stop **1.0893**, targeting the buy stops above the zone at **1.0965**: risk 12 pips, reward 60 pips, **R:R 1 : 5.0** (planned).

## What Counts as a Touch?

| Event                                       | Touch? |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| A wick into the zone                         | Yes    |
| A close inside the zone                      | Yes, and a deeper one |
| Price comes within a few pips but doesn't enter | Usually no; note it |
| A close beyond the distal line               | Zone invalidated |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Treating a tested zone like a fresh one      | Count the touches; size down or skip              |
| Only counting, not comparing reactions       | Shrinking reactions are the warning               |
| Buying the third touch of a zone             | The stops below it are now the target             |
| Keeping invalidated zones on the chart       | Delete a zone once price closes beyond the distal |

## Practice

1. On 10 charts, mark demand and supply zones and number every touch.
2. Record the reaction (in pips) to each touch.
3. Compare the average reaction to touch 1, 2 and 3 in your own data.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Aspect          | Key point                                            |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fresh**       | Not yet revisited; usually the best reaction         |
| **Tested**      | Each touch tends to react less                       |
| **Warning**     | Shrinking reactions and lows creeping to the distal  |
| **Finished**    | A close beyond the distal line                       |
| **Opportunity** | Stops beyond a well-tested zone become a target      |
