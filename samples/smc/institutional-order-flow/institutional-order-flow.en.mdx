## What Is Institutional Order Flow?

In SMC, **institutional order flow** means **the direction large participants are currently pushing**, read from price itself. You can't see their orders, but you can see their effect:

- Their **zones hold**: pullbacks stop at their order blocks and FVGs.
- Their **breaks follow through**: each pullback is followed by a new BOS in the same direction.

When that stops happening, order flow is changing.

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: execution claims">
  The original listed institutional size as "100–10,000+ lots" and said they "engineer liquidity, then fill". Order
  sizes vary hugely, and engineering is intent nobody can see. What's useful and observable is below: zones that hold,
  breaks that follow through, and the liquidity they aim for.
</Callout>

## Why Large Orders Leave Footprints

| Constraint                  | Footprint on the chart                         |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| They can't fill all at once | Ranges and pullbacks while positions build     |
| They need the other side    | Moves into liquidity (old highs/lows) before turning |
| They commit decisively      | **Displacement**: large candles, FVGs          |
| They defend their levels    | **Order blocks** that hold on the retest       |
| They act in liquid hours    | Big moves cluster in the [kill zones](/en/smc/kill-zones) |

| Step    | What happens                                  | Order flow           |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| OB 1    | Pullback holds at the OB, then a BOS          | **Bullish**          |
| OB 2    | Pullback holds again, another BOS             | **Bullish**          |
| OB 3    | A candle closes **through** the OB            | **Flips bearish**    |

While bullish, you only look for buys at bullish zones. After the flip, bullish zones are more likely to break and become [breakers](/en/smc/breaker-blocks); you look for sells instead.

## Top-Down Bias

| Timeframe | Question                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Weekly** | Is weekly order flow bullish or bearish? Where's the draw (last week's high/low, unswept weekly levels)? |
| **Daily** | Are daily zones holding in the same direction?               |
| **Session** | Is the London or NY move in that direction, with displacement? |

### A simple weekly read

| Last week's close…           | Tendency                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| In the top 25% of its range  | Continuation up is more likely    |
| In the bottom 25%            | Continuation down is more likely  |
| In the middle 50%            | No read; use other clues          |

The original said Tuesday and Wednesday are the "highest probability" days. That's a common tendency, not a measured rule; see [Weekly Profiles](/en/ict/weekly-profiles).

## Footprints Checklist

| Footprint            | What to check                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Displacement**     | Large-body candles in the direction of the move   |
| **FVGs**             | Gaps left by that displacement                    |
| **Order blocks**     | Do they hold on the retest?                       |
| **Liquidity sweeps** | Was a high or low taken before the move?          |
| **Timing**           | Did it happen in a kill zone?                     |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                 | Fix                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Calling order flow from one candle      | Read it from zones holding and BOS following         |
| Ignoring a failed zone                  | One clean failure is the first warning of a flip     |
| Trading against the weekly order flow   | Lower-timeframe setups against it are lower odds     |
| Claiming to know institutions' intent   | Trade what the chart shows                           |

## Practice

1. On a 4H chart, mark each pullback's order block for one month.
2. For each, note: held (and BOS followed) or failed.
3. Mark where order flow flipped and what came next. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

| **Order flow**   | The direction whose zones hold and breaks follow through |

| **Flip**         | A zone in that direction fails                  |
