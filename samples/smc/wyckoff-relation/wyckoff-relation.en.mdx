## Where SMC's Ideas Come From

**Richard Wyckoff** studied how large operators moved stock prices in the early 1900s and taught his method up to the 1930s. His idea of a **"Composite Man"**, one imaginary big player behind the market's moves, is close to what SMC calls **smart money**.

Much of SMC is Wyckoff's model in new words: ranges where positions are built, false breaks that take stops, and a break of structure that starts the trend.

---

## Wyckoff's Three Laws

| Law                     | Meaning                                                  | SMC echo                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Supply and demand**   | Price rises when demand exceeds supply, and vice versa   | Order flow                            |
| **Cause and effect**    | A bigger range (cause) tends to produce a bigger trend (effect) | Longer accumulation, bigger expansion |
| **Effort vs result**    | Volume (effort) should match the price move (result); a mismatch warns of a turn | Displacement vs weak pushes |

## Term-by-Term Translation

| Wyckoff                 | SMC                          | Meaning                              |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Accumulation            | Accumulation                 | Range at a low before markup         |
| Distribution            | Distribution                 | Range at a high before markdown      |
| Markup / markdown       | Expansion / delivery         | The trend                            |
| **Spring**              | **Sell-side sweep**          | False break below the range          |
| **UTAD**                | **Buy-side sweep**           | False break above the range          |
| Creek (accumulation)    | Range high / BOS level       | The level the SOS must clear         |
| Ice (distribution)      | Range low / BOS level        | The level the SOW must break         |
| **SOS**                 | **BOS** up                   | Break above the range with strength  |
| **SOW**                 | **BOS** down (a CHOCH relative to the prior uptrend) | Break below the range |
| **LPS / LPSY**          | **OB / FVG retest**          | The pullback entry after the break   |
| Composite Man           | Smart money                  | The large participants, taken as one |

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: SOW">
  The original mapped SOW straight to CHOCH. A sign of weakness breaks **below the distribution range**. Relative to the
  uptrend before it, that's a CHOCH; relative to the range, it's a BOS down. Both names are used.
</Callout>

## Using Both Together

| Tool        | Answers                                       |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Wyckoff** | Which phase is the market in? Is this a range at a low, mid-trend, or at a high? Does volume confirm? |
| **SMC**     | Exactly where to enter, where to put the stop, what liquidity to target |

A practical sequence: identify the phase on the 4H/daily → wait for phase C (the sweep) → enter on the lower-timeframe CHOCH and OB/FVG → manage through phases D and E.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                             | Fix                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Forcing every range into a schematic | Many ranges don't complete one                        |
| Ignoring volume                     | Wyckoff's method depends on effort vs result           |
| Trading phase B                     | It's the least predictable part; wait for C            |
| Treating the "Composite Man" as real | It's a teaching device, not a person                  |
