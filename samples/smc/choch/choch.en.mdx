## What Is a CHOCH?

A **change of character (CHOCH)** is the **first close against the prevailing trend**:

- In an **uptrend**: the first close **below the last higher low**.
- In a **downtrend**: the first close **above the last lower high**.

It's a warning, not a guarantee. Many CHOCHs turn out to be deep pullbacks. It tells you to **stop taking trades in the old direction** and start watching for the new one.

| Step | What happens                                          | Meaning                        |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ①    | Hour 31 closes below the 1.0900 higher low            | **CHOCH**: the uptrend's first crack |
| ②    | The bounce stalls at 1.0905, below the old highs      | A **lower high**: sellers defend |
| ③    | Hour 41 closes below the 1.0868 low                   | **Bearish BOS**: the downtrend is confirmed |

Before the CHOCH, the last high (1.0930) was a wick above 1.0925 that closed back below: a **liquidity sweep**. A sweep followed by a CHOCH is the strongest form.

## CHOCH vs BOS vs MSS

| Term      | What it is                                      | Direction        |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **BOS**   | Close beyond the last swing, with the trend     | Continuation     |
| **CHOCH** | First close beyond the last swing, against the trend | Possible reversal |
| **MSS**   | A CHOCH made with **displacement** (strong candles, usually an FVG) | Stronger reversal signal |

See [BOS](/en/smc/bos) and [Market Structure Shift](/en/smc/market-structure-shift).

## How to Confirm It

| Check                                  | Stronger CHOCH                         |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Liquidity taken first?                 | Yes: a sweep of the last high/low      |
| How was the swing broken?              | With displacement, not a slow drift    |
| Where on the higher timeframe?         | At premium (for bearish) or discount (for bullish), or an HTF zone |
| What happens next?                     | A lower high (or higher low) then a BOS in the new direction |

## Entry Models After a CHOCH

| Model                 | Entry                                          | Stop                         |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **CHOCH + FVG**       | Limit in the FVG the break left                | Beyond the sweep extreme     |
| **CHOCH + OB**        | The last opposite candle before the break      | Beyond the OB                |
| **Wait for the BOS**  | Enter on the pullback after the new-direction BOS | Above the lower high (or below the higher low) |

Waiting for the BOS gives fewer, later entries with more confirmation; entering after the CHOCH gives better prices with more failures.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Fix                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Treating a wick as a CHOCH           | Only a candle close counts                              |
| Calling every internal break a CHOCH | Use the last clear swing on your timeframe              |
| Reversing after one CHOCH on 1m      | Check the higher timeframe first                        |
| No liquidity taken before it         | Weaker; wait for more confirmation                      |

## Practice

2. For each, mark the sweep (if any), the CHOCH, the lower high/higher low and the confirming BOS.
3. Also find 10 CHOCHs that failed. What was different? Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Element      | Rule                                                 |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **CHOCH**    | First close beyond the last swing against the trend  |
| **Stronger** | After a sweep, with displacement, at an HTF zone     |
| **Confirm**  | Lower high + BOS (bearish) or higher low + BOS (bullish) |
| **Entry**    | FVG or OB from the break, or the pullback after the BOS |
