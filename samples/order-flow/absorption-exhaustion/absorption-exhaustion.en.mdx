## Two Clues

Both ideas are about **effort versus result** — see [Effort vs Result](/en/wyckoff/effort-vs-result) in the Wyckoff section:

| Clue           | What happens                                                    | What it suggests                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Absorption** | Heavy volume and strong one-sided delta, but price doesn't move through a level | The other side is meeting that pressure with passive orders |
| **Exhaustion** | A move continues while volume and delta **fade**, ending on a weak new extreme | Aggressive participation is running out               |

<Callout type="warning" title="Toned down">
  The original described absorption as "institutions absorbing retail orders" and exhaustion as "smart money has
  stopped". Order flow shows **what traded and where**, not who traded. Both are patterns in the data; they suggest a
  possible turn, and about as often they are followed by a pause or a continuation.
</Callout>

## Measuring Them

| Pattern        | Checks                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Absorption** | (1) At a clear level; (2) volume well above average (≥ ~1.8×); (3) delta strongly one-sided; (4) price **doesn't** make progress (the extreme holds, the close recovers) |
| **Exhaustion** | (1) After a run; (2) delta and volume **fading** over several bars; (3) a new extreme on low volume; (4) a close back off the extreme |

---

## Confirming

Neither pattern is a signal by itself. Common confirmations:

1. **A close away from the level** in the expected direction (above the absorbed low; below the exhaustion high).
2. **A change in delta direction** on the next bars (positive after buyer absorption; negative after exhaustion).
3. **A retest that holds** with low volume at the level.

### Example (no chart) — trading the absorption

Price holds **1.0850** through three heavy bars. Plan: buy a close above the highest of those bars (**1.0856**) after a pullback holds above **1.0852**; stop **1.0847** (below the absorbed low), target **1.0870** (the pre-drop swing): risk 5, reward 14, **1 : 2.8** planned. If price closes below **1.0848** on heavy volume, the absorption idea has failed.

### Example (no chart) — trading the exhaustion

After a rally on fading delta, a spike makes a high at **1.0876** on low volume and closes back at **1.0871**. Plan: sell a close below the spike bar's low (**1.0870**); stop **1.0878** (above the high), target **1.0855**: risk 8, reward 15, **1 : 1.9** planned. If price closes above **1.0878** on rising volume, it was not exhaustion.

## How They Fail

| Failure                                            | What it looks like                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Absorption is overwhelmed                          | Price closes through the level on heavy volume           |
| "Exhaustion" in a strong trend                     | Volume fades, price keeps going — trends can run on little |
| Mistaking a pause for a turn                       | Price consolidates and resumes                           |
| Data issues                                        | Delta is mis-tagged on thin or one-venue data            |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                        | Fix                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Calling any high-volume bar "absorption"       | Need a level and no progress                           |
| Calling any weak push "exhaustion"             | Need several fading bars and a new extreme on low volume |
| Entering before price confirms                 | Wait for the close away                                |
| No stop                                        | Beyond the absorbed low / exhaustion high              |
| Using tick volume as if it were real           | Prefer futures or exchange data                        |

## Practice

1. Find three clear support or resistance levels with heavy volume on a futures chart. Did price hold (absorption) or break?
2. Find two runs with fading delta. Did the last bar make a new extreme on low volume, and what happened next?
3. For each, note the confirmation (if any) and where the stop would have been.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Idea               | Detail                                                            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Absorption         | Heavy volume and delta at a level, no progress                    |
| Exhaustion         | Fading volume and delta, ending in a weak new extreme             |
| Use                | Clues at levels; confirm with price; stop beyond the extreme      |
| Failure            | Overwhelmed levels; trends that run on fading volume              |
