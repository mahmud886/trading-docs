## What Are Single Prints?

On a [Market Profile](/en/auction-market/market-profile) chart, a **single print** is a price row with **one letter only**: a single period traded there. A run of them in the middle of the profile means price **moved through that zone quickly** and no other period came back.

<Callout type="warning" title="Toned down">
  The original said single prints "act as support/resistance" and "future targets" as if by rule. They are a **record
  of a fast move**. Price returns to some of them, fills some, and ignores others. Treat the zone as a reference you
  watch, not a wall.
</Callout>

## Single Prints, Tails and Gaps

| Term             | Where                                | Meaning                                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Single prints** | Inside the profile, between bodies  | A fast move that left a thin zone                            |
| **Tail**         | At an extreme                        | A rejection; see [Excess and Poor Highs/Lows](/en/auction-market/excess-poor-highs-lows) |
| **Gap**          | Between sessions                     | Price jumped; no trades at all in between                    |

A tail is a single-print zone at the very top or bottom. Inside the profile, single prints separate two balance areas.

## Using the Zone

| Situation                                     | Common ideas                                                   | Caution                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Price returns to the zone from above or below | Watch for a hold at its edge, or for it being filled           | Both happen                                         |
| Price enters and moves through it quickly     | May continue to the other side, as it did the first time       | Speed in, speed out                                 |
| Price stalls inside the zone                  | The zone may be turning into a new balance area                | Then it is no longer a "single-print" zone          |

### Example (no chart) — a hold at the zone

Day 2 falls into the zone and stalls near **1.0862**, with a hold above the zone's low. Plan: buy **1.0864**, stop **1.0858** (below the zone's bottom edge), target **1.0874** (the upper balance's high): risk 6 pips, reward 10, **1 : 1.7** planned. In the chart price reaches 1.0874 on the last candle; the plan doesn't count on that. If price closes below **1.0859**, the zone has been filled and the idea is off.

## Where It Fails

| Failure                                        | What it looks like                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Price fills the zone and keeps going           | A close beyond the far edge                              |
| Price never returns                            | The zone stays untouched for days                        |
| Fast moves caused by news                      | Thin zones from a release can be arbitrary               |
| Wrong period length                            | Single prints change with the period length you use      |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                          | Fix                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Treating every thin zone as support              | Treat it as a reference; wait for price to react           |
| Mixing 30-minute and 60-minute profiles          | Keep one period length                                     |
| Counting two-letter rows as single prints        | Single means exactly one letter                            |
| Entering with no stop beyond the zone            | Put the stop beyond the zone's far edge                    |
| Forgetting the news                              | Check what caused the fast move                            |

## Practice

1. On five TPO days, mark every run of three or more single prints inside the profile.
2. Over the next three days, note whether price returned, filled, held or ignored each zone.
3. Write the tally. Keep it before relying on the idea.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
