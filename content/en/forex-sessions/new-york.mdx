## New York Session Overview

The New York session is the **second most liquid** session and, during its overlap with London, creates the **most volatile period** in the forex market. For Bangladeshi traders, this runs from **evening to late night**, making it highly accessible.

| Detail                        | Info                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 🕐 **BD Time**                | **6:00 PM → 3:00 AM**           |
| 🌐 **GMT/UTC**                | 12:00 PM → 9:00 PM              |
| 📊 **Volatility**             | High (Very High during overlap) |
| 💧 **Liquidity**              | Very High                       |
| 🏦 **Institutional Activity** | Very High (Fed, US banks)       |
| ⚡ **Risk Level**             | High                            |

## Market Behavior

### The New York Sequence (BD Time)

| Time (BD)           | Event                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| 6:00 PM             | Pre-market activity begins                           |
| 6:30 PM - 7:00 PM   | **Major US economic data releases** (NFP, CPI, etc.) |
| 7:00 PM - 7:30 PM   | Initial reaction to data                             |
| 7:30 PM             | **US stock market opens** (NYSE, NASDAQ)             |
| 7:30 PM - 10:00 PM  | **Peak volatility** — London + NY overlap            |
| 10:00 PM            | **London closes** — potential reversal point         |
| 10:00 PM - 12:00 AM | NY afternoon — reduced volatility                    |
| 12:00 AM - 3:00 AM  | Late NY — low volume, avoid trading                  |

<Callout type="tip" title="ICT NY Kill Zone 🇧🇩">
  The **ICT New York Kill Zone** in Bangladesh time is **7:00 PM to 10:00 PM BD**. This is when the highest probability
  ICT setups occur — Power of 3 (AMD), Silver Bullet, and Market Maker Models.
</Callout>

### The New York Reversal

One of the most powerful ICT concepts. At approximately **10:00 PM BD** (when London closes), the market often:

1. Reverses the London session move
2. Takes profit on London positions
3. Creates the "ICT New York Reversal" setup

This is especially common on **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**.

## Trading Strategies

### 1. London-NY Overlap Continuation

- Identify London's established trend (1:00 PM - 6:00 PM BD)
- Wait for NY to pull back into a FVG or order block
- Enter continuation trades at 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM BD
- Target the next major liquidity level

### 2. ICT Silver Bullet (NY)

- **Window: 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM BD** (2:00 - 3:00 PM EST)
- Look for a FVG formed within this 1-hour window
- Enter on the mitigation of the FVG
- Small stop, target previous swing high/low
- Works best on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday

### 3. NY Reversal Setup

- Wait for London close (10:00 PM BD)
- Look for exhaustion candles or bearish/bullish engulfing
- If London was bullish, look for bearish reversal and vice versa
- Enter with stop beyond London's extreme
- Target 50% of London's range

### 4. Equity Index Scalping

- Trade US30, NAS100 from 7:30 PM BD (market open)
- First 30 minutes provides the best volatility
- Use 1-5 minute charts
- Target 20-50 point moves on US30

## Key Takeaway for Bangladesh Traders

The New York session is your **evening session** (6:00 PM - 3:00 AM BD). Focus on:

1. **6:30 PM BD**: Watch for US economic data reactions
2. **7:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD**: NY Kill Zone — your prime trading window
3. **10:00 PM BD**: London close — potential reversal setups
4. **After 12:00 AM BD**: Avoid — volume drops significantly
