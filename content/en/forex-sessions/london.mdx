## London Session Overview

London is the **largest foreign-exchange trading centre** in the world, and for Bangladesh traders it runs through the afternoon and evening — easy hours to trade.

| Detail             | Info                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🕐 **BD time**     | **1:00 PM – 10:00 PM** (US/UK summer) · **2:00 PM – 11:00 PM** (winter) |
| 🌐 **Local / UTC** | 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM London · 07:00 – 16:00 UTC (summer)                   |
| 📊 **Activity**    | High; usually the most active session on its own                        |
| 💱 **Main pairs**  | EUR/USD, GBP/USD, EUR/GBP, USD/CHF, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY, gold              |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: London's share">
  The original said London handles "approximately 43% of all forex transactions". That was the UK's share in the BIS
  Triennial Survey of **2019**; in the **2022** survey it was about **38%**. Either way, London is the largest single
  centre.
</Callout>

## Scheduled Events in BD Time

| Event                             | Local time                | **BD (summer)** | **BD (winter)** |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| UK data (GDP, CPI, jobs)          | 7:00 AM London            | 12:00 PM        | 1:00 PM         |
| German / Eurozone data (PMI etc.) | 9:00 – 11:00 AM Frankfurt | 1:00 – 3:00 PM  | 2:00 – 4:00 PM  |
| Bank of England decision          | 12:00 PM London           | 5:00 PM         | 6:00 PM         |
| ECB decision                      | 2:15 PM Frankfurt         | 6:15 PM         | 7:15 PM         |
| **London 4 PM fix** (WM/Reuters)  | 4:00 PM London            | **9:00 PM**     | **10:00 PM**    |

The 4 PM London fix is a benchmark rate used by funds; month-end fixes in particular can bring sharp, short moves.

## How a London Day Often Unfolds

| Time (BD, summer) | What often happens                                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 12:00 – 1:00 PM   | UK data; positioning before the open                                         |
| 1:00 – 3:00 PM    | Opening moves; the Asian range is tested — sometimes swept, sometimes broken |
| 3:00 – 6:00 PM    | Trend continues or pauses; European data                                     |
| 6:00 – 10:00 PM   | Overlap with New York; US data at 6:30 PM                                    |
| 9:00 PM           | London 4 PM fix                                                              |
| 10:00 PM          | London closes                                                                |

"London sweeps the Asian range" is a **frequent pattern**, not a rule. See [London Open Manipulation](/en/forex-sessions/london-open-manipulation) for that setup.

<Callout type="tip" title="London kill zone (BD time)">
  The ICT London kill zone (2:00 – 5:00 AM New York) is **12:00 – 3:00 PM BD** in summer and **1:00 – 4:00 PM BD** in
  winter. The original gave 1:00 – 4:00 PM for summer.
</Callout>

## London Approaches

| Approach                       | Idea                                              | See                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Opening-range breakout**     | Trade a close outside the 1:00 – 1:30 PM range    | Chart above                                     |
| **Asian-range sweep**          | Fade a failed break of the Asian high/low         | [Asian Range](/en/forex-sessions/asian-range)   |
| **Order block / FVG pullback** | Buy/sell the first pullback into London's impulse | [Order Blocks](/en/smc/order-blocks)            |
| **Overlap continuation**       | NY pulls back into London's move                  | [Session Overlaps](/en/forex-sessions/overlaps) |

### Example (no chart) — EUR/USD after German PMI

German PMI at **1:30 PM BD** (summer) drops EUR/USD from 1.0880 to **1.0858**. Price pulls back to **1.0870**, under the pre-release low 1.0872, and a 15m bearish candle closes there. Sell **1.0869**, stop **1.0881**, target **1.0845**: risk 12 pips, reward 24, **1 : 2.0** planned.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                       | Fix                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Using summer times all year                   | London is 2:00 – 11:00 PM BD in winter      |
| Entering in the first minutes of the open     | Let the opening range form                  |
| Treating every Asian-range break as a fakeout | Wait for a close back inside before fading  |
| Ignoring 5:00 PM (BoE) and 9:00 PM (fix)      | Check the calendar; reduce size around them |
