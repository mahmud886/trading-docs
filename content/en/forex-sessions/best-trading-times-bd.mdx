## Best Trading Times for Bangladesh (GMT+6)

As a Bangladeshi trader, your prime trading window falls in the **afternoon and evening** — perfectly aligned with London and New York sessions.

---

## The BD Trader's Daily Schedule

| Time (BD) | Session | Activity | Priority |
|-----------|---------|----------|----------|
| 5:00 - 7:00 AM | Sydney/Asian early | Sleep / Rest | ❌ Skip |
| 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM | Asian (Tokyo) | Mark levels, analysis | 📋 Prepare |
| 12:00 - 1:00 PM | Pre-London | Mark Asian H/L, set bias | 📋 Prepare |
| **1:00 - 5:00 PM** | **London Open** | **Prime trading window #1** | ✅ **TRADE** |
| 5:00 - 6:00 PM | London mid | Manage trades, prepare for NY | ⚠️ Manage |
| **6:00 - 10:00 PM** | **London-NY Overlap** | **Prime trading window #2** | ✅ **TRADE** |
| 10:00 PM - 12:00 AM | NY mid/late | Wind down, close positions | ⚠️ Close |
| 12:00 AM - 5:00 AM | NY close/Asian open | Sleep | ❌ Skip |

---

## The Two Golden Windows

### Window 1: London Kill Zone (1:00 - 5:00 PM BD)
- Asian Range sweep occurs
- Real directional move begins
- Best for: SMC setups, trend entries

### Window 2: London-NY Overlap (6:00 - 10:00 PM BD)
- Highest volume globally
- Strongest moves of the day
- Best for: Continuations, news trades

---

## Tips for BD Traders

1. **Don't trade before 1:00 PM** — Asian is preparation time
2. **Your best hours: 2:00 - 4:00 PM and 7:00 - 10:00 PM**
3. **Close everything by 11:00 PM** — don't hold overnight
4. **Friday after 10:00 PM** — avoid (weekend gap risk)
5. **Part-time traders:** Focus ONLY on 2:00 - 5:00 PM window
6. **Full-time traders:** Use both windows
