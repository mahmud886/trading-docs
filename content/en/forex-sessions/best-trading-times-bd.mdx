## Best Trading Times for Bangladesh 🇧🇩
This guide provides the **optimal trading schedule** for traders living in Bangladesh (GMT+6).
## Quick Reference
| Priority | BD Time | What | Why |
|----------|---------|------|-----|
| 🥇 **#1** | **6:00 PM - 10:00 PM** | London-NY Overlap | Peak liquidity, tightest spreads, most setups |
| 🥈 **#2** | **1:00 PM - 4:00 PM** | London Kill Zone | Asian sweep + trend establishment |
| 🥉 **#3** | **7:00 PM - 9:00 PM** | NY Kill Zone / Silver Bullet | ICT high-probability window |
| #4 | 5:00 AM - 8:00 AM | Tokyo Open | JPY pairs, Asian range starts |
<Callout type="tip" title="The 2-Hour Rule">
If you can only trade **2 hours per day**, make it **7:00 PM to 9:00 PM BD**. This captures the NY Kill Zone during the London-NY overlap — the single highest-probability window in all of forex.
</Callout>
