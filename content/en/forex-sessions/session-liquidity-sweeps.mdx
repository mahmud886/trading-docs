## Session Liquidity Sweeps
Each forex session systematically targets the liquidity created by the previous session. Understanding this chain helps you anticipate where price will go next.
## The Liquidity Chain (BD Time)
### Sydney → Tokyo
- **Time**: 5:00 AM BD
- Tokyo may sweep Sydney's high or low
- Usually minor — both are low-volatility sessions
### Tokyo → London (Most Important)
- **Time**: 1:00 PM BD
- London ALWAYS targets Asian liquidity
- The most tradeable sweep of the day
- 75%+ probability of sweeping one side
### London → New York
- **Time**: 6:00 PM BD
- NY may continue London's trend or reverse
- If continuing: targets the next daily/weekly level
- If reversing: targets London's origin point
### New York → Next Day Asian
- **Time**: 3:00 AM BD
- Late NY creates range for next day's Asian session
- The cycle continues
<Callout type="info" title="The Key Pattern">
**Asian creates range → London sweeps it → New York continues or reverses.**
This is the fundamental daily cycle in forex. Once you see this pattern, you'll never look at charts the same way.
</Callout>
## Identifying Sweep Opportunities
1. **Equal highs/lows** from the previous session = high probability sweep target
2. **Clean levels** with minimal wicks = more resting orders = better target
3. **Time alignment** — sweeps happen in the first 1-2 hours of the new session
4. **Volume confirmation** — spike in volume during the sweep confirms institutional activity
