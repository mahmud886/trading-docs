## Session-Based Strategies

Each forex session has unique characteristics that favor specific strategies. Using the right strategy at the right time dramatically improves your win rate.

---

## Strategy Per Session

### 1. Asian Session Strategy (5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD)
```
Strategy: Range Trading + Level Marking
- Market consolidates (low volatility)
- Trade bounces within the range (advanced only)
- PRIMARY USE: Mark levels for London
- Mark Asian H/L for later sweep
- Identify key S/R for the day
- NOT recommended for active trading (for BD pairs)
```

### 2. London Session Strategy (1:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD)
```
Strategy: Breakout + Sweep Reversal
- Wait for Asian Range sweep (first 30-60 min)
- Trade the reversal after the manipulation
- Trend initiation — ride the directional move
- Best window: 2:00 - 5:00 PM BD
- Highest success rate for SMC setups
```

### 3. New York Session Strategy (6:00 PM - 3:00 AM BD)
```
Strategy: Continuation OR Reversal
- If London move is strong → trade continuation
- If London overextended → trade NY reversal
- High-impact US news creates opportunities
- Best window: 7:00 - 10:00 PM BD
```

### 4. Overlap Strategy (6:00 - 10:00 PM BD)
```
Strategy: Maximum Volatility Trades
- London + NY overlap = highest volume
- Strongest moves of the day
- Trade in direction of London's established trend
- OR trade reversals if London is exhausted
```

## Strategy Selection Flowchart

| Time (BD) | Bias | Strategy |
|-----------|------|----------|
| 1:00 - 2:00 PM | Wait | Mark levels, observe manipulation |
| 2:00 - 5:00 PM | Bullish/Bearish | London sweep + entry |
| 6:00 - 8:00 PM | Same or Counter | Continuation or NY reversal |
| 8:00 - 10:00 PM | Same | Ride the move, trail stops |
| After 10:00 PM | Close | Exit positions, done for day |
