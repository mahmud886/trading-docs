## Session-Based Trading Strategies
These strategies are designed specifically for **Bangladesh-based traders**, with all times in BD time (GMT+6).
## Strategy 1: Asian Range Breakout (London Entry)
**Time**: 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM BD
1. Mark Asian Range (5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD high/low)
2. Wait for London to sweep one side (1:00 PM - 2:00 PM)
3. Enter on MSS after the sweep
4. Stop: Behind sweep wick
5. Target: Opposite side of Asian Range
**Risk**: 1% | **Expected R:R**: 1:2 to 1:4 | **Best pairs**: EUR/USD, GBP/USD
## Strategy 2: London-NY Overlap Scalps
**Time**: 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM BD
1. Identify London's trend direction (since 1:00 PM)
2. Wait for NY to pull back into a FVG
3. Enter on FVG mitigation with trend
4. Stop: Beyond the FVG
5. Target: Next swing high/low
**Risk**: 0.5% | **Expected R:R**: 1:2 | **Best pairs**: EUR/USD, XAU/USD
## Strategy 3: NY Kill Zone OTE
**Time**: 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD
1. Confirm daily bias from HTF + London session
2. Wait for price to retrace to 62-79% Fibonacci level
3. Look for OB or FVG at the OTE zone
4. Enter with confirmation candle
5. Stop: Beyond OTE zone
6. Target: Next liquidity level
**Risk**: 1% | **Expected R:R**: 1:3+ | **Best pairs**: EUR/USD, GBP/USD
## Strategy 4: Silver Bullet Evening
**Time**: 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM BD
1. Only trade on Tuesday-Thursday
2. Look for FVG on 5min chart within 8:00-9:00 PM window
3. Enter on FVG mitigation
4. Stop: Beyond the FVG candle
5. Target: Next 15min swing point
**Risk**: 0.5% | **Expected R:R**: 1:2 to 1:3 | **Best pairs**: EUR/USD, NAS100
