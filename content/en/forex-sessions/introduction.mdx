## Forex Trading Sessions Overview

The forex market operates **24 hours a day, 5 days a week**, but not all hours are created equal. Understanding trading sessions is crucial for timing your entries, managing risk, and maximizing profitability.

## Key Session Overlaps

The most important overlaps for Bangladesh-based traders:

### Tokyo–London Overlap (1:00 PM — 2:00 PM BD)

- Brief but can produce sharp moves
- London often sweeps Asian session liquidity at this time
- Watch for breakout setups on EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY

### London–New York Overlap (6:00 PM — 10:00 PM BD)

- **The most volatile and liquid period in forex**
- 70%+ of daily volume occurs here
- Best time for scalping, day trading, and all major pairs
- US economic releases hit during this window
- Gold (XAU/USD) makes its biggest daily moves

<Callout type="warning" title="Peak Hours for BD Traders">
  The **golden window** for Bangladesh-based traders is **6:00 PM to 10:00 PM BD** — this is when London and New York
  overlap, creating maximum volatility and opportunity.
</Callout>

## Which Session Should You Trade?

**For Bangladeshi traders**, the most practical sessions are:

1. **London Session (1:00 PM – 10:00 PM BD)** — Best overall. High volatility during your daytime/evening.
2. **NY Overlap (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM BD)** — Peak opportunity. Perfect evening trading.
3. **Tokyo Session (5:00 AM – 2:00 PM BD)** — Good for early risers. Lower volatility but cleaner ranges.

<Callout type="info" title="Pro Tip">
  If you can only trade **2 hours per day**, make it **7:00 PM to 9:00 PM BD** — this captures the NY Kill Zone during
  London-NY overlap, where the highest-probability setups occur.
</Callout>
