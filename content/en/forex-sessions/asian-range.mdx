## What is the Asian Range?
The Asian Range is the **price range created during the Tokyo/Asian session** — from **5:00 AM to 1:00 PM Bangladesh time**. In ICT methodology, this range is critical because London session typically **sweeps one side** of this range to begin its move.
## How to Mark It
1. Open your chart at **1:00 PM BD** (just before London opens)
2. Mark the **highest price** from 5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD
3. Mark the **lowest price** from 5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD
4. These lines are your Asian Range boundaries
## Why It Matters
- The Asian Range creates **equal highs and equal lows** — liquidity pools
- Buy stops sit above the Asian high
- Sell stops sit below the Asian low
- London's algorithm is designed to **sweep this liquidity**
## Trading the Asian Range
### Bullish Scenario
1. London sweeps the **Asian LOW** (takes out sell stops)
2. MSS to the upside on 15min chart
3. Entry on FVG/OB with bullish bias
4. Target: Asian HIGH and beyond
### Bearish Scenario
1. London sweeps the **Asian HIGH** (takes out buy stops)
2. MSS to the downside on 15min chart
3. Entry on FVG/OB with bearish bias
4. Target: Asian LOW and beyond
<Callout type="tip" title="Success Rate">
The Asian Range sweep by London occurs approximately **75% of trading days**. This is one of the most reliable patterns in ICT methodology.
</Callout>
