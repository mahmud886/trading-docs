<Callout type="warning" title="News Trading Rules">
1. **Don't hold trades** through extreme-impact events (🔴) unless you specifically have a news strategy
2. **Close or reduce positions** 15 minutes before NFP, CPI, and FOMC
3. **Wait 5-15 minutes** after the release for the initial spike to settle
4. **Wider stops** are needed during news — spreads temporarily widen
</Callout>
