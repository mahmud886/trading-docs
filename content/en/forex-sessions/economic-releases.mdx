## Economic Releases by Session

High-impact economic data releases create massive volatility spikes. Knowing WHEN they happen (by session) helps you avoid or capitalize on them.

---

## Rules for News Trading

1. **Close or reduce positions 15 min before High-impact**
2. **Don't enter new trades 30 min before**
3. **Wait 15-30 min after for direction to emerge**
4. **Spread widens during news** — account for it
5. **NFP, CPI, FOMC** are the big 3 — always be aware

## BD Trader Schedule

Most important for BD traders:

- **12:00 AM BD** — FOMC decision
