## London Open Manipulation
The London Open Manipulation is one of the most consistent and tradeable patterns in forex. Every day at approximately **1:00 PM Bangladesh time**, London's institutional algorithms execute a predictable sequence designed to trap retail traders.
<Callout type="danger" title="This is How You Get Stopped Out">
If you've ever been stopped out right before the market moved in your direction, London Open Manipulation is likely the reason. Understanding this pattern is critical.
</Callout>
## The Manipulation Sequence (BD Time)
### Phase 1: Asian Range Formation (5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD)
- Price creates a clean range with defined high and low
- Retail traders place buy stops above the high, sell stops below the low
- These stops become **liquidity** for institutions
### Phase 2: The Judas Swing (1:00 PM - 2:00 PM BD)
- London opens and immediately drives price to **one side** of the Asian range
- This is a **false move** — the "Judas Swing" (betrayal move)
- Retail traders who entered breakout positions get trapped
- Their stop losses get triggered, providing liquidity to institutions
### Phase 3: The Reversal (2:00 PM - 4:00 PM BD)
- After collecting liquidity, price reverses sharply
- The true institutional direction is revealed
- This move often runs to the **opposite side** of the Asian range and beyond
## How to Trade It
1. **Mark Asian Range** at 1:00 PM BD (before London opens)
2. **Wait for the sweep** — watch for price to spike beyond Asian high or low
3. **Look for MSS** — Market Structure Shift on 5-15 minute chart
4. **Enter on FVG/OB** — Use the first Fair Value Gap or Order Block after the shift
5. **Stop loss** — Behind the sweep wick
6. **Target** — Opposite side of Asian range (minimum), then extend
## Example Setup
If the Asian range is 1.0850 (low) to 1.0880 (high) on EUR/USD:
1. London sweeps to **1.0845** (below Asian low) at 1:15 PM BD
2. Bullish engulfing candle forms on 15min
3. FVG forms between 1.0855 and 1.0860
4. **Entry**: 1.0858 (middle of FVG)
5. **Stop**: 1.0843 (below sweep wick) = 15 pips risk
6. **Target 1**: 1.0880 (Asian high) = 22 pips = 1:1.5 R:R
7. **Target 2**: 1.0910 (next liquidity) = 52 pips = 1:3.5 R:R
