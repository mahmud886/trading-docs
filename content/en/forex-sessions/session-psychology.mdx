## Session Psychology

Each session has distinct psychological characteristics driven by the participants, their goals, and the time of day. Understanding session psychology helps you anticipate price behavior.

---

## Psychology by Session

### Asian Session Psychology:

- **Mood:** Calm, indecisive, consolidating
- **Participants:** Japanese, Australian, Chinese institutions
- **Behavior:** Range-building, quiet accumulation
- **Retail trap:** Trying to trade breakouts in low volatility
- **Reality:** Setting up liquidity for London

### London Session Psychology:

- **Mood:** Aggressive, directional, volatile
- **Participants:** European banks, hedge funds, largest volume
- **Behavior:** Sweep and trend, manipulation then direction
- **Retail trap:** Trading the first move (manipulation)
- **Reality:** First 30 min is often false; real move comes after

### New York Session Psychology:

- **Mood:** Reactive, news-driven, sometimes reversing
- **Participants:** US banks, funds, retail heaviest
- **Behavior:** React to London, respond to US data
- **Retail trap:** Fighting London's trend / chasing
- **Reality:** Either continues London OR reverses at key level

## Key Psychological Patterns

| Pattern             | Psychology                 | How to Use               |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| FOMO at London open | Retail chases first move   | Wait for manipulation    |
| Fear after sweep    | Retail exits at worst time | Enter after they exit    |
| Greed at highs      | Retail buys tops           | Look for distribution    |
| Boredom in Asian    | Retail overtrades range    | Mark levels, don't trade |
