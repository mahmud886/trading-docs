## Session Psychology
Each trading session has a distinct psychological character driven by the traders, institutions, and algorithms active during that time.
## Asian Session Psychology (5:00 AM - 2:00 PM BD)
- **Patience and accumulation** — smart money builds positions quietly
- Retail traders are mostly asleep (in Western markets)
- Price action is methodical and range-bound
- **Your mindset**: Be patient. Mark levels. Don't force trades.
## London Session Psychology (1:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD)
- **Aggression and manipulation** — institutional algorithms activate
- FOMO kicks in as price moves sharply
- Retail traders chase breakouts (and get trapped)
- **Your mindset**: Wait for the manipulation to complete. Don't chase. Let the sweep happen, then enter.
## New York Session Psychology (6:00 PM - 3:00 AM BD)
- **Confirmation and follow-through** — the trend is established
- News events create emotional reactions
- Late-session fatigue leads to poor decisions
- **Your mindset**: Trade with the established bias. Don't revenge trade. Stop by midnight BD.
<Callout type="warning" title="The Biggest Trap">
**Trading too many sessions** is the #1 mistake for BD traders. You don't need to be in the market 24 hours. Pick 1-2 Kill Zone windows and stick to them. Quality over quantity.
</Callout>
