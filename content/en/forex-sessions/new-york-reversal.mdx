## The New York Reversal
The New York Reversal is a powerful ICT concept where the market **reverses the London session's move** during the New York afternoon, specifically around **London Close at 10:00 PM BD**.
## When Does It Happen?
| Detail | BD Time |
|--------|---------|
| Setup window | **9:30 PM → 11:00 PM BD** |
| Ideal entry | **10:00 PM BD** (London close) |
| Best days | Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday |
## Why It Works
1. London established a strong trend during the day
2. Institutional traders who bought/sold during London **take profits** at close
3. New York afternoon traders position for the **opposite direction**
4. Price creates a reversal pattern that often retraces 50-100% of London's move
## How to Identify
- London had a strong directional move (50+ pips on EUR/USD)
- Price reached a significant HTF liquidity level
- Exhaustion patterns appear: wicks, dojis, engulfing candles
- MSS on 15min chart near 10:00 PM BD
- Bearish/bullish divergence on lower timeframes
## Entry Model
1. Wait for London close at **10:00 PM BD**
2. Look for MSS on 5-15 minute chart
3. Enter on the first FVG after the structure shift
4. Stop: Beyond the session extreme
5. Target: 50% of London's range (minimum)
<Callout type="info" title="Risk Management">
The NY Reversal is a counter-trend trade. Use smaller position sizes (50% of normal) and strict stop losses.
</Callout>
