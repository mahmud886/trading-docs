## What Are Kill Zones?

Kill zones are **time windows** from ICT (Inner Circle Trader) teaching when the major centres open and volume arrives. They answer **when** to watch; the setup itself — a sweep, a structure shift, an FVG — still has to appear.

ICT defines every window in **New York time**. Converting to Bangladesh time is one rule:

| US clocks                                    | BD time = New York time + |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Summer** (EDT, mid-March – early November) | **10 hours**              |
| **Winter** (EST, early November – mid-March) | **11 hours**              |

## A Daily Schedule for BD Traders (Summer)

| Time (BD)          | Window                          | What to do                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM | Asian range                     | Let it form; mark high and low at noon                             |
| 12:00 – 3:00 PM    | **London kill zone**            | Watch for a sweep or break of the Asian range, then an MSS and FVG |
| 3:00 – 5:00 PM     | —                               | Manage trades; mark London's high and low                          |
| 5:00 – 8:00 PM     | **NY kill zone**                | US data at 6:30 PM; continuation or reversal of London             |
| 8:00 – 10:00 PM    | AM silver bullet / London close | Late entries; tighten management                                   |
| After 10:00 PM     | —                               | Lower activity; FOMC at 12:00 AM on decision days                  |

Most BD traders can't cover every window. Pick **one** — usually London (afternoon) or New York (evening) — and keep a journal of trades taken inside it.

### Example (no chart) — the London kill zone, step by step

1. **12:00 PM BD:** the Asian range on EUR/USD is **1.0852 – 1.0872**.
2. **12:40 PM:** price wicks to **1.0878** and closes back inside — a sweep of the Asian high.
3. **1:20 PM:** a 5m candle closes below the last higher low at **1.0866** — an MSS — leaving an FVG at **1.0866 – 1.0870**.
4. Sell the FVG retest at **1.0868**, stop **1.0880**, target **1.0850** (just below the Asian low): risk 12 pips, reward 18, **1 : 1.5** planned.

For worked charts of this setup see [ICT Kill Zones](/en/ict/ict-kill-zones) and [London Open Manipulation](/en/forex-sessions/london-open-manipulation).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                     | Fix                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Using summer times all year                 | Add one hour in US winter                   |
| Trading anything that happens in the window | The setup must still appear                 |
| Watching every window, every day            | Pick one window and journal it              |
| Mixing up AM/PM silver bullets              | AM = New York morning = 8:00 PM BD (summer) |

## Practice

1. Write all seven windows in BD time for **this** season, and set phone alarms for the one you'll trade.
2. For 20 days, record whether a setup formed inside your window and what happened.
3. Compare trades inside and outside the window in your journal.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
