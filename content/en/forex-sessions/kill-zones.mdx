## What are Kill Zones?
Kill Zones are **specific time windows** identified by ICT (Inner Circle Trader) where institutional trading activity is at its highest. These are the times when smart money enters positions, creating the highest-probability trade setups.
<Callout type="tip" title="Essential for BD Traders">
Knowing Kill Zones in Bangladesh time eliminates the need for constant time zone conversion. Memorize these windows.
</Callout>

## How to Trade Kill Zones
### Step 1: Mark the Asian Range (5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD)
- Draw horizontal lines at the Asian session high and low
- These become your **liquidity targets**
### Step 2: Watch London Kill Zone (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM BD)
- Wait for London to sweep the Asian high or low
- Look for a Market Structure Shift (MSS) after the sweep
- Enter on the FVG or order block that forms
### Step 3: NY Kill Zone Confirmation (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD)
- If London established a bullish trend, look for bullish continuation
- Use OTE (Optimal Trade Entry) at the 62-79% retracement
- Target the next external liquidity level
<Callout type="warning" title="Kill Zone Rules">
- Only trade INSIDE Kill Zone windows
- Trades taken outside Kill Zones have significantly lower probability
- If you miss the Kill Zone entry, wait for the next one — don't chase
</Callout>
