## Determining Daily Bias
Daily bias is your **directional expectation** for the trading day — bullish or bearish. Correct bias alignment dramatically improves win rate.
## Bias Determination Process (BD Time)
### Step 1: HTF Analysis (Before 1:00 PM BD)
- Check Daily and 4H chart for overall trend
- Identify the nearest liquidity targets (daily highs/lows, weekly levels)
- Determine if price is in premium or discount
### Step 2: Asian Session Analysis (5:00 AM - 1:00 PM BD)
- Was the Asian session bullish or bearish?
- Where are the Asian range highs/lows relative to the previous day?
- Is price above or below the previous day's close?
### Step 3: London Kill Zone Confirmation (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM BD)
- Did London sweep the Asian high (bearish signal) or low (bullish signal)?
- Did a MSS confirm the bias?
- If bias confirms, trade in that direction during NY Kill Zone
## Bias Rules
| Condition | Bias |
|-----------|------|
| Price above prev day close + London sweeps Asian low | **Bullish** |
| Price below prev day close + London sweeps Asian high | **Bearish** |
| Price in discount zone + bullish London sweep | **Strong Bullish** |
| Price in premium zone + bearish London sweep | **Strong Bearish** |
<Callout type="warning" title="Bias Conflicts">
If your bias analysis gives mixed signals, **don't trade**. Wait for the next day. Clarity over activity.
</Callout>
## Weekly Bias Framework
| Day | Typical Behavior |
|-----|-----------------|
| Monday | Range setting, avoid aggressive trades |
| Tuesday | Weekly direction often established |
| Wednesday | Weekly high or low often forms |
| Thursday | Continuation or reversal of weekly trend |
| Friday | Profit taking, reduced trading after London |
