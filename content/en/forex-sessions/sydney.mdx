## Sydney Session Overview

The Sydney session is the **first session** to open each trading week and marks the beginning of the forex trading day.

| Detail                        | Info                         |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 🕐 **BD Time**                | **3:00 AM → 12:00 PM**       |
| 🌐 **GMT/UTC**                | 9:00 PM (prev day) → 6:00 AM |
| 📊 **Volatility**             | Low                          |
| 💧 **Liquidity**              | Low to Medium                |
| 🏦 **Institutional Activity** | Minimal                      |
| ⚡ **Risk Level**             | Low                          |

## Best Currency Pairs

- **AUD/USD** — Most active during this session
- **NZD/USD** — Follows Australian market sentiment
- **AUD/JPY** — Cross-pair with moderate moves
- **AUD/NZD** — Regional pair activity

## Market Behavior

The Sydney session is characterized by:

- **Range-bound price action** — markets tend to consolidate
- **Low spreads on AUD pairs** but wider on majors
- **Gap fills** from Friday close to Sunday open happen here
- **Sets the initial range** that Tokyo often continues

<Callout type="info" title="For BD Traders">
  The Sydney session runs from **3:00 AM to 12:00 PM BD** — this means it's active during your early morning. Most
  BD-based traders will **skip this session** and wait for Tokyo or London.
</Callout>

## Typical Price Action

1. **Sunday Open (3:00 AM BD)**: Potential gaps from weekend news — gap trading opportunities
2. **First 2 hours**: Very low volume, wide spreads
3. **5:00 AM BD onward**: Volume picks up as Tokyo joins
4. **Range formation**: Sydney creates a range that later sessions often break

## Trading Strategies for Sydney

### Range Trading

- Identify the high and low of the first 2 hours
- Trade bounces between range boundaries
- Use tight stop losses due to low volatility

### Gap Trading (Sunday Only)

- Look for weekend gaps on major pairs
- Gaps tend to fill within the first few hours
- Enter in the direction of the gap fill

### Avoid

- Don't expect breakout moves
- Don't trade EUR/USD or GBP/USD — spreads are too wide
- Don't over-leverage — moves are small

## Scalping Suitability: ⭐⭐ (Poor)

Small pip ranges make scalping difficult. Spreads eat into profits.

## Intraday Suitability: ⭐⭐ (Poor)

Not recommended for intraday. Wait for London for proper intraday setups.

## Key Takeaway for Bangladesh Traders

Unless you're specifically trading AUD pairs or catching Sunday gaps, the Sydney session offers limited opportunity. **Focus your energy on London and NY sessions** which align with your evening hours.
