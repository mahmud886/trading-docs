## Sydney Session Overview

Sydney is the **first major centre to open** each trading day, and on Monday it opens the trading **week**. For Bangladesh traders it falls in the very early morning.

| Detail             | Info                                                                          |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🕐 **BD time**     | **3:00 AM – 12:00 PM** (US/UK summer) · **2:00 AM – 11:00 AM** (winter)       |
| 🌐 **Local / UTC** | 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM Sydney · 21:00 – 06:00 UTC (Sydney winter)                  |
| 📊 **Activity**    | Usually the quietest of the four sessions                                     |
| 💱 **Main pairs**  | AUD/USD, NZD/USD, AUD/JPY, AUD/NZD                                            |
| 🗓️ **Weekly open** | Sunday 5:00 PM New York = **Monday 3:00 AM BD** (summer), 4:00 AM BD (winter) |

Australia's daylight saving runs in the **northern winter**, so Sydney moves **earlier** in BD time just when London and New York move later.

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected">
  The original called the weekly open the "Sunday open (3:00 AM BD)". In Bangladesh it's **Monday** morning — 3:00 AM BD
  in US summer, 4:00 AM BD in US winter.
</Callout>

## The Weekly Open and Its Gap

News over the weekend can make Monday's first price differ from Friday's last — a **gap**.

| Step              | Time (BD, summer) | Price / rule                                                          |
| ----------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Friday's NY close | 3:00 AM Saturday  | **0.6650**                                                            |
| Monday open       | 3:00 AM Monday    | **0.6672** — gap **+22 pips**                                         |
| Wait              | 3:00 – 3:30 AM    | Spreads are often wide in the first minutes                           |
| Signal            | 3:30 AM           | A 15m candle closes back **below the open** → sell **0.6669**         |
| Stop / target     | —                 | Stop **0.6681** above the opening high; target **0.6650** (full fill) |

Planned: risk **12 pips**, reward **19 pips**, **1 : 1.6**. By 6:45 AM BD price has filled **17 of 22** pips — but not the whole gap.

<Callout type="warning" title='Removed: "gaps tend to fill within the first few hours"'>
  Many gaps fill partly; some fill fully, some extend. Treat the gap fill as a **target to plan**, with a stop, not as
  something that will happen.
</Callout>

## How Sydney Behaves

| Feature                | What to expect                                                              |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ranges**             | Usually small, especially on EUR/USD and GBP/USD                            |
| **Spreads**            | Often wider than in London/NY hours, especially right after the weekly open |
| **AUD and NZD**        | Most active pairs; Australian and New Zealand data land here                |
| **Hand-over to Tokyo** | Activity rises from 6:00 AM BD when Tokyo opens                             |

Data to know: Reserve Bank of Australia decisions are announced at **2:30 PM Sydney** — **10:30 AM BD** during Sydney's winter (northern summer) and **9:30 AM BD** during Sydney's summer.

## Approaches

| Approach                    | How                                                                                  | Notes                           |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Weekly-gap trade**        | Wait 30 minutes; trade back toward Friday's close only after a close against the gap | Stop beyond the opening extreme |
| **Range trade (AUD pairs)** | Mark the first two hours' high and low; fade the edges                               | Small targets; check the spread |
| **Skip it**                 | Sleep; mark Sydney's high/low for later sessions                                     | Reasonable for most BD traders  |

### Example (no chart) — NZD/USD range

NZD/USD holds **0.6015 – 0.6030** from 3:00 to 5:00 AM BD. At 5:30 AM a 15m candle rejects 0.6030 and closes at **0.6026**. Sell **0.6026**, stop **0.6034**, target **0.6017**: risk 8 pips, reward 9 pips, **1 : 1.1** planned — before costs. With a 2-pip spread the trade barely makes sense; that's typical of Sydney.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                         | Fix                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Trading EUR/USD or GBP/USD in Sydney hours      | Spreads and ranges rarely justify it |
| Entering the first minute after the weekly open | Wait for spreads to settle           |
| Assuming every gap fills                        | Stop beyond the opening extreme      |
| Forgetting Australia's opposite DST             | Check Sydney's BD hours each season  |

## Practice

1. For 10 Mondays, record Friday's close, Monday's open and the size of the gap on AUD/USD.
2. Note how much of each gap filled by 6:00 AM BD, and by 12:00 PM BD.
3. Check your broker's spread on AUD/USD at 3:05 AM BD and again at 7:00 PM BD.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
