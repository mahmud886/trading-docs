## What Are Session Overlaps?

An **overlap** is a window when two major centres are open at the same time. More banks, funds and dealers are active, so volume rises, spreads on major pairs are usually at their tightest, and hourly ranges usually grow.

---

## The Three Overlaps in BD Time

| Overlap             | **BD (US/UK summer)**  | **BD (winter)**        | Typical character                            |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Sydney–Tokyo**    | 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM     | 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM     | Quiet; AUD, NZD and JPY pairs                |
| **Tokyo–London**    | 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM      | 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM      | London takes over; the Asian range is tested |
| **London–New York** | **6:00 PM – 10:00 PM** | **7:00 PM – 11:00 PM** | Usually the busiest hours; US data           |

## Why Overlaps Behave Differently

| Factor           | During an overlap              | Outside              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Participants     | Two centres' banks and funds   | One centre           |
| Spreads (majors) | Usually tighter                | Usually wider        |
| Hourly ranges    | Usually larger                 | Usually smaller      |
| Scheduled data   | US data lands inside London–NY | Fewer major releases |

"Usually" matters: holidays (one centre closed), quiet summer weeks and pre-FOMC days can all make an overlap calm.

## Rules for the London–New York Overlap

1. Know London's direction before 6:00 PM BD — continuation trades follow it.
2. **US data at 6:30 PM BD** (7:30 PM in winter) can extend or reverse London's move — no new entries right before it.
3. Prefer pullbacks into London's move over chasing new highs/lows.
4. After **10:00 PM BD** (11:00 PM in winter) London closes — reduce activity or tighten management.
5. Check holidays: if London or New York is closed, there's no real overlap.

### Example (no chart) — GBP/USD reversal after data

London rallies GBP/USD to **1.2790** by 6:00 PM BD. US data at 6:30 PM sends it down through London's last higher low at **1.2768**, and an hourly candle closes at **1.2760**. Sell the retest at **1.2766**, stop **1.2782**, target **1.2730** (London's 3:00 PM low): risk 16 pips, reward 36, **1 : 2.3** planned.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                     | Fix                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Using summer overlap times in winter        | London–NY is 7:00 – 11:00 PM BD in winter |
| Treating every overlap as "high volatility" | Check holidays and the calendar           |
| Chasing the first overlap move              | Wait for a pullback and a close           |
| Staying active after London closes          | Set a stop time                           |
