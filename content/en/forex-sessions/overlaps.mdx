## What are Session Overlaps?

Session overlaps occur when **two major trading sessions are active simultaneously**. These periods generate the highest volume, tightest spreads, and strongest directional moves — making them ideal for trading.

---

## Major Overlaps

| Overlap | Sessions | Time (BD) | Character |
|---------|----------|-----------|----------|
| **London-Tokyo** | London + Asian | 1:00 - 3:00 PM | Moderate, London takes over |
| **London-New York** | London + NY | 6:00 - 10:00 PM | HIGHEST volatility |
| **Sydney-Tokyo** | Sydney + Asian | 5:00 - 8:00 AM | Quiet, AUD/JPY focus |

---

## London-New York Overlap (The Big One)

**Time: 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD**

This is the **most important trading window** globally:
- Highest volume of the entire day
- Tightest spreads on major pairs
- Strongest directional moves
- Most reliable breakouts
- ~70% of daily range is established here

### Best for:
- Continuation of London's move
- NY reversals
- News-reaction trades (US data at 6:30 PM BD)
- Momentum scalping

---

## Why Overlaps Matter

| Factor | During Overlap | Outside Overlap |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Volume | Very high | Low-moderate |
| Spread | Tightest | Wider |
| Moves | Strongest | Choppy |
| Patterns | Most reliable | Less reliable |
| Liquidity | Abundant | Thin |

---

## Trading Rules for Overlaps

1. **Prioritize the London-NY overlap** for all major pair trades
2. **Direction from London** usually continues into overlap
3. **US news at 6:30 PM BD** can reverse London's direction
4. **After 10:00 PM BD** volume drops — reduce activity
5. **Best pairs during overlap:** EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY
