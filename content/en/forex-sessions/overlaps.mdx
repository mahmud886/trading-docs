## Session Overlaps Overview
Session overlaps are when **two major sessions trade simultaneously**, creating peak liquidity and volatility. These are the most profitable windows for traders worldwide.
<Callout type="tip" title="Golden Rule">
Over 70% of the daily pip range on major pairs occurs during session overlaps. If you can only trade limited hours, trade during overlaps.
</Callout>
## Tokyo–London Overlap
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| 🕐 BD Time | **1:00 PM → 2:00 PM** |
| Duration | 1 hour |
| Volatility | Medium-High |
### What Happens
- London opens and immediately targets Asian session liquidity
- **Asian Range highs or lows get swept** in this first hour
- Sharp moves on EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY, EUR/USD
- The Judas Swing often forms here
### Best Pairs
- EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY (both sessions active)
- EUR/USD (London activation)
- AUD/USD (Asian pairs react to London)
## London–New York Overlap
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| 🕐 BD Time | **6:00 PM → 10:00 PM** |
| Duration | 4 hours |
| Volatility | **Maximum** |
### What Happens
- **The most liquid 4 hours in all of forex**
- US economic data releases hit at 6:30 PM BD
- US stock markets open at 7:30 PM BD
- Maximum institutional volume from both London and New York banks
- Gold makes its biggest daily moves
- EUR/USD trades over 200,000 lots in this window
### Best Pairs & Instruments
- **EUR/USD** — Tightest spreads, highest volume
- **GBP/USD** — Massive moves, 100+ pip potential
- **XAU/USD (Gold)** — $20-50 moves common
- **US30, NAS100** — Stock indices peak volatility
- **USD/CAD** — US + Canada both active
### Why This Matters for BD Traders
**6:00 PM to 10:00 PM BD** is your **perfect trading window**:
- After work/school hours for most people
- Peak global liquidity
- Best spreads
- Highest probability setups
- Most ICT/SMC strategies designed for this window
