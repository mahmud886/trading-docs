## Trendlines & Channels Guide

Trendlines are diagonal support/resistance levels that identify trend direction and provide strategic entry/exit opportunities.

### Drawing Trendlines Correctly

A valid trendline requires **minimum two significant points** (for uptrend: two lows; for downtrend: two highs).

#### Uptrend Trendline

**Rules:**
- Connect at least two swing lows (bottoms)
- Swing lows should be progressively higher
- Line should not cut through price action
- Third touch of trendline = confirmation

**Example:**
- EUR/USD low at 1.0800, bounces to 1.0900
- Pulls back to 1.0850 (higher low)
- Trendline connects 1.0800 and 1.0850
- If price tests trendline a third time = strong support

#### Downtrend Trendline

**Rules:**
- Connect at least two swing highs (tops)
- Highs should be progressively lower
- Line should not cut through price action
- Third touch confirms strength

**Example:**
- EUR/USD high at 1.1200, pulls back to 1.1100
- Rises to 1.1150 (lower high)
- Trendline connects 1.1200 and 1.1150
- Price approaching this line again = resistance

### Trendline Strength Factors

**Strong Trendlines Have:**
- Multiple touches (3+) without breaking
- Extended history (long-term, not just recent)
- High volume at touches
- Clear angle (not too steep)
- Confluence with support/resistance levels

**Weak Trendlines Have:**
- Only one or two touches
- Recent formation only
- Low volume at touches
- Extremely steep angle
- No confluence

### Channels

Channels are parallel trendlines that contain price movement between support and resistance.

#### Uptrend Channel

**Structure:**
- Lower trendline: connects swing lows (uptrend support)
- Upper trendline: parallel line through swing highs (resistance)
- Price bounces between the two lines

**Trading:**
- Buy: Near lower trendline (support)
- Sell: Near upper trendline (resistance)
- Stop loss: Below lower trendline
- Target: Upper trendline

**Channel Breakout:**
- Break below lower line = downtrend reversal
- Break above upper line = acceleration continuing

#### Downtrend Channel

**Structure:**
- Upper trendline: connects swing highs (downtrend resistance)
- Lower trendline: parallel line through swing lows (support)
- Price bounces between two lines

**Trading:**
- Short: Near upper trendline (resistance)
- Cover: Near lower trendline (support)
- Stop loss: Above upper trendline
- Target: Lower trendline

#### Horizontal Channels (Range)

**Structure:**
- Horizontal upper and lower lines
- Price consolidating within range
- No uptrend or downtrend

**Trading:**
- Buy: At support (lower line)
- Sell: At resistance (upper line)
- Continue until breakout

### Broken Trendlines & Role Reversals

When a trendline breaks:
- **Role reversal:** What was support becomes resistance (and vice versa)
- **Trend change:** Trendline break often signals trend exhaustion
- **Pullback:** Price often tests broken trendline from opposite side

**Example:**
- Uptrend trendline supported price for 200 pips
- Price breaks below trendline
- Often pulls back to test the broken line
- This old support now acts as resistance
- Confirms breakout if price bounces from it

### Advanced Trendline Concepts

#### 1. **Parallel Channel Breakouts**
When price breaks outside a channel:
- Close above upper line = strong upside move coming
- Close below lower line = strong downside move coming
- Target = Width of channel, projected from breakout

#### 2. **Accelerating/Decelerating Channels**
Channels can converge or diverge:
- **Converging:** Channels squeeze together (like triangle)
- **Diverging:** Channels expand (increasing volatility)
- **Accelerating uptrend:** Upper line slopes steeper than lower line

#### 3. **Speed Lines**
Divide a move into three equal parts:
- Fast speed line: 2/3 height of move
- Medium speed line: 1/3 height of move
- Used as support in pullbacks

#### 4. **Andrew's Pitchfork**
- Drawn from a swing (top/bottom)
- Three parallel lines
- Middle line = median line
- Price bounces off parallel lines

### Trendline Trading Strategies

#### Strategy 1: Trendline Bounce Trading

**Setup:**
1. Identify clear uptrend or downtrend
2. Draw trendline through swing points
3. Price pulls back toward trendline
4. Wait for bounce confirmation

**Entry:**
- Wait for candlestick to form on the trendline
- Look for reversal signal (hammer, pin bar, etc.)
- Enter on confirmation candle close

**Stop Loss:**
- Below trendline (for long trades)
- Above trendline (for short trades)
- Add buffer for volatility (5-10 pips)

**Target:**
- Previous swing high/low
- Fibonacci extension of move
- 2:1 risk-reward minimum

#### Strategy 2: Trendline Breakout Trading

**Setup:**
1. Price in clear channel
2. Breakout above/below trendline on high volume
3. Confirm with candlestick close outside channel

**Entry:**
- On breakout confirmation
- Can wait for pullback to broken line
- Volume should confirm breakout

**Stop Loss:**
- Inside broken channel
- Small buffer for noise

**Target:**
- Previous range width × 2
- Next major support/resistance
- Fibonacci extensions

#### Strategy 3: Channel Range Trading

**Setup:**
1. Price trading in clear parallel channel
2. Identify support (lower line) and resistance (upper line)
3. Trade bounces within channel

**Entry (Long):**
- Price near support line
- Reversal confirmation signal
- Stop: Below support

**Entry (Short):**
- Price near resistance line
- Reversal confirmation signal
- Stop: Above resistance

**Exit:**
- Opposite trendline
- Partial profits at midline
- Trail stop as trend continues

### Common Trendline Mistakes

1. **Forcing trendlines:** Lines should fit naturally, not be forced
2. **Using wicks:** Use closes, not wicks, for drawing
3. **Too many trendlines:** Has too many = no value (be selective)
4. **Wrong timeframe:** Trade on timeframe you're analyzing
5. **No confluence:** Don't trade trendline alone; combine with other factors
6. **Steep angles:** Unrealistic steep lines often break immediately
7. **Ignoring volatility:** Add 5-10 pip buffer for normal movement

### Trendline Strength Assessment

**Very Strong (3+ touches, long history):**
- Profitable bounces 70%+ of time
- Travel full distance within channel before breakout
- Size positions larger for this

**Strong (2-3 touches, medium history):**
- Bounce probability 60-70%
- Usually reaches opposite side
- Standard position sizes

**Weak (1-2 recent touches):**
- Bounce probability 50-60%
- May not reach opposite side
- Smaller position sizes or skip

### Multiple Timeframe Trendlines

**Best approach:**
- Draw trendlines on daily chart (major trend)
- Draw trendlines on 1H chart (intermediate trend)
- Trade 1H trendline bounces in direction of daily trend
- Confluence = higher probability

**Example:**
- Daily: Uptrend trendline (price above ma)
- 1H: Price pulls to 1H uptrend support
- Setup: Long entry at 1H support, within daily uptrend

## Summary

Trendlines & Channels:
- **Simple yet powerful:** One of oldest and best tools
- **Multiple touches confirm:** Stronger after 3+ bounces
- **Channels:** Trading between parallel support/resistance
- **Role reversal:** Broken becomes resistance/support
- **Confluence:** Combine with other analysis for high probability
- **Multiple timeframes:** Align for strongest signals

Master trendline drawing and you'll have a reliable, visual method to identify high-probability trading setups.
