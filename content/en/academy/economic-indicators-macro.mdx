## Economic Indicators & Macro Trading

Economic indicators influence currency values, stock prices, and commodity prices. Understanding them is crucial for trading profitably.

### What Are Economic Indicators?

**Economic indicators** are statistics showing economic health and guiding monetary policy decisions

**Types:**
- **Leading:** Forward-looking, predict future (ISM, Consumer Confidence)
- **Coincident:** Current economic activity (GDP, Income)
- **Lagging:** Historical, confirm trends (Unemployment, Inflation)

### Major Economic Indicators

#### 1. **Interest Rates (Most Important)**

**Central Bank Rates:**
- Fed Funds Rate (US Federal Reserve)
- ECB Rate (European Central Bank)
- BOE Rate (Bank of England)
- BOJ Rate (Bank of Japan)

**Impact:**
- Higher rates = Stronger currency
- Lower rates = Weaker currency
- Most important for forex trading

**Example:**
```
Fed raises rates → USD strengthens → EUR/USD down
ECB cuts rates → EUR weakens → EUR/USD down
```

**Trading:**
- FOMC meetings (Fed announcements)
- Central bank decision dates
- Rate expectations drive markets
- Often move 100-200 pips

#### 2. **Inflation**

**Measures:** Consumer Price Index (CPI), Producer Price Index (PPI)

**Impact:**
- Rising inflation → Central banks raise rates
- Falling inflation → Central banks cut rates
- High inflation negative for bonds, positive for commodities

**Trading Levels:**
- Expectations vs Actual
- More important than the previous number
- Can move markets 50-150 pips

#### 3. **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)**

**Measures:** Economy's total output

**Impact:**
- Strong GDP growth → Currency strengthens
- Weak GDP → Currency weakens
- Forward guidance affects more than actual

**Frequency:** Quarterly

**Trading:** Usually 50-100 pip moves

#### 4. **Employment Data**

**Non-Farm Payrolls (US - Most Important):**
- First Friday of each month
- Expected vs Actual jobs added
- Unemployment rate
- Wage growth

**Impact:**
- Strong jobs → Higher rates likely
- Weak jobs → Fed cuts rates
- Massive market impact

**Trading:** Often 200-500 pip moves
- EUR/USD moves heavily
- USD's biggest mover
- High volatility around release

#### 5. **Retail Sales**

**Measures:** Consumer spending

**Impact:**
- Reflects consumer health
- Affects GDP projections
- Strong sales = healthy economy

**Frequency:** Monthly
**Impact:** 50-100 pip moves

#### 6. **Manufacturing Data**

**ISM Manufacturing Index (US):**
- Business sentiment
- Production levels
- Factory orders

**Frequency:** Monthly
**Impact:** 50-100 pip moves

#### 7. **Trade Balance**

**Measures:** Exports vs Imports

**Impact:**
- Large deficits negative for currency
- Surpluses positive
- Longer-term indicator

**Frequency:** Monthly

#### 8. **Consumer Confidence**

**Measures:** Consumer spending mood

**Impact:**
- High confidence = spending
- Low confidence = saving
- Forward economic indicator

**Frequency:** Monthly
**Impact:** 30-70 pip moves

### Central Banks & Monetary Policy

#### **Federal Reserve (US)**

**Key Meetings:**
- FOMC Meetings: 8 times per year
- Announcement dates pre-set
- Biggest forex mover
- Rate decisions move currencies 200-500+ pips

**Watch:**
- Interest rate decisions
- Forward guidance
- Inflation outlook

#### **European Central Bank (ECB)**

**Key Meetings:**
- Every 6 weeks typically
- Less consistent than Fed
- Rate changes rarer
- Draghi's press conferences massive

**Watch:**
- Policy rate decisions
- Euro strength/weakness

#### **Bank of England (BOE)**

**Key Meetings:**
- Monthly typically
- Monetary Policy Committee
- UK inflation focus

**Watch:**
- Brexit impact on rates
- Inflation targets

#### **Bank of Japan (BOJ)**

**Key Meetings:**
- More frequent communication
- Long history of low rates
- Yen strength matters

**Watch:**
- Rate hiking commentary (rare!)
- Yield curve control

### Economic Calendar Events

**High Impact Events:**
- Central bank interest rate decisions
- Non-Farm Payrolls (US)
- GDP reports
- Inflation reports (CPI)
- Manufacturing PMI

**Medium Impact:**
- Retail sales
- Consumer confidence
- Factory orders
- Housing data

**Lower Impact:**
- Initial jobless claims
- ISM services
- Consumer sentiment
- Durable goods orders

### Trading Economic News

#### Pre-News Strategy:

**Before High-Impact Event:**
1. Close existing positions (avoid volatility)
2. Set alerts for release
3. Calculate expected pip movement
4. Prepare for gaps

#### During-News Strategy:

**Immediate After Release:**
1. **Reaction phase** (first 30 seconds): Knee-jerk reaction
2. **Correction phase** (1-5 minutes): Market digests
3. **Sustained move** (5+ minutes): Directional trend

#### Post-News Strategy:

1. Wait for volatility to settle
2. Identify directional bias
3. Enter on pullbacks
4. Trade continuation moves

### Impact of Economic Data

#### Expectations vs Actual

**Positive Surprise:**
- Actual > Forecast → Move up
- Example: Jobs expected +200k, actual +300k = USD up

**Negative Surprise:**
- Actual < Forecast → Move down
- Example: Inflation expected +3%, actual +4% = complications

**In-Line Result:**
- Actual ≈ Forecast → Subdued move
- Often causes sell-the-news

### Major Economic Events Calendar

**Monthly:**
- Non-Farm Payrolls (US): 1st Friday
- Manufacturing PMI: Early month
- Retail sales: Mid-month
- Consumer confidence: Mid/late month
- CPI: Mid-month
- GDP (preliminary): End of month

**Quarterly:**
- GDP (final): Later month
- Earnings season impact

**Market Holiday Schedule:**
- US: Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Independence Day, Labor Day, Memorial Day
- UK: Bank holidays vary
- EUR: Different holidays by country
- Reduced liquidity during holidays = wider spreads

### Trading Geopolitics & News

**Geopolitical risk:**
- Brexit (affected GBP heavily)
- US-China trade wars
- Russia-Ukraine conflict
- Middle East tensions

**Impact:**
- Risk-on: Weak currencies (CHF, JPY)
- Risk-off: Safe havens (XAU, yield drops)
- Market correlations break down

### Advanced Macro Trading

#### 1. **Interest Rate Differentials**
- Trade pairs where one rate is rising, other falling
- Higher rates attract capital (currency strengthens)
- Carry trades based on rate differences

#### 2. **GDP Growth Discrimination**
- Trade countries with strongest growth
- Can sustain higher rates
- Attracts foreign investment

#### 3. **Inflation Expectations**
- Monitor inflation expectations
- Impact rate hike probability
- Affects bond yields
- Strong inflation = rising rates = currency strength

#### 4. **Policy Divergence**
- When central banks diverge
- One tightening, another easing
- Creates strong trends
- Example: Fed raising, ECB cutting = EUR/USD falls

### Money Supply & Quantitative Easing

**When Central Banks Print Money:**
- Inflation pressures rise
- Currency weakens
- Commodity prices up
- Stock markets typically up initially
- Then correction possible

**Recent Examples:**
- 2008-2010: Fed quantitative easing → USD weakness
- 2010-2015: Continued QE → Commodities crashed then stabilized
- 2020: COVID QE → USD weakness, stocks up

## Summary

**Economic Indicators Impact Trading:**
- Interest rates: Most important
- Employment: Monthly major event
- Inflation: Ongoing indicator
- Growth (GDP): Quarterly importance
- Central bank policy: Drives everything

**Key Takeaways:**
- Mark economic calendar
- Avoid major news if inexperienced
- Expect 100-500 pip moves on big releases
- Trade technical setups pre-news
- Trade fundamentals post-news confirmation
- Understand what numbers "mean" for currencies
