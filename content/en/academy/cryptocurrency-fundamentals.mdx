## Cryptocurrency Fundamentals

Cryptocurrency is a digital asset class with unique characteristics. Understanding crypto basics is essential for trading.

### What is Cryptocurrency?

**Cryptocurrency** = Digital money using blockchain technology

**Key Characteristics:**
- Decentralized (no central bank)
- Peer-to-peer transactions
- Cryptographically secure
- Immutable ledger (blockchain)
- Limited supply (most coins)
- 24/7 trading
- High volatility
- Global accessibility

### What is Blockchain?

**Blockchain** = Distributed ledger recording transactions

**How it works:**
1. Transaction initiated
2. Broadcast to network
3. Miners validate transaction
4. Added to previous block (chain)
5. Immutable record created
6. Distributed across network

**Key properties:**
- Transparent (anyone can see)
- Immutable (cannot be changed)
- Decentralized (no single authority)
- Secure (cryptographic)

### Major Cryptocurrencies

#### **Bitcoin (BTC)**
- First cryptocurrency (2009)
- Digital gold narrative
- Max supply: 21 million
- Largest by market cap (~$1 trillion)
- Store of value focus
- "HODL" culture strong

#### **Ethereum (ETH)**
- "Smart contract" platform
- Can program on blockchain
- Max supply: Unlimited
- DeFi ecosystem hub
- Developer-friendly
- Second largest market cap

#### **Other Major Coins:**
- **Binance Coin (BNB):** Exchange token
- **Ripple (XRP):** Cross-border payments
- **Solana (SOL):** Fast blockchain
- **Cardano (ADA):** Research-focused
- **Polygon (MATIC):** Layer 2 scaling
- **Chainlink (LINK):** Oracle provider

#### **Altcoins:**
- Alternative coins to Bitcoin
- Thousands exist
- Most pump and dump
- High speculation
- Most worthless long-term

### Cryptocurrencies Use Cases

#### Store of Value:
- Bitcoin alternative to gold
- Long-term wealth storage
- Inflation hedge narrative
- Controversial as inflation hedge (not yet proven it works)

#### Medium of Exchange:
- Payment method
- El Salvador national currency (experiment)
- Limited merchant acceptance
- Slow adoption (Bitcoin particularly slow)

#### Smart Contracts:
- Self-executing contracts
- Ethereum platform
- Automate enforcement
- DeFi applications

#### Decentralized Finance (DeFi):
- Banking services without banks
- Lending, borrowing, swapping
- Yield generation
- High risk, high reward

#### Speculation:
- Pure trading/gambling
- Meme coins (Doge, Shib, etc.)
- Pump and dump schemes
- Gambling rather than investing

### Crypto Exchanges

#### **Centralized Exchanges (CEX):**
- Binance (World's largest)
- Coinbase (US most trusted)
- Kraken (Professional)
- OKX (Asian focused)
- Advantages: Liquidity, features, KYC controls
- Disadvantages: Custody risk, fees, regulation

#### **Decentralized Exchanges (DEX):**
- Uniswap (Ethereum focused)
- PancakeSwap (BSC focused)
- dYdX (Derivatives)
- Advantages: No custody, censorship resistant
- Disadvantages: Less liquid, more complex, scams common

### Crypto Trading Mechanics

#### Market Hours:
- 24/7/365
- No day trading restrictions
- Sunday gap risk (Friday-Sunday close)
- Highly liquid major pairs

#### Volatility:
- Bitcoin: 5-10% daily swings normal
- Altcoins: 10-50%+ daily swings
- More volatile than stocks
- Require wider stops
- More leverage risk

#### Leverage:
- Binance: Up to 125x (dangerous)
- OKX: Up to 100x
- Unregulated exchanges: 500x+ (suicidal)
- Most retail traders blown with margin

#### Trading Pairs:
- BTC/USD, ETH/USD (fiat pairs)
- ALT/USDT (stablecoin pairs)
- ALT/BTC (cryptocurrency pairs)
- Cross-pairs (BTC/ETH, etc.)

### Stablecoins

**Stablecoins** = Cryptocurrencies pegged to stable assets (USD usually)

**Major Stablecoins:**
- **USDT (Tether):** Most liquid, largest, greatest controversy
- **USDC (USD Coin):** Coinbase backed, regulated backing
- **BUSD (Binance USD):** Centralized, Binance-backed
- **DAI (Decentralized):** Collateralized crypto-backed

**Use:**
- Trading pairs (BTC/USDT)
- Reducing volatility exposure
- Quick exits without selling crypto
- Earning yield on deposits

### Funding Rates & Perpetual Futures

**Perpetual Futures** = Forever leveraged contracts (no expiry)

**Funding Rates:**
- Interest paid between longs/shorts
- 8-hour settlement periods
- Positive: Longs pay shorts (bullish funding)
- Negative: Shorts pay longs (bearish funding)
- High rates = Overextension risk

**Funding Rate trading:**
- When funding extremely high (longs overextended): Initial shorts profitable
- When funding negative (shorts overextended): Initial longs profitable
- Reversion play (fade extreme funding rates)

### On-Chain Analysis

**Blockchain data analysis:**
- On-chain address activity
- Whale movements (large holder transfers)
- Exchange inflows/outflows (selling pressure)
- Holder distribution (fragmentation risk)
- Transaction costs (network health)

**For advanced traders:**
- Track large address movements
- Monitor exchange inflows (selling risk)
- Follow whale wallets
- Analyze MACD on blockchain metrics

### Tokenomics

**Tokenomics** = Token supply, distribution, incentives

**Elements:**
- **Total supply:** How many coins exist
- **Max supply:** Hard cap on supply
- **Circulating supply:** Currently in circulation
- **Inflation:** Annual creation of new coins
- **Distribution:** Who holds the coins
- **Vesting:** Release schedule of founder coins

**Analysis:**
- High inflation = Selling pressure increases
- Vesting release = Potential downside if insider selling
- Concentrated supply = Whale risk
- Fair distribution = Usually better

### Crypto vs Bitcoin vs Altcoins

**Bitcoin:**
- Most secure
- Most adopted
- Limited supply (hardcoded)
- Store of value narrative
- Slowest (10 min blocks)
- Most regulated

**Ethereum:**
- Programmable
- Smart contract hub
- Large ecosystem (DeFi, NFTs)
- Faster than Bitcoin
- Inflation (continuous)
- Developer friendly

**Altcoins:**
- High volatility
- Technology experiments
- Most fail
- Speculation heavy
- Pump & dump risk
- Potentially huge gains (or 0)

### Crypto Trading Cycles

**4-Year Halving Cycle:**
- Bitcoin halves every 4 years
- Historically followed by bull markets
- Price often peaks ~1-2 years after halving
- Cyclical boom-bust pattern

**Recent Halving Dates:**
- 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024 (likely)
- Following bull runs common (not guaranteed)
- Post-peak crashes also predictable

### Risks in Crypto

**Market Risks:**
- Extreme volatility
- Cryptocurrency crashes (90%+ possible)
- Manipulation (pump & dumps)
- Leverage blowups

**Technical Risks:**
- Code bugs (smart contract risk)
- Network attacks (51% attacks possible)
- Exchange hacks (Mt. Gox, FTX examples)
- Wallet security
- Private key loss (irreversible)

**Regulatory Risks:**
- Bans possible (China example)
- Restrictions (margin limits)
- Tax complexity
- Overnight regulatory changes

**Speculative Risk:**
- Most altcoins become zero
- Meme coin gambling
- Rug pulls (developer abandons project)
- Ponzi schemes common

### Crypto Trading Guidelines

**Safe Crypto Trading:**
- Major coins only (BTC, ETH)
- Minimize leverage (2:1 max)
- Risk 1-2% per trade (max)
- Take profits regularly
- Never margin trade altcoins
- HODL strategy requires conviction
- Trade what you can afford to lose

**Unsafe Practices:**
- High leverage (50x+)
- Altcoin leverage
- FOMO buying
- No stop losses
- Margin on unproven coins
- Leaving coins on exchange (custody risk)
