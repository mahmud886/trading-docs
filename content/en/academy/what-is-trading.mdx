## What is Trading? - Complete Guide

Trading is the practice of buying and selling financial instruments (currencies, stocks, commodities, cryptocurrencies) to make profit from price movements. Understanding trading basics is essential before starting.

### Definition of Trading

**Trading** is:
- Buying and selling assets to profit from price movements
- Typically short-term focused (minutes to weeks)
- Active, requiring continuous monitoring
- Different from long-term investing
- Involves frequent transactions

**Core Concept:** Buy low, sell high (or sell high, buy low for shorts)

### Types of Financial Instruments

#### 1. **Forex (Foreign Exchange)**
- Trading currency pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD)
- Largest, most liquid market ($6+ trillion daily)
- 24/5 trading
- Accessible to retail traders

#### 2. **Stocks/Equities**
- Shares of companies
- Trade on stock exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, etc.)
- Market hours specific
- Dividends possible
- Fundamental analysis important

#### 3. **Cryptocurrencies**
- Digital assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.)
- 24/7 trading
- Highly volatile
- Blockchain-based
- Emerging market

#### 4. **Commodities**
- Physical goods (Gold, Oil, Coffee, etc.)
- Bullion trading
- Energy markets
- Agricultural products
- Inflation hedges

#### 5. **Futures**
- Contracts obligating trade at future date
- Standardized
- Leverage available
- Used for hedging and speculation
- Expiry dates

#### 6. **Options**
- Right (not obligation) to buy/sell
- Leveraged exposure
- Limited risk
- Premium paid
- Expiry dates

#### 7. **Indices**
- Basket of stocks (S&P 500, DAX, Nikkei)
- Track market segments
- Lower volatility than individual stocks
- Good for market sentiment

#### 8. **Bonds & ETFs**
- Bonds: Debt instruments, fixed income
- ETFs: Exchange-traded funds, diversified baskets
- Lower volatility
- Income generation
- Diversification tools

### Where Trading Happens

#### **Stock Exchanges**
- NYSE, NASDAQ, LSE, Tokyo Exchange
- Regulated markets
- Market hours: typically 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM (varies by country)
- Centralized order matching

#### **Foreign Exchange Markets**
- Decentralized, over-the-counter (OTC)
- No physical location
- Operates 24 hours/5 days
- Banks, brokers, retailers

#### **Cryptocurrency Exchanges**
- Centralized: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken
- Decentralized: Uniswap, PancakeSwap
- 24/7 trading
- High volatility
- Newer, less regulated

#### **Futures/Commodities Exchanges**
- CME (Chicago Mercantile Exchange)
- NYMEX (Energy)
- Trading floors (some automated)
- Specific market hours
- High volume

### Prerequisites for Trading

#### 1. **Starting Capital**
- Minimum varies by broker
- Forex: $100+
- Stocks: $25,000+ (pattern day trading rule in US)
- Crypto: $10+
- Futures: $2,000-10,000+

#### 2. **Broker Account**
- Choose regulated broker
- Set up trading account
- Deposit funds
- Understand fees and commissions

#### 3. **Trading Platform**
- MetaTrader 4/5 (Forex/Commodity)
- TradingView (Analysis & stocks)
- Exchange platforms (crypto)
- Interactive Brokers (stocks/futures)

#### 4. **Basic Knowledge**
- Understand risk
- Know your entry/exit rules
- Grasp position sizing
- Comprehend leverage dangers

### Key Differences from Investing

| Aspect | Trading | Investing |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Timeframe | Days to weeks | Months to years |
| Approach | Active | Passive |
| Frequency | Frequent trades | Few transactions |
| Analysis | Technical | Fundamental |
| Risk | Higher | Lower |
| Capital | Can be small | Usually large |
| Leverage | Common | Rare |
| Dividends | Ignored | Important |

### How Trading Works

#### 1. **Analysis Phase**
- Identify opportunity
- Analyze technical/fundamental setup
- Determine entry price
- Calculate risk/reward

#### 2. **Entry Phase**
- Place order at specific price
- Wait for execution
- Confirm position opened
- Record entry

#### 3. **Holding Phase**
- Monitor position
- Manage active trade
- Adjust stops if needed
- Watch for exit signals

#### 4. **Exit Phase**
- Take profit at target
- Or cut loss if wrong
- Close position
- Record result

#### 5. **Review Phase**
- Analyze the trade
- Document lesson learned
- Update trading journal
- Improve for next trade

### Trading Timeframes

**Scalping:**
- Seconds to minutes
- 5-50 pips profits
- High frequency
- Tight stops
- Professional traders

**Day Trading:**
- Minutes to hours
- Intraday close required
- 50-200 pips typical
- Medium frequency
- US pattern rule applies

**Swing Trading:**
- Days to weeks
- Holds overnight
- 100-500+ pips potential
- Lower frequency
- Most retail traders

**Position Trading:**
- Weeks to months
- Buy and hold
- 500+ pips potential
- Very low frequency
- Long-term perspective

### Essential Trading Rules

1. **Always Use Stop Loss** - Limit maximum loss
2. **Risk-Reward Ratio** - Minimum 1:2
3. **Position Sizing** - Risk 1-2% per trade
4. **Trade with Trend** - Higher probability
5. **Confirm Entry Signals** - Multiple confirmations
6. **Manage Emotions** - Follow your rules
7. **Track Performance** - Keep journal
8. **Continuous Learning** - Study and improve

### Common Trading Mistakes

🚩 Trading without stop loss
🚩 Risking too much per trade (over 5%)
🚩 Trading against the trend
🚩 Entering without confirmation
🚩 Moving stops against you
🚩 Not following your plan
🚩 Overtrading (too many trades)
🚩 Emotional decision making
🚩 Not keeping a journal
🚩 Inadequate capitalization

### Realistic Trading Expectations

**Monthly Return Targets:**
- Conservative: 2-5%
- Balanced: 5-10%
- Aggressive: 10-20%+

**Win Rate:**
- 40-60% is healthy if risk-reward is good
- Consistency matters more than win rate

**Profitability Timeline:**
- Learning phase: 6-12 months
- Consistency phase: 12-24 months
- Sustainable phase: 2+ years

## Summary

Trading is:
- Active, short-term profit taking
- Different from investing
- Available in many markets
- Requires capital, platform, knowledge
- Demands discipline and rules
- Provides opportunities with managed risk

Start with the fundamentals, choose your market, develop a plan, and trade with discipline.
