## Crypto Trading Overview

Cryptocurrency trading involves buying and selling digital assets like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and altcoins on exchanges. Unlike traditional markets, crypto trades **24 hours a day, 7 days a week** — creating both opportunities and unique risks.

<Callout type="info" title="Crypto vs Traditional Markets">
Crypto markets have higher volatility, lower regulation, and operate continuously. This means both larger potential gains and larger potential losses compared to forex or stocks.
</Callout>

## Spot vs Derivatives Trading

### Spot Trading

- Buy actual crypto assets at current market price
- You own the underlying asset
- No liquidation risk (cannot lose more than invested)
- Suitable for beginners and long-term holders

### Derivatives Trading

- Trade contracts based on crypto prices
- **Perpetual futures** — no expiry date, uses funding rates
- **Options** — right to buy/sell at a specific price
- Leverage available (2x to 125x on some exchanges)
- **Liquidation risk** — can lose entire position rapidly

<Callout type="warning" title="Leverage Warning">
Over 90% of leveraged crypto traders lose money. If you are new to crypto, start with spot trading only. Leverage above 5x is essentially gambling for retail traders.
</Callout>

## Crypto Market Cycles

Crypto follows predictable market cycle phases:

### 1. Accumulation Phase
- Price is flat after a major decline
- Low volume, low interest from retail
- Smart money is buying quietly
- Duration: 6-18 months

### 2. Markup Phase (Bull Market)
- Price starts rising, breaks key resistance levels
- Media attention increases, retail enters
- Altcoins begin outperforming Bitcoin
- Duration: 12-18 months

### 3. Distribution Phase
- Euphoria — everyone thinks it will keep going
- Smart money starts selling to retail
- New all-time highs with declining volume
- Social media is peak bullish
- Duration: 2-6 months

### 4. Markdown Phase (Bear Market)
- Sharp decline, panic selling
- Altcoins lose 80-95% of value
- Projects fail, exchanges collapse
- Duration: 12-18 months

### Bitcoin Halving Cycle

Bitcoin halves its mining reward approximately every 4 years. Historically, this has preceded major bull runs:

| Halving | Date | Pre-Halving Price | Cycle Peak |
|---------|------|-------------------|------------|
| 1st | Nov 2012 | ~$12 | ~$1,100 |
| 2nd | Jul 2016 | ~$650 | ~$19,700 |
| 3rd | May 2020 | ~$8,750 | ~$69,000 |
| 4th | Apr 2024 | ~$64,000 | TBD |

## On-Chain Analysis

On-chain analysis reads **blockchain data** directly — something unique to crypto:

### Key On-Chain Metrics

| Metric | What It Shows | Bullish Signal |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Exchange Outflows** | Coins leaving exchanges | Coins moving to cold storage (holding) |
| **Active Addresses** | Daily unique users | Growing network usage |
| **Whale Transactions** | Large transfers | Smart money accumulation |
| **MVRV Ratio** | Market value vs realized value | Below 1.0 = undervalued |
| **NVT Ratio** | Network value vs transactions | Low = healthy valuation |
| **Hash Rate** | Mining power | Growing = network security |

### On-Chain Tools

- **Glassnode** — Professional on-chain analytics
- **CryptoQuant** — Exchange flow data
- **Dune Analytics** — Custom blockchain queries
- **Nansen** — Wallet labeling and tracking

## Funding Rates and Open Interest

### Funding Rates

Funding rates are periodic payments between long and short traders on perpetual futures:

- **Positive funding** = longs pay shorts (market is overly bullish)
- **Negative funding** = shorts pay longs (market is overly bearish)
- Extreme positive funding often precedes corrections
- Extreme negative funding often precedes bounces

### Open Interest

Total value of outstanding futures contracts:

- **Rising OI + Rising Price** = New longs entering (bullish continuation)
- **Rising OI + Falling Price** = New shorts entering (bearish continuation)
- **Falling OI + Rising Price** = Short squeeze (shorts closing)
- **Falling OI + Falling Price** = Long liquidations (longs giving up)

## DeFi Trading Basics

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) allows trading without centralized exchanges:

### Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)

| DEX | Blockchain | Type |
|-----|-----------|------|
| **Uniswap** | Ethereum | AMM |
| **Jupiter** | Solana | Aggregator |
| **PancakeSwap** | BNB Chain | AMM |
| **Curve** | Ethereum | Stablecoin focused |

### DeFi Concepts

- **Liquidity pools** — Users provide trading liquidity and earn fees
- **Yield farming** — Earning rewards by staking tokens in protocols
- **Impermanent loss** — Risk of providing liquidity when prices change
- **Slippage** — Price difference between expected and executed trades

## Meme Coin Trading

<Callout type="warning" title="Extreme Risk">
Meme coins are the highest-risk segment of crypto. Most go to zero. Only trade with money you can afford to lose completely. Never invest more than 1-5% of your portfolio in meme coins.
</Callout>

### Meme Coin Cycle Pattern

1. **Launch** — New token created, small community
2. **Social viral** — Twitter/Telegram hype, early buyers 10-100x
3. **Exchange listing** — Major exchange listing causes final pump
4. **Distribution** — Early holders sell to new buyers
5. **Decline** — 90-99% crash, most holders left with losses

## Risk Management for Crypto

### Portfolio Allocation

| Category | Allocation | Examples |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Bitcoin** | 40-50% | BTC |
| **Large Cap** | 20-30% | ETH, SOL |
| **Mid Cap** | 10-20% | LINK, AAVE, ARB |
| **Small/Meme** | 5-10% max | High risk bets |
| **Stablecoins** | 10-20% | Dry powder for dips |

### Crypto-Specific Risks

- **24/7 market** — Set stop losses, you cannot watch charts all day
- **Exchange risk** — Use reputable exchanges, withdraw to cold storage
- **Smart contract risk** — DeFi protocols can be hacked
- **Regulatory risk** — Government actions can affect prices
- **Rug pulls** — Developers can steal liquidity in small tokens

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These Errors">

**❌ Going all-in on altcoins** — Always maintain a Bitcoin/ETH core position.

**❌ Using high leverage** — 10-125x leverage in crypto is a guaranteed way to get liquidated.

**❌ Not using cold storage** — "Not your keys, not your coins." Use hardware wallets.

**❌ Buying at all-time highs** — The best entries are during fear, not euphoria.

**❌ Ignoring market cycles** — Crypto is cyclical. Know where you are in the cycle.

**❌ FOMO into pumps** — If you see it on mainstream news, smart money is already selling.

</Callout>
