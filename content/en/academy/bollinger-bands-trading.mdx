## Bollinger Bands Trading

Bollinger Bands are a volatility indicator that helps identify support, resistance, overbought, and oversold conditions.

### Understanding Bollinger Bands

**Components:**
- **Middle Band:** 20-period Simple Moving Average
- **Upper Band:** Middle Band + (2 × 20-period Standard Deviation)
- **Lower Band:** Middle Band - (2 × 20-period Standard Deviation)
- **Bandwidth:** Distance between upper and lower bands

**Default Settings:**
- Period: 20 days/candles
- Standard Deviation: 2

### What Bollinger Bands Tell You

#### 1. **Volatility Measurement**
- **Wide Bands:** High volatility
- **Narrow Bands:** Low volatility
- **Expanding Bands:** Volatility increasing
- **Contracting Bands:** Volatility decreasing

#### 2. **Support & Resistance**
- **Upper Band:** Acts as resistance
- **Lower Band:** Acts as support
- **Middle MA:** Acts as mid-line support/resistance

#### 3. **Overbought/Oversold**
- **Price at Upper Band:** Potentially overbought
- **Price at Lower Band:** Potentially oversold
- **Not guaranteed reversal signals**
- **In strong trends, can stay extended for long periods**

#### 4. **Squeeze Pattern**
- **Bands contract together:** "Squeeze"
- **Indicates low volatility**
- **Typically followed by breakout**
- **Largest moves follow biggest squeezes**

### Bollinger Bands Trading Strategies

#### Strategy 1: Band Bounce Trading

Trade bounces from the bands in ranging markets.

**Setup:**
- Identify flat trend (bands not expanding)
- Chart is range-bound
- Price touches lower band
- Setup forms for buying into support

**Entry:**
- Buy at lower band
- Or buy after bounce confirmation from lower band
- Wait for price action signal

**Stop Loss:**
- Below the lower band
- Or below recent swing low
- Usually 10-20 pips

**Target:**
- Middle band (first target)
- Upper band (second target)
- Half position at middle, half at upper band

**Risk-Reward:** Typically 1:1.5 to 1:2

#### Strategy 2: Breakout from Squeeze

Trade breakouts that occur after a squeeze.

**Setup:**
1. Bands contract significantly (squeeze)
2. Volatility is very low
3. Price is consolidated in middle
4. Preparation for major move

**Trigger:**
- Price breaks above upper band = Long signal
- Price breaks below lower band = Short signal
- Look for closing outside band, not just wick

**Entry:**
- Enter on break with volume confirmation
- Or wait for pullback to band after break

**Stop Loss:**
- Below lower band (for long breaks)
- Above upper band (for short breaks)

**Target:**
- Often extends 1-3x the previous squeeze width
- Use ATR for distance measurement
- Take partial profits on strong moves

#### Strategy 3: Mean Reversion

Revert to the middle band when price extends too far.

**Setup:**
- Price is at or beyond upper/lower band
- Extended away from middle band
- Overbought or oversold condition

**Entry Signal:**
- Price touches band and starts reversing
- Or candle closes back inside bands
- Momentum oscillator confirms reversal

**Target:**
- Middle band is primary target
- Further targets at opposite band

**Risk Management:**
- This is less reliable in strong trends
- Confirm with other indicators
- Keep stops tight

#### Strategy 4: Confirmation with Bollinger Bands

Use BB to confirm other trading signals.

**Example:** Price Action + Bollinger Bands
- Support level found at lower band = Stronger
- Resistance level found at upper band = Stronger
- Breakout from support above middle = Confirmed
- Pullback to middle band = Strong support/resistance

### Bollinger Bands with Other Indicators

#### BB + RSI
- RSI at 70+ AND price at upper band = Strong overbought
- RSI at 30- AND price at lower band = Strong oversold
- Better for reversals than bands alone

#### BB + MACD
- MACD positive divergence at lower band = Strong buy
- MACD negative divergence at upper band = Strong sell
- Confluence improves signal quality

#### BB + Volume
- Band touch with high volume = Stronger signal
- Band touch with low volume = Weaker, likely bounce
- Volume confirms band strength

#### BB + Moving Averages
- Price at lower band + above 200 MA = Stronger buy
- Price at upper band + below 200 MA = Stronger sell
- Longer-term trend confirmation

### Bollinger Bands Settings Optimization

**For Different Timeframes:**

**Weekly Charts:**
- Period: 20 (standard)
- S.D.: 2 (standard)
- Wide bands, strong signals

**Daily Charts:**
- Period: 20 (standard)
- S.D.: 2 (standard)
- Most commonly used settings

**1-Hour Charts:**
- Period: 20 (or try 10)
- S.D.: 2 (or try 1.5)
- More responsive to intraday moves

**5-Minute Charts:**
- Period: 10-12
- S.D.: 1.5-2
- Faster response to price changes

### Professional Bollinger Bands Usage

#### 1. **Volatility Regime Identification**
Monitor bandwidth over time:
- Increasing bandwidth = Trending market
- Decreasing bandwidth = Consolidation
- Affects strategy selection
- Squeeze = Expect breakout soon

#### 2. **Predicting Volatility Changes**
- Squeeze period = Low volatility
- After squeeze = Expect volatility expansion
- Time position sizes accordingly
- Widen stops when expansion happens

#### 3. **Multi-Timeframe Analysis**
- Daily BB shows major trend
- 1H BB shows entry points
- 5M BB shows precise entries
- Better confluence = Better trades

#### 4. **Volatility Breakouts**
Watch for squeeze-to-breakout sequences:
- Long squeeze phase
- Sudden expansion
- Large move in one direction
- Ride the volatility expansion

### Common Bollinger Bands Mistakes

1. **Thinking Price at Upper Band = Sell**
   - In uptrends, price stays at upper band
   - Not always a sell signal
   - Needs confirmation from other factors

2. **Not Considering Trend Direction**
   - More likely bounce from lower band in uptrend
   - More likely bounce from upper band in downtrend
   - Trade with trend, not against it

3. **Ignoring Volume**
   - Band touch with no volume = False signal
   - Band touch with high volume = Strong signal
   - Volume confirms band significance

4. **Tight Stops at Bands**
   - Bands aren't hard barriers
   - Price wicks beyond often
   - Add buffer (5-10 pips) outside band

5. **Fixed Settings for All Markets**
   - Different markets need different settings
   - Adjust period for volatility
   - Test before using live

6. **Over-Trading Band Bounces**
   - Not all band touches work
   - Need other confirmation factors
   - Trade higher probability setups only

### Bollinger Bands Band Walk

A "Band Walk" is when price moves along the band consistently.

**Characteristics:**
- Price touches band multiple times
- Moves in same direction
- Indicates strong trend
- Band acts as dynamic support/resistance

**Trading Band Walks:**
- Add to winners in trending markets
- Trail stop along lower band (for uptrend)
- Scalp partial profits after each band touch
- Very profitable in strong trends

### Advanced: Bollinger Bands %B

The %B indicator shows where price is within the bands:
- **%B > 1.0:** Price above upper band
- **%B = 1.0:** Price at upper band
- **%B = 0.5:** Price at middle band
- **%B = 0.0:** Price at lower band
- **%B < 0.0:** Price below lower band

**Usage:**
- Confirms overbought/oversold conditions
- Better than just visual interpretation
- Add as secondary confirmation

## Summary

Bollinger Bands are powerful for:
- **Identifying volatility:** Wide vs. narrow bands
- **Support & Resistance:** Bands act as dynamic levels
- **Mean reversion:** Price reverts to middle band
- **Breakouts:** Squeeze precedes large moves
- **Confirmation:** Enhances other signals

Key to success: Use BB with trend analysis and other confirmations. Bands alone generate false signals. Combine with volume, price action, and other indicators for best results.
