## Trading Costs & Spreads Impact

Many traders overlook trading costs, but they can dramatically impact profitability over time. A 1% cost on 100 trades is significant lost capital.

### Types of Trading Costs

#### 1. **Spreads**
The difference between bid and ask prices.

**EUR/USD spread examples:**
- Tight spread: 1.0 pips (tight competition, highly liquid)
- Average spread: 1.5-2.0 pips (normal conditions)
- Wide spread: 3-5+ pips (low liquidity, volatile times)

**Cost calculation:**
- Spread cost = Spread (pips) × Position Size × Pip Value
- EUR/USD 1 lot, 1.5 pip spread = 1.5 × 100,000 × 0.0001 = $15

#### 2. **Commissions**
Direct fees charged by broker per trade.

**Types:**
- Per lot commission: $5-10 per lot traded
- Percentage commission: 0.1%-0.5% of trade value
- No commission (market maker model)

**Major brokers:**
- ECN brokers: Often charge 3-8 pips equivalent (tight spread + commission)
- Market makers: May have wider spreads, no commission
- Prop firms: Usually tighter spreads with no commission to traders

#### 3. **Slippage**
Difference between expected execution price and actual price.

**Causes:**
- Market gaps during news
- Order size too large for liquidity
- Fast market movement
- Poor broker execution quality

**Typical slippage:**
- Normal conditions: 0-2 pips
- Volatile news times: 5-20+ pips
- Fast-moving markets: Can be unpredictable

#### 4. **Swap/Overnight Fees**
Interest charged to hold positions overnight.

**Calculation:**
- Swap = Position size × Contract size × Swap rate
- EUR/USD overnight hold might be -5 to +15 pips depending on direction
- Accumulates daily

**Strategies to minimize:**
- Close positions before daily reset (5 PM EST)
- Trade intraday only (no overnight holds)
- Hold during positive swap rates

### How Costs Impact Your Profitability

#### Example: 100 Trades Per Month

**Scenario 1: 2% Average Win (Tight Spread)**
- Cost per trade: 2 pips (spread)
- Win rate: 55% (55 wins, 45 losses)
- Average win: 30 pips
- Average loss: 30 pips
- Gross profit: (55 × 30) - (45 × 30) = 300 pips
- Cost: 100 × 2 = 200 pips
- **Net profit: 100 pips (33% eaten by costs)**

**Scenario 2: 5% Average Cost (Wide Spread + Commission)**
- Cost per trade: 5 pips (spread + commission)
- Same win rate: 55% (55 wins, 45 losses)
- Same PnL: 300 pips gross
- Cost: 100 × 5 = 500 pips
- **Net result: -200 pips (LOSS!)**

This shows how critical cost management is!

### Brokers & Cost Comparison

#### Market Maker Brokers
**Spreads:**
- EUR/USD: 2-3 pips
- GBP/USD: 3-5 pips
- Exotics: 5-10+ pips

**Commissions:**
- Usually none
- May have overnight swap fees
- Total cost: Mainly spreads

**Best for:** Swing traders, position traders (hold days)

#### ECN / STP Brokers
**Spreads:**
- EUR/USD: 0.5-1.2 pips
- GBP/USD: 1-2 pips
- Exotics: 2-5 pips

**Commissions:**
- $5-8 per lot typical
- Or 0.1%-0.3% of trade value
- Total cost: Spread + commission

**Best for:** Scalpers, news traders (need tight spreads)

#### Prop Trading Firms
**Costs:**
- Usually tight spreads (0.5-1.0)
- No commission
- Swap fees on some overnight positions

**Best for:** Funded traders (already paid the firm)

### Minimizing Trading Costs

#### 1. **Choose Right Broker**
**Comparison criteria:**
- Spread width vs. commission structure
- Liquidity during your trading hours
- Execution quality
- Swap rate structure

**Action:**
- Get multiple brokers
- Test cost efficiency (not just spreads)
- Calculate total cost per trade

#### 2. **Trade During Liquid Hours**
Spreads are tightest during peak overlap hours:
- **London-NY overlap:** 1-5 PM London time
- **Asian-European overlap:** 6-9 AM London time
- **Widest spreads:** Asian-NY overlap (low liquidity)

**Impact:**
- Peak hours: 1.0-1.5 pip spread on majors
- Off-hours: 3-5+ pip spread

#### 3. **Focus on Liquid Pairs**
**Tight spread pairs:**
- EUR/USD: 1.0-1.5 pips
- GBP/USD: 1.5-2.0 pips
- USD/JPY: 1.0-1.5 pips

**Wide spread pairs:**
- Exotics: 5-10+ pips
- Emerging markets: 10+ pips
- Illiquid times: Much wider

**Action:** Avoid trading wide-spread pairs if possible, or trade only during peak hours

#### 4. **Trade Longer Timeframes**
Longer trades justify the cost more:
- Scalping 10 pips: Can't sustain with 2-3 pip cost
- Swing trading 50+ pips: Cost is only 4-6% of profit
- Position trading 100+ pips: Cost is 2-3% of profit

**Implication:** Adjust trading style to your broker/costs

#### 5. **Reduce Trade Frequency**
Fewer trades = Lower total costs:
- 200 trades × 2 pips = 400 pip cost
- 100 trades × 2 pips = 200 pip cost
- Focus on HIGH-quality setups only

**Approach:** Quality over quantity

#### 6. **Manage Position Size Based on Costs**
If spread is 2 pips:
- Average profit target needs to be 20+ pips (10X the cost)
- Risk-reward at minimum 1:10 after costs
- Adjust entries to higher probability setups

**Math:**
- Cost: 2 pips
- Target: 20 pips profit
- Effective R:R: Net 18:20 = less than 1:1! (need to improve)
- Better: 40 pip target (38 net = 1:1.9)

### Cost-Adjusted Position Sizing

**Traditional formula:**
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ (Stop Loss × Pip Value)

**Cost-adjusted formula:**
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ ((Stop Loss + Spread × 2) × Pip Value)

The "× 2" accounts for entry spread and exit spread

**Example:**
- Account: $10,000
- Risk per trade: 1% = $100
- Stop loss: 50 pips
- Spread: 2 pips
- EUR/USD pip value: $10
- Position size = $100 ÷ ((50 + 2×2) × $10) = $100 ÷ $540 = 0.185 lots

#### Impact of Costs on Profitability Target

**Spread cost per trade:**
1 pip spread = Must win at least 1 pip just to break even on cost!

**Monthly costs (100 trades):**
- 1 pip spread: 100 pips/month cost
- 2 pip spread: 200 pips/month cost
- 5 pip spread: 500 pips/month cost

**Monthly profitability needed to break even:**
- 1 pip spread: Need 100+ pips profit/month
- 2 pip spread: Need 200+ pips profit/month
- 5 pip spread: Need 500+ pips profit/month

### Cost Comparison Examples

**Scenario: Professional Trader, 100 trades/month, $50,000 account**

**Tight cost setup:**
- Broker: ECN with 0.5 pip spread + $5/lot commission
- Trading: 0.5 lots average
- Cost per trade: 0.5 + (5÷100,000) ≈ 1 pip
- Monthly cost: 100 pips
- Can generate: 200+ pips profit

**High cost setup:**
- Broker: Market maker, 3 pip spread
- Trading: 1 lot
- Cost per trade: 3 pips
- Monthly cost: 300 pips
- Must generate: 300+ pips just to break even!

**Difference:** Cost optimization could mean profit vs. loss!

### Cost vs. Features Trade-off

Some brokers charge higher spreads but offer:
- Better research tools
- Educational content
- Better customer support
- More stable execution

**When worth it:**
- Educational content saves you thousands in learning
- Better tools = better entries (worth the spread cost)
- Stability = fewer slippage events

**Calculate ROI:**
Will better tools/service earn back the extra cost?

## Summary

Trading Costs & Spreads:
- **Critical factor:** Often forgotten but huge impact
- **Accumulates quickly:** Small spreads become large costs over 100+ trades
- **Minimize spreads:** Trade liquid pairs, peak times, tight-spread brokers
- **Cost-adjusted sizing:** Account for spread in position sizing
- **Quality targets:** Adjust profit targets to account for costs
- **Broker selection:** Can make or break your profitability

A professional trader knows their exact costs and builds strategy profitability AFTER costs, not before.
