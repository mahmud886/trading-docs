## Volatility Analysis & VIX Index

Volatility measures price movement intensity. Understanding volatility is essential for risk management and strategy selection.

### What is Volatility?

**Volatility** measures how much and how fast a price changes

**Low Volatility:**
- Small daily price movements
- Calm, predictable market
- Tight trading ranges
- Good for scalpers/day traders

**High Volatility:**
- Large daily price movements
- Unpredictable, turbulent market
- Wide trading ranges
- Risk management critical

### Measuring Volatility

#### 1. **Average True Range (ATR)**

**ATR** measures average daily price range

**Calculation:**
- For last 14 periods
- True range = highest high - lowest low
- Average across 14 periods

**Interpretation:**
- EUR/USD ATR 100: Average 100 pip daily move
- EUR/USD ATR 50: Average 50 pip daily move
- EUR/USD ATR 200: Very volatile (rare)

**Use ATR for:**
- Stop loss placement (1-1.5 × ATR)
- Profit target sizing (2 × ATR)
- Determining position size (volatility adjusted)

#### 2. **Bollinger Bands Width**

**Bands Width = Upper Band - Lower Band**

**Interpretation:**
- Wide bands: High volatility
- Narrow bands: Low volatility
- Expanding bands: Volatility increasing
- Contracting bands: Volatility decreasing

**Trading:**
- Narrow bands → Expect breakout soon
- Wide bands → Don't fight volatility
- Mean reversion in normal times
- Breakout trading in high volatility

#### 3. **Historical Volatility**

**Measures:** Past volatility (looks backward)

**Calculation:**
- Standard deviation of returns
- Over specific period (20, 50, 100 days)
- Shows how volatile it WAS

**Use:**
- Confirm volatility regime
- Compare to normal ranges
- Adjust strategy accordingly

#### 4. **Implied Volatility (Options)**

**Measures:** Expected future volatility (options market prediction)

**Where to find:**
- Options more expensive when IV high
- IV index for various assets
- Traders' expectation of forthcoming volatility

**Use:**
- High IV: Options expensive (sell)
- Low IV: Options cheap (buy)
- Gamma risk in volatile markets

### VIX Index (Volatility Index)

**VIX** = Market's expected volatility over next 30 days

**Scale:**
- 10-15: Very low volatility
- 15-20: Normal volatility
- 20-30: Elevated volatility
- 30-40: High volatility
- 40+: Extreme volatility / panic

**VIX Levels:**
```
VIX < 15: Complacent market (low fear)
VIX 15-20: Normal (healthy market)
VIX 20-30: Concern (some uncertainty)
VIX 30-40: Fear (significant concern)
VIX > 40: Panic (extreme fear)
```

**Sample VIX Events:**
- Normal times: 12-18
- Trade wars: 18-25
- Brexit: 25-35
- COVID crash: 50-80
- 2008 Crisis: 80+

### Volatility and Asset Prices

#### **Risk-On (Low Volatility):**
- Stock markets up
- Risky assets UP (equities, commodities, emerging markets)
- Safe havens DOWN (gold, Swiss francs, US treasuries down)
- VIX low (< 20)

#### **Risk-Off (High Volatility):**
- Stock markets down
- Risky assets DOWN
- Safe havens UP (gold +, CHF +, USD +)
- VIX high (> 25)

**Example:**
```
Risk-ON Day:
- S&P 500: UP
- USD/JPY: UP (risk-on sentiment)
- EUR/USD: UP (risk appetite)
- Gold: DOWN (less demand as fear hedge)
- VIX: DOWN (15-18 range)

Risk-OFF Day:
- S&P 500: DOWN
- USD/JPY: DOWN
- EUR/USD: DOWN
- Gold: UP (hedge demand)
- VIX: UP (25-35 range)
```

### Trading Volatility Strategies

#### 1. **Low Volatility Strategy**

**When VIX < 15:**
- Trade mean reversion (Bollinger Band bounces)
- Follow support/resistance bounces
- Scalp tight ranges
- Use tight stop losses

**Avoid:**
- Breakout trading (usually fails in low vol)
- Large position sizes
- Wide stops

#### 2. **High Volatility Strategy**

**When VIX > 25:**
- Trade breakouts (momentum increases)
- Use wider stops
- Larger moves coming
- Trend trading works better

**Avoid:**
- Mean reversion trades
- Tight scalping (gaps happen)
- Counter-trend trades

#### 3. **Volatility Expansion Strategy**

**When volatility expanding (ATR increasing, bands widening):**
- Good for trend traders
- Expect larger moves
- Momentum likely
- Widen stops accordingly

#### 4. **Volatility Contraction Strategy**

**When volatility contracting (ATR decreasing, bands narrowing):**
- Squeeze predicates breakout
- Prepare for breakout soon
- Large move likely coming
- Wait for confirmation before entering

### Risk Management with Volatility

**Position Size Adjustment:**
- High volatility: Smaller position sizes
- Low volatility: Normal position sizes
- Very high vol: Maybe 50% of normal size

**Stop Loss Adjustment:**
- Higher ATR: Wider stops (more realistic)
- Lower ATR: Tighter stops (reasonable)
- Example: If ATR 100, stop 100-150 pips makes sense
- If ATR 30, stop 30-50 pips makes sense

**Profit Target Adjustment:**
- More volatility: Larger profit targets possible
- Rising volatility: Extend targets
- Falling volatility: Tighten targets

### Volatility During Different Times

**Economic Calendar Events:**
- Pre-News: Volatility often drops (awaiting news)
- At Release: Volatility explodes (news hits)
- Post-News: Volatility gradually normalizes

**Market Hours:**
- Quiet hours: Low volatility (overnight)
- Peak hours: Higher volatility (major centers)
- Weekend: No trading (gaps on Monday)

**Geopolitical Events:**
- Tensions: Risk-off (up volatility)
- Peace news: Risk-on (down volatility)
- Surprises: Spike volatility

### Volatility Arbitrage

**Strategy:**
- Own implied volatility too high, sell
- Own implied volatility too low, buy
- Options strategy (advanced)
- Not for retail traders typically

### Volatility Mean Reversion

**Concept:**
- Extreme volatility doesn't last forever
- Very high vol eventually normalizes
- Very low vol eventually increases

**Application:**
- High vol (VIX 35+): Not sustainable, expect reversion to lower
- Low vol (VIX 10-12): Expect increase as market adjusts
- Trade accordingly

## Summary

**Volatility is:**
- Price movement intensity
- Measured by ATR, Bollinger Bands, VIX
- Higher in risk-off periods
- Lower in complacent times
- Critical for position sizing
- Determinant of strategy choice

**Key Guidelines:**
- Low vol: Mean reversion, tight stops
- High vol: Breakouts, wider stops
- Always adjust position size for volatility
- Use ATR to size stops
- Watch VIX for market regime
- Expand stops as volatility expands

Understanding volatility allows you to adapt your strategy to market conditions and size positions appropriately.
