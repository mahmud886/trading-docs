## What Are Prop Firms?

Proprietary trading firms (prop firms) provide traders with **funded accounts** to trade with the firm's capital. Instead of risking your own money, you trade the firm's money and share the profits — typically keeping 70-90% of what you earn.

<Callout type="info" title="The Prop Firm Advantage">
Prop firms solve the biggest barrier in trading: capital. A skilled trader with $500 can access $100,000+ in buying power by passing a challenge evaluation.
</Callout>

---

## How Prop Firms Work

### The Business Model

1. **You pay a challenge fee** (typically $100-$500 depending on account size)
2. **You trade a demo account** with specific rules and targets
3. **If you pass**, you receive a funded account with real capital
4. **You trade the funded account** and keep 70-90% of profits
5. **If you violate rules**, you lose the funded account

### Typical Challenge Structure

| Phase | Goal | Duration | Key Rules |
|-------|------|----------|-----------|
| **Phase 1 (Evaluation)** | Hit 8-10% profit target | 30 days | Max 5% daily DD, 10% total DD |
| **Phase 2 (Verification)** | Hit 5% profit target | 60 days | Same drawdown rules |
| **Funded Account** | Trade profitably | Unlimited | Same drawdown rules, profit splits |

## Challenge Rules Explained

### Daily Drawdown (Typically 5%)

- Your **daily loss** cannot exceed 5% of starting equity for that day
- Resets every day at a specific time (varies by firm)
- Example: $100,000 account = max $5,000 loss per day
- **This is the #1 reason traders fail challenges**

### Total/Maximum Drawdown (Typically 10%)

- Your account cannot drop more than 10% from initial balance
- Some firms use **trailing drawdown** (moves up with equity, never comes back down)
- Example: $100,000 account = account cannot go below $90,000

### Profit Target

- Phase 1: Usually 8-10% of account size
- Phase 2: Usually 5% of account size
- Must be achieved within the time limit

### Additional Rules

- **Minimum trading days:** 4-10 days required
- **No holding over weekends:** Some firms restrict this
- **News trading restrictions:** Some firms prohibit trading during high-impact news
- **Lot size limits:** Maximum position size may be capped
- **Consistency rules:** Some firms require no single day to contribute more than a certain percentage of total profit

## Strategies for Passing Challenges

### 1. Risk Management First

- **Risk 0.5-1% per trade maximum** (not 2%)
- Lower risk = more trades to hit target = more consistent
- Example: 1% risk on $100K = $1,000 per trade
- Need 8-10 winning trades at 1:1 R:R to pass Phase 1

### 2. Trade Your Best Setup Only

- Do NOT experiment during a challenge
- Use your highest win-rate setup exclusively
- Quality over quantity — 2-3 trades per day maximum

### 3. Session Selection

- Trade only during your best-performing session
- For most traders: London or London-NY overlap
- Avoid low-liquidity periods (Asian session for forex majors)

### 4. Scale Gradually

- Start conservatively: risk 0.5% for the first week
- Once you have a profit buffer, increase to 0.75-1%
- Never max out your daily drawdown limit

### 5. Plan Your Math

For a $100,000 account, 8% target = $8,000 needed:

| Strategy | Risk/Trade | Avg R:R | Trades Needed |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------------|
| Conservative | $500 (0.5%) | 1:2 | 8 winners |
| Standard | $1,000 (1%) | 1:2 | 4 winners |
| Moderate | $1,000 (1%) | 1:1.5 | 5-6 winners |

### 6. Protect Your Drawdown

- If down 2-3% for the day, **stop trading immediately**
- If down 5% overall, reduce risk to 0.25% until you recover
- Your **only job** is to not get disqualified

## Funded Account Management

Once funded, your priorities change:

### Priority 1: Keep the Account

- Your funded account is an **asset** worth far more than any single trade
- Trade even more conservatively than the challenge
- Risk 0.5-0.75% per trade

### Priority 2: Consistent Payouts

- Most firms require a minimum trading period before payout (14-30 days)
- Aim for steady 2-4% monthly returns
- Small, consistent profits beat occasional big wins

### Priority 3: Scaling

Many firms offer scaling plans:

| Performance | Account Growth |
|------------|---------------|
| 10% profit (consistent) | Account doubled ($100K to $200K) |
| Continued performance | Further scaling available |
| Multiple payouts | Profit split may increase to 90% |

## Tips for Bangladeshi Traders

1. **Time zone advantage** — London session starts at 2:00 PM BD time, NY overlap at 7:30 PM. Perfect evening trading schedule.

2. **Challenge fee payments** — Use international payment methods (Wise, Payoneer, or crypto payment if available)

3. **Payout methods** — Check if the firm supports withdrawal methods accessible in Bangladesh (Wise, Payoneer, crypto)

4. **Start small** — Try a smaller account size ($10K-$25K) for your first challenge to minimize fee cost

5. **Practice first** — Pass a simulated challenge on a demo account before paying for a real one

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Why Traders Fail Challenges">

**❌ Over-leveraging** — Risking 3-5% to hit target fast. One bad day and you are disqualified.

**❌ Not tracking daily drawdown** — Forgetting that daily DD resets. Getting kicked for exceeding daily limit, not overall limit.

**❌ Revenge trading** — Taking a loss, then immediately taking another trade to recover. This cascades into bigger losses.

**❌ Trading during high-impact news** — NFP, FOMC, CPI can cause 100+ pip moves in seconds. Many firms restrict this.

**❌ Changing strategy mid-challenge** — Stick with what you know. A challenge is not the time to learn new setups.

**❌ Treating it as gambling** — A challenge is a professional evaluation. Trade it like a job interview, not a casino.

</Callout>

## Challenge Preparation Checklist

1. ✅ Have a proven strategy with 50+ demo trades showing profitability
2. ✅ Know your exact win rate and average R:R
3. ✅ Read ALL challenge rules completely (every single rule)
4. ✅ Practice the challenge on a demo account first
5. ✅ Calculate how many trades you need to hit target
6. ✅ Set daily loss limits stricter than the firm requires
7. ✅ Choose your trading sessions in advance
8. ✅ Prepare mentally — treat it as a job, not a game
