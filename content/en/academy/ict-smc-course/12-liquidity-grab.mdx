### Definition

A Liquidity Grab is a **rapid, aggressive wick beyond a key level that immediately reverses**, taking out resting orders in a single candle's wick. Unlike a Liquidity Sweep (which may involve 1-3 candles beyond the level), a Grab is characterized by its SPEED and VIOLENCE — price pierces the level and snaps back within the same candle or the very next one.

**Key distinction:**
- **Liquidity Sweep:** Price may spend 1-3 candles beyond the level before reversing
- **Liquidity Grab:** Price WICKS beyond and reverses within the SAME candle (long wick, small body)

The Liquidity Grab is often visible as:
- A long upper wick (Bearish Grab of BSL)
- A long lower wick (Bullish Grab of SSL)
- A pin bar / hammer / shooting star at a Liquidity level
- Price action known as "Stop Hunt" in retail terminology

### Why It Matters

- Liquidity Grabs are the FASTEST confirmation that Smart Money has taken Liquidity
- They provide the tightest Stop Loss placement (just beyond the wick)
- They occur at the EXACT moment of institutional order filling
- Candle closes after a grab often confirm immediate reversal direction
- They're especially common during Kill Zone opens (London, NY)

### How It Works

**Bullish Liquidity Grab (SSL Taken):**
1. Key low exists (swing low, equal lows, session low)
2. A single candle drops below with a long lower wick
3. The candle CLOSES back above the level
4. The long wick = Smart Money grabbed SSL and bought
5. Next candles move UP — the real direction

**Bearish Liquidity Grab (BSL Taken):**
1. Key high exists (swing high, equal highs, session high)
2. A single candle spikes above with a long upper wick
3. The candle CLOSES back below the level
4. The long wick = Smart Money grabbed BSL and sold
5. Next candles move DOWN — the real direction

**Grab vs Sweep vs Break:**
| Price Action | Description | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Grab** | Wick beyond, same candle close back | Strongest reversal signal |
| **Sweep** | 1-3 candles beyond, then reversal | Strong reversal signal |
| **Break** | Multiple candle CLOSES beyond level | Genuine breakout — not a grab |

### Institutional Logic

The Liquidity Grab represents the MOST EFFICIENT institutional entry:
- Smart Money fills orders in milliseconds during the grab
- The wick represents the exact price range where institutional orders were filled
- The body close direction shows their intended direction
- They don't NEED to stay beyond the level — they got what they needed (counterparty Liquidity)

**Why grabs are aggressive:**
- High-frequency algorithms execute thousands of orders during the grab
- Market makers widen spreads momentarily during the grab
- The entire process happens in seconds to minutes
- Retail traders see a "spike" — institutions see an "order fill"

### Trading Application

**Trading the Liquidity Grab:**

1. **Identify Liquidity Level** — Mark where stops are clustered
2. **Wait for Grab Candle** — Long wick that goes beyond and closes back
3. **Confirm with candle close** — The grab candle should close in your intended direction
4. **Enter on next candle** — After the grab candle closes:
   - If Bullish Grab (long lower wick) → Enter Buy on next candle open
   - If Bearish Grab (long upper wick) → Enter Sell on next candle open
5. **Stop Loss** — Beyond the wick extreme (the very tip of the grab wick)
6. **Target** — Opposing Liquidity pool

**Best Timeframe for Grabs:**
- 15M grabs at 4H/1H levels = Excellent swing entries
- 5M grabs at 15M levels = Day trade entries
- 1M grabs at 5M levels = Scalp entries
- 4H grabs at Daily/Weekly levels = Position entries

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ **Confusing a grab with a breakout** — If the candle CLOSES beyond = Not a grab
- ❌ **Entering during the grab** — Wait for the candle to CLOSE first
- ❌ **Stop Loss at the level** — Put it beyond the WICK tip, not at the level
- ❌ **Expecting grabs at every level** — Not all levels produce grabs. Context matters
- ❌ **Trading grabs against HTF trend** — Only trade grabs in your bias direction

### Advanced Notes

- **Grab + Order Block** — If the grab wick reaches into a HTF OB → Highest probability
- **Grab + FVG Fill** — If the grab wick fills a previous FVG → Institutional fill confirmed
- **Multiple grabs at same level** — If price grabs the same level twice → Second grab is usually final
- **Grab timing** — First 30 minutes of London/NY often produce the best grabs
- **Grab on news** — News events can create valid grabs but require wider stops
- **Grab wick = Future support/resistance** — The tip of the grab wick often acts as a future reference point

### Example

**USD/CAD — Bearish Liquidity Grab:**

1. **Setup:** 4H Equal Highs at 1.3650 (BSL above)
2. **Daily Bias:** Bearish (price in Premium, making LH)
3. **NY Open (5:30 PM BD):** Single 15M candle spikes to 1.3665 (grabs BSL)
4. **Candle closes** at 1.3640 with massive upper wick → Bearish Liquidity Grab confirmed
5. **Entry:** Sell at 1.3638 (open of next candle)
6. **Stop Loss:** 1.3670 (above grab wick tip)
7. **Target:** 1.3550 (SSL — swing low below)
8. **R:R:** 1:2.75, +88 pips
