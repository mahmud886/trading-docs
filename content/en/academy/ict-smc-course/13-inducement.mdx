### Definition

Inducement is a **minor structural break within a pullback** designed to lure retail traders into premature entries. It's a small BOS or swing point break that looks like a valid entry but is actually a TRAP to build Liquidity that Smart Money will sweep before the real move.

Think of Inducement as "bait" — it creates a false sense of confirmation that tricks traders into entering, only for their Stop Losses to be taken shortly after.

**How Inducement forms:**

- In a bullish trend, during a pullback, price briefly breaks a minor low → Retail thinks "bearish continuation" and sells
- OR price breaks a minor high within the pullback → Retail thinks "bullish continuation" and buys early
- Smart Money then sweeps these new Stop Losses before delivering the true move

### Why It Matters

- Inducement explains WHY your "valid" setups get stopped out before working
- It creates the Liquidity that Smart Money needs for the REAL entry
- Recognizing Inducement keeps you OUT of losing trades
- The sweep of Inducement is often your TRUE high-probability entry signal
- It separates patient, professional traders from impulsive retail traders

### How It Works

**Bullish Trend Inducement (During Pullback):**

1. Price is in a bullish trend (HH/HL)
2. Price begins pulling back (normal retracement)
3. Within the pullback, price breaks a MINOR low → Creates a small "bearish BOS"
4. Retail traders see this mini-BOS and enter Short (or early buyers get stopped)
5. Their Stop Losses are placed above the broken low = New Liquidity ABOVE
6. Smart Money sweeps this Liquidity (takes their stops)
7. Price then continues the ACTUAL bullish move

**Bearish Trend Inducement (During Pullback):**

1. Price is in a bearish trend (LH/LL)
2. Price begins pulling back (retracing up)
3. Within the pullback, price breaks a MINOR high → Creates a small "bullish BOS"
4. Retail traders enter Long, their stops placed below
5. Smart Money sweeps these stops
6. Price then continues the actual bearish move

### Institutional Logic

**Why Smart Money creates Inducement:**

1. **Additional Liquidity** — The pullback alone doesn't provide enough counterparty orders
2. **Better entry price** — By sweeping Inducement, they enter slightly deeper in Discount
3. **Eliminate weak hands** — Traders who entered too early get stopped out (less competition)
4. **Create panic** — The minor break makes the pullback look like a reversal, creating more sellers
5. **Perfect timing** — Inducement sweep often coincides with Kill Zone entry windows

**The psychology:**

- Retail trader sees minor BOS in pullback → "The trend is reversing! I'll sell!"
- Smart Money sees: "Excellent. More stops for me to fill against. Wait for Kill Zone, sweep, enter."

### Trading Application

**Identifying Inducement:**

- Look at pullbacks within a clear trend
- Mark MINOR swing points within the pullback (not the major trend structure)
- If a minor swing gets broken during the pullback = INDUCEMENT
- DON'T trade this minor break — it's bait

**Using Inducement for Entry:**

1. Identify the trend (bullish/bearish on HTF)
2. Wait for pullback to begin
3. Watch for Inducement to form (minor structural break within pullback)
4. Wait for Inducement to be SWEPT (price takes out the stops created by Inducement)
5. After sweep → Look for MSS on LTF confirming trend continuation
6. Enter at the PD Array (OB/FVG) formed after the Inducement sweep
7. Stop Loss: Beyond the sweep of Inducement
8. Target: Next major structural high/low (Liquidity)

**Red flags that it's Inducement (NOT real reversal):**

- The break is very small (internal structure only, not major swing)
- It occurs within a CLEAR HTF trend
- No significant displacement on the break
- Happens before a Kill Zone (building Liquidity for the real move)
- The "break" is against the HTF bias

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ **Trading every minor BOS as a new entry** — Minor BOS within pullbacks = Inducement
- ❌ **Not distinguishing internal vs external structure** — External BOS = Real. Internal = Possible Inducement
- ❌ **Getting impatient and entering at Inducement level** — Wait for it to be swept
- ❌ **Placing stops right at Inducement levels** — Smart Money WILL take them
- ❌ **Ignoring Inducement and wondering why stops keep getting hit** — Learn to wait

### Advanced Notes

- **Inducement always forms within PD Arrays** — It's built inside Order Blocks and FVG zones
- **Multiple Inducements** — Sometimes 2-3 form in a single pullback before the real entry
- **Inducement on multiple TFs** — A 15M Inducement within a 4H pullback is textbook
- **Speed of Inducement creation** — Faster formation = more likely to be swept soon
- **Inducement + OTE Zone** — When Inducement aligns with 62-79% Fib = highest probability
- **Failed Inducement sweep** (price doesn't sweep it) — May indicate the pullback is becoming a genuine reversal

### Example

**EUR/USD — Bullish Inducement Sweep Entry:**

1. **HTF:** Daily bullish (HH/HL). Price pulling back
2. **4H Pullback:** Price retraces from 1.0920 to 1.0860 zone
3. **Inducement:** Within pullback, 15M shows a minor low at 1.0875 being broken → Price makes new low at 1.0868
4. **Retail reaction:** Traders who sold at 1.0868 placed SL above 1.0878 = Inducement Liquidity
5. **Sweep:** During London KZ, price drops to 1.0855 (sweeps the Inducement + deeper SSL)
6. **MSS:** 5M Bullish CHOCH at 1.0865
7. **Entry:** Buy at 1.0860 (Bullish OB formed after sweep)
8. **Stop Loss:** 1.0850 (below sweep extreme)
9. **Target:** 1.0920+ (BSL above — previous high)
10. **R:R:** 1:6.0, +60 pips
