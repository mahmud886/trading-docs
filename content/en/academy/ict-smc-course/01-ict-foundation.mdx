### Definition

ICT (Inner Circle Trader) is a trading methodology developed by Michael J. Huddleston that teaches traders how to understand and trade alongside institutional market participants — banks, hedge funds, and market makers. Unlike retail trading methods that rely on lagging indicators, ICT focuses on understanding the **algorithm** that delivers price between Liquidity pools and Imbalances.

The core philosophy: The market is NOT random. It is an algorithm designed to seek Liquidity, fill Imbalances (Fair Value Gaps), and deliver price between Premium and Discount zones.

### Why It Matters

- **90%+ of retail traders lose money** because they trade against institutional order flow
- ICT teaches you to read the footprints that Smart Money leaves on charts
- Understanding ICT gives you the ability to predict WHERE price will go and WHEN it will move
- Instead of guessing with indicators, you trade with institutional logic
- ICT concepts apply to ALL markets: Forex, Crypto, Stocks, Indices, Commodities

### How It Works

ICT methodology operates on these foundational principles:

1. **Algorithmic Price Delivery** — Price is delivered by the IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm) that follows predictable patterns
2. **Liquidity is the Fuel** — Every move in the market is designed to seek and capture Liquidity (Stop Losses and Pending Orders)
3. **Time-Based Trading** — Specific hours of the day (Kill Zones) produce the highest-probability setups
4. **Premium & Discount** — Institutions buy below 50% (Discount) and sell above 50% (Premium) of any range
5. **PD Arrays** — Institutional footprints like Order Blocks, FVGs, and Breaker Blocks show where Smart Money operated

The learning path:
- **Phase 1:** Market Structure, Liquidity, BOS/CHOCH
- **Phase 2:** PD Arrays (Order Blocks, FVGs, Breaker Blocks)
- **Phase 3:** Time & Price Theory (Kill Zones, Sessions, Daily Bias)
- **Phase 4:** Execution Models (Market Maker Model, Silver Bullet, 2022 Model)

### Institutional Logic

Smart Money (banks, hedge funds, market makers) operates differently from retail traders:

| Smart Money                          | Retail Traders                       |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Need Liquidity to fill large orders  | Use market orders randomly           |
| Engineer moves to trap retail        | Fall into traps (Buy high, Sell low) |
| Trade at specific times (Kill Zones) | Trade any time                       |
| Use Premium/Discount zones           | Use RSI overbought/oversold          |
| Target opposing Liquidity pools      | Use fixed Take Profit levels         |
| Create and then fill Imbalances      | Don't understand FVGs                |

The algorithm's job:
1. Build Liquidity (create obvious highs/lows that attract orders)
2. Sweep Liquidity (take out Stop Losses)
3. Displace to fill Imbalances (create the real move)
4. Deliver price to the opposing Liquidity pool

### Trading Application

To apply ICT in your trading:

1. **Top-Down Analysis** — Start from Monthly → Weekly → Daily → 4H → Entry Timeframe
2. **Identify Liquidity** — Mark equal highs/lows, obvious swing points, session levels
3. **Determine Bias** — Is price in Premium or Discount? What is the HTF direction?
4. **Wait for Kill Zone** — Trade only during London (2-5 AM EST) or NY AM (7-10 AM EST)
5. **Find PD Array** — Look for Order Block, FVG, or Breaker Block in the OTE zone
6. **Execute after MSS** — Enter after Market Structure Shift confirms direction
7. **Target opposing Liquidity** — Your Take Profit is the next Liquidity pool

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ Trying to learn everything at once — ICT has 50+ concepts. Master ONE model first
- ❌ Trading every session — Stick to ONE Kill Zone until profitable
- ❌ Ignoring Daily Bias — Every trade needs HTF directional conviction
- ❌ No patience — Best ICT setups come 1-2x per day. Wait for the setup
- ❌ Confusing ICT with SMC — SMC simplifies ICT. Learn the original source
- ❌ Using indicators with ICT — ICT is pure Price Action + Time. No RSI, no MACD
- ❌ Backtesting without time — Must use replay mode to see time-based setups

### Advanced Notes

- The IPDA operates on 20-day, 40-day, and 60-day lookback cycles
- Every Daily candle follows Power of 3: Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution
- Quarterly shifts occur around FOMC meetings, NFP, and significant news events
- The algorithm respects institutional dealing ranges formed on Monthly and Weekly timeframes
- True institutional traders use time as their primary edge, not just price levels
- ICT Macros (:50-:10 of specific hours) show exact moments of algorithmic displacement

### Example

**EUR/USD — Complete ICT Day Trade:**

1. **Daily Bias:** Bullish — Price is in Weekly Discount, Daily making Higher Lows
2. **Asian Range:** 1.0870 - 1.0890 (Mark this before London)
3. **London Kill Zone (2:00 PM BD):** Price drops below Asian Low to 1.0862 — Liquidity Sweep of Sell Side Liquidity
4. **MSS:** 5M shows Bullish Market Structure Shift at 2:35 PM BD
5. **PD Array:** 15M Bullish Order Block at 1.0865, overlapping with FVG
6. **Entry:** Buy at 1.0867 (inside OB + FVG)
7. **Stop Loss:** 1.0855 (below Manipulation low)
8. **Target:** 1.0930 (Buy Side Liquidity — equal highs above)
9. **Result:** +63 pips, Risk:Reward 1:5.25
