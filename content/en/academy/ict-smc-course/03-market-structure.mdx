### Definition

Market Structure is the **foundation of all price action trading**. It describes the pattern of Swing Highs and Swing Lows that price creates as it moves through a market. By reading Market Structure, you can determine:

- The current **TREND** (Bullish, Bearish, or Ranging)
- **WHERE** price is likely heading next
- **WHEN** a trend is changing (reversal)
- **WHO** is in control — Buyers or Sellers

**Bullish Market Structure:**
- Series of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
- Each pullback makes a HIGHER low than the previous
- Each impulse makes a HIGHER high than the previous

**Bearish Market Structure:**
- Series of Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
- Each pullback makes a LOWER high than the previous
- Each impulse makes a LOWER low than the previous

**Ranging Market Structure:**
- Price oscillates between a defined high and low
- No clear HH/HL or LH/LL pattern
- Equal Highs and Equal Lows form (Liquidity builds on both sides)

### Why It Matters

Market Structure tells you THE TREND — and the trend is your greatest edge:

- **Bullish Structure = Buy opportunities only** — Every pullback is a chance to buy
- **Bearish Structure = Sell opportunities only** — Every rally is a chance to sell
- **When Structure BREAKS = Potential reversal** — Time to reassess bias

Without Market Structure understanding:
- You buy in a Bearish trend and wonder why you keep losing
- You sell in a Bullish trend against the institutional flow
- You don't know when to STOP buying and start selling (trend change)
- You take random trades with no directional bias

### How It Works

**Identifying Swing Points:**

A **Swing High** is formed when:
- The candle has a HIGHER high than the candles on both sides
- Minimum 3-candle formation (high flanked by lower highs)
- Represents a point where SELLERS temporarily overcame BUYERS

A **Swing Low** is formed when:
- The candle has a LOWER low than the candles on both sides
- Minimum 3-candle formation (low flanked by higher lows)
- Represents a point where BUYERS temporarily overcame SELLERS

**Reading the Structure:**

1. Mark the most recent Swing Highs and Swing Lows
2. Connect them to see the pattern:
   - HH + HL = Bullish
   - LH + LL = Bearish
3. Identify the MOST RECENT significant swing point — this is your reference for BOS/CHOCH
4. Determine if price is making new extremes or failing

**Strong vs Weak Highs/Lows:**

| Type | Characteristic | Implication |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| **Strong High** | Created by BOS in bearish trend | Protected by institutions — not easily broken |
| **Weak High** | Last HH before CHOCH/BOS bearish | Will likely be taken (Liquidity target) |
| **Strong Low** | Created by BOS in bullish trend | Protected — holds as support |
| **Weak Low** | Last LL before CHOCH/BOS bullish | Will likely be swept |

### Institutional Logic

Institutions USE Market Structure to:

1. **Build the trend** — They accumulate positions during pullbacks (HL in uptrend, LH in downtrend)
2. **Trap retail traders** — They create false breaks of structure to sweep Liquidity
3. **Signal their intentions** — BOS confirms they want price to continue; CHOCH signals they're changing sides
4. **Create Liquidity** — Each Swing Point creates Stop Losses above/below it

**The Institutional Perspective on Structure:**
- A bullish trend exists because institutions are NET BUYERS
- They buy at Discount (pullback lows) targeting previous highs (Liquidity)
- When they're done buying, they ALLOW structure to break (CHOCH) and begin selling
- The new bearish structure means they are now NET SELLERS

### Trading Application

**Step 1: Determine HTF Structure (Daily/4H)**
- Is the Daily making HH/HL? → Bullish bias → Only look for Buys on LTF
- Is the Daily making LH/LL? → Bearish bias → Only look for Sells on LTF

**Step 2: Identify Key Structural Levels**
- Mark the most recent significant Swing High and Swing Low
- Identify which is STRONG (protected) and which is WEAK (likely to be taken)

**Step 3: Wait for LTF Confirmation**
- On 15M/5M, wait for price to pull back INTO a Discount zone (for Buys)
- Wait for MSS (Market Structure Shift) confirming the LTF aligns with HTF

**Step 4: Enter with Structure**
- Buy when LTF turns Bullish (aligning with HTF Bullish)
- Sell when LTF turns Bearish (aligning with HTF Bearish)
- Stop Loss beyond the recent structural point

**Multi-Timeframe Structure Rules:**
- Monthly → Macro trend direction
- Weekly → Swing trade bias
- Daily → Day trade bias
- 4H → Intraday structure
- 15M/5M → Entry structure
- 1M → Sniper entry (optional)

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ **Confusing minor and major structure** — Not every candle high/low is a Swing Point
- ❌ **Trading against HTF structure** — A 5M BOS doesn't override a Daily downtrend
- ❌ **Not distinguishing Strong vs Weak levels** — Weak levels WILL be broken; Strong levels provide protection
- ❌ **Changing bias too quickly** — One BOS doesn't always mean trend change. Context matters
- ❌ **Over-marking structure** — Keep it clean. Only mark the MOST significant swing points
- ❌ **Ignoring the pullback** — Structure isn't just highs and lows; the DEPTH of pullback matters

### Advanced Notes

- **Sub-structure** within a pullback shows whether the retracement is corrective or impulsive
- **Order Flow** within structure: If each HL in a bullish trend is getting SHALLOWER, momentum is fading
- **Time-based structure**: New York session often creates or breaks the day's structure
- **Fractal nature**: Every timeframe has its own structure. What looks bullish on 5M can be a pullback on 4H
- **Displacement**: The SPEED of structural breaks matters. Fast breaks with FVGs = institutional. Slow grinds = weak
- **Protected structures**: If a structural low has a large Bullish OB below it with no FVG, it's STRONG

### Example

**GBP/USD — Multi-Timeframe Structure Analysis:**

**Daily Structure:** Bullish — HH at 1.2750, HL at 1.2580. Current price: 1.2650
- Bias: BULLISH (only look for Buy setups)
- Weak High: 1.2750 (will likely be taken as target)
- Strong Low: 1.2580 (protected — unlikely to break without CHOCH)

**4H Structure:** Price pulling back from 1.2720, making internal LH/LL
- This is NORMAL in a bullish trend — just a pullback
- Looking for 4H to shift bullish again (BOS above recent LH)

**15M Structure:** After sweeping a 4H HL at 1.2630 (Liquidity Sweep):
- 15M shows Bullish CHOCH at 1.2645
- Bullish OB at 1.2635-1.2640 (inside 4H Discount)
- Entry: Buy at 1.2638
- Stop Loss: 1.2625 (below sweep low)
- Target: 1.2720+ (4H high / Buy Side Liquidity)
- R:R: 1:6.3
