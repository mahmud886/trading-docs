### Definition

Premium & Discount is the ICT framework that divides ANY price range into two zones using the **50% level (Equilibrium)**:

- **Premium Zone (above 50%):** Price is EXPENSIVE → Only look for SELL setups
- **Discount Zone (below 50%):** Price is CHEAP → Only look for BUY setups
- **Equilibrium (50%):** Fair value — no edge for either side

The concept is simple: **Institutions BUY cheap (Discount) and SELL expensive (Premium)**. Just like a business buys wholesale and sells retail, Smart Money accumulates positions at Discount and distributes at Premium.

**How to calculate:**
```
Equilibrium = (Swing High + Swing Low) / 2
Premium = Everything ABOVE Equilibrium
Discount = Everything BELOW Equilibrium
```

### Why It Matters

- Premium/Discount gives you IMMEDIATE clarity on trade direction
- It prevents the #1 retail mistake: buying at tops and selling at bottoms
- If price is in Premium → You should ONLY sell (or wait for Discount)
- If price is in Discount → You should ONLY buy (or wait for Premium)
- It aligns your trades with institutional order flow automatically

### How It Works

**Applying Premium/Discount to a Dealing Range:**
1. Identify the most recent significant Swing High and Swing Low
2. Draw the 50% level (Equilibrium) between them
3. Everything above 50% = Premium (Sell zone)
4. Everything below 50% = Discount (Buy zone)

**Fibonacci Levels for Precision:**
| Fib Level | Zone | Usage |
|-----------|------|-------|
| 0% | Swing Low (extreme Discount) | Deepest Buy zone |
| 23.6% | Deep Discount | Strong Buy zone |
| 38.2% | Discount | Buy zone |
| 50% | Equilibrium | No trade / Fair value |
| 61.8% | Premium | Sell zone |
| 70.5% | Deep Premium (OTE for sells) | Sweet spot for Sells |
| 79% | Extreme Premium | Highest probability Sell zone |
| 100% | Swing High (extreme Premium) | Deepest Sell zone |

**OTE (Optimal Trade Entry):** 62-79% of a range. For Buys = 62-79% of Discount leg. For Sells = 62-79% of Premium leg.

### Institutional Logic

**Why institutions follow Premium/Discount:**

- **Capital efficiency:** Buying at Discount maximizes profit potential per unit of risk
- **Order flow mechanics:** At Discount, there's SSL (Sell Side Liquidity) → Counterparty for Buy orders
- **Statistical edge:** Price in Discount has positive expectancy for longs; Premium for shorts
- **Risk management:** Buying at Discount means your Stop Loss is smaller (closer to structure low)

**Smart Money Premium/Discount cycle:**
1. Price drops to Discount → Institutions BUY (accumulate)
2. Price rises through Equilibrium (trending)
3. Price reaches Premium → Institutions SELL (distribute)
4. Price drops back → Cycle repeats

### Trading Application

**Step 1: Define your range**
- Use the current HTF Dealing Range (the most recent major swing high to low)
- OR use the most recent expansion leg (BOS to BOS)
- Mark 50% (Equilibrium) on your chart

**Step 2: Determine current position**
- Where is price NOW? Premium or Discount?
- If Premium → Only look for SHORT entries
- If Discount → Only look for LONG entries
- At Equilibrium → WAIT for price to commit to a zone

**Step 3: Find entry within correct zone**
- In Discount: Look for Bullish OB, FVG, Breaker in the 62-79% Discount range
- In Premium: Look for Bearish OB, FVG, Breaker in the 62-79% Premium range
- Entry at these PD Arrays within the correct zone = Institutional alignment

**Step 4: Set targets**
- If buying in Discount → Target: Premium zone (specifically BSL in Premium)
- If selling in Premium → Target: Discount zone (specifically SSL in Discount)
- Minimum target = Equilibrium (50%)
- Maximum target = Opposing Liquidity pool

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ **Buying in Premium** — Price is expensive. Wait for Discount
- ❌ **Selling in Discount** — Price is cheap. Wait for Premium
- ❌ **Ignoring the 50% level** — Equilibrium is a powerful magnet and decision point
- ❌ **Using wrong range** — Make sure you're using the CURRENT relevant dealing range
- ❌ **Not updating the range** — When structure breaks, the range changes. Update your levels
- ❌ **Trading at Equilibrium** — 50% offers no statistical edge. Wait for commitment to a zone

### Advanced Notes

- **Nested Premium/Discount:** The Daily range has P/D, the 4H range within it has its own P/D, and the 1H has its own. Best entries = Discount on ALL timeframes simultaneously
- **Premium of a Discount move:** Within a bullish retracement, the 62-79% zone is the "Premium of the Discount" — ideal entry
- **Equilibrium as magnet:** Price is drawn to 50% of ANY range. Use this for initial targets
- **Time-based P/D:** Price spends about 50% of its time in each zone over complete cycles
- **P/D inversion:** When structure breaks, Premium becomes new Discount (and vice versa)
- **Combining P/D with time:** Entering at Discount during a Kill Zone = Maximum probability

### Example

**NAS100 — Buy in Discount:**

1. **4H Range:** Swing High 18,500 / Swing Low 17,800
2. **Equilibrium:** (18,500 + 17,800) / 2 = 18,150
3. **Current price:** 17,950 → IN DISCOUNT ✓ (below 18,150)
4. **OTE Zone (62-79% of Discount):** 17,890 - 17,845
5. **Setup:** 15M Bullish OB at 17,870 (inside Discount OTE)
6. **Entry:** Buy at 17,870
7. **Stop Loss:** 17,790 (below Swing Low)
8. **Target:** 18,400 (BSL in Premium)
9. **R:R:** 1:6.6, +530 points
