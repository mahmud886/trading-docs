### Definition

Equal Lows occur when price reaches the **same low point two or more times**, creating a flat or near-flat support level. In ICT/SMC, equal lows are NOT support — they are **Liquidity magnets** representing the maximum concentration of Sell Side Liquidity (SSL).

Equal Lows are the mirror of Equal Highs:
- Equal Highs = Buy Side Liquidity above (Smart Money targets to SELL)
- Equal Lows = Sell Side Liquidity below (Smart Money targets to BUY)

Every trader who bought at these lows has a Stop Loss BELOW them. Every breakdown trader has a Sell Stop below. This creates a massive pool of Sell orders that Smart Money uses as counterparty Liquidity to fill their Buy positions.

### Why It Matters

- Equal Lows = **Guaranteed future target** for price to sweep
- They indicate WHERE Smart Money will accumulate Buy positions
- They help set Take Profit for Short trades (TP at Equal Lows)
- They warn you NOT to Buy at this level until it's swept (your stop will be taken)
- After the sweep of Equal Lows, the BEST Long entries form

### How It Works

**Formation:**
1. Price drops to a level (e.g., 1.0850) and bounces → First low
2. Price rallies, pulls back to approximately 1.0850 again → Second low (Equal Low formed)
3. Each touch adds Stop Losses and Pending Sell orders below
4. Retail sees "double bottom" or "strong support" → Buys with stops below
5. Smart Money waits, then sweeps below → Fills Buy orders against retail Sell stops

**What happens when Equal Lows are swept:**
- Price drops below the equal lows briefly (5-20+ pips)
- All Sell Stops trigger → Creates massive Sell order flow
- Smart Money BUYS against this Sell order flow
- Price reverses aggressively upward
- This is the IDEAL Buy entry point

**Equal Lows Classification:**
| Type | Liquidity Level | Action |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| Double Lows (2 touches) | High SSL | Wait for sweep to Buy |
| Triple Lows (3 touches) | Very High SSL | High priority sweep target |
| Quad+ Lows (4+ touches) | Maximum SSL | Extremely high probability sweep |
| Near-Equal (within 1-5 pips) | Counts as Equal Lows | Same trading approach |

### Institutional Logic

**Why Smart Money loves Equal Lows:**

1. **Maximum counterparty Liquidity** — Thousands of Sell Stops below = Perfect for filling Buy orders
2. **Retail psychology exploit** — "Support has held 3 times, it must be strong" → More buyers = More stops below
3. **Controlled entry price** — They can buy at Discount (below previous support) with minimal slippage
4. **Narrative control** — The "breakdown" panics retail into selling, providing even more Liquidity

**The institutional sequence:**
- Build Equal Lows (push price to same level repeatedly)
- Allow retail to accumulate Buy positions (more stops build below)
- Sweep below (trigger all stops, create panic selling)
- Absorb all Sell orders with institutional Buy orders
- Rally price aggressively (distribution phase up)

### Trading Application

**Strategy 1: Equal Lows as Take Profit (Bearish Trade)**
- You're Short targeting the next SSL
- Equal Lows below = Your logical TP level
- Place TP just above Equal Lows (2-5 pips above)
- Example: Short from 1.0920, Equal Lows at 1.0850 → TP at 1.0853

**Strategy 2: Equal Lows Sweep as Buy Entry**
1. Mark Equal Lows on chart (2+ touches at same level)
2. Confirm HTF is BULLISH (price in Discount, Daily making HL)
3. Wait for price to SWEEP below Equal Lows (goes 5-20+ pips below)
4. Watch for Bullish MSS/CHOCH on 5M/15M immediately after sweep
5. Enter Buy at OB/FVG formed after the reversal
6. Stop Loss: Below the sweep extreme (the lowest wick)
7. Target: Buy Side Liquidity above (equal highs, swing highs)

**Strategy 3: Avoid the Trap**
- Never Buy AT Equal Lows — they will be swept
- Never place Stop Loss just below Equal Lows — it will be taken
- Wait patiently for the sweep, THEN buy
- The entry after sweep is always better than trying to "buy support"

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ **Buying at Equal Lows** — They WILL be swept. Wait for the sweep
- ❌ **Stop Loss just below Equal Lows** — First target for Smart Money
- ❌ **Treating double bottoms as Buy signals** — In ICT, they're Liquidity targets
- ❌ **Missing the sweep entry** — After sweep + MSS, you must act quickly
- ❌ **Expecting support to hold** — "Support" in ICT = Liquidity pool to be swept

### Advanced Notes

- **Equal Lows + HTF Discount** = Highest probability Buy zone after sweep
- **Equal Lows + Bullish OB below** = Price sweeps lows into OB then reverses
- **Failed sweep** (price sweeps and continues lower) = Genuinely bearish. Exit immediately
- **Multiple Equal Low levels** — Price may sweep one level then continue to sweep another
- **Equal Lows on Weekly/Monthly** = Major institutional accumulation zones
- **Equal Lows + SMT Divergence** = Strongest reversal confirmation after sweep
