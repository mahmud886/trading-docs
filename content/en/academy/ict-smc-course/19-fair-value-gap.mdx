### Definition

A Fair Value Gap (FVG) is a **3-candle price imbalance** where the wicks of Candle 1 and Candle 3 do NOT overlap, leaving a gap created by Candle 2's displacement. This gap represents inefficient price delivery — an area where price moved too aggressively in one direction, and the algorithm will likely return to "fill" or "rebalance" this imbalance.

- **Bullish FVG (BISI):** Gap between Candle 1's HIGH and Candle 3's LOW → Price returns DOWN to fill (Buy zone)
- **Bearish FVG (SIBI):** Gap between Candle 1's LOW and Candle 3's HIGH → Price returns UP to fill (Sell zone)

**Consequent Encroachment (CE):** The 50% midpoint of any FVG — the optimal entry point within the gap.

### Why It Matters

- FVGs are your PRECISION entry tool — tighter than Order Blocks
- The algorithm is programmed to return and fill imbalances
- FVGs created by displacement = Institutional footprints
- CE (50% of FVG) provides the highest probability entry within the gap
- FVGs confirm the validity of Order Blocks (OB + FVG overlap = best entry)

### How It Works

**Bullish FVG Formation:**
1. Candle 1: Any candle (note its HIGH)
2. Candle 2: Strong BULLISH displacement candle (creates the gap)
3. Candle 3: Any candle (note its LOW)
4. Gap = Candle 1 HIGH to Candle 3 LOW (if C3 Low > C1 High = FVG exists)
5. Price returns to this gap = Buy opportunity

**Bearish FVG Formation:**
1. Candle 1: Any candle (note its LOW)
2. Candle 2: Strong BEARISH displacement candle
3. Candle 3: Any candle (note its HIGH)
4. Gap = Candle 1 LOW to Candle 3 HIGH (if C3 High < C1 Low = FVG exists)
5. Price returns to this gap = Sell opportunity

**FVG Entry Methods:**
| Method | Entry Point | Risk Level |
|--------|------------|------------|
| Full gap fill | Bottom of Bullish FVG / Top of Bearish FVG | Conservative |
| CE (50%) | Midpoint of FVG | Optimal (recommended) |
| Partial fill | First touch of FVG edge | Aggressive |

### Institutional Logic

**Why FVGs exist:**
- Institutional displacement is SO aggressive that price cannot deliver efficiently
- The algorithm MUST return to fill these gaps for efficient price delivery
- Unfilled FVGs act as magnets — price is drawn to them
- Filled FVGs lose their magnetic pull (already rebalanced)

**FVG as entry vehicle:**
- Smart Money creates FVGs during displacement
- They EXPECT price to return to the FVG before continuing
- The FVG is where they place their limit orders for re-entry
- CE (50%) is the mathematical sweet spot for institutional fills

### Trading Application

**Trading Bullish FVG:**
1. Identify displacement UP that creates a gap (C1 High < C3 Low)
2. Mark the FVG zone (C1 High to C3 Low)
3. Calculate CE: (C1 High + C3 Low) / 2
4. Set Buy Limit at CE
5. Stop Loss: Below C1 High (or below the OB if one exists beneath FVG)
6. Target: Next BSL above

**FVG Quality Assessment:**
- FVG created during Kill Zone = High quality
- FVG that creates BOS/CHOCH = Institutional
- FVG within OTE zone of a larger move = Best context
- FVG on HTF (4H, Daily) = More significant than LTF FVG
- Multiple FVGs stacked = Price likely fills the deepest one

**Inversion FVG:**
- When a Bullish FVG gets fully traded through (becomes resistance) = Bearish signal
- When a Bearish FVG gets fully traded through (becomes support) = Bullish signal
- Inverted FVGs provide re-entry opportunities in the NEW direction

### Common Mistakes

- ❌ Trading every FVG — Only trade FVGs with HTF alignment and proper context
- ❌ Entering at the edge instead of CE — CE gives better R:R
- ❌ Not distinguishing institutional FVG from retail FVG — Must have displacement + structure break
- ❌ Expecting all FVGs to fill — Some remain open (price is too strong to retrace)
- ❌ Trading FVGs against the trend — Only trade FVGs in your bias direction

### Advanced Notes

- **BISI** (Buy-Side Imbalance, Sell-Side Inefficiency) = Bullish FVG
- **SIBI** (Sell-Side Imbalance, Buy-Side Inefficiency) = Bearish FVG
- **Consequent Encroachment** = 50% of FVG. If price trades THROUGH CE → FVG is likely to be fully filled
- **Implied FVG** = When wicks DO slightly overlap but bodies don't — treated as FVG because body range was inefficient
- **FVG as invalidation** = If price closes through the FULL FVG → Setup is invalid
- **Time-based FVG** = FVGs created during Kill Zones are most reliable

### Example

**EUR/USD — Bullish FVG CE Entry:**

1. **Context:** 4H Bullish. 15M shows displacement up creating FVG
2. **FVG:** C1 High = 1.0885, C3 Low = 1.0895. Gap = 1.0885-1.0895
3. **CE:** (1.0885 + 1.0895) / 2 = 1.0890
4. **Entry:** Buy Limit at 1.0890 (CE)
5. **Stop Loss:** 1.0882 (below FVG / below OB beneath)
6. **Target:** 1.0940 (BSL above)
7. **R:R:** 1:6.25, +50 pips on 8 pip risk
