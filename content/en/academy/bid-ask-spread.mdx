## Bid, Ask & Spread Mechanics

Understanding bid-ask spreads is fundamental to grasping how trading markets function and costs that reduce profits.

### Bid Price

The **bid price** is the highest price a buyer is willing to pay for an asset.

**Key Points:**
- What you receive if you SELL immediately
- Lower than ask price
- Set by buyers (demand)
- Always lower side of quote
- Determines sell entry price

**Example:**
EUR/USD bid: 1.0950
- If you sell now, you get 1.0950
- Market maker buys at this price
- This is the price shown on sell orders

### Ask Price

The **ask price** is the lowest price a seller is willing to accept for an asset.

**Key Points:**
- What you pay if you BUY immediately
- Higher than bid price
- Set by sellers (supply)
- Always higher side of quote
- Determines buy entry price

**Example:**
EUR/USD ask: 1.0952
- If you buy now, you pay 1.0952
- Market maker sells at this price
- This is the price shown on buy orders

### The Spread

The **spread** is the difference between bid and ask prices.

**Formula:**
Spread (pips) = Ask - Bid

**Example:**
- Bid: 1.0950
- Ask: 1.0952
- Spread: 2 pips (0.0002)

**Spreads in different currencies:**
```
EUR/USD: 1.0950 / 1.0952 = 2 pips
GBP/USD: 1.2500 / 1.2505 = 5 pips
USD/JPY: 150.50 / 150.53 = 3 pips
```

### How Spreads Work

#### 1. **Market Maker Creates Spread**
```
Buyers want: 1.0950 (BID)
Sellers want: 1.0952 (ASK)
Market maker: Profit = 2 pips per trade
```

#### 2. **Your Cost**
When you buy and immediately sell:
```
Buy at ASK:  1.0952
Sell at BID: 1.0950
Loss:        -2 pips (spread cost)
```

You lose money just from the spread!

#### 3. **Real-World Example**
Buy 1 lot EUR/USD:
- Cost: 1.0952 × 100,000 = 109,520
- Immediately sell
- Receive: 1.0950 × 100,000 = 109,500
- **Loss: $20 (the spread cost)**

### Factors Affecting Spread Size

#### 1. **Liquidity**
- **High liquidity:** Tight spreads (0.5-1.0 pips)
- **Low liquidity:** Wide spreads (3-10+ pips)

**Example:**
- EUR/USD (most liquid): 1.0 pip spread
- Exotic pairs: 5-10 pip spread
- Illiquid times: 2-3x spreads

#### 2. **Market Conditions**
- **Calm markets:** Tight spreads
- **Volatile markets:** Wide spreads
- **News events:** Spreads widen dramatically

**Example:**
- Normal: 1.5 pip spread
- During Fed announcement: 10-50+ pip spread

#### 3. **Time of Day**
- **Peak hours (London-NY overlap):** Tightest spreads (0.5-1.5 pips)
- **Asian hours:** Wider (2-3 pips)
- **Off-hours:** Widest (5-10+ pips)

#### 4. **Broker Type**
- **Market makers:** Wider spreads (2-5 pips), no commission
- **ECN/STP:** Tight spreads (0.5-1.5 pips) + commission
- **Prop firms:** Usually tight (0.5-1.0 pips)

#### 5. **Asset Volatility**
- **Calm assets:** Tight spreads
- **High volatility:** Wide spreads
- **Crypto:** Extremely wide (0.2-2% typical!)

### Spread Cost Impact

#### Monthly Impact Example:

**Tight Spread (1.5 pips):**
- 100 trades/month
- Cost: 100 × 1.5 = 150 pips
- Must win 150+ pips just to break even

**Wide Spread (5 pips):**
- 100 trades/month
- Cost: 100 × 5 = 500 pips
- Must win 500+ pips just to break even

**Difference:** 350 pips!
At $10 per pip = $3,500 monthly difference

### Calculating Spread in Money Terms

**Formula:**
Spread Cost $ = Spread (pips) × Position Size × Pip Value

**Example EUR/USD:**
- Spread: 1.5 pips
- Position: 1 lot (100,000 units)
- Pip value: $10
- **Cost: 1.5 × 1 × $10 = $15 per trade**

**Example EUR/USD 0.5 lots:**
- Spread: 2 pips
- Position: 0.5 lots (50,000 units)
- Pip value: $5 (50% of 1 lot)
- **Cost: 2 × 0.5 × $5 = $5 per trade**

### Minimizing Spread Costs

#### 1. **Trade During Peak Liquidity**
- London open: 7-8 AM London time
- London-US overlap: 1-5 PM London
- US open: 2-4 PM London

**Savings:** 50-70% spread reduction during peak hours

#### 2. **Choose Tight Spread Brokers**
- Compare spreads across brokers
- ECN brokers often tighter
- Prop firms often tightest
- Test with small trades first

**Savings:** 50-75% depending on broker

#### 3. **Trade Liquid Pairs Only**
- EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY
- Avoid exotic pairs for active trading
- Or trade exotics only during peak hours

**Savings:** 75-90% vs exotic pairs

#### 4. **Avoid High-Risk Periods**
- Before major news (close positions)
- During high volatility
- During low liquidity hours
- Post-market hours

**Savings:** 90-95% by avoiding worst times

#### 5. **Batch Orders**
- Consolidate multiple trades into one
- Fewer transactions = lower spread cost
- Wait for fewer, better setups
- Quality over quantity

**Savings:** 50% through trade reduction

### Example: Spread Impact on Profitability

**Scenario: 100 trades/month, 55% win rate, 1:2 risk-reward**

**Tight Spread (1.0 pip):**
- Spread cost: 100 pips
- Wins (55): +2,750 pips (1:2 R:R)
- Losses (45): -2,250 pips
- Net before spread: +500 pips
- **Net after spread: +400 pips**
- Efficiency: 80%

**Wide Spread (5.0 pips):**
- Spread cost: 500 pips
- Wins (55): +2,750 pips
- Losses (45): -2,250 pips
- Net before spread: +500 pips
- **Net after spread: 0 pips (BREAK EVEN!)**
- Efficiency: 0%

Same exact strategy but with different spreads results in profit vs. break-even!
