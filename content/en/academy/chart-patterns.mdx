## Chart Patterns Guide

Chart patterns are visual formations that repeat reliably and provide high-probability trading setups when confirmed properly.

### Understanding Chart Patterns

Chart patterns work because:
- **Psychological levels:** Traders recognize and trade these patterns
- **Historical repetition:** Similar patterns produce similar results
- **Supply/demand visualization:** Show accumulation and distribution
- **Breakout opportunities:** Provide clear entry and exit points

### Major Reversal Patterns

#### 1. **Head and Shoulders**
A major reversal pattern consisting of three peaks.

**Pattern Structure:**
- **Left shoulder:** Initial high with pullback
- **Head:** New high above left shoulder, pullback lower
- **Right shoulder:** Lower high than head, pullback up
- **Neckline:** Support line connecting the two pullback lows

**Trading Setup:**
- Entry: Below neckline (close below for confirmation)
- Stop loss: Above right shoulder high
- Target: Neckline distance projected down from break
- Confirms strong downtrend

**Example:**
- EUR/USD uptrend reaches 1.1200 (left shoulder)
- Pulls back to 1.1050
- Rises to 1.1400 (head, new high)
- Pulls back to 1.1050 (neckline)
- Rises to 1.1300 (right shoulder, lower high)
- Breaks below 1.1050 = SHORT signal
- Target: 1.1050 - (1.1400-1.1050) = 1.0700

#### 2. **Inverse Head and Shoulders**
Bullish reversal pattern (opposite of head and shoulders).

**Structure:**
- **Left shoulder:** Initial low with bounce
- **Head:** New low below left shoulder
- **Right shoulder:** Higher low than head
- **Neckline:** Horizontal resistance connecting bounces

**Trading:**
- Entry: Above neckline
- Target: Neckline distance projected up
- Confirms strong uptrend coming

#### 3. **Double Top**
Bearish reversal pattern with two equal peaks.

**Pattern:**
- First peak at resistance
- Pullback 15-25%
- Second peak attempts to break above but fails
- Breaks lower than first pullback

**Trading:**
- Entry: Below lower of the two bottoms
- Stop loss: Above the peaks
- Target: Distance from peaks to support projected down

#### 4. **Double Bottom**
Bullish reversal pattern with two equal lows (opposite of double top).

**Pattern:**
- First low at support
- Bounce up 15-25%
- Second low tests but holds above first low
- Breaks higher than first bounce

**Trading:**
- Entry: Above the resistance level between two bottoms
- Target: Distance from support projected up

#### 5. **Triple Top & Triple Bottom**
Even more bearish/bullish: Three touches of a level with failure.

**Trading:**
- Entry: On break of the resistance/support
- More reliable than double (more confirmations)
- Stronger signal than double patterns

### Continuation Patterns

#### 1. **Triangle Patterns**

**Symmetrical Triangle:**
- Descending highs and ascending lows
- Converges to apex
- Can break either direction
- Entry on breakout with volume

**Ascending Triangle:**
- Higher lows, flat resistance
- Breakout typically upward ✓
- Bullish pattern

**Descending Triangle:**
- Flat support, lower highs
- Breakout typically downward ✓
- Bearish pattern

**Trading Triangles:**
- Entry: Break above resistance (ascending) or below support (descending)
- Stop loss: Below breakout point
- Target: Triangle height projected from breakout

#### 2. **Flags and Pennants**

**Flag Pattern:**
- Strong move up or down
- Consolidation against trend (slight angle)
- Quick breakout in original direction
- Usually means strong momentum continuing

**Pennant Pattern:**
- Strong move up or down
- Small triangle consolidation
- Quick breakout in original direction
- Even stronger momentum signal than flag

**Trading:**
- Entry: On breakout in direction of original trend
- Stop loss: Inside flag/pennant
- Target: Original move height, repeated from breakout

#### 3. **Wedge Patterns**

**Falling Wedge:**
- Prices compress (converging highs and lows)
- Downward bias but bullish within structure
- Often results in upward breakout

**Rising Wedge:**
- Prices compress with upward bias
- Converging highs and lows
- Often results in downward breakout (despite upward angle)

**Trading:**
- Entry: On breakout from the wedge
- Direction can break either way
- Volume confirmation essential

#### 4. **Rectangle Patterns**

**Pattern:**
- Prices bounce between two parallel lines
- Resistance and support equidistant
- No uptrend or downtrend
- Eventually breaks out

**Trading:**
- Entry: On breakout above resistance or below support
- Short sell: Short rallies to resistance
- Long buy: Buy bounces at support
- Target: Previous move height

### Validating Chart Patterns

**Volume Confirmation:**
- Entry breakout should have volume increase
- Volume should support breakout direction
- Low volume breaks are suspect

**Multiple Timeframe Confirmation:**
- Pattern on 1H should confirm with 4H trend
- Don't trade 5M patterns against daily downtrend
- Confluence improves probability

**Support/Resistance Alignment:**
- Pattern neckline doubles as support/resistance
- Nearby major support/resistance matters
- Confluence increases probability

### Common Pattern Mistakes

1. **Trading unconfirmed patterns:** Wait for breakout confirmation
2. **Ignoring volume:** Volume confirms pattern validity
3. **Wrong timeframe:** Pattern should align with trade duration
4. **No stop loss:** Always place below/above pattern extremes
5. **Trading partial patterns:** Wait for pattern completion
6. **Fighting the trend:** Trade patterns in trend direction when possible
7. **Too tight stops:** Add 5-10 pip buffer for normal volatility

### Pattern Statistics

**Most Reliable Patterns:**
1. Head and shoulders: 67-73% success
2. Inverse H&S: 67-73% success
3. Double top/bottom: 65-70% success
4. Ascending triangle: 65-70% success
5. Descending triangle: 60-65% success
6. Flags in trends: 65-75% success

**Less Reliable:**
- Wedges: 55-60% success
- Rectangles: 50-55% success
- Symmetrical triangles: 50-55% success

### Pattern Trading Strategy

**Setup Process:**
1. Identify pattern on chart (clear formation)
2. Confirm with volume analysis
3. Define breakout level precisely
4. Place stop loss logical distance beyond pattern
5. Calculate target using pattern measurements
6. Risk-reward minimum 1:2
7. Enter on confirmed breakout only
8. Manage trade with trailing stop or partial closes

**Risk Management:**
- Max 2% account risk per pattern trade
- Stop loss width = pattern volatility
- Target = 2-3X stop loss distance
- Partial profits at 1:1 and 1:2 risk-reward

## Summary

Chart Patterns:
- **Reversal patterns:** Head & shoulders, double tops/bottoms
- **Continuation patterns:** Triangles, flags, wedges
- **Volume confirmation:** Essential for validity
- **Multiple timeframes:** Strengthen odds
- **Best probability:** 1:2+ risk-reward with confluence factors

Master these patterns and you'll have reliable, high-probability setups throughout your trading career.
