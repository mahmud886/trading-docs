## The Foundation: Why Premium & Discount Matters

Every successful institutional trader understands one core principle: **buy cheap, sell expensive**. In Smart Money Concepts, this translates to buying in **Discount zones** and selling in **Premium zones**.

<Callout type="info" title="The Golden Rule">
  Bullish entries ONLY in Discount (below 50%). Bearish entries ONLY in Premium (above 50%). This single rule eliminates
  most bad trades and dramatically improves your R:R.
</Callout>

---

## Understanding the Dealing Range

A **Dealing Range** is the distance between a significant swing high and swing low. It represents the current "playing field" where price is operating.

```
Swing High ─────────────── $2,450  (Premium extreme)
│
│   PREMIUM ZONE (above 50%)
│   ───── 75% level ─────── $2,437.50
│         (Deep premium - best sells)
│
│   ───── Equilibrium 50% ── $2,425  (Fair value)
│
│   DISCOUNT ZONE (below 50%)
│   ───── 25% level ─────── $2,412.50
│         (Deep discount - best buys)
│
Swing Low ──────────────── $2,400  (Discount extreme)
```

### Calculating the Levels:

```
Range = Swing High - Swing Low
Example: $2,450 - $2,400 = $50

Equilibrium (50%) = Swing Low + (Range × 0.5) = $2,425
Premium (75%) = Swing Low + (Range × 0.75) = $2,437.50
Discount (25%) = Swing Low + (Range × 0.25) = $2,412.50
```

## The Trading Rules

### For BULLISH Setups (Buying):

| Zone                   | Action                      | Probability |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------- |
| Deep Discount (0-25%)  | **STRONG BUY** zone         | Highest     |
| Discount (25-50%)      | **BUY** zone                | High        |
| Equilibrium (50%)      | Neutral — can go either way | Medium      |
| Premium (50-75%)       | **DO NOT BUY**              | Low         |
| Deep Premium (75-100%) | **NEVER BUY** here          | Very Low    |

### For BEARISH Setups (Selling):

| Zone                   | Action               | Probability |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- | ----------- |
| Deep Premium (75-100%) | **STRONG SELL** zone | Highest     |
| Premium (50-75%)       | **SELL** zone        | High        |
| Equilibrium (50%)      | Neutral              | Medium      |
| Discount (25-50%)      | **DO NOT SELL**      | Low         |
| Deep Discount (0-25%)  | **NEVER SELL** here  | Very Low    |

## How Institutions Use Premium & Discount

Institutional traders (banks, hedge funds) follow this logic:

1. **They accumulate positions in Discount** — Buying when price is "cheap"
2. **They distribute positions in Premium** — Selling when price is "expensive"
3. **They manipulate price INTO their zones** — Engineering price to Discount to buy, or to Premium to sell
4. **They never chase** — If price is in Premium and they want to buy, they WAIT for a pullback to Discount

This is why price often:

- Sweeps highs (Premium) then drops — institutions distributed
- Sweeps lows (Discount) then rises — institutions accumulated

## Multi-Timeframe Premium & Discount

The most powerful application is using P&D across multiple timeframes:

### The Nested Approach:

```
DAILY: Identify the major dealing range
  └── Price is in Daily DISCOUNT ✓ (bullish bias)

4H: Within Daily discount, find 4H dealing range
  └── Wait for price to reach 4H DISCOUNT ✓

1H/15M: Within 4H discount, find entry
  └── Enter at OB/FVG in 1H DISCOUNT ✓
```

When ALL timeframes align in the same P&D zone, you get the **highest probability setups**.

### Example: Triple Discount Confluence

1. **Daily:** Price in discount of weekly range (below 50% of weekly swing)
2. **4H:** Price in discount of daily range (below 50% of daily swing)
3. **15M:** OB entry in discount of 4H range (below 50% of 4H swing)
4. **Result:** Buying at the cheapest possible level across all timeframes = **exceptional R:R**

## Practical Application: Step by Step

### Setup Process:

1. **Draw the HTF Dealing Range**
   - On Daily/4H, identify the most recent significant swing high and swing low
   - Draw the Fibonacci tool from swing low to swing high (or use manual lines)
   - Mark 50% (Equilibrium), 75% (Premium), 25% (Discount)

2. **Determine Your Bias**
   - If HTF structure is bullish → look for BUYS in Discount
   - If HTF structure is bearish → look for SELLS in Premium

3. **Wait for Price to Enter Your Zone**
   - Don't trade if price is at Equilibrium (50%) — wait for it to pull into your zone
   - Best entries come from the 61.8-78.6% retracement area (OTE within P&D)

4. **Find Your Entry Trigger**
   - In Discount: Look for bullish OB, bullish FVG, or bullish CHOCH
   - In Premium: Look for bearish OB, bearish FVG, or bearish CHOCH

5. **Execute with Proper Risk**
   - Stop: Beyond the dealing range extreme
   - Target: The opposite side (Premium if you bought Discount, Discount if you sold Premium)

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Premium & Discount Errors">

**❌ Buying in Premium** — If price is above 50% of the dealing range, do NOT take bullish trades. Wait for a pullback to discount.

**❌ Selling in Discount** — If price is below 50%, do NOT take bearish trades. Wait for a rally to premium.

**❌ Using the wrong dealing range** — The dealing range must be RELEVANT to your timeframe. Don't use a 5M dealing range for a 4H trade.

**❌ Ignoring structure** — P&D alone isn't enough. You still need BOS/CHOCH + OB/FVG for entry.

**❌ Trading at Equilibrium** — The 50% level is no man's land. Avoid entries here — wait for price to pull deeper.

</Callout>
