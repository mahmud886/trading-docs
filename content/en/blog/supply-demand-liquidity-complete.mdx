## What are Supply & Demand Zones?

**Supply & Demand zones** are areas where large order clusters exist.

- **Demand Zone** = Below price, many buyers waiting (big buy orders)
- **Supply Zone** = Above price, many sellers waiting (big sell orders)

### Why They Matter?

Institutional traders know where these zones are. They push price there to fill orders and profit.

## Understanding Liquidity Sweeps & Grabs

### What is a Liquidity Sweep?

A **sweep** is when price quickly collects stops to fuel momentum.

**Example: Bearish Trap (also called a Sweep**

```
Stop Loss Area (all retail traders' SL)
        ↓ ← 1.0815
        |
1.0820  ├─ ← Swing Low
        |
1.0810  ├─ ← Stop collection zone (Liquidity Pool)
        |   ↓ ↓ ↓ (everyone's SL here)
        |
1.0805  ├─ Limited support
```

**What Happens:**
1. Price starts forming (people buy)
2. Dips to collect stops
3. Then bounces hard upward

### What is a Liquidity Grab?

A **grab** is when price collects liquidity and continues in direction.

**Example:**

```
           Uptrend
1.0850
    |
1.0840  ├─ ← Institutional buyers cluster (Demand Zone)
    |       ↑ ↑ (huge buy orders)
1.0830  ├─
    |
1.0820  ├─ ← Swing High (previous)
    |     ↑
1.0815  ├─ GRAB - Takes liquidity
    |
```

Then price rallies hard and quickly upward.

<Callout type="tip" title="Why Smart Money Does This">
Institutional traders grab stops because stops = liquidity. They buy the liquidity cheap, then push price to their targets.
</Callout>

```

5. Stop Clusters: Many traders' SL at same area
```

## Supply Zones in Detail

A **Supply Zone** is historic resistance where massive selling pressure exists.

### Supply Zones Form When:
1. Price rallies fast then reverses
2. Many retail traders bought at the high (now losing)
3. They want to "break even" by selling
4. This creates sell pressure

**Chart Example:**
```
1.0900  ┐
        │ ← Bought here by many (wrong timing)
        │   Now losing money
        │   = Supply Zone (sell pressure)
1.0880  │
        │ Price came down and now rising
1.0850  ┘
```

### What Happens When Price Reaches Supply:
- All those losing traders sell
- **Sell pressure increases** → Price stalls
- **Or** price breaks through (gets squeezed)

---

## Demand Zones in Detail

A **Demand Zone** is historic support where massive buying pressure exists.

### Demand Zones Form When:
1. Price crashes fast then bounces
2. Many traders shorted at the bottom (now losing)
3. They want to "break even" by buying
4. This creates buy pressure

**Chart Example:**
```
1.0900  ┐
        │ Price falls
        │
1.0850  ├─ ← Shorted here by many
        │   Now losing money
        │   = Demand Zone (buy pressure)
1.0800  └─ Lowest point
```

### What Happens When Price Reaches Demand:
- All those losing traders buy
- **Buy pressure increases** → Price bounces
- **Or** price breaks below (gets squeezed)

<Callout type="warning" title="Critical Insight">
Supply/Demand zones = real people, real emotions, real money flows—not just chart lines.
</Callout>
