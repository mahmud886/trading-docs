## What is a Liquidity Sweep?

A liquidity sweep (stop hunt) is when price briefly moves beyond obvious support or resistance to clear stops and collect liquidity before reversing and continuing the primary move.

Institutions need liquidity to execute large orders. Sweeps create the liquidity necessary for those executions.

---

## How to Spot a Sweep

- Sudden long wick beyond a recent high/low
- Quick reversal back inside the structure
- Often occurs near session opens (London, NY)

If you see these signs, it’s often smarter to wait for the retrace and trade the reaction instead of trading into the sweep.

---

## Trading the Sweep

1. Mark structural highs/lows and liquidity clusters
2. Wait for the sweep and subsequent rejection
3. Look for confluence: OB/FVG, CHOCH, or MSS
4. Enter on the retrace with a clean invalidation

---

## Example

- Price breaks the Asian high with a long wick (sweep)
- Rapid reversal into FVG and Order Block
- Entry taken on lower TF confirmation during NY session

---

## Key Takeaways

- Liquidity sweeps are fundamental to how big players execute
- They create repeatable trade setups when combined with structure and confluence
- Trade the reaction, not the sweep itself
