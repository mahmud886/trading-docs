## What is Daily Bias?

**Daily bias** is your directional plan for the day. It answers one simple question: are you looking to buy, sell, or stay flat?

Without a bias, traders often take random trades from both sides of the market. With a bias, you know which setups are worth your attention and which ones to ignore.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bias is Not a Prediction">
Daily bias is a probability framework, not a guarantee. You are simply deciding which side has the better structure, liquidity, and timing.
</Callout>

---

## How to Build Daily Bias

### Step 1: Read the Higher Timeframe
Look at the Daily and 4H charts first.

Ask:
- Is the market making higher highs and higher lows?
- Is price breaking recent lows or highs?
- Where is the nearest liquidity pool?

### Step 2: Mark Key Levels
Mark:
- Previous day high and low
- Asia session high and low
- Weekly high and low
- Major Order Blocks or FVGs

### Step 3: Check the Session Context
The same bias behaves differently depending on the session.
- **Asian session** often builds range
- **London session** often manipulates or sweeps liquidity
- **New York session** often delivers the real move

### Step 4: Decide the Day's Direction
If structure is bullish, liquidity is below price, and the sweep happens during London, the probability may favor a bullish continuation in New York.

---

## Daily Bias Checklist

Before you trade, ask yourself:

1. What is the HTF trend?
2. Which side has resting liquidity?
3. Did the market sweep a key level?
4. Did CHOCH or MSS confirm the shift?
5. Is price in premium or discount?
6. Is the setup inside a Kill Zone?

If the answers are unclear, do not force a trade.

---

## Example: Bullish Bias on EUR/USD

- Daily chart is making higher lows
- Price sweeps the Asian low during London
- 15M prints bullish CHOCH and MSS
- Price retraces into a bullish FVG
- Entry is taken during the New York AM Kill Zone
- Target is the previous day high

This is a clean bias-to-execution workflow: **analysis first, entry second**.

## Common Daily Bias Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These Errors">

**1. Changing bias too often** — Let structure tell the story.

**2. Trading before London or New York** — The best moves often happen in the right session window.

**3. Ignoring liquidity** — Bias without liquidity context is incomplete.

**4. Buying and selling the same day without a plan** — Pick a side and wait for confirmation.

</Callout>

## Key Takeaways

- Daily bias helps you trade with direction
- Build it from higher-timeframe structure, liquidity, and session timing
- Use it to filter trades, not force trades
- The best biases often align with a sweep, CHOCH/MSS, and a clean retracement entry
