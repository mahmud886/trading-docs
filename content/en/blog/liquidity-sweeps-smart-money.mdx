## What is a Liquidity Sweep?

A **liquidity sweep** is when price moves to a level where lots of stop losses are clustered, triggers them all, and then reverses sharply in the opposite direction.

**In simple terms:** Smart money "hunts" stops to accumulate liquidity, then pushes price in their preferred direction.

### Real Example: EUR/USD

```
Scenario:
─────────────────────
Retail traders place BUY orders near 1.0800 (a previous low)
Their stops are clustered at 1.0790 (5 pips below)

Smart money sees this...
→ They push price DOWN to 1.0789
→ BOOM! All the retail stops trigger
→ Liquidity released (forced sellers)
→ Smart money buys all that liquidity
→ Price reverses UP to 1.0850

Retail traders:
❌ Got stopped out at 1.0789 (loss)
❌ Watched price go to 1.0850 (frustration)
😤 "The market tricked me!"

Smart money:
✅ Bought the stops
✅ Got liquidity at best price
✅ Profit as price reverses
😎 "Works as intended"
```

This happens **hundreds of times per day** in forex and crypto.

## Where Do Retail Stops Cluster?

### 1. At Recent Swing Lows (Most Common)

```
Price makes a low at 1.0800
Retail: "I'll put my stop 5 pips below this"
Smart Money: "Perfect. Let's go get those stops"
Price dips to 1.0795 → SWEEP
```

### 2. At Round Numbers

```
EUR/USD at 1.0900 (round number)
Retail places stops at 1.0890 (10 pips below round)
Smart money sweeps down to 1.0888
```

### 3. At Moving Averages

```
200-day MA at 1.0850
Retail: "Support!"
Retail stops clustered 5 pips below
Smart money sweeps, shakes out weak hands
```

### 4. At Previous Highs/Lows

```
Previous resistance at 1.0950
New buys enter with stops at 1.0920 (30 pip stop)
Smart money sweeps down to 1.0918 → triggers stops
Then price melts up to 1.1000
```

## How to Spot a Sweep Coming

### Signal #1: Failed Breakout

```
Price breaks above resistance level
Retail FOMO buys
Then price reverses sharply downward
→ This is usually a SWEEP

Better Entry: Wait for the SECOND breakout
```

### Signal #2: Long Wick on Rejection Candle

```
Price shoots down to sweep stops
Creates a massive lower wick
Then closes strongly in opposite direction
→ This is the sweep happening in real-time
```

### Signal #3: Volume Spike on Reversal

```
Normal volume → suddenly MASSIVE volume spike
Price reverses hard
→ Liquidity just got taken
→ Now smart money is pushing the real direction
```

## The Sweep + Follow Pattern (Easiest Trade Ever)

Once you spot a sweep, the **follow** is almost mechanical:

**Setup:**
1. Price breaks level (you expect this to hold)
2. Sweeps PAST the level (takes out the stops)
3. Creates a wick or reversal candle
4. Then reverses sharply

**Entry After Sweep:**
```
Step 1: Price sweeps down, creates lower wick
Step 2: Price closes back above sweep level
Step 3: YOU ENTER with confluences (now price is going up with momentum)

Entry: Above the sweep low + reversal candle close
Stop: Below the sweep low
Target: Next resistance level or previous high

R:R: Usually 1:2 or better (big reward for small risk)
```

## The Trap Patterns to Avoid

### Trap #1: Double Top Sweep

```
Price makes high → dips → makes high again
Retail: "Higher high, bull market resumes!"
Retail goes ALL IN with tight 10-pip stop

Price sweeps down through lows
Retail stops trigger
Then price reverses
```

**Avoid:** Don't assume double tops always break higher. Sometimes the second top is a TRAP.

### Trap #2: Breakout Fake-Out

```
Price breaks level on high volume
You enter (standard breakout entry)
Price reverses HARD, sweeping your stops

This is especially common at major resistance levels where smart money KNOWS retail will buy.
```

**Avoid:** Use the 2-bar rule: Wait for second bar to confirm breakout isn't fake.

### Trap #3: Gap Sweep

```
Market gaps down (opens with a big negative gap)
Retail panic sells
Smart money buys the panic

Price then closes the gap and goes higher
Panic sellers: "Worst timing ever"
```

**Avoid:** Don't panic sell gaps. Smart money expects this.

## How to Trade WITH Smart Money (Not Against)

### The Playbook:

1. **Identify where retail stops are** (round numbers, obvious levels, previous wicks)
2. **Anticipate a sweep** (watch for weak breakouts, rejected moves)
3. **Wait for the sweep to happen** (price goes beyond the level)
4. **See the reversal** (V-shaped or hammer candle)
5. **Enter after the sweep clears** (go with the direction smart money is now pushing)

**Key:** You're not trying to catch the sweep. You're entering AFTER the sweep, when you have confirmation that smart money is pushing in one direction.
