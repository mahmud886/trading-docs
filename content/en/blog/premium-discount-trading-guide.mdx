## What is Premium and Discount?

Premium and discount are context tools that tell you whether price is above (premium) or below (discount) a fair reference point — often the equilibrium (50% EQ) or a moving average.

- **Discount** = favorable buy area (value below fair price)
- **Premium** = favorable sell area (value above fair price)

<Callout type="tip" title="Why it helps">
Trading into discount/premium improves probability because you align entries with where institutional orders prefer to execute.
</Callout>

---

## How to Use P&D with Order Blocks and FVGs

1. Identify HTF bias
2. Mark key POIs: OBs, FVGs, session highs/lows
3. Ask whether the POI is in premium or discount relative to EQ or structure
4. Prefer entries that align: buy in discount, sell in premium

---

## Example

- HTF bias: bullish
- OB lies below current price (discount) — higher probability for buys on retrace
- If OB is in premium, skip or treat as contrarian with smaller size

---

## Pitfalls

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These Mistakes">

**1. Using P&D in isolation** — Must combine with structure and liquidity

**2. Rigidly using 50% EQ** — Use it as a guide, not a strict rule

</Callout>

## Key Takeaways

- Premium/Discount gives context to POIs
- Use it with OBs, FVGs, and structure for higher probability trades
- Prefer buying in discount and selling in premium within the HTF bias
