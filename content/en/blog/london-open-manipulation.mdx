## Why the London Open Matters — Institutional Perspective

The London session handles **35-40% of total daily Forex volume** — more than any other session. This is when European banks, hedge funds, and institutional traders take their primary daily positions.

But this institutional entry follows a pattern: **manipulation first, then the real move.**

<Callout type="tip" title="The Judas Swing">
A Judas Swing is a short, sharp move designed to lure retail traders into the wrong side before the true directional move follows. It occurs around the London open as institutional liquidity collection.
</Callout>

### Why Manipulation Happens — Market Microstructure

```
Institutional Problem:
- European Bank wants to buy $300M EUR/USD
- Asian Session has low liquidity (few sellers)
- Direct buying = Price spike → Bad average fill

Solution (Judas Swing):
1. Push price DOWN (sweep Asian Low)
2. Retail panic sells (Stop losses hit)
3. These sell orders = Institutional buy opportunity
4. Enough liquidity collected → Push price UP
5. Real direction = UP (but went DOWN first)

Result: London opens → Fake move down → Sharp reversal → Real move up
```

### Statistical Evidence

| Statistic                          | Value               |
|------------------------------------|---------------------|

## Asian Session: The Setup Phase

The Asian Session builds the "trap box" for London:

```
Asian Session Characteristics:
- Low Volatility (Tight Range)
- Retail traders place breakout orders
- Stop losses cluster above/below range
- Liquidity BUILDS on both sides
- Smart Money observes (doesn't act)

Asian Range = Trap Box:
- Above High: Buy stops + Breakout buy orders
- Below Low: Sell stops + Breakout sell orders
- London will TARGET one (or both) sides
```

### Asian Range Protocol

```
1. Mark Asian High and Low (00:00-06:00 GMT)
2. Note range size (< 30 pips = tight = strong breakout expected)
3. Identify nearby liquidity (EQLs, PDH/PDL)
4. Set alerts at Asian High/Low
```

## London Manipulation Patterns

### Pattern 1: Single-Side Sweep (~50% of days)

```
06:30-07:30: Judas Swing (sweeps one side of Asian range)
07:30-08:30: Reversal (real direction begins)
08:30-12:00: Expansion (full move develops)
```

### Pattern 2: Double-Side Sweep (~20% of days)

```
06:30: Sweeps Asian HIGH (buy stops taken)
07:00: Reverses, sweeps Asian LOW (sell stops taken)
07:30: FINAL direction begins

Rule: After double sweep, real move = direction of LAST recovery
Double sweep = Maximum fuel = Largest moves
```

### Pattern 3: No Manipulation (~30% of days)

```
When: Very strong HTF trend, post-FOMC, Friday
How to identify: Body close beyond Asian range (not just wick)
Action: If no Judas Swing by 08:00 GMT, look for BOS trade instead
```

## Trading Strategy: Post-Manipulation Entry

### Step-by-Step Model

```
Step 1: WAIT (06:00-07:30 GMT) — Do NOT trade the first move
Step 2: IDENTIFY SWEEP — Price goes beyond Asian H/L then rejects
Step 3: WAIT FOR DISPLACEMENT — Strong candle AWAY from sweep + FVG
Step 4: ENTER AT FVG — Retrace to FVG after reversal
Step 5: MANAGE — TP1 at 1:2, trail rest to PDH/PDL
```

### Entry Details

```
Entry: FVG (or CE of FVG) after reversal displacement
Stop: Below/Above Judas Swing extreme
TP1: Opposite side of Asian Range
TP2: PDH/PDL (Previous Day High/Low)
TP3: Next major liquidity pool
```

```
Asian = ACCUMULATION (Range, liquidity builds)
London = MANIPULATION (Judas Swing, false move)
London-NY = DISTRIBUTION (Real move, trend extends)

The complete daily framework:
Asia → London Fake → London-NY Real
```

## When NOT to Trade London Manipulation

<Callout type="warning" title="Skip These Conditions">

**1. Monday morning** — Low reliability, institutions positioning
**2. Friday afternoon** — Low participation
**3. Major news within 30 minutes** — NFP/FOMC override patterns
**4. Wide Asian Range (>60 pips)** — Manipulation may have occurred in Asia
**5. No clear HTF bias** — Direction unpredictable
**6. After 3 consecutive losses** — Not your day

</Callout>
