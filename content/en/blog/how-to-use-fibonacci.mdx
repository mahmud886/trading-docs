## Why Fibonacci?

Fibonacci retracements provide reference levels where market participants often place orders. When used with structure and confluence, they become valuable decision points.

<Callout type="tip" title="Rule of thumb">
  Use Fibonacci levels only as a guide — they are strongest when combined with OB, FVG, or session highs/lows.
</Callout>

---

## Common levels and how to use them

- 38.2% / 50% / 61.8% retracements for pullback entries
- Extensions (127.2%, 161.8%) as profit targets

Combine with:

- HTF trend
- Order Blocks/FVG near the level
- Session context (Kill Zones)

---

## Example

- 4H uptrend, price retraces to 61.8% near a bullish OB and an FVG — high probability buy zone
- Place stop below invalidation, target first extension, scale out

---

## Key takeaways

- Fibonacci is a tool, not a rule
- Use for confluence and to size risk/reward
