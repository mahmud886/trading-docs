## Beyond Basic Risk Management

Most traders know "risk 1-2% per trade." But professional risk management goes far deeper. This guide covers the advanced concepts that separate funded traders from account-blowers.

<Callout type="info" title="The Professional Standard">
Prop firms don't just look at per-trade risk. They monitor daily drawdown, weekly drawdown, maximum drawdown, correlation exposure, and win-rate adjusted expectancy. Master these and you'll pass any prop firm challenge.
</Callout>

## The Complete Risk Framework

### Level 1: Per-Trade Risk

| Account Size | Recommended Risk | Max Risk (Aggressive) |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| $1,000 - $5,000 | 1% | 1.5% |
| $5,000 - $25,000 | 0.5% - 1% | 1.5% |
| $25,000 - $100,000 | 0.5% | 1% |
| $100,000+ | 0.25% - 0.5% | 0.75% |

**Why smaller accounts can risk slightly more:** You need growth. But NEVER exceed 2%.

### Level 2: Daily Risk Cap

Your total daily loss limit should be **3-5% maximum**:

```
Daily Risk Cap = Max 3% of account

If account = $10,000:
- Daily max loss = $300
- At 1% per trade = maximum 3 losing trades before stopping

If you lose 3 trades in a row → STOP TRADING FOR THE DAY
No exceptions. No "one more trade." Done.
```

### Level 3: Weekly Risk Cap

Maximum weekly drawdown: **5-8%**

```
Weekly Risk Cap = Max 5% of account

If account = $10,000:
- Weekly max loss = $500
- If hit by Wednesday → No more trading until Monday
- Use remaining days for study and review
```

### Level 4: Maximum Drawdown

Total drawdown before reassessment: **10-15%**

```
If you hit 10% drawdown from peak equity:
1. Stop trading IMMEDIATELY
2. Review all trades from the drawdown period
3. Identify the root cause (strategy? psychology? execution?)
4. Paper trade / demo for minimum 1 week
5. Return with HALF position size until new equity high
```

## Advanced Position Sizing Formulas

### Formula 1: Fixed Percentage (Standard)

```
Position Size (lots) = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop Loss pips × Pip Value)

Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk, 25-pip stop, EUR/USD
= ($10,000 × 0.01) ÷ (25 × $10)
= $100 ÷ $250
= 0.40 lots
```

### Formula 2: ATR-Based Position Sizing

Uses Average True Range to adjust for volatility:

```
Stop Loss = 1.5 × ATR(14)
Position Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (ATR-Stop × Pip Value)

Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk, ATR(14) = 80 pips
Stop = 1.5 × 80 = 120 pips
Position = ($10,000 × 0.01) ÷ (120 × $10)
= $100 ÷ $1,200
= 0.083 lots (use 0.08)
```

**Why ATR-based is better:** Automatically reduces size in volatile markets and increases in calm markets.

### Formula 3: Kelly Criterion (Advanced)

Mathematically optimal bet size based on your actual statistics:

```
Kelly % = W - [(1-W) / R]

Where:
W = Win rate (decimal)
R = Average win / Average loss (R:R)

Example: 55% win rate, 1:2.5 average R:R
Kelly = 0.55 - [(1-0.55) / 2.5]
Kelly = 0.55 - 0.18
Kelly = 0.37 (37%)

NEVER use full Kelly! Use Quarter Kelly for safety:
Recommended: 37% ÷ 4 = 9.25% → Use 5-8%
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Kelly Criterion Warning">
Full Kelly is extremely aggressive and assumes perfect execution with zero psychological factors. ALWAYS use Quarter Kelly (÷4) or less. Most professionals use 1-2% which is well below even Quarter Kelly.
</Callout>

## Portfolio Heat: Managing Multiple Positions

**Portfolio Heat** = Total risk across all open positions

### Rules:

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Max portfolio heat** | 5% (never have more than 5% total at risk) |
| **Max correlated positions** | 2 (e.g., max 2 USD pairs in same direction) |
| **Max positions** | 3-4 total open |
| **Same direction max** | 3% exposure in one direction |

### Example:

```
Account: $50,000 | Max Heat: 5% = $2,500 total risk

Position 1: EUR/USD long — risking 1% ($500) ✓
Position 2: GBP/USD long — risking 1% ($500) ⚠️ (correlated with #1)
Position 3: Gold long — risking 1% ($500) ✓
Position 4: USD/JPY short — risking 0.5% ($250) ✓

Total heat: 3.5% ✓ (under 5%)
But: EUR/USD + GBP/USD = both USD shorts = correlated ⚠️
Action: Reduce one to 0.5% or skip one entirely
```

## Correlation Risk

Correlated trades multiply your risk unknowingly:

### High Correlation Pairs (move together):

| Pair 1 | Pair 2 | Correlation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| EUR/USD | GBP/USD | +0.85 |
| EUR/USD | AUD/USD | +0.70 |
| USD/CHF | USD/JPY | +0.65 |
| Gold | Silver | +0.90 |
| NAS100 | S&P500 | +0.92 |

### Negative Correlation (move opposite):

| Pair 1 | Pair 2 | Correlation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| EUR/USD | USD/CHF | -0.90 |
| EUR/USD | DXY | -0.95 |
| Gold | DXY | -0.80 |

**Rule:** If trading two positively correlated instruments in the same direction, count it as ONE position with DOUBLE the risk.

## The Expectancy Formula

Know your mathematical edge:

```
Expectancy = (Win Rate × Average Win) - (Loss Rate × Average Loss)

Example:
Win rate: 55%, Average win: $150 (1.5R), Average loss: $100 (1R)
Expectancy = (0.55 × $150) - (0.45 × $100)
Expectancy = $82.50 - $45.00
Expectancy = +$37.50 per trade

Over 100 trades: 100 × $37.50 = +$3,750
```

**If your expectancy is negative, NO position sizing will save you.** Fix your strategy first.

### Minimum Sample Size:

- Need **minimum 30 trades** for any statistical significance
- Ideally **100+ trades** before trusting your numbers
- Re-calculate every month as market conditions change

## Scaling Strategies

### Scaling In (Adding to Winners):

```
Initial Entry: 0.5% risk at OB
Price confirms direction (BOS on LTF):
Add: 0.3% risk at new OB (stop moved to breakeven on first)
Price continues:
Add: 0.2% risk at next OB (stops trailed)

Total max risk at any time: Still 1% (because stops trail)
But position size has GROWN with the trend
```

### Scaling Out (Partial Takes):

```
Entry at OB: Full position (1% risk)
Hit 1R: Take 33% profit, move stop to breakeven
Hit 2R: Take 33% profit, trail stop to 1R
Let final 33% run: Target 3-5R with trailing stop

Result: Guaranteed breakeven minimum + potential for large R on runner
```

## Prop Firm Risk Rules (Reference)

| Rule | Typical Prop Firm | Your Personal Rule |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Daily drawdown | 5% max | 3% max (stricter) |
| Total drawdown | 10% max | 8% max (stricter) |
| Per-trade risk | Not specified | 0.5-1% |
| Position limit | 10-20% margin used | Max 3-4 positions |
| News trading | Often restricted | Skip high-impact news |

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Always trade stricter than the prop firm rules. If they allow 5% daily, your limit is 3%. If they allow 10% total, yours is 8%. This buffer protects you from being cut on a bad day.
</Callout>

## The Mathematical Proof: Why 1% Works

Starting with $10,000, risking 1% per trade at 1:3 R:R with 50% win rate:

```
100 trades:
- 50 wins × $300 (3R) = +$15,000
- 50 losses × $100 (1R) = -$5,000
- Net profit: +$10,000 (100% return!)

Maximum drawdown (10 consecutive losses):
- 10 × 1% = ~9.6% drawdown (compounded)
- Easily recoverable

Same scenario at 5% risk:
- 10 consecutive losses = ~40% drawdown
- Need +67% just to recover
- Emotional spiral almost guaranteed
```
