## Why Scale In and Out?

Scaling lets you participate in a move while managing risk and improving the average entry price. Instead of one large position, break entries into smaller pieces to adapt to market uncertainty.

<Callout type="tip" title="Rule of thumb">
Use scaling when conviction is moderate — full size only when the market confirms your idea.
</Callout>

---

## Simple scaling plan

1. Define full position size before entry
2. Use two or three staggered entry points (e.g., 50% / 25% / 25%)
3. Move stops to breakeven after the first partial fills and confirmation
4. Scale out at logical liquidity pools or extensions

Scaling reduces emotional pressure and helps you stay in high-probability moves longer.

---

## Examples & When Not to Scale

- In high-confidence HTF setups, a full single entry may be preferable
- In uncertain session conditions or near news, prefer smaller scaling steps

---

## Key Takeaways

- Plan size and price levels before trading
- Use scaling to manage uncertainty and improve R:R
