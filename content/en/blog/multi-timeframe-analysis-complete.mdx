## Why Multi-Timeframe Analysis is Non-Negotiable

The #1 mistake retail traders make is trading on a single timeframe. They see a setup on 15M and take it — without checking if the 4H or Daily agrees. This is like driving while only looking through a telescope: you see detail but miss the big picture.

**Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTF)** means analyzing price across multiple timeframes to ensure your trade aligns with the larger market structure.

<Callout type="info" title="The Professional Edge">
  Institutional traders ALWAYS start with higher timeframes and work down. They determine direction on Weekly/Daily,
  find setups on 4H/1H, and execute on 15M/5M. This top-down approach is what gives them their edge.
</Callout>

---

## The Three-Timeframe Framework

Every trade should use exactly **3 timeframes**:

| Role             | Timeframes     | Purpose           |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ----------------- |
| **HTF (Higher)** | Daily / Weekly | Direction & Bias  |
| **MTF (Middle)** | 4H / 1H        | Setup & Structure |
| **LTF (Lower)**  | 15M / 5M       | Entry & Execution |

### Timeframe Combinations:

| Trading Style           | HTF    | MTF   | LTF    |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- | ------ |
| **Swing Trading**       | Weekly | Daily | 4H     |
| **Intraday (Standard)** | Daily  | 4H    | 15M    |
| **Intraday (Scalp)**    | 4H     | 1H    | 5M     |
| **ICT Model**           | Daily  | 4H/1H | 15M/5M |

## Step 1: HTF — Determine Direction

The Higher Timeframe tells you ONE thing: **which direction to trade**.

### What to Analyze on HTF:

1. **Market Structure** — Is it making HH/HL (bullish) or LH/LL (bearish)?
2. **Key Levels** — Where are the major swing highs/lows?
3. **Liquidity Pools** — Where are equal highs/lows and obvious stops?
4. **Premium/Discount** — Is price in a zone where your bias makes sense?

### HTF Decision Framework:

```
Daily Structure = Bullish (HH/HL)
  → Only look for BUYS
  → Even if LTF shows bearish setup, IGNORE IT

Daily Structure = Bearish (LH/LL)
  → Only look for SELLS
  → Even if LTF shows bullish setup, IGNORE IT

Daily Structure = Ranging
  → Trade the range extremes
  → Buy at range bottom, sell at range top
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Critical Rule">
  NEVER trade against the HTF direction. If Daily is bullish, you are ONLY a buyer, regardless of what lower timeframes
  show. This single rule eliminates 50%+ of losing trades.
</Callout>

---

## Step 2: MTF — Find the Setup

The Middle Timeframe is where you find your **specific trading setup**.

### What to Analyze on MTF:

1. **BOS / CHOCH** — Structural confirmations
2. **Order Blocks** — Mark the OBs that caused the structure breaks
3. **Fair Value Gaps** — Note any unfilled FVGs
4. **Premium/Discount** — Is the setup in the correct zone?
5. **Liquidity** — What liquidity was or will be taken?

### The MTF Setup Checklist:

```
□ HTF says BULLISH
□ MTF shows BOS (trend continuation confirmed)
□ OB identified from the BOS move
□ OB is in Discount (below 50% of MTF dealing range)
□ Unmitigated FVG overlaps with OB (bonus confluence)
□ Liquidity below the OB (stops to be swept before reaction)
```

---

## Step 3: LTF — Execute the Trade

The Lower Timeframe is ONLY for **timing your entry precisely**.

### What to Do on LTF:

1. **Wait for price to reach your MTF POI** (OB, FVG, or Breaker)
2. **Watch for LTF confirmation:**
   - Bullish CHOCH (for buy setups)
   - Bearish CHOCH (for sell setups)
   - Displacement candle showing aggression
3. **Enter at the LTF OB** created during the CHOCH
4. **Set stop loss** below the LTF swing low (for buys) or above swing high (for sells)
5. **Set target** at the MTF/HTF liquidity target

### Why LTF Entry Matters:

Without LTF refinement:

- Entry at MTF OB: Stop = 40 pips, Target = 120 pips → **3R**

With LTF refinement:

- Entry at LTF OB within MTF OB: Stop = 12 pips, Target = 120 pips → **10R**

Same trade, same target, but **3x better R:R** through LTF refinement.

## Complete MTF Trade Example

### EUR/USD Buy Setup

**Step 1 — Daily (HTF): Direction**

- Daily structure: HH/HL — Bullish ✓
- Price is in Daily Discount (pulled back to 50% fib of last Daily leg) ✓
- Liquidity target: Equal highs at 1.0950 (BSL)

**Step 2 — 4H (MTF): Setup**

- 4H BOS confirmed (broke above previous 4H swing high) ✓
- Bullish OB at 1.0830-1.0845 (last red candle before BOS) ✓
- OB is in 4H Discount (below 50% of 4H dealing range) ✓
- FVG at 1.0835-1.0848 overlaps with OB ✓
- SSL below at 1.0820 (previous swing low)

**Step 3 — 15M (LTF): Entry**

- Price drops to 4H OB zone (reaches 1.0838)
- SSL at 1.0820 gets swept (liquidity taken ✓)
- 15M CHOCH confirmed — first higher high after the sweep
- 15M Bullish OB at 1.0825-1.0832 (created during CHOCH)
- **Entry:** 1.0828
- **Stop:** 1.0815 (13 pips — below the 15M swing low)
- **Target:** 1.0950 (122 pips — Daily BSL target)
- **R:R = 1:9.4** 🔥

**Result:** Price rallied to 1.0950 over 2 days → **+122 pips, 9.4R** ✅

## Common MTF Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These">

**❌ Trading LTF setups against HTF** — A 5M bullish CHOCH means nothing if Daily is bearish. Always respect HTF.

**❌ Using too many timeframes** — Stick to exactly 3. More = analysis paralysis.

**❌ Entering on MTF without LTF refinement** — You're leaving R:R on the table. Always refine.

**❌ Switching timeframes mid-trade** — Once entered, manage on your entry TF. Don't zoom out and panic.

**❌ Looking for perfection** — Not all 3 TFs will perfectly align every day. If HTF + MTF agree and LTF gives a clean entry, that's enough.

</Callout>
