## Why Overtrading Kills Performance

Overtrading increases transaction costs, reduces discipline, and destroys edge. The best traders are selective and patient.

---

## Practical Rules to Avoid Overtrading

1. Set a maximum number of trades per Kill Zone
2. Use a checklist before every entry
3. Use position sizing to limit exposure per day
4. Take breaks after consecutive losses

<Callout type="tip" title="Checklist example">
- HTF bias?
- Kill Zone?
- Confluence (OB/FVG)?
- Stop and target defined?
- Positive expectancy setup?
</Callout>
