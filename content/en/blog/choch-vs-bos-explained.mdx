## CHOCH vs BOS: what's the difference?

CHOCH (Change of Character) and BOS (Break of Structure) are both structure signals, but they serve different purposes:

- CHOCH: early warning — price behavior changes and momentum shifts
- BOS: confirmation — a structural break that validates continuation

Use CHOCH as a watch signal and BOS as confirmation for more reliable trades.

<Callout type="tip" title="One-line rule">
If you see CHOCH, pay attention; if you see BOS, consider execution if other confluence exists.
</Callout>

---

## How to use them together

1. Identify HTF trend
2. On lower TF, watch for CHOCH as the first sign of weakening momentum
3. Wait for BOS to confirm the new direction (or lack thereof)
4. Use FVG/OBs for entries with stop beyond invalidation

---

## Example sequence

- HTF: bullish
- Lower TF prints CHOCH — momentum slowing
- Later, BOS occurs and price breaks structure — confirmation
- Enter on retrace into OB/FVG with HTF alignment

---

## Common pitfalls

- Treating CHOCH as BOS — leads to premature entries
- Ignoring higher timeframe context
