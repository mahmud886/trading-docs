## Why Market Structure Matters

If you cannot read market structure, you are trading blind.

**Market structure** shows whether price is making higher highs and higher lows, or lower highs and lower lows. Once you understand the structure, concepts like CHOCH, BOS, and MSS become much easier to use in real trading.

<Callout type="tip" title="Structure First, Entry Second">
A strong trade setup starts with structure. First identify direction, then wait for the shift, and only then look for an entry.
</Callout>

---

## BOS vs CHOCH vs MSS

These three terms are related, but they are not identical.

| Term | Meaning | Trader Use |
|------|---------|------------|
| **BOS** | Break of Structure | Confirms continuation in the current trend |
| **CHOCH** | Change of Character | Early sign that momentum may be shifting |
| **MSS** | Market Structure Shift | A cleaner confirmation that structure has changed |

### Simple Interpretation
- **BOS** says the current trend is still alive
- **CHOCH** says momentum may be weakening
- **MSS** says the market has likely shifted direction

---

## How to Trade a Structure Shift

1. Identify the higher-timeframe trend
2. Mark the key swing highs and lows
3. Wait for liquidity to be taken
4. Watch for CHOCH on a lower timeframe
5. Confirm with displacement and an MSS
6. Enter on retracement into an FVG or Order Block
7. Place your stop beyond the invalidation level

The best structure-shift setups usually happen after a **liquidity sweep**. That is when the market grabs stops first and then reverses.

---

## Example: Bearish Shift on EUR/USD

- Daily trend is bullish, but price is approaching resistance
- London takes the previous high
- 15M prints a bearish CHOCH
- A cleaner MSS follows with strong displacement
- Price retraces into a bearish FVG
- Entry is taken with stop above the sweep high
- Target is the opposing liquidity pool

This kind of sequence keeps you aligned with how the market actually moves instead of chasing candles.

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These Errors">

**1. Calling every pullback a CHOCH** — A real CHOCH needs structure confirmation.

**2. Trading BOS as a reversal** — BOS usually confirms continuation, not a full shift.

**3. Ignoring displacement** — Without momentum, the shift is weak.

**4. Entering before the sweep** — Let liquidity get taken first when possible.

</Callout>

---

## Key Takeaways

- BOS confirms continuation
- CHOCH warns of a possible shift
- MSS confirms the shift more clearly
- Structure shift setups are strongest after liquidity sweeps and displacement
- Use them with FVGs, Order Blocks, and premium/discount context
