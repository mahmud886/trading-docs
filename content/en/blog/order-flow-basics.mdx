## What is Order Flow?

Order flow refers to the actual buying and selling pressure in the market. Reading order flow helps you understand which side (buyers or sellers) is aggressive and likely to move price.

---

## Simple signals to watch

- Aggressive market orders showing as long candles
- Volume spikes near levels of interest
- Imbalances where one side dominates

Combine order flow cues with structure and POIs for better entries.

---

## Key Takeaways

Order flow is a practical complement to SMC and ICT — it tells you who is in control in the short term.
