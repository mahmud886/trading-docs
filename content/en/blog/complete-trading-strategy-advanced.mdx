## Risk Scenarios & Management

### Scenario 1: Price Misses FVG

```
If: Price 1.0895 not touched, goes straight up

Action:
• Skip entry (it's OK)
• Wait for next opportunity
• Never chase missed breakouts
```

### Scenario 2: Breaks Below Stop Area

```
If: Price goes below 1.0875

Meaning: Our idea was wrong. Downtrend is stronger.

Action:
• Exit at stop (full 2% loss acceptable)
• Analyze setup failure
• Wait for structure change
```

### Scenario 3: Slow Movement / Chop

```
If: Price stuck 1.0895-1.0910, no upward push

Meaning: Equilibrium—signal weak probably.

Action:
• Don't scale in (inefficient)
• Change stop to breakeven
• Exit if 2 hours with no movement
```
