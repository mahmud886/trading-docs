## Why Overlaps Matter

Session overlaps concentrate liquidity and participation — London-New York overlap is often the most tradable window for forex and major commodities.

---

## How to Trade Overlaps

1. Mark session highs/lows and Asian range
2. Watch for liquidity grabs at overlap start
3. Prefer HTF-aligned setups during the overlap
4. Use smaller size near news and scale into confirmed directions

---

## Example

- Mark Asian range
- During London-NY overlap, look for retraces into FVG/OB aligned with HTF
- Enter on lower TF confirmation

---

## Key Takeaways

- Overlaps amplify valid HTF setups
- Be prepared; these windows are short but high-probability
