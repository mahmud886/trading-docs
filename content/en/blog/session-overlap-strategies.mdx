## Why Session Overlaps Matter

Session overlaps concentrate liquidity and institutional participation into tight windows. The **London-New York overlap** is the most tradable period in all of forex — where both European and American institutions are simultaneously active.

### Overlap Statistics

| Metric                           | Value                       |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| London-NY Overlap Duration       | 3-4 hours (08:00-12:00 EST) |
| % of Daily Volume During Overlap | 40-50%                      |
| Average Pip Range (Majors)       | 60-120 pips                 |
| Best Pairs                       | EUR/USD, GBP/USD, XAU/USD   |
| Setup Quality                    | Highest of the day          |
| Institutional Activity           | Maximum                     |

<Callout type="tip" title="The Professional Window">
If you could only trade 3 hours per day, choose the London-NY overlap. It has the highest volume, strongest displacement, and most reliable setups.
</Callout>

## Understanding the Three Overlaps

### 1. Tokyo-London Overlap (Moderate)

```
Time: 03:00-04:00 EST (08:00-09:00 GMT)
Duration: ~1 hour
Volume: Moderate (Tokyo winding down, London starting)
Best Pairs: EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY, EUR/GBP

What Happens:
- London sweeps Asian Range liquidity
- Judas Swing occurs here
- Direction for London session established
- Good for those who trade early London

Strategy: Mark Asian H/L → Trade the sweep → FVG entry
```

### 2. London-New York Overlap (THE BEST)

```
Time: 08:00-12:00 EST (13:00-17:00 GMT)
Duration: ~4 hours
Volume: MAXIMUM (both sessions fully active)
Best Pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/CAD, XAU/USD, NAS100

What Happens:
- US Economic Data Released (08:30 EST)
- London positions get confirmed or reversed
- Maximum displacement and FVG creation
- Largest daily moves often happen here
- Institutional rebalancing at peak liquidity

Strategy: HTF bias + London direction + NY catalyst = Trade
```

### 3. New York-Tokyo Overlap (Weak)

```
Time: 19:00-00:00 EST
Duration: ~5 hours
Volume: LOW (NY winding down, Tokyo just starting)
Best Pairs: AUD/JPY, NZD/JPY (limited)

What Happens:
- Very low volatility
- Price consolidates/ranges
- NOT recommended for active trading
- Use for analysis and planning

Strategy: DO NOT trade this overlap. Prepare for London instead.
```

## London-NY Overlap: Complete Trading Framework

### Pre-Overlap Preparation (07:00-08:00 EST)

```
1. Check Daily/4H Bias (Already established from London)
2. Note London Session Direction (What did London establish?)
3. Mark London Session High/Low
4. Mark Key Levels for NY (PDH/PDL, OBs, FVGs near price)
5. Check US Economic Calendar (08:30 releases?)
6. Decide: Trade direction aligned with London? Or reversal?
```

### Overlap Phase 1: Data Release (08:30-09:00 EST)

```
If High-Impact Data (NFP, CPI, GDP):
- DO NOT trade 5 minutes before
- Watch initial reaction
- Wait 5-15 minutes for dust to settle
- Then apply standard entry model

If No Major Data:
- NY session begins fresh
- Look for London High/Low sweep
- Standard Judas Swing pattern possible
```

### Overlap Phase 2: Peak Activity (09:00-10:30 EST)

```
This is THE window for execution:
- Maximum institutional activity
- Strongest displacement
- Highest probability FVG/OB entries
- Most reliable CHOCH/BOS signals

Entry Model:
1. London established direction? (Y/N)
2. NY confirms same direction? (BOS) → Continuation trade
3. NY reverses London? (CHOCH + Sweep) → Reversal trade
4. Enter at FVG/OB with LTF confirmation
5. Target: Next major liquidity pool
```

### Overlap Phase 3: Wind Down (10:30-12:00 EST)

```
Activity decreasing:
- Take profits or tighten stops
- New entries less reliable
- Don't start new trades after 11:00 EST
- Focus on managing existing positions
- Prepare journal notes
```

## Overlap Entry Models

### Model 1: London Continuation in NY

```
London Direction: Bullish (Made Higher Highs)
NY Open: Price pulls back (Normal retracement)
Overlap Signal: Pullback finds support at OB/FVG in discount
Enter: Long at OB/FVG with 5M CHOCH confirmation
Target: New daily high (above London high)

This is THE most common overlap pattern:
- London sets direction
- NY briefly dips (sweeps London move's local low)
- Then CONTINUES London's direction
- Win Rate: ~62-68% (highest probability)
```

### Model 2: London Reversal at NY

```
London Direction: Bullish (Strong rally)
NY Open: Sweeps London High (BSL taken!)
Reversal: Price drops sharply with displacement
Enter: Short at FVG/OB created by reversal
Target: London session low or below

When This Happens:
- London overextended (into Premium)
- US Data surprises opposite direction
- Major institutional repositioning
- Win Rate: ~55-60% (less common but high R:R)
```

### Model 3: Data-Driven Sweep

```
Before Data: Price consolidating (building energy)
Data Release: Spike in one direction (takes liquidity)
5-15 Minutes: Reversal begins
Enter: After reversal confirmed (FVG entry)
Target: Pre-data range opposite side + beyond

Key Rule: Initial data spike direction is often WRONG direction
Wait for the TRUE move after the volatility settles
```

## Real-World Overlap Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD London Continuation

```
London Session (02:00-08:00 EST):
- Asian Low Swept → Bullish CHOCH → BOS Up
- London direction: BULLISH
- London High: 1.0890

Overlap (08:30 EST):
- US data slightly weaker → EUR initially spikes to 1.0895
- Then normal pullback to 1.0870 (15M OB in discount)
- 5M Bullish CHOCH at 1.0873
- Entry: 1.0875, Stop: 1.0862 (13 pips)
- Target: 1.0920 (45 pips = 3.5R)
- Result: Hit by 10:15 EST ✓
```

### Example 2: Gold Data Reversal

```
London Session:
- Gold rallied from $2,400 to $2,420 (Bullish)
- London High: $2,420

Overlap (08:30 EST — Strong NFP):
- Initial spike: Gold to $2,425 (BSL swept above London High)
- Sharp reversal: Gold drops to $2,408 in 10 minutes
- FVG created: $2,413-$2,418
- Retrace to FVG at 09:00 EST
- Entry: $2,415 (Short), Stop: $2,423 ($8)
- Target: $2,390 ($25 = 3.1R)
- Result: Target hit by 11:00 EST ✓
```

## Session Overlap and Risk Management

```
Overlap-Specific Rules:
- Maximum 2 trades during overlap
- If first trade wins: Second trade at HALF size (lock in gains)
- If first trade loses: Second trade only if A+ setup (no revenge)
- Close/trail all positions by 11:30 EST
- No new positions after 11:00 EST

News Events:
- No entries 5 minutes before US data
- Wait minimum 5 minutes after release
- Use wider stops on news-related trades (+50%)
- Reduce position size by 30-50% on news days
```

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These">

**1. Trading too early** — Don't enter at 08:00. Wait for 08:30+ catalyst.

**2. Ignoring London direction** — London established the bias. Respect it unless clear reversal signals.

**3. Fighting data** — If data creates strong displacement, trade WITH it (after confirmation).

**4. Overtrading the overlap** — Maximum 2 entries. Quality over quantity.

**5. Holding through London Close** — After 12:00 EST, liquidity drops significantly. Trail or exit.

</Callout>
