## What is Market Structure?

**Market Structure** is the pattern of price movement up and down. It shows when buyers (bullish) are in control and when sellers (bearish) are in control.

In simple terms:
- **Moving upward** = Swing Highs and Swing Lows are being created higher (bullish trend)
- **Moving downward** = Swing Highs and Swing Lows are being created lower (bearish trend)

<Callout type="tip" title="Why This Matters">
Market structure tells you "who has control of the game". It determines your trading direction and bias.
</Callout>

---

## Understanding Swing Highs and Swing Lows

### What is a Swing High?
A **Swing High** is a peak where:
- At least 2 smaller candles on the left side
- This peak is the highest point
- At least 2 smaller candles on the right side

### What is a Swing Low?
A **Swing Low** is a valley where:
- At least 2 higher candles on the left side
- This valley is the lowest point
- At least 2 higher candles on the right side

### Professional Market Structure Chart:

**In the chart analysis below:**
- **T1:** First Swing Low (1.0800) - Starting point
- **T3:** First Swing High (1.0950) - Higher than T1 ✓
- **T4:** Second Swing Low (1.0870) - HIGHER than T1 ✓ (HL)
- **T5:** Second Swing High (1.0980) - HIGHER than T3 ✓ (HH)
- **T6:** Third Swing Low (1.0900) - HIGHER than T4 ✓ (HL)
- **T7:** Third Swing High (1.1000) - HIGHER than T5 ✓ (HH)

**Pattern Recognition:** Every new High is higher, every new Low is higher = **CONFIRMED STRONG UPTREND**

This is the foundation of reading market structure professionally.

## CHOCH Explained (Change Of CHaracter)

**CHOCH (Change Of Character)** means the market structure behavior has changed.

### Bullish to Bearish CHOCH:
```
Swing High 1330  ← old peak broken
    / \
   /   \
  /     \
 /       \ 1310 ← New Swing Low is lower
/         \
Swing Low  \
1300        \  ← CHOCH! Now bearish
```

**What it means:** Buyers can no longer maintain control. Sellers are now in charge.

### Bearish to Bullish CHOCH:
```
1300 ← Swing Low is broken (upward)
    /
   / 1310 ← New Swing High is higher
  /
 /  ← CHOCH! Now bullish
Old High
```

**What it means:** Sellers have been stopped. Buyers are now in control.

<Callout type="warning" title="Remember">
CHOCH only confirms the current trend has ended—it doesn't create a new trend by itself!
</Callout>

## What is BOS? (Break Of Structure)

**BOS (Break Of Structure)** is when price breaks below a level that showed the trend direction.

### Bullish BOS:
```
     Previous Swing Low = 1300
            |
            |
           \|/
            V ← Price breaks below
           1295 ← This is bearish, BUT...
```

**However**, if a new higher Swing Low and High form afterward, the bullish trend resumes.

## Complete Market Structure Steps

### Step 1: Identify Swing High/Low

```
1350 ─── Swing High 1
       /\
      /  \
1340 /    \ Swing Low 1
    /      \
   /        \
1330 ─────────\─ Previous Support
```

### Step 2: Identify Uptrend
- New Swing High > old Swing High? ✓
- New Swing Low > old Swing Low? ✓
- Then **uptrend is active**

### Step 3: Look for CHOCH
- Old Swing Low is broken?
- New Swing Low is created below?
- Then **possible trend reversal beginning**

## Tradeable Levels (Trading Levels)

### Premium Area (Bullish Strength):
```
Small candles at Swing High = strength at level
Price stays above = Buyers in control
```

### Discount Area (Bearish Weakness):
```
Small candles at Swing Low = weakness at level
Price stays below = Sellers in control
```

<Callout type="tip" title="Trading Tip">
When you see small-bodied candles with small wicks at premium levels, buyers are strong. Shorting here is risky.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These">

**1. Seeing CHOCH in every small move** — Real structure changes are usually larger

**2. Trading on one timeframe alone** — A 15M CHOCH might reverse on 4H

**3. Moving stops too close** — Near pending Order Blocks

**4. No invalidation plan** — Always define the level that proves you wrong

</Callout>
