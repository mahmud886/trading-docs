## Why 90% of Traders Fail

Here's the hard truth: **Most traders don't know why they're losing money.**

They'll say: "The market was rigged" or "My strategy doesn't work" — but they have NO DATA to prove it.

They don't track their trades. They don't know their actual win rate. They don't analyze their mistakes. They just... trade and hope.

**The professionals?** They journal every single trade.

A trading journal is the difference between:

- **Guessing** at what went wrong
- **Knowing exactly** what went wrong and how to fix it

## What to Track in Your Journal

### 1. Trade Setup Information

| Field               | Example             | Why It Matters                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Date & Time**     | 2026-05-12, 2:34 PM | Track when your edge is strongest           |
| **Pair/Instrument** | EUR/USD             | Some pairs are more profitable for you      |
| **Timeframe**       | 4H                  | Different timeframes have different results |
| **Entry Price**     | 1.0850              | For calculating actual R:R                  |
| **Stop Loss**       | 1.0800              | Risk amount (50 pips)                       |
| **Take Profit**     | 1.0950              | Reward target (100 pips)                    |
| **Position Size**   | 0.5 lot             | Did you follow your 1% rule?                |
| **Risk Amount**     | $50                 | Confirm it was 1% of account                |

### 2. Trade Management

| Field                 | Example | Why It Matters                |
| --------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Exit Price**        | 1.0840  | Track actual outcome          |
| **P&L**               | -$50    | Know if you won/lost          |
| **Exit Reason**       | Hit SL  | Did you follow plan or panic? |
| **Actual Win Rate %** | Loss    | Calculate R:R achieved        |
| **Time in Trade**     | 2 hours | Track if you hold too long    |

### 3. Setup Analysis (The Gold)

| Field               | Example                          | Why It Matters             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Setup Type**      | Breakout at 4H resistance        | Identify which setups work |
| **Market Context**  | Uptrend, pullback                | Did overall trend help?    |
| **What Worked**     | Good risk:reward ratio           | Replicate this             |
| **What Went Wrong** | Got nervous, moved SL            | Avoid this next time       |
| **Emotions**        | Felt FOMO, wanted to hold longer | Track psychological issues |

## How to Analyze Your Journal (Weekly Review)

Every **Friday evening**, spend 30 minutes reviewing your journal:

### 1. Calculate Your Stats

```
Total Trades This Week: 15
Wins: 9
Losses: 6
Win Rate: 60% ✅

Total $ Profit: $450
Total $ Loss: $-200
Net P&L: $250 ✅

Average Risk per Trade: $50
Average Reward per Trade: $75
Profit Factor: $450 / $200 = 2.25 (Good)
```

### 2. Identify Patterns

Look for:

- Which pairs were profitable? (EUR/USD +$200, GBP/USD -$50)
- Which setups worked? (Breakouts 65% win rate, S/R zones 55%)
- Which timeframes? (4H better than 1H)
- Time of day? (London session stronger)

### 3. List Your Mistakes

```
Week of May 6:
❌ Moved stop loss 2 times (Trades 3, 7) — cost me $80
❌ Traded too large position on Trade 12 (risked 2% instead of 1%)
❌ Didn't wait for confirmation on Trade 9 (FOMO trade)
❌ Held winner too long on Trade 14 (should have taken 50% at TP)
```

### 4. Write Your Action Plan

```
THIS WEEK I WILL:
1. Never move a stop loss (trade is invalid if I move it)
2. Stick to 1% risk on EVERY trade (use checklist before entry)
3. Only trade EUR/USD and GBP/USD (avoid underperforming pairs)
4. Only trade breakouts (they have higher win rate)
5. Take 50% profit at first target, let 50% run (fix greedy exits)
```

## Common Journal Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake #1: Not Recording Trades

"I'll remember this one..."

**No you won't.** After 50 trades, details blur. Write it down immediately after exit.

### ❌ Mistake #2: Only Recording Wins

Only noting winning trades gives a false sense of your actual edge. **Track losses honestly.**

### ❌ Mistake #3: Vague Analysis

"Market went up, I made money." — This teaches you nothing.

Be specific: "Broke above daily resistance at 1.0900 with bullish engulfing; held to daily TP at 1.0950; profit $75."

### ❌ Mistake #4: Never Reading Your Journal

You journal 60 trades then never review. Pointless.

**Rule:** Weekly review mandatory. Monthly deep dive mandatory.

### ❌ Mistake #5: Getting Too Complex

Don't track 50 data points per trade. Start with 15. Add more as you evolve.

## The Real Power of Journaling

After 3 months of consistent journaling, you'll have **irrefutable data** about:

1. **Your actual edge** — "My breakout strategy is 62% win rate on EUR/USD"
2. **Your weak points** — "I lose money when I move stops" or "Falling FOMO costs $100/week"
3. **Your profitable periods** — "I'm profitable only during London session"
4. **Your best instruments** — "Forex beats crypto for me"

**This data makes you unstoppable** because now you're trading based on YOUR statistics, not ego.

## Journal Tools

### Option 1: Spreadsheet

- Simple, free, flexible
- Pro: You fully control it
- Con: Easy to stop using after a month
- Best for: Organized people

### Option 2: Notion

- Database approach, lookups work well
- Pro: Can filter by pair/setup type
- Con: Slightly slower entries
- Best for: People who like dashboards

### Option 3: Pen & Notebook

- Immediate emotional release
- Pro: Free, tactile, deep thinking
- Con: Hard to search later
- Best for: Reflective traders

### Option 4: Trading Platform Built-in

- Many platforms have journal features
- Pro: Integrated with charts
- Con: Limited customization
- Best for: Lazy traders
