## A One-Page Checklist

Before every trade, run through this checklist:

- HTF bias? (Daily/4H)
- Kill Zone timing? (London/NY?)
- POI: OB/FVG/level?
- Lower TF confirmation (CHOCH/MSS)?
- Stop location & invalidation clear?
- Position size & risk defined?
- News/events scheduled?

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep it simple">
Use a physical or digital checklist and refuse trades that don't pass — discipline beats intuition.
</Callout>

---

## Execution discipline

Good execution is as important as the trade idea. Plan entries and exits, and follow the checklist every time.

---

## Key Takeaways

A short checklist prevents emotional errors and improves repeatability.
