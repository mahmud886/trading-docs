## What is Liquidity in Price Action?

Liquidity refers to **resting orders** in the market — stop losses, pending orders, and limit orders that accumulate at predictable levels. Price moves TOWARD liquidity because that's where the orders are needed to fill large institutional positions.

<Callout type="info" title="Key Insight">
"Price is drawn to liquidity like a magnet. Every swing high has buy stops above it. Every swing low has sell stops below it. Understanding this changes how you see support and resistance forever."
</Callout>

---

## Types of Liquidity

### Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)
- Stop losses from short sellers sitting above swing highs
- Buy stop orders from breakout traders
- Accumulates above equal highs, previous day highs, session highs

### Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)
- Stop losses from long traders sitting below swing lows
- Sell stop orders from breakdown traders
- Accumulates below equal lows, previous day lows, session lows

---

## Liquidity Concepts for Price Action Traders

### Stop Hunts
When price briefly pierces a key level to trigger stops before reversing:
- Long wick beyond support → stop hunt below (bullish signal)
- Long wick beyond resistance → stop hunt above (bearish signal)
- Most common during session opens (London, NY)

### Liquidity Pools
Areas where many orders accumulate:
- **Equal Highs/Lows** — Most obvious, most targeted
- **Trendline touches** — Stop losses placed along trendlines
- **Round numbers** — Psychological levels (1.0800, 2,000 Gold)
- **Previous session H/L** — PDH, PDL, Asian H/L

---

## How to Trade with Liquidity

### Step-by-Step
1. **Mark obvious levels** — Equal H/L, PDH/PDL, session levels
2. **Identify the draw** — Which liquidity pool is nearest?
3. **Wait for the sweep** — Price takes out the liquidity
4. **Confirm reversal** — Pin bar, engulfing, or structure shift after sweep
5. **Enter** — In the direction opposite to the sweep
6. **Target** — Next liquidity pool on the other side

### Example: EUR/USD
- **Level:** Equal lows at 1.0850 (SSL below)
- **Sweep:** Price dips to 1.0843 during London session
- **Confirmation:** Bullish engulfing at 1.0848
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0852
- **SL:** 1.0840 | **TP:** 1.0900 (BSL target)
- **R:R:** 1:4

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Liquidity Errors">

**❌ Placing stops at obvious levels** — Your stop IS the liquidity. Place beyond sweep zones

**❌ Buying support / selling resistance** — These levels are TARGETS for sweeps, not entries

**❌ Not marking session levels** — PDH, PDL, and session H/L are critical reference points

</Callout>
