## Advanced Price Action Overview

Advanced Price Action goes beyond basic patterns to understand the **institutional logic** behind every candle. At this level, you read the market like a professional — seeing order flow, manipulation, and institutional footprints in raw price data.

---

## Advanced Concepts

### 1. Institutional Candle Signatures
- **Displacement candle:** Large body, small wicks = institutional commitment
- **Absorption candle:** Large wick, small body at key level = orders being absorbed
- **Exhaustion candle:** Large wick AFTER extended trend = smart money exiting

### 2. Multi-Leg Analysis
Complex moves occur in 3 or 5 legs (Elliott-like):
- **Leg 1:** Impulse (strong, creates FVGs)
- **Leg 2:** Correction (shallow, 38-62% pullback)
- **Leg 3:** Impulse (typically longest and strongest)
- **Leg 4:** Correction (deeper, 50-78% pullback)
- **Leg 5:** Final impulse (weakest, often divergent)

### 3. Reading the Open
The daily open price is critical:
- If price is above the open → bullish bias for the day
- If price is below the open → bearish bias for the day
- Price often returns to the daily open before the true move

### 4. Price Action Fractals
Every timeframe shows the same Price Action patterns:
- A 1M pin bar works the same way as a Daily pin bar
- HTF patterns contain LTF patterns within them
- Master ONE timeframe → apply to all
