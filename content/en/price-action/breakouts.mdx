## What Is a Breakout?

A **breakout** is when price **closes** beyond a level that held it before: a range edge, a support or resistance zone, a trendline or the boundary of a pattern. It often starts a new move, because the orders that held the level are used up and stops beyond it are triggered.

---

## Types of Breakouts

| Type           | What breaks                          | Example                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **Horizontal** | A range top/bottom or an S/R zone    | Close above a 100-pip range      |
| **Trendline**  | A diagonal line through swing points | Close below a rising trendline   |
| **Pattern**    | A triangle, flag or wedge boundary   | Close out of a bull flag         |
| **Structural** | The last swing high or low (a BOS)   | Close above the last higher high |

---

## Real Breakout or Fakeout?

| Clue               | Real breakout                       | Fakeout                            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **The close**      | Well beyond the level               | Back inside, or just a wick beyond |
| **The candle**     | Large body, small wicks             | Long wick back toward the level    |
| **Follow-through** | The next candles continue           | The next candles return inside     |
| **The retest**     | Holds the level from the other side | Breaks back through                |
| **Context**        | With the higher-timeframe trend     | Against it, or into a major level  |

See [Fakeouts](/en/price-action/fakeouts) for the failed side.

## Four Ways to Confirm

| Method          | Rule                                               | Trade-off                                   |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Close**       | Enter only after a candle closes beyond the level  | Simple; some closes still fail              |
| **Retest**      | Wait for price to come back and hold the level     | Fewer fakeouts; some breakouts never retest |
| **Two candles** | Two consecutive closes beyond the level            | More proof; later entry                     |
| **Momentum**    | A large-bodied candle, ideally leaving a gap (FVG) | Shows intent; needs judgment                |

---

## Three Strategies

| Strategy                       | Entry                       | Stop                           | Target                 |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| **Breakout + retest**          | On the held retest          | Back inside the level + buffer | Range height projected |
| **Momentum**                   | On the breakout close       | Inside the range or base       | 2–3× the range height  |
| **Failed breakout** (reversal) | After the close back inside | Beyond the fakeout wick        | The opposite edge      |

---

## Measuring Targets

1. **Measured move:** target = breakout level ± the range height. In the chart, 150.50 + 1.00 = **151.50**.
2. **Next level:** the next swing high or low, or S/R zone, beyond the breakout.

Use whichever comes first.

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. EUR/USD trendline break

A falling trendline has capped EUR/USD for two weeks. A 4H candle closes above it at **1.0905**, and the next candle holds. Buy **1.0910**, stop **1.0880** below the breakout candle, target **1.0980**: risk 30 pips, reward 70 pips, **R:R 1 : 2.3** (planned).

### 2. Gold failed breakout

Gold pokes above **2,400** resistance to **2,410** and closes back below. Sell **2,395**, stop **2,412** above the wick, target the range bottom at **2,360**: risk $17, reward $35, **R:R 1 : 2.1** (planned).

### 3. NAS100 session breakout (BD time)

During the Asian session (**6 AM – 12 PM BD**), NAS100 ranges **18,200 – 18,280**. Around the London open (**1:00 PM BD** in summer), it closes above 18,280, and retests it at about **2:30 PM BD**. Buy **18,290**, stop **18,260**, target **18,380**: risk 30 points, reward 90 points, **R:R 1 : 3.0** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                      | Fix                                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Entering on a wick through the level         | Wait for the close                            |
| Chasing a candle that's already far away     | Wait for the retest, or skip                  |
| Stop inside the old range by only a pip      | Give it room below the retest, or reduce size |
| Trading breakouts in the Asian session       | Ranges usually hold until London or New York  |
| Breaking into a major higher-timeframe level | The move often stalls there; target before it |

## Summary

| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Breakout** | A close beyond a level that held before          |
| **Confirm**  | Close, retest, two candles, or momentum          |
| **Entry**    | The retest is the most common conservative entry |
| **Target**   | Range height projected, or the next level        |
| **Danger**   | Wick breaks and breaks against the trend         |
