## What is a Breakout?

A breakout occurs when price moves **decisively beyond a defined level** — support, resistance, trendline, or pattern boundary — signaling the start of a new directional move. Breakouts represent moments when the **balance of power shifts** between buyers and sellers.

<Callout type="info" title="Breakout Importance">
  Breakouts are where the biggest moves begin. When price consolidates, energy builds like a compressed spring. The
  breakout releases that energy into a strong directional impulse. Learning to distinguish real breakouts from traps is
  one of the most valuable skills in trading.
</Callout>

---

## Types of Breakouts

### 1. Horizontal Breakout

Price breaks above resistance or below support after consolidation:

```
───── Resistance ($150.00) ─────
  ╱╲  ╱╲  ╱╲  ╱╲  ╱╲
 ╱  ╲╱  ╲╱  ╲╱  ╲╱  ╲╱──── BREAKOUT! →
╱    ╲╱    ╲╱    ╲╱
───── Support ($148.50) ────────
```

**Most common type**. Look for at least 3 touches of the level before breakout.

### 2. Trendline Breakout

Price breaks a diagonal trendline connecting swing highs or lows:

```
  ╲ Descending Trendline
   ╲   LH
    ╲ ╱  ╲
     ╲    ╲  LH
      ╲  ╱ ╲╱╲
       ╲╱    ╲ ╲  ← BREAKOUT above trendline
        LL    ╲  ╱
                ╲╱ HL (higher low = structure shift)
```

### 3. Pattern Breakout

Price breaks out of chart patterns:

- **Triangle** (ascending, descending, symmetrical)
- **Wedge** (rising, falling)
- **Flag / Pennant** (continuation patterns)
- **Rectangle** (range breakout)

### 4. Structural Breakout (BOS)

Price breaks a previous swing high or low in the direction of the trend:

- **Bullish BOS**: Price closes above previous swing high
- **Bearish BOS**: Price closes below previous swing low

## True Breakout vs. False Breakout (Fakeout)

### True Breakout Characteristics ✅

1. **Strong momentum candle** — Large body, small wicks
2. **Body closes beyond the level** — Not just a wick poke
3. **Increased volume/momentum** — Displacement candle
4. **Follow-through** — Next candle continues in breakout direction
5. **Clean retest** — Price comes back to the level and holds

### False Breakout (Fakeout) Characteristics ❌

1. **Long wick, small body** — Price poked through but got rejected
2. **Quick reversal** — Price immediately snaps back inside the range
3. **No follow-through** — Next candles don't continue
4. **Wick-only break** — Body never closes beyond the level
5. **Low momentum** — No displacement, no aggressive candles

```
TRUE BREAKOUT:                FAKEOUT:

   ──Resistance──                ──Resistance──
        ╱█║                           │╱╲│
       ╱ ║█ Strong body             ╱ │  │╲
      ╱  ║  close above           ╱  │  │  ╲
─────╱───║──────────           ──╱───│──│────╲
    ╱    ║                      ╱    │  │     ╲
                                     Long wick
                                     = rejection
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Institutional Trap">
  Many breakouts are engineered by institutions to trap retail traders. They push price beyond a level to trigger
  breakout orders, collect stops, and then reverse. This is why CONFIRMATION is essential — never enter on the initial
  break alone.
</Callout>

## Breakout Confirmation Methods

### Method 1: Candle Close Confirmation

Wait for a **full candle close** beyond the level. A wick poke is not enough.

- 4H candle close = more reliable than 15M
- Daily candle close = highest confidence

### Method 2: Retest Entry (Most Reliable)

Wait for price to:

1. Break the level
2. Pull back to retest the level (old resistance → new support)
3. Show rejection at the retested level
4. Enter on the rejection

```
Retest Breakout Entry:
                          ╱ Entry after
                         ╱  rejection at
           ╱────────────╱   retested level
          ╱  Retest    ╱
──────────╱────────────╱──── (Old Resistance = New Support)
    ╱    ╱ Breakout
   ╱    ╱  candle
  ╱────╱
```

### Method 3: Two-Candle Rule

After the breakout candle, wait for the NEXT candle to also close beyond the level. Two consecutive closes = stronger confirmation.

### Method 4: Volume / Momentum Confirmation

The breakout candle should show significantly more momentum than the consolidation candles. Look for:

- Body at least 2x larger than average candle in the range
- Little to no wick in breakout direction
- Displacement (gap between candles)

## Breakout Trading Strategies

### Strategy 1: Breakout + Retest (Conservative) ⭐

**Best for beginners. Highest win rate.**

1. Identify a clear range/level with at least 3 touches
2. Wait for a strong candle close beyond the level
3. Wait for price to pull back to the broken level
4. Look for a rejection candle at the retest (pin bar, engulfing)
5. Entry: Above the rejection candle
6. Stop: Below the retested level (+ buffer)
7. Target: Width of the range projected from breakout

### Strategy 2: Momentum Breakout (Aggressive)

**For experienced traders. Higher reward, lower win rate.**

1. Identify consolidation with tightening range
2. Set alert at the boundary
3. When a displacement candle breaks out, enter immediately
4. Stop: Below the consolidation / opposite boundary
5. Target: 2-3x the range width

### Strategy 3: Failed Breakout Reversal (Counter-Trend)

**Trade the fakeout for quick reversal profits.**

1. Price breaks above resistance / below support
2. Watch for immediate rejection (long wick, quick reversal)
3. Price closes back inside the range
4. Enter opposite direction (short after failed upside break)
5. Stop: Beyond the fakeout wick
6. Target: Opposite boundary of the range

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: USD/JPY Range Breakout

- **Setup**: USD/JPY consolidating between 149.50-150.50 for 3 days
- **Breakout**: Strong bullish candle closes at 150.72 (above 150.50)
- **Retest**: Price pulls back to 150.50, forms a bullish hammer
- **Entry**: 150.55 | **Stop**: 150.30 | **Target**: 151.50
- **Risk**: 25 pips | **Reward**: 95 pips = **3.8R** ✅
- **Result**: Price reached 151.45, +90 pips

### Example 2: EUR/USD Trendline Break

- **Setup**: Descending trendline from 1.1050, tested 4 times
- **Breakout**: 4H candle closes above trendline at 1.0920
- **Retest**: Price retests trendline at 1.0905
- **Entry**: 1.0910 | **Stop**: 1.0880 | **Target**: 1.0980
- **R:R**: 2.3:1 | **Result**: +67 pips ✅

### Example 3: Gold Fakeout Reversal

- **Setup**: Gold at $2,400 resistance (tested 3 times)
- **Fakeout**: Price spikes to $2,408 but closes at $2,395 (long wick)
- **Entry (Short)**: $2,395 | **Stop**: $2,412 | **Target**: $2,360
- **R:R**: 2.1:1 | **Result**: +32 points ✅

### Example 4: NAS100 Session Breakout (BD Time)

- **Asian Session** (2:00 AM - 10:00 AM BD): NAS100 ranges 18,200-18,280
- **London Open** (1:00 PM BD): Breakout above 18,280
- **Retest**: Price retests 18,280 at 2:30 PM BD
- **Entry**: 18,290 | **Stop**: 18,260 | **Target**: 18,380
- **Result**: +90 points, 3R

## Measuring Breakout Targets

### 1. Range Width Projection

Measure the height of the consolidation range. Project that distance from the breakout point.

```
Target = Breakout Level + Range Height
Example: Range = 150.50 - 149.50 = 100 pips
Breakout at 150.50 → Target = 151.50
```

### 2. Previous Swing Point

Target the next significant swing high/low or structural level.

### 3. Fibonacci Extension

Use 1.618 or 2.0 extension from the range for extended targets.

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Breakout Trading Errors">

**❌ Entering on the break candle** — Wait for confirmation (close or retest). Most breakouts that reverse do so within the first candle.

**❌ No volume/momentum filter** — Weak breakouts with small candles often fail. Look for displacement.

**❌ Trading breakouts in choppy markets** — Breakouts work best after tight consolidation. Wide, choppy ranges produce more fakeouts.

**❌ Stop too tight** — Place stops beyond the range, not just beyond the level. Give the trade room to breathe.

**❌ Not considering HTF context** — A breakout against the Daily trend has much lower probability. Trade breakouts WITH the HTF trend.

</Callout>
