## How News Affects Price Action

High-impact news creates **extreme volatility** that either triggers existing Price Action setups or creates new ones. Understanding the news-Price Action relationship gives you an edge.

---

## News Trading Framework

### Before News (Pre-Release)
- **DO:** Mark key S/R levels, Asian range, session levels
- **DO:** Identify which direction a surprise would push price
- **DON'T:** Enter new positions within 30 mins of high-impact news
- **DON'T:** Move stops to breakeven just before news

### During News (Release)
- **DO:** Watch the initial spike and identify the FVG/OB created
- **DON'T:** Chase the spike — wait for the noise to settle
- **DON'T:** Trade the first 5 minutes — whipsaw is common

### After News (Post-Release)
- **DO:** Look for the FVG/imbalance created by the news candle
- **DO:** Trade the pullback to the news-created FVG
- **DO:** Use the news candle high/low as your S/R

## Real Example: NFP Trade on EUR/USD

- **Pre-NFP Level:** Key support at 1.0850
- **NFP Release:** Price spikes to 1.0810 (big miss on USD jobs)
- **News FVG:** 1.0815-1.0830 created by the spike
- **30 mins later:** Price retraces to 1.0828 (FVG zone)
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0828
- **SL:** 1.0805 | **TP:** 1.0880
- **R:R:** 1:2.3
