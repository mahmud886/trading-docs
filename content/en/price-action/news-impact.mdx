## How News Affects Price Action

Economic news creates **sudden volatility spikes** that can invalidate technical setups or create new opportunities. Understanding news behavior is essential for Price Action traders.

---

## News Impact Tiers

| Tier | Examples | Typical Impact |
|------|---------|---------------|
| **High** | NFP, CPI, Rate Decision | 50-200+ pips |
| **Medium** | Retail Sales, PMI | 20-60 pips |
| **Low** | Housing, Consumer Confidence | 10-30 pips |

---

## Pre-News Rules

1. **Close or reduce** positions 15-30 min before high-impact
2. **Widen stops** if holding through news
3. **Don't enter** new positions 30 min before
4. **Mark key levels** for post-news trading
5. **Know the consensus** — understand expected vs actual

---

## News Candle Behavior

### Common Patterns:
```
1. Spike + Continuation: Price spikes one direction, continues
   → Trade if aligns with HTF bias

2. Spike + Reversal: Price spikes, then reverses completely
   → Wait for reversal confirmation, then trade

3. Double Spike: Price spikes both directions, then chooses
   → Wait 15-30 min, trade the clear direction

4. No Reaction: Data in-line with expectations
   → Resume normal trading
```

---

## Post-News Trading (Best Approach)

```
Don't trade the NEWS — trade the AFTERMATH:

1. Wait 15-30 minutes after release
2. Let initial volatility settle
3. Identify new structure formed by news candle
4. Trade the continuation or retest
5. Use news-created levels as new S/R

This avoids slippage, spread widening, and whipsaws.
```

---

## News as Catalyst

- News often sweeps liquidity (stop hunts on both sides)
- The REAL direction usually emerges 15-30 min after
- News candles create powerful Order Blocks
- Use news volatility to YOUR advantage by waiting
