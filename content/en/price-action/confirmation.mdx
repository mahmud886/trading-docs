## What is Confirmation?

Confirmation is the **evidence that validates your trade idea** before you enter. In Price Action, confirmation comes from candlestick patterns, structure breaks, and momentum signals at key levels.

---

## Types of Confirmation

### 1. Candlestick Confirmation
| Pattern | What It Confirms |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Pin Bar** | Rejection at key level |
| **Engulfing** | Momentum shift |
| **Inside Bar Breakout** | Direction after compression |
| **Morning/Evening Star** | Multi-candle reversal |

### 2. Structure Confirmation
- **Break of Structure (BOS)** — Trend continues when structure breaks in trend direction
- **Change of Character (CHOCH)** — Reversal when structure breaks against trend
- **Higher Low / Lower High** — Structural confirmation of new direction

### 3. Momentum Confirmation
- Strong displacement candle (large body, small wicks)
- Follow-through candle in the same direction
- Volume increase supporting the move

---

## Confirmation Rules

### What Counts as Confirmation
- ✅ Candle CLOSES beyond the key level (not just a wick)
- ✅ Follow-through candle supports the direction
- ✅ Occurs during a Kill Zone (London/NY)
- ✅ Aligns with HTF trend direction

### What Does NOT Count
- ❌ A single wick beyond a level (could be a false break)
- ❌ Small-bodied candle (doji) without follow-through
- ❌ Confirmation against HTF trend
- ❌ Confirmation outside active sessions

## Real Example: Bearish Confirmation at Resistance

- **Key Level:** 4H resistance at 1.0950
- **Approach:** Price reaches 1.0950 during NY AM session
- **Confirmation:** Bearish engulfing candle with strong body closes at 1.0940
- **Follow-through:** Next candle also bearish
- **Entry:** Sell at 1.0938
- **SL:** 1.0955 | **TP:** 1.0880
- **R:R:** 1:3.4

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Confirmation Errors">

**❌ Entering before confirmation** — Anticipating = gambling. Wait for the candle to CLOSE

**❌ Weak confirmation** — A small doji is not confirmation. Need strong candle at key level

**❌ Confirmation on wrong timeframe** — If trading 4H, confirm on 4H. Don't use 1M confirmation for 4H trades

</Callout>
