## What is Confirmation?

Confirmation is **additional evidence** validating your trade idea before entry. It separates high-probability trades from gambles.

---

## Confirmation Types

### 1. Candlestick Confirmation
- Bullish: Engulfing, Hammer, Morning Star, Inside Bar break up
- Bearish: Engulfing, Shooting Star, Evening Star, Inside Bar break down
- Wait for candle CLOSE — wicks can deceive

### 2. Structural Confirmation (LTF)
```
At HTF zone, drop to LTF and look for:
- CHOCH toward your trade direction
- BOS confirming momentum shift
- LTF Order Block respect

Example:
H4 zone reached → M15 shows bullish CHOCH → CONFIRMED
```

### 3. Volume Confirmation
- High-volume rejection = strong
- High-volume breakout = real
- Low-volume break = likely fakeout

### 4. Multi-Timeframe Confirmation
```
Strongest: All timeframes agree
D1: Bullish ✓
H4: At key level ✓
H1: CHOCH forming ✓
M15: Entry pattern ✓
= MAXIMUM CONFIDENCE
```

---

## Rules

1. Never enter without at least 1 confirmation (2 preferred)
2. HTF confirmation > LTF confirmation
3. Structural > Candlestick
4. Volume confirms conviction
5. Patience — wait even if it means missing moves
6. False confirmations exist — always use stops
