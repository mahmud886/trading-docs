## What Is Confirmation?

**Confirmation** is evidence, after price reaches your level, that the level is actually working. A zone tells you **where** to look; confirmation tells you **whether** to act.

Reaching a level is **not** confirmation. Price touches thousands of levels it then breaks through.

---

## Four Kinds of Confirmation

| Type                 | What you look for                                                      | Rule                                                  |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Candlestick**      | Hammer, engulfing, pin bar, inside-bar break — in the zone's direction | Only on the **close**; a wick mid-candle can vanish   |
| **Structure (LTF)**  | A CHOCH or BOS in your direction on a lower timeframe                  | A **close** beyond the swing, not a wick              |
| **Volume**           | Higher volume on the rejection or breakout candle                      | Supporting evidence only; forex volume is tick volume |
| **Higher timeframe** | The trade agrees with the daily/H4 trend and is at an HTF level        | Context, checked before the lower timeframe           |

### What confirmation costs

Buying at the hammer's close (1.0854) with the same stop and target would have planned **1 : 3.4** (18 pips risk, 61 reward). Waiting for structure moved the entry 8 pips higher and the R:R down to 1 : 2.0. Each piece of evidence is paid for in price — see the break-even win rates in [Entry Models](/en/price-action/entry-models).

  The original called all-timeframe agreement "maximum confidence". Confirmation raises the odds; it never makes a trade

</Callout>

## Which Confirmation Weighs More

| Stronger                               | Weaker                                   |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Higher-timeframe agreement             | Lower-timeframe signal alone             |
| Structure break (CHOCH/BOS) on a close | A single candle pattern                  |
| Candle at a marked level               | The same candle in the middle of nowhere |
| Close beyond a level                   | Wick beyond a level                      |

### Example (no chart) — gold short with two confirmations

Gold rallies into H4 supply at **2,420 – 2,425** with the daily trend down. A 15m bearish engulfing closes at **2,414** (candle), then a close below the last higher low at **2,409.5** (structure). Sell the retest at **2,412**, stop **2,428**, target **2,388**: risk $16, reward $24, **1 : 1.5** planned.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Treating a touch of the zone as a signal   | Location first, evidence second                     |
| Acting on a candle before it closes        | Wait for the close                                  |
| Waiting for 5 confirmations                | Set the number (1–2) in your plan; more costs price |
| Confirmation against the HTF trend         | Check context first                                 |
| Skipping the stop because "it's confirmed" | Confirmed trades still fail                         |

## Summary

| Rule                              | Why                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Location is not confirmation      | Levels break all the time                 |
| Closes, not wicks                 | Wicks can disappear before the close      |
| HTF context before LTF signals    | The bigger trend outweighs a small signal |
| Decide how much evidence you need | More evidence = worse price               |
