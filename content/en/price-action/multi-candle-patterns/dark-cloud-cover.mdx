## What is Dark Cloud Cover?

Dark Cloud Cover is a **2-candle bearish reversal** — the opposite of Piercing Line. The first candle is bullish, and the second opens above C1's high but closes below the midpoint of C1's body.

---

## Rules

- C1: Large bullish candle
- C2: Opens above C1's high (gap up)
- C2: Closes below the 50% midpoint of C1's body
- C2: Must NOT close below C1's open (that would be Engulfing)
- Location: At resistance or after uptrend

---

## Psychology

- Buyers continue pushing higher (C1)
- C2 opens even higher (bulls still confident)
- Then sellers take over aggressively, pushing deep into C1
- The "dark cloud" is covering the previous bullish optimism

---

## Trading

- Entry: Below C2's low on next confirmation candle
- Stop: Above C2's high
- Target: Next support
- Weaker than Bearish Engulfing
- Needs additional confluence for reliability
