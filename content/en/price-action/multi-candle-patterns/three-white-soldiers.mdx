## What is Three White Soldiers?

Three White Soldiers is a **3-candle bullish pattern** consisting of three consecutive large bullish candles, each opening within the previous candle's body and closing at or near its high. It signals strong buyer conviction.

---

## Identification

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| 3 consecutive bullish candles | Each with large bodies |
| Progressive closing | Each closes higher than the previous |
| Opening | Each opens within previous body |
| Wicks | Small upper wicks (full conviction) |
| Location | After downtrend or at support (reversal) |

---

## Psychology

- Three consecutive sessions of buyer domination
- No significant selling at any point
- Each session builds on the previous with no weakness
- Very strong momentum signal — trend change or continuation

---

## Trading

- Used as confirmation of trend change (not entry signal)
- Enter on pullback after the pattern completes
- Stop: Below the first soldier's low
- Caution: If candles are too large, price may be overextended
- Best when appearing after a consolidation/base
