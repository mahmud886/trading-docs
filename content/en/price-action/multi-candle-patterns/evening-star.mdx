## What is the Evening Star?

The Evening Star is a **3-candle bearish reversal** pattern — the opposite of Morning Star. It forms at the top of uptrends and signals buyers losing control to sellers.

---

## Structure

```
Candle 1: Large bullish (trend continuation)
Candle 2: Small body/doji (indecision - the "star")
Candle 3: Large bearish (reversal confirmation)

                 ┏┓ C2 (star)
   ┏━━┓        ┗┛
   ┃  ┃ C1          ┏┓
   ┃  ┃             ┃┃ C3 (big red)
   ┗━━┛             ┃┃
                    ┗┛
```

---

## Rules

| Candle   | Requirement                                           |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| C1       | Large bullish candle                                  |
| C2       | Small body or doji — gaps up preferred                |
| C3       | Large bearish candle closing into or below C1's body  |
| Location | At resistance, supply zone, or after extended uptrend |

---

## Trading

- Entry: Below C3's low or at C3 close
- Stop: Above C2's high (the star)
- Target: Next support level
- Stronger when C3 closes below C1's midpoint
