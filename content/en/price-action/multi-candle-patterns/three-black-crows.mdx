## What is Three Black Crows?

Three Black Crows is the **bearish opposite** of Three White Soldiers — three consecutive large bearish candles, each opening within the previous body and closing at or near its low. Signals strong selling pressure.

---

## Identification

- 3 consecutive large bearish candles
- Each opens within previous body
- Each closes lower than previous
- Small/no lower wicks (full selling conviction)
- Best at resistance or after extended uptrend

---

## Psychology

- Three consecutive sessions of seller domination
- No meaningful buying at any point
- Aggressive distribution or panic selling
- Strong bearish momentum signal

---

## Trading

- Confirms bearish reversal or continuation
- Enter on pullback/retest after pattern
- Stop: Above first crow's high
- Target: Next support level
- Caution: May be overextended — wait for retest
