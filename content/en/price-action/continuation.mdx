## What are Continuation Patterns?

Continuation patterns are **pauses within a trend** that resolve in the same direction as the trend. They represent temporary consolidation where the market "catches its breath" before continuing.

---

## Key Continuation Patterns

### 1. Bull/Bear Flag
- **Shape:** After a strong move, price pulls back gently in a channel
- **Entry:** Break above the flag (bull) or below (bear)
- **Target:** Measure the "pole" (initial move) and project from breakout
- **Reliability:** ★★★★★ in strong trends

### 2. Pennant
- **Shape:** Converging triangle after a strong move
- **Entry:** Break of the pennant in the trend direction
- **Target:** Same as flag — measure the pole
- **Key:** Should form quickly (5-15 candles max)

### 3. Ascending/Descending Triangle
- **Ascending:** Flat top + rising lows → bullish breakout
- **Descending:** Flat bottom + falling highs → bearish breakout
- **Entry:** On breakout with volume confirmation

### 4. Inside Bars
- **Shape:** Candle contained within previous candle's range
- **Signal:** Consolidation → breakout in trend direction
- **Best:** Multiple inside bars = stronger breakout

---

## Price Action Continuation Signals

### In an Uptrend
- Higher Low after a pullback = continuation confirmed
- Pullback to 50% of the last leg → strong base for next move
- Bullish engulfing at the pullback low = entry signal

### In a Downtrend
- Lower High after a rally = continuation confirmed
- Rally into previous demand-turned-supply zone
- Bearish engulfing at the rally high = entry signal

## Real Example: Bull Flag on Gold

- **Pole:** Gold rallies from 2,000 to 2,040 in 2 hours
- **Flag:** Price pulls back to 2,030 over 4 hours in a gentle channel
- **Breakout:** Bullish candle closes above 2,040
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,041
- **SL:** 2,028 (below flag low) | **TP:** 2,080 (pole = 40pts projected)
- **R:R:** 1:3

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Continuation Errors">

**❌ Trading continuation against the trend** — Continuation = WITH the trend only

**❌ Entering before the breakout** — Wait for confirmation, don't anticipate

**❌ Deep pullbacks** — If pullback retraces 78%+, it might be a reversal, not continuation

</Callout>
