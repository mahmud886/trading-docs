## What is a Marubozu?

A Marubozu is a candle with a **full body and no wicks** (or very small wicks). It represents complete dominance by one side — no opposition whatsoever.

---

## Types

### Bullish Marubozu:
- Large green body, no/tiny wicks
- Opens at the low, closes at the high
- **Signal:** Extreme buyer control, continuation likely

### Bearish Marubozu:
- Large red body, no/tiny wicks
- Opens at the high, closes at the low
- **Signal:** Extreme seller control, continuation likely

---

## Psychology

- One side had TOTAL control the entire session
- No counter-pressure at any point
- Strongest momentum signal a single candle can give
- Often marks the beginning of a new trend or acceleration

---

## How to Trade

- **Don't fade it** — Never trade against a Marubozu
- **Wait for pullback** — Enter on retracement to 50% of the Marubozu
- **Use as bias** — Marubozu direction = bias for next session
- **Creates Order Block** — The candle before a Marubozu is often a strong OB
