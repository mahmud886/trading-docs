## What is the Inverted Hammer?

The Inverted Hammer appears at the **bottom of a downtrend** and signals potential bullish reversal. It has a long upper wick and small body at the bottom — same shape as Shooting Star but at the opposite location.

---

## Identification

- Long upper wick (2x+ body)
- Small body at the bottom of range
- Little/no lower wick
- Location: After downtrend or at support

---

## Psychology

- In a downtrend, buyers attempt a rally (creating the upper wick)
- Sellers push back but can't push below the open
- Shows buying interest is returning
- Confirmation needed: Next candle must close bullish

---

## Trading

- Entry: Above Inverted Hammer high after bullish confirmation
- Stop: Below the Inverted Hammer low
- Target: Next resistance or swing high
- Stronger at key demand zones and Fibonacci levels
