## What is a Spinning Top?

A Spinning Top has a **small body with wicks on both sides** of roughly equal length. Similar to a Doji but with a slightly larger body. It represents indecision with a slight lean.

---

## Identification

- Small real body (any color)
- Upper and lower wicks roughly equal
- Body about 1/3 of total range
- Wicks about 2/3 of total range

---

## Psychology

- Both buyers and sellers were active but neither won decisively
- The body shows a slight directional lean
- After a trend = warning of exhaustion
- In a range = just noise, no signal

---

## Trading Rules

- **Not a standalone signal** — needs context and confirmation
- At resistance after uptrend + spinning top = potential bearish reversal
- At support after downtrend + spinning top = potential bullish reversal
- Confirm with next candle direction
- Use as additional confluence, not primary signal
