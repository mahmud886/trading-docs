## What is the Hanging Man?

The Hanging Man looks identical to a Hammer but appears at the **top of an uptrend**. Same shape, opposite meaning — it warns of potential bearish reversal.

---

## Identification

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| Shape | Same as Hammer (small body top, long lower wick) |
| Location | MUST be at the top of an uptrend |
| Signal | Bearish warning (buyers losing grip) |
| Confirmation | Requires bearish candle next to confirm |

---

## Psychology

- During an uptrend, price opens and sells off significantly
- Buyers recover price back to the open
- BUT the selloff itself is a warning — sellers are becoming active
- If next candle confirms bearish, the reversal is likely

---

## Trading Rules

- **Never trade the Hanging Man alone** — must confirm next candle
- Entry: Below Hanging Man's low after bearish confirmation
- Stop: Above the Hanging Man's high
- Target: Next support level
- Best when combined with resistance, divergence, or exhaustion

---

## Hanging Man vs. Hammer

| Feature | Hammer | Hanging Man |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Shape | Same | Same |
| Location | Bottom (support) | Top (resistance) |
| Signal | Bullish | Bearish |
| Reliability | High | Moderate (needs confirmation) |
