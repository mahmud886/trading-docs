## What are Price Action Reversals?

A reversal occurs when the prevailing trend changes direction. Price Action traders identify reversals through **exhaustion signals**, **key level rejections**, and **structural changes** — all without indicators.

---

## Signs of Trend Exhaustion

### In an Uptrend (Bearish Reversal Incoming)
1. **Decreasing momentum** — Each new high is smaller than the last
2. **Long upper wicks** — Sellers becoming active at highs
3. **Volume decline** — Less participation on new highs
4. **Divergence from equal pace** — Price slows while previous legs were sharp

### In a Downtrend (Bullish Reversal Incoming)
1. **Decreasing momentum** — Each new low is shallower
2. **Long lower wicks** — Buyers stepping in at lows
3. **Volume increase on bounces** — Accumulation signs
4. **Higher low formation** — First structural sign

## Reversal Candlestick Patterns

### Single Candle Reversals
| Pattern | Signal | Strength |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Pin Bar** | Long wick rejection at key level | ★★★★★ |
| **Hammer/Shooting Star** | Wick 2x+ body length | ★★★★☆ |
| **Doji** | Indecision at key level | ★★★☆☆ |

### Multi-Candle Reversals
| Pattern | Signal | Strength |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Engulfing** | Full body engulfs previous candle | ★★★★★ |
| **Morning/Evening Star** | 3-candle reversal at extremes | ★★★★☆ |
| **Tweezer Top/Bottom** | Equal high/low rejection | ★★★★☆ |
| **Three White Soldiers/Black Crows** | Strong momentum shift | ★★★★☆ |

## Structural Reversals

### The Reversal Sequence
1. Trend makes a new extreme (HH in uptrend or LL in downtrend)
2. Failed follow-through — price can't continue
3. **Break of structure** — Price breaks the last significant swing in the opposite direction
4. **Retest** — Price retests the broken structure as new S/R
5. **Continuation** — New trend direction confirmed

### Example: Bullish Reversal on EUR/USD
- **Downtrend:** Making LH/LL on 4H
- **Exhaustion:** Last LL at 1.0780 has massive lower wick (rejection)
- **Break:** Price breaks above last LH at 1.0830
- **Retest:** Price pulls back to 1.0830 (now support)
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0835 with pin bar confirmation
- **Target:** 1.0900+

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Reversal Errors">

**❌ Catching falling knives** — Don't reverse without exhaustion AND structure change

**❌ Reversing against strong momentum** — Strong trends can keep going. Wait for signs

**❌ No key level** — Reversals work best at major S/R, not in the middle of nowhere

**❌ Single candle = reversal** — One pin bar isn't enough. Need context + structure shift

</Callout>
