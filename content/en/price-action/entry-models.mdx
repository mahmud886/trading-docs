## Price Action Entry Models

### Model 1: Pullback Entry
The highest-probability Price Action entry:
1. Identify trend direction on HTF
2. Wait for pullback to key level (S/R, trendline, 50-61.8% Fib)
3. Look for confirmation candle (pin bar, engulfing)
4. Enter with the trend
5. SL beyond the pullback extreme | TP at next structure

### Model 2: Breakout Entry
Enter after price breaks key structure:
1. Identify consolidation or key S/R level
2. Wait for strong close beyond the level
3. Enter on the retest of the broken level
4. SL beyond the opposite side | TP at next major level

### Model 3: Reversal Entry
Counter-trend entries at exhaustion points:
1. Identify overextended trend at major S/R
2. Wait for exhaustion signals (decreasing momentum, wicks)
3. Confirm with reversal candle pattern
4. Enter with tight stop beyond the extreme
5. Target: Previous structure (smaller R:R but at key levels)

## Real Example: Pullback Entry on EUR/USD
- **4H Trend:** Bullish (HH/HL)
- **Pullback:** Price retraces to 1.0860 (previous resistance = new support)
- **Confirmation:** 4H bullish pin bar at 1.0860
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0865
- **SL:** 1.0845 | **TP:** 1.0920
- **R:R:** 1:2.75
