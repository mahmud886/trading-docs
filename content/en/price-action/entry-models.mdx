## Entry Models Overview

Entry models are **specific techniques for timing your entry** after identifying a valid setup. The right model maximizes R:R while minimizing stop-out risk.

---

## Three Core Models

### 1. Risk Entry (Aggressive)

- Enter immediately at zone (limit order)
- No candle confirmation needed
- Best R:R (1:3 to 1:5)
- Lower win rate (~45-55%)
- Use with 5+ confluences

### 2. Confirmation Entry (Standard)

- Wait for rejection/engulfing candle at zone
- Enter after candle close
- Good R:R (1:2 to 1:3)
- Higher win rate (~55-65%)
- Use with 3-4 confluences

### 3. Break-Retest Entry (Conservative)

- Wait for LTF structure shift (CHOCH)
- Enter on retest of broken structure
- Moderate R:R (1:1.5 to 1:2.5)
- Highest win rate (~60-75%)
- Use for counter-trend or uncertain setups

---

## Model Selection Guide

| Situation                  | Model             | Why                    |
| -------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| Strong HTF confluence zone | Risk              | Trust the level        |
| Moderate setup             | Confirmation      | Balance R:R & win rate |
| Counter-trend              | Break-Retest      | Maximum safety         |
| Scalping                   | Risk/Confirmation | Speed needed           |
| Swing trading              | Confirmation/B-R  | Time allows patience   |
