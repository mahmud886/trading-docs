## What is Momentum in Price Action?

Momentum is the **strength and speed** of price movement. Price Action traders read momentum directly from candle characteristics — large bodies show strong momentum, long wicks show exhaustion.

---

## Reading Momentum from Candles

### Strong Momentum (Trend Likely Continues)
- **Large-bodied candles** with small or no wicks
- **Consecutive candles** in the same direction
- **Increasing candle size** — each candle bigger than the last
- **Gaps or FVGs** between candles

### Weak Momentum (Reversal or Pause Likely)
- **Small bodies** with long wicks
- **Alternating candles** — bull, bear, bull, bear
- **Decreasing candle size** — each candle smaller
- **Doji candles** — indecision

---

## Momentum Shifts

### How to Identify a Momentum Shift
1. **First sign:** A candle in the opposite direction that's larger than recent trend candles
2. **Confirmation:** Follow-through candle in the same opposite direction
3. **Structure:** Break of the most recent swing point against the trend

### Example: Bullish Momentum Shift on EUR/USD
- **Downtrend:** 5 consecutive bearish candles averaging 15 pips each
- **Shift candle:** Massive 40-pip bullish candle with full body
- **Follow-through:** Next candle is also bullish (25 pips)
- **Structure:** Price breaks above last Lower High
- **Signal:** Momentum has shifted — look for buy entries on pullback

---

## Momentum-Based Trading

### Momentum Entry Rules
1. **Strong trend candle** creates displacement
2. **Pullback** retraces 38-62% of the displacement
3. **Entry** at the pullback with candle confirmation
4. **Target:** Next swing point or 1:3 R:R minimum

### Avoid Trading When
- Momentum is unclear (choppy, small mixed candles)
- No dominant direction visible
- After extended moves (momentum exhaustion)

---

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Momentum Errors">

**❌ Chasing momentum** — Enter on PULLBACKS, not mid-displacement

**❌ Confusing volatility with momentum** — Big candles both ways = volatility. Momentum = one direction

**❌ Ignoring exhaustion** — Decreasing candle size at extremes = momentum dying

</Callout>
