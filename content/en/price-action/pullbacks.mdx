## What is a Pullback?

A pullback is a **temporary retracement** against the dominant trend before price continues in the original direction. In an uptrend, a pullback is a dip. In a downtrend, a pullback is a rally. Pullbacks provide the **best entry opportunities** because you're entering WITH the trend at a better price.

<Callout type="info" title="Buy the Dip, Sell the Rally">
  The phrase "buy the dip" is essentially pullback trading. Instead of chasing price after an impulsive move, you wait
  for price to retrace to a key level and enter at a discount. This gives you a tighter stop loss and a better
  risk-to-reward ratio.
</Callout>

---

## Why Pullbacks Happen

1. **Profit-taking** — Traders who bought lower take profits, causing temporary selling
2. **New sellers** — Counter-trend traders attempt shorts at highs
3. **Institutional reloading** — Smart money allows a pullback to add to positions at better prices
4. **Natural market rhythm** — Markets move in waves (impulse → correction → impulse)

## Healthy vs. Unhealthy Pullbacks

### Healthy Pullback ✅ (Trade This)

- Retraces **38.2% to 61.8%** of the impulse move
- Maintains structure (no BOS against the trend)
- Pullback candles are SMALLER than impulse candles
- Volume decreases during pullback

### Unhealthy Pullback ❌ (Avoid This)

- Retraces **beyond 78.6%** of the impulse
- Structure breaks (CHOCH against the trend)
- Pullback candles are LARGER than impulse candles
- Increasing volume on the pullback

## Fibonacci Retracement Levels

The key Fibonacci levels for pullback entries:

| Level     | Significance                             |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **38.2%** | Shallow pullback — strong trend          |
| **50.0%** | Moderate pullback — most common          |
| **61.8%** | Deep pullback — optimal entry (OTE zone) |
| **70.5%** | ICT OTE sweet spot                       |
| **78.6%** | Maximum healthy pullback                 |

### The OTE Zone (Optimal Trade Entry)

ICT traders focus on the **61.8% - 78.6% zone** as the "sweet spot" for entries. This is where institutions typically reload their positions.

## Pullback Entry Strategies

### Strategy 1: Fibonacci + S/R Confluence ⭐

1. Identify an impulse move
2. Draw Fibonacci from swing low to swing high (uptrend)
3. Wait for price to pull back to 50-61.8% zone
4. Check if a S/R level aligns with the Fib level
5. Look for rejection candle at the confluence
6. Enter with stop below the 78.6% level

### Strategy 2: EMA Pullback

1. In an uptrend, wait for price to pull back to 20/50 EMA
2. Look for rejection candle at the EMA
3. Enter with stop below the EMA
4. Target: Previous high or next structural level

### Strategy 3: Structure Pullback (BOS Retest)

1. After a BOS, wait for price to pull back to the broken level
2. The broken level should now act as S/R flip
3. Enter on rejection at the retested level
4. Stop below the level, target next structural point

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD 61.8% Pullback

- **Impulse**: EUR/USD rallied from 1.0800 to 1.0920 (+120 pips)
- **61.8% retrace**: 1.0920 - (120 × 0.618) = **1.0846**
- **S/R confluence**: Previous resistance at 1.0850 aligns
- **Signal**: Bullish engulfing at 1.0848
- **Entry**: 1.0852 | **Stop**: 1.0825 | **Target**: 1.0940
- **R:R**: 3.3:1 | **Result**: +88 pips ✅

### Example 2: Gold Pullback to 50 EMA

- **Trend**: Gold in strong 4H uptrend
- **Pullback**: Price dips to 50 EMA at $2,410
- **Signal**: Hammer candle at $2,410 with long lower wick
- **Entry**: $2,412 | **Stop**: $2,395 | **Target**: $2,450
- **R:R**: 2.2:1 | **Result**: +$38 ✅

### Example 3: NAS100 BOS Retest (BD Time)

- **NY session** (8:00 PM BD): NAS100 breaks above 18,300 (BOS)
- **Pullback**: Price retests 18,300 at 9:15 PM BD
- **Signal**: Price holds 18,300 with small-bodied candles
- **Entry**: 18,310 | **Stop**: 18,280 | **Target**: 18,400
- **Result**: +90 points, 3R ✅

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Pullback Errors">

**❌ Entering without confirmation** — Don't buy just because price reached a Fib level. Wait for a rejection candle.

**❌ Catching falling knives** — If the pullback is aggressive (large bearish candles), it might be a reversal, not a pullback.

**❌ Too shallow entries** — Entering at the 23.6% retrace gives a wide stop and low R:R. Wait for at least 38.2%.

**❌ Ignoring HTF context** — Only trade pullbacks when the HTF trend is clear and strong.

</Callout>
