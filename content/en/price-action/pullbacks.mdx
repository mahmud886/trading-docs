## What Is a Pullback?

A **pullback** (or retracement) is a move against the trend that doesn't end it: a dip in an uptrend, or a rally in a downtrend. Trends move in steps, impulse then pullback, and pullbacks are where trend traders get in at a better price.

---

## Why Pullbacks Happen

- **Profit-taking:** traders who caught the impulse close part of their positions.
- **Counter-trend traders** try to fade the move.
- **Late traders wait** for a better price instead of chasing.

When the profit-taking is done and the counter-trend traders give up, the trend resumes.

---

## Healthy vs Unhealthy

| Sign          | Healthy pullback                | Unhealthy (the trend may be ending) |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Depth**     | 38.2–61.8% of the impulse       | Beyond 78.6%                        |
| **Candles**   | Smaller and overlapping         | Large and fast, against the trend   |
| **Structure** | Holds above the last higher low | Breaks the last higher low (CHOCH)  |
| **Duration**  | Shorter than the impulse        | Longer than the impulse             |

See [Trend Analysis](/en/price-action/trend-analysis) for the signs a trend is ending.

2. **The impulse:** price rallies from **1.0800 to 1.0920** (120 pips), breaking through it.
3. **The Fibonacci levels** on that impulse: 38.2% at **1.0874**, 50% at **1.0860**, 61.8% at **1.0846**, 78.6% at **1.0826**.
4. **The pullback** stops at **1.0846**: the 61.8% level, right on the old resistance. Two reasons to expect buyers in one place.

| Level   | Price       | Note                               |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0852      | In the 61.8% / old-resistance zone |

The 61.8% level is calculated as 1.0920 − (120 × 0.618) = 1.0846.

## Fibonacci Levels

| Level     | Reading                                                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **38.2%** | Shallow; typical of strong trends                              |
| **50%**   | Middle; the most common pullback depth                         |
| **61.8%** | Deep but still healthy                                         |
| **70.5%** | The middle of ICT's OTE zone                                   |
| **78.6%** | The deepest healthy pullback; beyond it, the trend is in doubt |

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: the OTE zone">
  The original said the 61.8–78.6% zone is "where institutions typically reload their positions". The OTE (optimal trade
  entry) zone is an ICT teaching about where to look for entries; it isn't evidence of where institutions trade.
  Fibonacci levels work best when they line up with something else: a level, a swing or an order block. See [ICT
  OTE](/en/ict/ote).
</Callout>

## Three Entry Strategies

| Strategy                 | Setup                                                                            | Stop                     | Target                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| **Fibonacci + level** ⭐ | Pullback reaches 50–61.8% where an S/R level also sits; a rejection candle forms | Below 78.6%              | The impulse high or beyond |
| **EMA pullback**         | In a trend, price pulls back to the 20 or 50 EMA and holds                       | Below the pullback low   | The previous high          |
| **BOS retest**           | Price breaks a swing high, then retests it from above                            | Below the retested level | The next structural level  |

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. Gold pullback to the 50 EMA

Gold is in a 4H uptrend. It pulls back to the 50 EMA near **2,410** and forms a bullish engulfing. Buy **2,412**, stop **2,395** below the pullback low, target **2,450**: risk $17, reward $38, **R:R 1 : 2.2** (planned).

### 2. NAS100 BOS retest (BD time)

In the NY session (about **8:00 PM BD** in US summer), NAS100 breaks above **18,300**. At about **9:15 PM BD**, it retests 18,300 from above and holds. Buy **18,310**, stop **18,280**, target **18,400**: risk 30 points, reward 90 points, **R:R 1 : 3.0** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Buying a pullback in a range or downtrend  | Confirm the trend first                                |
| Entering on the first touch of a Fib level | Wait for a rejection candle or a lower-timeframe shift |
| Using Fibonacci alone                      | Look for a level, swing or OB at the same price        |
| Stop just below 61.8%                      | Below 78.6%, or below the pullback low                 |
| Treating an 85% pullback as a bargain      | It's a warning that the trend may be ending            |

## Summary

| Aspect           | Key point                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Pullback**     | A counter-move that doesn't break the trend  |
| **Healthy**      | 38.2–61.8%, small candles, holds the last HL |
| **Warning**      | Beyond 78.6%, or a break of the last HL      |
| **Best entries** | Fibonacci + a level + a rejection candle     |
| **Stop**         | Below 78.6% or the pullback low              |
