## What is Trade Management?

Trade management is everything that happens AFTER you enter — moving stops, taking partials, trailing, and knowing when to exit. Poor management can turn winners into losers.

---

## Management Rules

### Rule 1: Move to Breakeven at 1:1
Once price reaches 1:1 R:R, move your stop to entry (breakeven). This eliminates risk and gives you a "free trade."

### Rule 2: Partial Profits
Take 30-50% off at 1:2 R:R. This locks in profit and reduces emotional pressure on the remaining position.

### Rule 3: Trailing Stop
Trail your stop behind swing structure:
- **Buys:** Trail behind 1H/4H swing lows
- **Sells:** Trail behind 1H/4H swing highs
- Never trail too tight — give the trade room

### Rule 4: Honor Your Stop
If price hits your stop → accept the loss. NEVER widen your stop or remove it.

---

## Adding to Winners (Pyramiding)

### When to Add
- Only add when trade is in profit (at least 1:1)
- Add at the NEXT pullback entry within the trend
- New position must have its own valid SL
- Total risk never exceeds 3% (combined positions)

### When NOT to Add
- Trade is in drawdown
- Near your take-profit zone
- News events approaching

---

## Real Example: Managing a EUR/USD Long

- **Entry:** 1.0860 | **SL:** 1.0840 (20 pip risk)
- **1:1 (1.0880):** Move SL to 1.0860 (breakeven)
- **1:2 (1.0900):** Take 50% off, trail SL to 1.0880
- **Pullback to 1.0890:** Add 30% position, SL at 1.0880
- **1:4 (1.0940):** Take 30% more, trail SL to 1.0910
- **Runner:** Trail behind 1H swing lows until stopped
