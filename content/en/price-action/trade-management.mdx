## What is Trade Management?

Trade management is everything that happens **between entry and exit** — moving stops, taking partials, trailing, and decision-making while in a live position.

---

## Management Framework

### Phase 1: Initial (After Entry)

- Hands off — let the trade develop
- Don't move stop for at least 1-2 candles
- Trust your analysis

### Phase 2: Break-Even

- Move SL to entry price after gaining 1R
- This makes the trade "risk-free"
- Only do this if structure supports it

### Phase 3: Lock Profit

- Trail stop behind new swing points
- Take 50% partial at 1:2 R:R
- Let remainder run to full target

### Phase 4: Final Exit

- Hit take profit target
- OR trailing stop triggered
- OR thesis invalidated by new structure

---

## Partial Profit Strategy

```
Position: 1.0 lot

At 1:1 R:R → Close 0.5 lot (50%) → Move SL to BE
At 1:2 R:R → Close 0.3 lot (30%)
At 1:3 R:R or target → Close 0.2 lot (20%)

Result: Guaranteed some profit once 1:1 hit
Psychological benefit: Huge pressure relief
```

---

## When to Move Stop Loss

| Move SL When...                   | Move SL To...      |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Trade gained 1R                   | Entry (break-even) |
| New swing forms in your direction | Behind new swing   |
| Price creates new BOS             | Behind BOS candle  |
| Approaching major opposing level  | Lock more profit   |

### NEVER move SL when:

- Trade is in initial phase (first 1-2 candles)
- Based on emotion (fear/greed)
- Against your original plan

---

## Scenario Handling

| Scenario                         | Action                           |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Price goes immediately to target | Great — take full profit         |
| Price consolidates near entry    | Wait — give it time              |
| Price goes against you           | Trust your stop — don't widen it |
| Price hits 1R then stalls        | Take partial, trail rest         |
| News event approaching           | Tighten stop or close before     |
| Higher TF changes direction      | Close immediately                |
