## Why Sessions Matter

Each trading session has a **unique character** that creates specific Price Action patterns. Trading the wrong pattern in the wrong session reduces your edge.

---

## Session Characteristics

### Asian Session (6:00 AM - 12:00 PM BD)
- **Character:** Low volatility, range-bound
- **Best Patterns:** Inside bars, tight ranges
- **Action:** Mark the range. Don't trade — prepare
- **Pairs:** AUD, NZD, JPY crosses most active

### London Session (12:00 PM - 8:00 PM BD)
- **Character:** High volatility, breakouts, reversals
- **Best Patterns:** Breakout of Asian range, pin bars at session extremes
- **Action:** Trade breakouts and reversals
- **Pairs:** EUR, GBP, CHF most active, Gold volatile

### New York Session (5:00 PM - 2:00 AM BD)
- **Character:** Highest volatility (London-NY overlap), trends
- **Best Patterns:** Continuation setups, trend trades
- **Action:** Continue London setups or enter on NY sweep
- **Pairs:** All USD pairs, indices, Gold

---

## Session Overlap (5:00 - 8:00 PM BD)

London-NY overlap is the **most volatile and profitable window**:
- Both London and New York traders are active
- Highest volume of the day
- Best for: Momentum trades, breakout continuations
- Most reversals ALSO occur during this window

---

## Real Example: Asian Range → London Breakout

- **Asian Range (6-12 PM BD):** EUR/USD 1.0870-1.0885
- **London Open (12:15 PM):** Bearish pin bar at Asian High
- **Breakout:** Price breaks below 1.0870 with momentum
- **Entry:** Sell at 1.0868 on retest
- **SL:** 1.0888 | **TP:** 1.0830
- **R:R:** 1:1.9
