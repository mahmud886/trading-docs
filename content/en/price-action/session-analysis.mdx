## Why Sessions Matter

The Forex market operates 24/5 but is NOT equally active at all times. Each **trading session** has unique characteristics, volume patterns, and behavior. Professional traders align their strategy with session tendencies.

---

## Major Trading Sessions

| Session               | Time (EST)         | Character              | Volatility |
| --------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------- | ---------- |
| **Asian/Tokyo**       | 7:00 PM - 4:00 AM  | Ranging, consolidation | Low        |
| **London**            | 3:00 AM - 12:00 PM | Trending, breakouts    | High       |
| **New York**          | 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM  | Continuation/reversal  | High       |
| **London/NY Overlap** | 8:00 - 12:00 PM    | Maximum volatility     | Highest    |

---

## Session Characteristics

### Asian Session:

- Creates the daily range (consolidation)
- Defines support/resistance for London
- Low volume, tight spreads
- Ideal for: Range trading, identifying levels

### London Session:

- Breaks the Asian range (directional move)
- Often creates the daily high or low
- Highest forex volume session
- Ideal for: Breakouts, trend initiation, sweeps

### New York Session:

- Continues London's direction OR reverses it
- Creates the other daily extreme
- High-impact US news occurs here
- Ideal for: Continuation trades, news reactions

## Session-Based Strategies

### Asian Range Breakout:

```
1. Mark Asian session high and low
2. London opens → watch for break of Asian range
3. First break may be a fakeout (sweep)
4. Trade the second move with confirmation
5. Target: 1-2× Asian range width
```

### London Sweep → NY Continuation:

```
1. London sweeps previous day's high/low
2. Creates the daily extreme
3. NY session continues in same direction
4. Enter on NY pullback to OB/FVG
5. Target: Next daily level
```
