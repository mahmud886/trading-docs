## What Is Confluence?

**Confluence** is when several **independent** reasons to trade land at the **same price**. One level is a guess; three unrelated reasons at one price make a zone worth watching.

The key word is **independent**. Two indicators built from the same closes (RSI and stochastic, say) are one reason counted twice.

<Callout type="warning" title="Removed: confidence percentages">
  The original said one level gives "~50%", adding Fibonacci "~65%", an order block "~75%" and trend alignment "~85%".
  Those numbers have no source. Confluence improves a setup; it doesn't give it a known probability.
</Callout>

## Confluence Factors

| Group                | Factor                                              | What to look for                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Structure**        | Support/resistance, flipped level                   | Several clear reactions; a broken level retested   |
|                      | Previous day/week high or low                       | Levels many traders watch                          |
|                      | Equal highs/lows                                    | Liquidity resting beyond them                      |
|                      | Round number                                        | 1.0850, 1.1000, 2,400                              |
| **Fibonacci**        | 50%, 61.8%, 78.6% retracement of the latest impulse | Draw from a clear swing low to swing high          |
| **Order flow (SMC)** | Order block, FVG, breaker                           | See the [SMC section](/en/smc/supply-demand-zones) |
| **Timeframe**        | HTF trend and HTF level                             | The trade agrees with the daily/H4 picture         |

## A Simple Checklist

Rather than a "confidence score", use a checklist with **required** items and **extra** items:

| Required (all)                                | Extra (count them)                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| HTF trend or HTF level supports the direction | Fibonacci 50–78.6% inside the zone   |
| A clear structural level at the zone          | Round number inside the zone         |
| A stop that fits your risk rules              | Order block / FVG inside the zone    |
| R:R at least your minimum (e.g. 1 : 2)        | Liquidity swept just beyond the zone |

Missing a required item = no trade, no matter how many extras. Keep your position size **the same** for every trade that passes; don't size up because a setup "looks perfect".

### Example (no chart) — gold short at a confluence zone

Gold's daily trend is down. A rally runs into **2,420 – 2,425**, where a broken support (now resistance), the 61.8% retracement of the last down-leg (**2,421**) and the 2,425 previous-day high meet. Sell **2,420**, stop **2,428**, target **2,388**: risk $8, reward $32, **1 : 4.0** planned — with a confirmation candle required first (see [Confirmation](/en/price-action/confirmation)).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                         | Fix                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Counting indicators that measure the same thing | Count only independent factors                          |
| Drawing Fibonacci until it matches              | Use the obvious last impulse only                       |
| A "zone" 60 pips wide                           | Factors must overlap tightly, or the stop gets too wide |
| Sizing up on "perfect" setups                   | Same risk per trade                                     |
| Trading confluence against the HTF trend        | HTF agreement is a required item                        |

## Practice

1. On 10 past charts, mark the last impulse and its 50/61.8/78.6% levels.
2. Mark structural levels and round numbers. Circle only zones where 3+ independent factors overlap within ~15 pips (forex) or ~$5 (gold).
3. Record what happened at each zone — and at zones with only one factor.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Rule                         | Why                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Independent factors only     | Repeated information isn't extra evidence |
| Tight overlap                | Keeps the stop reasonable                 |
| Required items before extras | No amount of extras fixes a bad context   |
| Same size every trade        | Confluence isn't certainty                |
