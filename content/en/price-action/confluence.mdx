## What is Confluence?

Confluence occurs when **multiple independent technical factors align at the same price level**. The more factors that agree, the higher the probability of a successful trade.

---

## Types of Confluence

### Level Confluence
- Support/Resistance + Round number (e.g., 1.0800 + horizontal S/R)
- Previous day high + Swing high (two different reasons for the same level)
- Trendline + Horizontal level intersection

### Pattern Confluence
- Pin bar AT a key support level (candlestick + S/R)
- Engulfing candle at trendline confluence (pattern + trendline)
- Inside bar breakout at session level (pattern + time)

### Time Confluence
- Key level + London Kill Zone timing
- Structure shift + NY AM session open
- Pattern formation + High-impact news time

## The Confluence Scoring System

Rate each trade on a 5-point scale:
| Factor | Points |
|--------|--------|
| HTF trend alignment | ★ |
| Key S/R level | ★ |
| Candlestick confirmation | ★ |
| Kill Zone timing | ★ |
| Fibonacci/OTE zone | ★ |

### Trade Rules
- **5 stars** → Full position (maximum confidence)
- **4 stars** → Standard position
- **3 stars** → Reduced position
- **2 or fewer** → No trade

## Real Example: 5-Star Confluence Trade

### EUR/USD Long
1. ★ **HTF Trend:** Daily making HH/HL (bullish)
2. ★ **Key Level:** 4H support at 1.0850 (tested 3 times)
3. ★ **Candle:** Bullish engulfing with lower wick at 1.0850
4. ★ **Timing:** London Kill Zone (12:30 PM BD)
5. ★ **Fib Zone:** 61.8% retracement of last 4H swing
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0855
- **SL:** 1.0838 | **TP:** 1.0920
- **R:R:** 1:3.8

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Confluence Errors">

**❌ Too many confluences needed** — 5 is ideal, but waiting for 7+ means no trades

**❌ Counting the same factor twice** — S/R and "demand zone" at the same level = 1 confluence, not 2

**❌ No minimum standard** — Always require at least 3 confluences

</Callout>
