## What is Swing Trading?

Swing trading holds positions for **days to weeks**, capturing larger moves using Daily and 4H charts. It requires less screen time than intraday trading but demands patience and proper HTF analysis.

---

## Swing Trading Framework

### Analysis Process
1. **Weekly:** Determine the major trend and key levels
2. **Daily:** Identify the current swing and next target
3. **4H:** Find the entry zone (S/R, OB, FVG)
4. **Entry:** Use 4H or 1H confirmation candle

### Entry Types
- **Support/Resistance bounce** — Buy at Daily support with 4H pin bar
- **Breakout + retest** — Enter after Daily BOS on 4H pullback
- **Trendline bounce** — Buy at ascending trendline on 4H

---

## Position Management

| Stage | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Entry** | Full position, SL beyond structure |
| **1:2 R:R reached** | Move SL to breakeven |
| **1:3 R:R reached** | Take 50% profit |
| **1:5+ R:R** | Trail stop behind Daily swing lows/highs |

## Real Example: Gold Swing Trade

- **Weekly:** Bullish trend, price pulling back
- **Daily:** Bullish pin bar at 2,010 (key support)
- **4H:** Bullish engulfing confirms at 2,012
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,015
- **SL:** 1,998 (below Daily pin bar) | **TP:** 2,070
- **R:R:** 1:3.2
- **Duration:** 5 trading days

## Swing vs Day Trading

| Factor | Swing | Day |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Timeframe | Daily/4H | 15M/1H |
| Duration | Days-weeks | Hours |
| Screen time | 30 min/day | 4-6 hrs/day |
| R:R typical | 1:3-10 | 1:2-5 |
| Trades/month | 4-8 | 20-40 |
