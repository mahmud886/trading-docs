## Why Volume Matters

Price shows **what** happened. Volume shows **how much participation** was behind it. A move on heavy volume had many traders behind it; the same move on light volume may not last.

---

## Core Rules

| Price move    | Volume  | Reading                                 |
| ------------- | ------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Breakout      | High    | Real participation; more likely to hold |
| Breakout      | Low     | Little interest; more likely to fail    |
| Trend impulse | Rising  | Healthy trend                           |
| Trend impulse | Falling | Trend losing fuel                       |
| Pullback      | Low     | Healthy pause                           |
| Pullback      | High    | Possible reversal                       |

## Volume at Key Levels

- **At support/resistance:** a rejection on high volume shows the level is being defended; a slow drift into a level on falling volume often precedes a break.
- **At sweeps:** a spike in volume as price runs beyond a level and closes back inside shows the stops were triggered and absorbed.

---

## Accumulation and Distribution Clues

| Phase            | Volume clue               | Price clue                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Accumulation** | High volume on down-moves | But no new lows (absorption) |
| **Distribution** | High volume on up-moves   | But no new highs             |

Both often end with a strong move out of the range. See [Accumulation](/en/smc/accumulation) and [Distribution](/en/smc/distribution).

---

## Volume Divergence

| Divergence  | Price        | Volume                    | Meaning                           |
| ----------- | ------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Bearish** | Higher highs | Lower highs on each push  | The rally is losing participation |
| **Bullish** | Lower lows   | Falling on each push down | Selling is drying up              |

Divergence is a warning, not an entry. Wait for structure to break before acting on it.

## Volume in Forex

<Callout type="warning" title="Clarified: tick volume">
  Spot forex has no central exchange, so there's no true market volume. Brokers show **tick volume**: the number of
  price changes in each candle, from that broker's feed. The original said tick volume "correlates ~90% with actual
  volume". Studies have found tick and futures volume often move together, but the figure varies by broker, pair and
  period, so treat tick volume as a rough guide.
</Callout>

| Market                                            | Volume available     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Stocks, futures (incl. currency and gold futures) | Real exchange volume |
| Crypto (per exchange)                             | Exchange volume      |
| Spot forex                                        | Tick volume only     |

Tip: for EUR/USD or gold, the futures contracts' volume can be used as a cross-check.

### Example (no chart) — gold breakout

Gold breaks above **2,400** resistance on the NY open (about **7:30 PM BD** in summer), with volume about 2× its 20-candle average. The retest at **2,402** comes on light volume. Buy **2,403**, stop **2,394**, target **2,425**: risk $9, reward $22, **R:R 1 : 2.4** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                 | Fix                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading volume without price            | Volume confirms or questions a price signal                   |
| Comparing tick volume across brokers    | Compare only within one feed                                  |
| Treating every volume spike as a signal | Spikes at news times are normal                               |
| Ignoring session effects                | Asian-session volume is usually lower; compare like with like |

## Practice

1. Add a volume panel to your 1H chart and a 20-candle volume average.
2. Mark 10 breakouts and note volume relative to the average.
3. Compare how many high-volume and low-volume breakouts held.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
