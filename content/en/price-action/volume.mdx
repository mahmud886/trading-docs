## Why Volume Matters

Volume is the **fuel** behind price movement. A breakout without volume is likely to fail. A reversal with increasing volume is likely to succeed. Volume confirms what price is telling you.

---

## Volume Signals

### Volume + Breakout
| Volume During Breakout | Meaning | Action |
|----------------------|---------|--------|
| **High volume** | Genuine breakout — institutional participation | Trade with the breakout |
| **Low volume** | Likely false breakout — no commitment | Avoid or fade the breakout |

### Volume + Reversal
| Volume Pattern | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Climactic volume at extreme | Exhaustion — reversal likely |
| Increasing volume against trend | New participants entering opposite |
| Decreasing volume with trend | Trend losing steam |

### Volume + Trend
| Pattern | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Volume increases with trend | Healthy trend — continuation |
| Volume decreases with trend | Trend weakening — caution |

---

## Volume Analysis Without Indicators

In Forex (no centralized volume), use **tick volume** or read volume through:
- **Candle size** — Large candles = high volume activity
- **Wicks** — Long wicks = high volume rejection
- **Speed of move** — Fast moves = high volume participation
- **FVGs/gaps** — Gaps form on high volume displacement

---

## Real Example: Volume Confirms Gold Breakout

- **Resistance:** 2,050 (tested 3 times)
- **Breakout attempt 1:** Small candle closes above — low wick, small body → LOW volume → FAILS
- **Breakout attempt 2:** Large-bodied candle closes at 2,058 with minimal wick → HIGH volume → SUCCEEDS
- **Action:** Buy on retest of 2,050
