## What is Market Structure?

Market Structure is the **backbone of Price Action trading**. It refers to the pattern of swing highs and swing lows that price creates as it moves through time. By analyzing these swing points, you can determine:

- Whether the market is **trending** (bullish/bearish) or **ranging**
- Where **momentum shifts** occur (CHOCH/BOS)
- The **direction of least resistance** for your next trade

<Callout type="info" title="Structure is Everything">
  Without understanding market structure, you're trading blind. Structure tells you WHO is in control — buyers or
  sellers — and WHEN control shifts. Every strategy in SMC, ICT, and Price Action relies on structure as the first
  filter.
</Callout>

## Swing Highs and Swing Lows

A **swing high** is a candle high that is higher than the candles on both sides of it. A **swing low** is a candle low that is lower than the candles on both sides.

```
Swing High (SH):         Swing Low (SL):

    ╱╲                        ╲╱
   ╱  ╲                      ╱  ╲
  ╱    ╲                    ╱    ╲
```

### Standard Definition (3-candle rule):

- **Swing High**: A candle with at least 1 lower high on each side
- **Swing Low**: A candle with at least 1 higher low on each side

### Strong Swing Points (5-candle rule):

For more significant swing points, look for candles with **2 lower highs** on each side (swing high) or **2 higher lows** on each side (swing low). These create stronger structural levels.

## The Four Structure Patterns

### 1. Higher High (HH) & Higher Low (HL) = UPTREND ✅

```
         HH₃
        ╱
    HH₂╱
   ╱  ╲╱
  ╱ HL₂
HH₁╱
╱ ╲╱
  HL₁

Buyers are in control. Each push higher exceeds the previous.
Each pullback finds support HIGHER than the last.
```

### 2. Lower High (LH) & Lower Low (LL) = DOWNTREND ❌

```
LH₁╲
╲  ╱╲
 ╲╱  ╲ LH₂
 LL₁  ╲╱╲
       LL₂╲
            ╲ LH₃
             ╲╱
             LL₃

Sellers are in control. Each rally fails lower than the previous.
Each drop creates a new lower low.
```

### 3. Higher High + Higher Low → Lower High = CHOCH (Change of Character)

```
      HH
     ╱  ╲
    ╱    ╲ LH ← First sign of weakness
   ╱    ╱ ╲
  HL   ╱   ╲
      ╱     ╲ Break below HL = CHOCH confirmed
     HL      ╲
              LL ← Structure shift confirmed
```

### 4. Range / Consolidation

```
  ─────── Resistance ───────
  ╱╲    ╱╲    ╱╲    ╱╲
 ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲
╱    ╲╱    ╲╱    ╲╱    ╲
  ─────── Support ─────────

Equal highs and equal lows. No clear direction.
Wait for a decisive break above or below.
```

## BOS — Break of Structure

A **Break of Structure** occurs when price breaks a previous swing high (bullish BOS) or swing low (bearish BOS), **confirming** the continuation of the current trend.

### Bullish BOS

```
      BOS! (price breaks above previous HH)
       ↑
    ──HH──────
   ╱        ╲
  ╱     HL   ╲  ← Price respects HL and pushes through HH
 ╱       ╲    ╲
HL        ╲    New HH
```

**Interpretation**: Buyers remain in control. Trend continues up.

### Bearish BOS

```
LL        ╱    New LL
 ╲       ╱    ╱
  ╲     LH   ╱  ← Price respects LH and pushes through LL
   ╲        ╱
    ──LL──────
       ↓
      BOS! (price breaks below previous LL)
```

**Interpretation**: Sellers remain in control. Trend continues down.

## CHOCH — Change of Character

A **Change of Character** (also called **MSS — Market Structure Shift**) signals a potential **trend reversal**. It occurs when price breaks a key swing point in the OPPOSITE direction of the trend.

### Bullish CHOCH (Downtrend → Uptrend)

1. Price is making LH/LL (downtrend)
2. Price sweeps a low (takes liquidity)
3. Price then breaks above the most recent **LH**
4. **CHOCH confirmed** — Buyers have taken control

### Bearish CHOCH (Uptrend → Downtrend)

1. Price is making HH/HL (uptrend)
2. Price sweeps a high (takes liquidity)
3. Price then breaks below the most recent **HL**
4. **CHOCH confirmed** — Sellers have taken control

<Callout type="warning" title="BOS vs CHOCH">
  **BOS** = Structure break in the SAME direction as the trend (continuation). **CHOCH** = Structure break in the
  OPPOSITE direction (potential reversal). Always wait for a **candle body close** beyond the level — wicks alone don't
  confirm.
</Callout>

## Internal vs. External Structure

### External Structure (Macro)

The **major** swing highs and lows visible on your analysis timeframe (4H/Daily). These define the overall trend direction.

### Internal Structure (Micro)

The **minor** swing points within each leg of external structure. Internal structure shows you the sub-waves within the larger move.

```
External Structure (Daily):
HH ─────────────────── New HH
 ╲                    ╱
  ╲                  ╱
   HL ──────────── HL

Internal Structure (1H, within the HL→HH leg):
    int.HH    int.HH   int.HH
   ╱    ╲    ╱    ╲   ╱
  ╱  int.HL ╱ int.HL ╱
 ╱         ╱       ╱
HL ────────────── → HH
```

### Why This Matters

- **External BOS** = High-probability continuation signal
- **Internal CHOCH** = Early warning of potential pullback
- Trade **with** external structure, use internal structure for **entries**

## How to Map Structure — Step by Step

### Step 1: Start with Higher Timeframe (Daily/4H)

- Mark the **most obvious** swing highs and lows
- Determine the trend: HH/HL or LH/LL?

### Step 2: Identify the Most Recent BOS/CHOCH

- Where did the last structure break occur?
- Is the trend intact or shifting?

### Step 3: Drop to Entry Timeframe (1H/15M)

- Map internal structure within the HTF leg
- Look for internal CHOCH as potential entry signal

### Step 4: Mark Key Structural Levels

- The swing point that was broken (BOS/CHOCH level)
- The origin of the move (order block)
- Equal highs/lows (liquidity targets)

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD Bullish BOS

- **Daily**: Price in uptrend (HH at 1.0950, HL at 1.0870)
- **4H**: Price pulls back to 1.0870 (HL) and bounces
- **1H**: Internal structure shifts bullish (CHOCH above 1.0900)
- **Entry**: Buy at 1.0905 (after CHOCH confirmation)
- **Stop**: 1.0865 (below the HL) = 40 pips
- **Target**: 1.0980 (above previous HH) = 75 pips
- **R:R**: 1.9:1 | **Result**: +72 pips ✅

### Example 2: Gold (XAU/USD) Bearish CHOCH

- **4H**: Gold in uptrend, HH at $2,450, HL at $2,410
- **Signal**: Price breaks below $2,410 HL with strong momentum candle
- **CHOCH confirmed**: First HL broken = trend shift
- **Entry**: Sell at $2,405 (retest of broken HL)
- **Stop**: $2,420 | **Target**: $2,370
- **R:R**: 2.3:1 | **Result**: +35 points ✅

### Example 3: NAS100 Structure Analysis (BD Time)

- **Session**: NY Open at 7:30 PM Bangladesh Time
- **4H**: Bullish structure (HH/HL)
- **15M**: During London session (1:00 PM BD), price sweeps Asian low
- **Internal CHOCH**: 15M breaks above London session high
- **Entry**: At the 15M OB after CHOCH
- **Result**: +180 points to previous 4H HH

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Structure Mapping Errors">

**❌ Too many swing points** — Don't mark every minor swing. Focus on the OBVIOUS ones that are visible from a distance.

**❌ Confusing BOS with CHOCH** — BOS continues the trend; CHOCH reverses it. Know the difference.

**❌ Using wicks as confirmation** — A wick through a level is NOT a confirmed break. Wait for a **body close**.

**❌ Ignoring HTF structure** — A 5M CHOCH is meaningless if the Daily trend is strongly in the opposite direction.

**❌ Not updating structure** — Structure is dynamic. Update your swing points as new ones form.

</Callout>

| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Market Structure**   | Pattern of swing highs and lows defining trend |
| **Uptrend**            | HH + HL pattern (buyers in control)            |
