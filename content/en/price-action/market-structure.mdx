## What Is Market Structure?

**Market structure** is the pattern of swing highs and swing lows that price leaves behind. Reading it answers the first question of every trade: **is price trending up, trending down, or ranging?**

---

## Swing Highs and Swing Lows

| Rule                 | Swing high                               | Swing low                               |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **3-candle (minor)** | A high with a lower high on each side    | A low with a higher low on each side    |
| **5-candle (major)** | A high with two lower highs on each side | A low with two higher lows on each side |

Use the 5-candle rule on your analysis timeframe so only meaningful swings are marked. See [Swing Highs & Lows](/en/price-action/swing-high-low).

## The Four States

| State          | Pattern                          | Bias                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Uptrend**    | Higher highs + higher lows       | Look for buys on pullbacks           |
| **Downtrend**  | Lower highs + lower lows         | Look for sells on pullbacks          |
| **Range**      | Highs and lows at similar levels | Trade the edges, or wait for a break |
| **Transition** | A CHOCH has just happened        | Wait for the new trend to confirm    |

---

## BOS and CHOCH

| Break                           | What breaks                                                                                                 | Meaning                  |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **BOS** (break of structure)    | The last swing **in the trend's direction**: a high in an uptrend, a low in a downtrend                     | The trend continues      |
| **CHOCH** (change of character) | The last swing **against the trend**: the last higher low in an uptrend, the last lower high in a downtrend | The trend may be turning |

<Callout type="warning" title="Body close, not a wick">
  Count a break only when a candle **closes** beyond the level. A wick through it that closes back inside is a sweep,
  not a break. In the chart, every BOS and the CHOCH are marked at the first close beyond the level.
</Callout>

A CHOCH is also called an **MSS** (market structure shift). It's a warning, not a guarantee: a new trend is confirmed when price makes its first BOS in the new direction (here, the break of 1.0862).

## Internal vs External Structure

| Type         | What it is                                             | Use                                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **External** | The major swings on your analysis timeframe (4H/daily) | Defines the trend                           |
| **Internal** | The smaller swings inside each leg                     | Timing entries; early warning of a pullback |

Trade **with** external structure and use internal structure to time the entry. An internal CHOCH against the external trend usually just means a pullback is starting.

---

## Mapping Structure, Step by Step

1. **Daily / 4H:** mark the obvious swing highs and lows (5-candle rule).
2. **Label them:** HH, HL, LH, LL. Decide the state: up, down or range.
3. **Find the most recent break:** was it a BOS (continuation) or a CHOCH (possible turn)?
4. **Mark the key levels:** the last HL in an uptrend (or LH in a downtrend) is the level that would turn the structure.
5. **Drop to 1H / 15m:** wait for internal structure to turn back in the external direction, then enter.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                   | Fix                                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Marking every tiny swing                  | Use the 5-candle rule on the analysis timeframe |
| Counting a wick as a break                | Wait for a body close beyond the level          |
| Treating one CHOCH as a new trend         | Wait for the first BOS in the new direction     |
| Trading internal CHOCHs against the trend | They're usually pullbacks                       |
| Forcing a trend onto a range              | No new highs or lows means range                |

## Practice

1. On a 4H chart, mark 20 swing points with the 5-candle rule and label them HH, HL, LH or LL.
2. Mark every BOS and CHOCH at the first close beyond the level.
3. Note how often a CHOCH was followed by a BOS in the new direction.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Concept       | Key point                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Uptrend**   | HH + HL                                                      |
| **Downtrend** | LH + LL                                                      |
| **Range**     | No new highs or lows                                         |
| **BOS**       | Close beyond the last swing with the trend: continuation     |
| **CHOCH**     | Close beyond the last swing against the trend: possible turn |
| **Confirm**   | Body close; then a BOS in the new direction                  |
