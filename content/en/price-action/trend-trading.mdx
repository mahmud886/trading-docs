## What is Trend Trading?

Trend trading means **only trading in the direction of the established trend**. "The trend is your friend" — Price Action trend traders enter on pullbacks and ride until the trend shows exhaustion.

---

## Identifying the Trend

### Price Action Trend Rules
- **Uptrend:** Price making Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
- **Downtrend:** Price making Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
- **No trend:** Neither pattern → don't trade

### How to Draw Trendlines
1. Connect at least 2 swing lows (uptrend) or swing highs (downtrend)
2. 3rd touch confirms the trendline
3. Price bouncing off trendline = entry opportunity
4. Trendline break with momentum = potential trend change

---

## Trend Trading Strategy

### Pullback Entry within Trend
1. Confirm trend on HTF (Daily: HH/HL or LH/LL)
2. Wait for price to pull back ON THE LTF (4H/1H)
3. Look for pullback to reach key level (S/R, trendline, 50-61.8% Fib)
4. Enter on confirmation candle (pin bar, engulfing)
5. SL beyond the pullback extreme
6. TP at the next HH (buys) or LL (sells)

### The "Buy the Dip" Model
- In uptrend, every correction (dip) is a buying opportunity
- Buy when price pulls back to the previous breakout level
- The old resistance becomes new support

---

## When the Trend Ends

### Warning Signs
1. Momentum decreasing (smaller HH distances)
2. Failed new HH or LL
3. CHOCH — first break of trend structure
4. Trendline break with volume
