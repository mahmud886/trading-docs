## What Is Trend Trading?

**Trend trading** means trading in the direction of the established trend and staying in while the trend continues. Entries come on pullbacks; exits come when the trend stops making new highs (or lows).

<Callout type="info" title="Removed: '70% of profitable trades'">
  The original said "over 70% of profitable trades come from trend-following" and that fighting trends is "the #1 reason
  retail traders lose". Neither figure has a source. Trend following is a well-established approach; check its value in
  your own results.
</Callout>

## Identifying the Trend

| Trend       | Structure                  | Trade                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Bullish** | Higher highs + higher lows | Buy pullbacks to higher lows |
| **Bearish** | Lower highs + lower lows   | Sell rallies to lower highs  |

See [Market Structure](/en/price-action/market-structure) and [Trend Analysis](/en/price-action/trend-analysis).

The stop moves only after a **break of structure**, and only to just beyond the most recent swing. That keeps you in the trend through normal pullbacks and takes you out when the pattern of lower highs stops.

## Three Entry Methods

| Method                       | Setup                                                                                           | Stop                 | Target                     |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **1. Pullback to structure** | Price returns to the last HL/LH, a flipped level or the 50–61.8% zone; a rejection candle forms | Beyond the swing     | The last extreme, or trail |
| **2. BOS continuation**      | Price breaks the last swing; enter on the retest of the broken level                            | Beyond the retest    | The next structure level   |
| **3. Trendline bounce**      | Price touches a trendline with 2+ prior touches and rejects                                     | Beyond the trendline | The previous swing extreme |

See [Pullbacks](/en/price-action/pullbacks) and [Continuation](/en/price-action/continuation).

## When Trends End

### Exit signals

- **Structure breaks:** a close beyond the last HL (uptrend) or LH (downtrend) — the CHOCH.
- **Momentum fades:** smaller impulse candles, deeper pullbacks ([Momentum](/en/price-action/momentum)).
- **A major higher-timeframe level** is reached.
- **The trailed stop** is hit.

### Exit strategy

1. Take **part** of the position at a fixed R (1R–2R) or at the first sign of weakness.
2. **Trail** the rest behind each new swing after every BOS.
3. Close the remainder at the trailed stop or the CHOCH, whichever comes first.

## Examples (No Chart)

### 1. GBP/USD uptrend, pullback entry

Daily uptrend. On 4H, GBP/USD pulls back 50% to the last higher low at **1.2650** and forms a bullish engulfing. Buy **1.2658**, stop **1.2632**, first target the last high **1.2740**: risk 26 pips, reward 82 pips, **R:R 1 : 3.2** (planned). Trail below each new higher low after that.

### 2. NAS100 BOS continuation (BD time)

In the NY session (from about **7:30 PM BD** in summer), NAS100 breaks above **18,400** and retests it at about **8:45 PM BD**. Buy **18,410**, stop **18,380**, target **18,500**: risk 30 points, reward 90 points, **R:R 1 : 3.0** (planned).

## Trend-Trading Rules

1. Trade **with** the higher-timeframe trend.
2. Enter on **pullbacks**, not after large impulse candles.
3. Put the initial stop **beyond the swing** that defines the trend.
4. Take **partial profit**, then **trail** behind structure.
5. Move the stop **only after a BOS**, never into the middle of a pullback.
6. **Leave** when structure breaks.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                          | Fix                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Buying after three big green candles             | Wait for the pullback                |
| Trailing the stop too tight                      | Trail only beyond confirmed swings   |
| Taking the whole profit too early                | Take part, trail the rest            |
| Holding after the CHOCH                          | Structure break = exit the remainder |
| Trading lower-timeframe trends against the daily | Align with the higher timeframe      |

## Summary

| Stage      | Rule                                     |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Trend**  | HH/HL or LH/LL                           |
| **Entry**  | Pullback, BOS retest or trendline bounce |
| **Stop**   | Beyond the defining swing                |
| **Manage** | Partial at 1–2R; trail after each BOS    |
| **Exit**   | Trailed stop hit or CHOCH                |
