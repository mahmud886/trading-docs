## Why Exits Matter More Than Entries

A perfect entry with a poor exit = mediocre trade. Exit strategy determines your actual profit. Price Action provides clear exit signals.

---

## Exit Models

### Model 1: Target-Based Exit
- Set fixed target at next key S/R level
- Set and forget — purely mechanical
- Best for: Day trades, news trades

### Model 2: Trailing Stop
- Move stop loss as price moves in your favor
- Trail behind swing lows (buys) or swing highs (sells)
- Best for: Trend-following, swing trades

### Model 3: Partial Profit Taking
1. TP1 at 1:2 R:R → move SL to breakeven, take 50%
2. TP2 at 1:4 R:R → take 30% more
3. Runner → trail remaining 20% to max target

### Model 4: Signal-Based Exit
Exit when Price Action shows opposing signal:
- Reversal candle against your position (engulfing, pin bar)
- Structure break against your trade (CHOCH)
- Momentum exhaustion (decreasing candle size)

---

## Real Example: Partial Profit + Trailing Stop

- **Entry:** Buy EUR/USD at 1.0860
- **SL:** 1.0840 (20 pip risk)
- **TP1 (1:2):** 1.0900 → take 50%, SL to breakeven
- **TP2 (1:4):** 1.0940 → take 30%
- **Runner:** Trail behind 1H swing lows → eventually stopped at 1.0925
