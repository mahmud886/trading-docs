## Risk Management is Non-Negotiable

Without risk management, even the best Price Action analysis will result in account loss. Risk management is the ONLY thing separating professional traders from gamblers.

---

## Position Sizing

### The 1-2% Rule
Never risk more than 1-2% of your account on a single trade.

```
Position Size = (Account × Risk %) / (Entry - Stop Loss)

Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk, 20-pip stop
Position = ($10,000 × 0.01) / 20 pips = $100 / 20 = $5/pip = 0.5 lots
```

---

## Stop Loss Placement in Price Action

### Where to Place Stops
| Setup | Stop Placement |
|-------|---------------|
| **Pin Bar** | Beyond the pin bar's wick |
| **Engulfing** | Beyond the engulfing candle's range |
| **Breakout** | Below the breakout level (buys) |
| **Pullback** | Below the pullback low (buys) |
| **Support bounce** | Below the support zone + buffer |

### The Buffer Rule
Always add a buffer beyond the obvious level:
- Forex: 5-10 pips
- Gold: 50-100 pips (30-50 cents)
- Indices: 10-20 points

## Risk:Reward Framework

| Minimum R:R | Trade Type |
|-------------|-----------|
| 1:2 | Scalping |
| 1:3 | Day Trading |
| 1:4+ | Swing Trading |

### Rule: Never take a trade with R:R below 1:2

## Daily Risk Limits

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Max risk per trade | 1-2% |
| Max daily loss | 3-5% |
| Max weekly loss | 5-8% |
| Max monthly loss | 10% |
| After 2 consecutive losses | Take a break |
