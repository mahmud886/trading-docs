## Risk Management for PA Traders

Risk management in Price Action trading uses **structural stop placement** — stops go beyond swing points, Order Blocks, or liquidity levels rather than arbitrary pip distances.

---

## Stop Loss Placement Rules

| Setup Type | Stop Location |
|-----------|---------------|
| Order Block entry | Beyond the OB (body) |
| Fibonacci entry | Beyond 78.6% or swing point |
| S/R bounce | Beyond the zone + buffer |
| Trendline bounce | Beyond the trendline |
| CHOCH entry | Beyond the CHOCH swing point |
| Liquidity sweep | Beyond the sweep wick |

---

## Position Sizing Formula

```
Lot Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop Loss pips × Pip Value)

Example:
- Account: $10,000
- Risk: 1% = $100
- Stop: Structure-based = 35 pips
- Pip value: $10 (standard lot)
- Lot size: $100 ÷ (35 × $10) = 0.29 lots
```

---

## Risk Rules for Price Action

1. **1-2% max per trade** — non-negotiable
2. **Stop BEYOND structure** — not at structure (give buffer)
3. **Wider stop = smaller position** (not no stop)
4. **Max 3 correlated positions** — EUR/USD + GBP/USD = correlated
5. **Daily loss limit: 3%** — stop trading for the day
6. **Weekly loss limit: 5%** — reduce size next week
7. **Max drawdown: 10%** — full strategy review

---

## Structural vs. Fixed Stops

| Method | Description | Pro | Con |
|--------|-------------|-----|-----|
| Structural | Beyond swing/OB/zone | Logical, less random SL hit | Sometimes wide |
| Fixed pip | Always X pips | Simple, consistent size | May be inside structure |
| ATR-based | 1.5-2× ATR | Volatility-adjusted | Doesn't respect structure |

**Best approach: Structural stop, then calculate position size to fit your risk %.**
