## What Is Expansion?

**Expansion** is a phase of large, fast candles moving in one direction. It usually follows a quiet period (compression) and is when most of a trend's distance is covered.

### The cycle

**Compression → expansion → pullback → (compression or a new expansion)**

Quiet markets don't stay quiet, and fast markets don't stay fast. Knowing which phase you're in tells you whether to wait, join or take profit.

## What Expansion Looks Like

- Large bodies, small wicks, in one direction.
- Little overlap between candles.
- Gaps (FVGs) left behind.
- Breaks of structure in the direction of the move.

---

## Types of Expansion

| Type              | Trigger                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Breakout**      | Price leaves a range or triangle                                    |
| **News-driven**   | A high-impact release (NFP, CPI, central-bank decisions)            |
| **Session open**  | London (about 1 PM BD in summer) or New York (about 6 PM BD)        |
| **After a sweep** | Stops beyond a level are taken, then price moves hard the other way |

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: 'highest R:R setups'">
  The original called sweep expansions the "highest R:R setups" and said the stop hunt "triggers institutional entry".
  Sweep entries often have tight stops, which helps R:R, but that varies trade to trade, and who entered can't be seen
  on a chart.
</Callout>

## Three Entry Methods

| Method                               | Entry                                         | Stop                         | Target                                |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **1. Breakout (aggressive)**         | On the close out of compression               | Inside the compression range | 1–3× the range height                 |
| **2. First pullback**                | On the first shallow pullback after expansion | Below the pullback low       | The next liquidity or level           |
| **3. Post-expansion (conservative)** | In the FVG or OB the expansion left           | Beyond the FVG/OB            | A new expansion in the same direction |

The chart shows method 3.

---

## Measuring Targets

- **Measured move:** the compression range's height projected from the breakout. Range 1.1000 – 1.1050 (50 pips) → target **1.1100**.
- **Fibonacci extension** of the prior impulse: **127.2%** for a normal continuation, **161.8%** for strong trends, **200%** for very strong momentum.

Use the nearest meaningful level if it comes before the measured target.

### Example (no chart) — gold after NFP

Gold ranges **2,380 – 2,395** before NFP (**6:30 PM BD** in summer). The release candle closes at **2,410**, leaving an FVG at **2,396 – 2,403**. Buy the pullback at **2,401**, stop **2,393** below the FVG, target **2,425**: risk $8, reward $24, **R:R 1 : 3.0** (planned).

## When Not to Chase

- After three or more large candles without a pause: the move is often stretched.
- Into a major higher-timeframe level.
- When the expansion candle has a long wick back against it.
- Late in a session, as volume fades.

---

## Managing the Trade

- **Take partials:** close about half at 1 : 1 and trail the rest.
- **Trail behind structure:** move the stop below each new higher low (for longs).
- **Expect a pullback:** expansions are usually followed by a retracement; don't move the stop too tight.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                | Fix                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Chasing the third or fourth big candle | Wait for the pullback                           |
| Entering expansion against the HTF     | Join expansions with the higher-timeframe trend |
| Stop too tight after expansion         | Beyond the FVG/OB or the pullback low           |
| No target                              | Use the measured move or the next level         |
