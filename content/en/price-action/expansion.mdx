## What is Expansion?

Expansion is the **high-momentum directional move** that follows compression or manipulation. This is where price travels rapidly from one level to another — the phase where the most profit is captured.

---

## Expansion Characteristics

- **Large-bodied candles** with minimal wicks
- **Consecutive candles** in one direction
- **Creates FVGs/imbalances** as price moves fast
- **Breaks structure** (BOS) easily
- **High volume** supporting the move

---

## When Expansion Occurs

| Trigger | Context |
|---------|---------|
| **After compression** | Tight range breaks → explosive move |
| **After liquidity sweep** | Stop hunt → true direction move |
| **At session opens** | London/NY open brings fresh orders |
| **On news events** | High-impact data creates displacement |
| **After accumulation/distribution** | Smart money releases positions |

---

## How to Trade Expansion

### Don't Chase — Wait for Pullbacks
1. Identify expansion move in progress
2. Wait for a pullback (38-62% of the expansion)
3. Enter at pullback with confirmation
4. Target: Next key level or opposing liquidity

### Example: EUR/USD Expansion
- **Compression:** 1.0860-1.0875 for 6 hours
- **Expansion:** Breakout to 1.0920 in 2 hours (45-pip move)
- **Pullback:** Retraces to 1.0895 (55% pullback)
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0897 with bullish engulfing
- **SL:** 1.0885 | **TP:** 1.0950
- **R:R:** 1:4.4

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Expansion Errors">

**❌ Chasing the expansion** — Never enter mid-move. Wait for pullback

**❌ Fighting expansion** — Don't counter-trade strong expansion moves

**❌ Not taking profit** — Expansion doesn't last forever. Have clear targets

</Callout>
