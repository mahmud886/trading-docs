## What is Trend Analysis?

Trend analysis is the process of identifying the **dominant direction** of price movement. A trend represents the path of least resistance — the direction price is most likely to continue moving. Trading with the trend is the single most important edge a trader can have.

<Callout type="info" title="The Trend is Your Friend">
Over 70% of profitable trades come from trading WITH the trend. Counter-trend trading requires exceptional skill and timing. As a rule, always identify the trend first and trade in its direction.
</Callout>

## Three Types of Trends

### 1. Uptrend (Bullish) 📈
Price makes **Higher Highs (HH)** and **Higher Lows (HL)**. Buyers are in control.

```
           HH₃ ←── New higher high
          ╱
     HH₂ ╱
    ╱   ╲╱
   ╱   HL₂ ←── Each pullback is HIGHER
  ╱
HH₁
╱  ╲
    HL₁ ←── Higher low confirmed

Action: Look for BUY opportunities at HL pullbacks
```

### 2. Downtrend (Bearish) 📉
Price makes **Lower Highs (LH)** and **Lower Lows (LL)**. Sellers are in control.

```
LH₁ ←── Each rally is LOWER
╲  ╱
 ╲╱
  LL₁
   ╲
    LH₂
   ╱  ╲
  ╱    ╲╱
       LL₂
        ╲
         LH₃
        ╱  ╲
            ╲╱
            LL₃ ←── New lower low

Action: Look for SELL opportunities at LH rallies
```

### 3. Sideways / Range (Neutral) ↔️
Price oscillates between **defined support and resistance** without clear HH/HL or LH/LL.

```
═══════ Resistance ═══════
  ╱╲    ╱╲    ╱╲    ╱╲
 ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲  ╱  ╲
╱    ╲╱    ╲╱    ╲╱    ╲
═══════ Support ══════════

Action: Buy at support, sell at resistance, OR wait for breakout
```

## How to Identify a Trend

### Method 1: Swing Point Analysis (Most Reliable)
1. Mark the most recent swing highs and swing lows
2. Are highs getting HIGHER and lows getting HIGHER? → **Uptrend**
3. Are highs getting LOWER and lows getting LOWER? → **Downtrend**
4. Are highs and lows roughly EQUAL? → **Range**

### Method 2: 200 EMA Quick Filter
- Price above 200 EMA → **Bullish bias** (look for buys)
- Price below 200 EMA → **Bearish bias** (look for sells)
- Price criss-crossing 200 EMA → **No trend** (stay out or range trade)

### Method 3: 45-Degree Rule
A healthy trend moves at approximately **45 degrees** on the chart. Steeper angles (70°+) indicate unsustainable moves likely to be followed by sharp corrections. Flatter angles (20°) indicate weak trends.

## Trendlines

A trendline is a diagonal line connecting **two or more swing points** that shows the angle and direction of the trend.

### How to Draw Trendlines:
1. **Uptrend trendline**: Connect 2+ swing LOWS (ascending line)
2. **Downtrend trendline**: Connect 2+ swing HIGHS (descending line)
3. **Minimum 2 touches** to draw; 3 touches = confirmed trendline

```
Uptrend Trendline:
              ╱
         ╱╲  ╱
        ╱  ╲╱ HL₃
    ╱╲ ╱
   ╱  ╲ HL₂
  ╱────────────── Trendline (connects HL₁, HL₂, HL₃)
 ╱ HL₁

Downtrend Trendline:
LH₁ ╲
  ╲  ╱╲────────── Trendline (connects LH₁, LH₂, LH₃)
   ╲╱  ╲
    LH₂ ╲  ╱╲
          ╲╱  ╲
          LH₃  ╲
```

### Trendline Break Rules:
- A wick break is NOT confirmation
- Wait for a **candle body close** beyond the trendline
- Volume/momentum should increase on the break
- Look for a **retest** of the broken trendline for entry

## Trend Channels

A trend channel adds a **parallel line** to the trendline, creating a channel within which price oscillates.

```
Ascending Channel:
         ╱══════════╱ Upper channel line (resistance)
        ╱  ╱╲      ╱
       ╱  ╱  ╲    ╱
      ╱  ╱  ╱╲╲  ╱
     ╱  ╱╲╱   ╲╱╱
    ╱  ╱        ╱
   ╱──╱────────╱── Lower channel line (support = trendline)
```

### How to Trade Channels:
- **Buy** at the lower channel line (trendline support)
- **Sell/Take Profit** at the upper channel line
- **Breakout below** channel = potential trend reversal
- **Breakout above** channel = acceleration (often unsustainable)

## Trend Strength Assessment

### Strong Trend ✅
- Clear HH/HL or LH/LL pattern
- Impulse moves are LONGER than pullbacks
- Pullbacks are shallow (38.2-50% of impulse)
- Price stays above/below 20 EMA
- Candles have large bodies in trend direction

### Weak / Exhausting Trend ⚠️
- Impulse moves getting SHORTER
- Pullbacks getting DEEPER
- Price starts crossing the 50 EMA
- More wicks appearing in trend direction
- Equal highs/lows forming (structure losing clarity)

---

## Trend Exhaustion Signals

### Signs the Trend is Ending:
1. **Divergence in momentum** — Price makes new high, but candle bodies are getting smaller
2. **Deep pullback** — Pullback retraces 78.6%+ of the previous impulse
3. **CHOCH** — First structural break in the opposite direction
4. **Climactic candle** — Extremely large candle with long wick (blow-off top / selling climax)
5. **Volume spike on reversal** — Suggest institutional unloading / accumulation

```
Uptrend Exhaustion:
                 ╱╲ ← Climactic candle (long wick = rejection)
                ╱  │
         HH₃  ╱   │ Shrinking impulses
        ╱╲   ╱    │
   HH₂╱  ╲ ╱     │
  ╱╲  ╱    ╲      │
 ╱  ╲╱   Deep PB  │
HH₁              ╲╱ ← CHOCH (breaks below HL)
```

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: GBP/USD Uptrend Pullback
- **Daily**: Clear uptrend — HH at 1.2750, HL at 1.2650
- **4H**: Price pulls back to 1.2660 (HL zone)
- **Signal**: Bullish hammer at 1.2660 + bouncing off ascending trendline
- **Entry**: 1.2665 | **Stop**: 1.2630 | **Target**: 1.2780
- **R:R**: 3.3:1 | **Result**: +115 pips ✅

### Example 2: GBP/USD Downtrend Continuation
- **Daily**: Downtrend — LH at 1.2500, LL at 1.2350
- **4H**: Price rallies to 1.2490 (LH zone, near 50 EMA)
- **Signal**: Bearish engulfing at 1.2490 + rejection from descending trendline
- **Entry**: 1.2480 | **Stop**: 1.2510 | **Target**: 1.2380
- **R:R**: 3.3:1 | **Result**: +95 pips ✅

### Example 3: Gold Trend Exhaustion
- **Daily**: Gold in strong uptrend for 3 weeks
- **Signal**: Price hits $2,480 with very long upper wick (rejection)
- **4H**: Impulses getting shorter ($40 → $25 → $10)
- **CHOCH**: Price breaks below 4H HL at $2,445
- **Entry**: Sell at $2,445 retest | **Stop**: $2,465 | **Target**: $2,390
- **R:R**: 2.75:1 | **Result**: +50 points ✅

### Example 4: NAS100 Trend Trading (BD Time)
- **NY Session** (7:30 PM BD): NAS100 in intraday uptrend
- **15M**: Price making HH/HL since NY open
- **Pullback**: Price dips to 15M HL at 18,280
- **Entry**: 18,285 | **Stop**: 18,255 | **Target**: 18,350 (new HH)
- **Session**: Trade taken at 8:30 PM BD, target hit by 10:00 PM BD
- **Result**: +65 points, 2.2R ✅

## Multi-Timeframe Trend Alignment

| HTF Trend | LTF Action | Probability |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Daily Uptrend | Buy 4H pullbacks | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High |
| Daily Uptrend | Sell 4H rallies | ⭐ Very Low |
| Daily Downtrend | Sell 4H rallies | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High |
| Daily Range | Buy 4H at support | ⭐⭐⭐ Medium |
| Daily Range | Sell 4H at resistance | ⭐⭐⭐ Medium |

<Callout type="warning" title="Never Fight the HTF Trend">
If the Daily chart is in a strong uptrend, do NOT look for shorts on the 15M chart. Even if you see a "perfect" short setup, the HTF trend will overpower it more often than not. Your LTF trades should ALWAYS align with the HTF direction.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Trend Analysis Errors">

**❌ Counter-trend trading as a beginner** — Trade WITH the trend until you have 12+ months of experience.

**❌ Calling a trend too early** — A single HH doesn't make an uptrend. Wait for at least 2 HHs and 2 HLs.

**❌ Ignoring trend exhaustion** — Don't keep buying into an exhausted uptrend just because "trend is your friend."

**❌ Wrong trendline drawing** — Connect swing points, not random candle bodies. Use the most obvious, clean connections.

**❌ Trading the middle of the range** — In sideways markets, trade at the EDGES (support/resistance), not in the middle.

</Callout>
