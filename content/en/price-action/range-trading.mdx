## What is Range Trading?

Range trading involves buying at support and selling at resistance within a **defined horizontal range**. About 70% of the time, markets are ranging — making this skill essential.

---

## Identifying a Range

### Range Criteria
1. At least **2 touches** at both support and resistance
2. Price rejected at both boundaries (not just wicking through)
3. No clear trend direction (no HH/HL or LH/LL pattern)
4. Range has existed for at least 4-8 candles on your trading timeframe

---

## Range Trading Strategy

### Buy at Support
1. Price reaches range bottom
2. Bullish confirmation candle (pin bar, engulfing)
3. Enter long with SL below range bottom
4. TP at range top or midpoint

### Sell at Resistance
1. Price reaches range top
2. Bearish confirmation candle
3. Enter short with SL above range top
4. TP at range bottom or midpoint

### The Midpoint
The 50% level of the range is important:
- Often acts as interim S/R
- Use as partial profit target
- Break of midpoint hints at which boundary will break

---

## When to STOP Range Trading

Ranges don't last forever. Exit range trading when:
- Price closes strongly beyond a boundary (breakout)
- Candle size increases dramatically (expansion coming)
- High-impact news is imminent
- Volume increases significantly
