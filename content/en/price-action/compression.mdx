## What is Compression?

Compression occurs when price creates **tightening ranges** — each candle or swing is smaller than the last, squeezing into a narrow band. This "stored energy" typically releases in an explosive breakout move.

---

## Types of Compression

### 1. Horizontal Compression (Rectangle)
- Price bounces between flat support and resistance
- Range narrows over time
- Breakout direction determined by trend context

### 2. Diagonal Compression (Wedge/Triangle)
- Converging trendlines squeeze price
- **Rising Wedge** → typically bearish breakout
- **Falling Wedge** → typically bullish breakout
- **Symmetrical Triangle** → breakout in trend direction

### 3. Inside Bar Compression
- Multiple consecutive inside bars
- Each bar contained within the previous
- More inside bars = more powerful breakout

---

## How to Trade Compression

### Entry Methods
1. **Breakout Entry** — Enter when price closes beyond the compression boundary
2. **Retest Entry** — Wait for breakout, then pullback to the broken boundary
3. **Anticipation Entry** — Enter within compression in trend direction (higher risk)

### Steps
1. Identify tightening range (3+ candles getting smaller)
2. Draw compression boundaries
3. Determine most likely breakout direction (HTF trend)
4. Set alerts at boundaries
5. Enter on breakout with confirmation candle
6. SL beyond opposite boundary | TP: compression range × 2-3

---

## Real Example: Symmetrical Triangle on Gold

- **Compression:** Price between 2,030-2,040 with converging trendlines over 8 hours
- **HTF Bias:** Bullish (Daily in discount)
- **Breakout:** Strong bullish candle closes above 2,040
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,041
- **SL:** 2,033 (below compression low) | **TP:** 2,060
- **R:R:** 1:2.4

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Compression Errors">

**❌ Trading inside compression** — Wait for the BREAKOUT, don't trade the chop

**❌ No trend context** — Compression without HTF bias = 50/50 gamble

**❌ Too tight stops** — False breakouts happen. SL must be beyond opposite boundary

</Callout>
