## What Is Intraday Trading?

**Intraday trading** means opening and closing every position on the same day. With price action on the 15-minute and 1-hour charts, the aim is usually 20–80 pips during the active sessions, with no positions held overnight.

<Callout type="info" title="Clarified: 'daily income'">
  The original said intraday trading "provides daily income potential". It avoids overnight risk and gives regular
  opportunities, but many days have no good setup and some days lose. Treat it as a way to trade, not a paycheque.
</Callout>

## Kill Zones in Bangladesh Time

| Kill zone         | New York time       | BD (US summer)  | BD (US winter)  | Character                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **London open**   | 2:00 – 5:00 AM      | 12:00 – 3:00 PM | 1:00 – 4:00 PM  | Trend start; Asian-range sweeps |
| **New York open** | 7:00 – 10:00 AM     | 5:00 – 8:00 PM  | 6:00 – 9:00 PM  | Continuation or reversal        |
| **London close**  | 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 8:00 – 10:00 PM | 9:00 – 11:00 PM | Profit-taking                   |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: time zone">
  The original listed these in "EST" only. EST is New York's winter time; the windows follow New York's clock all year,
  so the BD times shift by an hour between US summer and winter. See [Kill Zones](/en/smc/kill-zones).
</Callout>

## Setting the Daily Bias

1. **Daily candle and structure:** is the daily trend up or down?
2. **4H structure:** HH/HL or LH/LL?
3. **Location:** is price near a daily level, in premium or discount?
4. **Yesterday:** did the last session close strong or reverse?

The bias is the direction you'll trade **today**. Skip setups against it.

3. **Midday:** price drifts lower; the gap stays open.
4. **New York (5:00 PM BD):** price retraces into the gap.

| ------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |

## Three Intraday Entry Methods

| Method                   | Steps                                                                                   | Stop             | Target                             |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Session sweep**        | Mark the Asian high/low; wait for London to sweep one side and reverse                  | Beyond the sweep | The opposite session extreme       |
| **Bias + order block**   | Mark 1H OBs in the bias direction; wait for a pullback in a kill zone; 15m confirmation | Beyond the OB    | A daily level or the day's extreme |
| **FVG fill** (the chart) | London impulse leaves an FVG; NY retraces into it                                       | Beyond the FVG   | The London extreme or beyond       |

## Intraday Rules

1. **Trade the kill zones**; skip quiet hours.
2. **One direction per day**, the bias.
3. **At most three trades a day.**
4. **Flat by the end of the NY session** (around 11 PM – 2 AM BD depending on season).
5. **Daily loss limit 2%.**
6. **No entries in the 15 minutes around high-impact news** (US data is at 6:30 PM BD in summer).

### Example (no chart) — GBP/USD session sweep

Bias bearish. The Asian range is **1.2700 – 1.2725**. At about **12:30 PM BD** London takes the Asian high to **1.2731** and closes back inside, then breaks the last 15m higher low. Sell **1.2720**, stop **1.2736**, target the Asian low area **1.2690**: risk 16 pips, reward 30 pips, **R:R 1 : 1.9** (planned).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Trading both directions in one day         | One bias, one direction                    |
| Trading the Asian session on EUR/GBP pairs | Wait for London                            |
| Holding an intraday trade overnight        | Close by the end of the session            |
| Revenge trading after a loss               | Daily trade cap and loss limit             |
| Using winter times in summer               | Check which column applies to today's date |

## Practice

1. For two weeks, write the daily bias before 12 PM BD.
2. Mark the Asian range, London's impulse and any FVG it leaves.
3. Note whether NY retraced into the FVG and what happened next.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
