## What is Intraday Trading?

Intraday trading means opening and closing all positions **within the same trading day**. Using Price Action on M15-H1, intraday traders focus on high-volume sessions (kill zones) for 30-100 pip moves.

<Callout type="info" title="Intraday Edge">
  Intraday trading eliminates overnight risk and provides daily income potential. Combined with session analysis and
  higher-timeframe bias, it offers repeatable daily opportunities.
</Callout>

## Kill Zones

| Kill Zone        | Time (EST)     | Character             |
| ---------------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| **London Open**  | 2:00-5:00 AM   | Trend initiation      |
| **NY Open**      | 7:00-10:00 AM  | Continuation/reversal |
| **London Close** | 10:00-12:00 PM | Profit-taking         |

## Daily Bias Determination

```
1. D1 candle direction (bullish/bearish)
2. H4 structure (HH/HL or LH/LL)
3. Price position relative to D1 levels
4. Previous session behavior

Bias = most likely direction for today
Only trade aligned with this bias.
```

## Intraday Entry Methods

### Session High/Low Sweep:

1. Identify previous session range (Asian H/L)
2. Wait for new session to sweep one side
3. Enter on reversal after sweep
4. Stop: Beyond sweep wick
5. Target: Opposite session extreme

### Bias + Order Block:

1. Determine daily bias
2. Mark H1 Order Blocks aligned with bias
3. Wait for pullback to OB during kill zone
4. Enter on M15 confirmation
5. Target: D1 key level or daily extreme

### FVG Fill Entry:

1. London creates impulse (leaves FVG)
2. NY session retraces to fill FVG
3. Enter at FVG with bias
4. Target: London extreme or beyond

## Intraday Rules

1. **Only trade during kill zones**
2. **One direction per day**
3. **Maximum 3 trades per day**
4. **Close all by end of NY session**
5. **Daily loss limit: 2%**
6. **Align with D1/H4 bias**
7. **Avoid 15 min before/after high-impact news**
