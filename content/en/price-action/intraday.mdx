## What is Intraday Trading?

Intraday trading means opening and closing all positions within the same day. No overnight risk. Price Action intraday traders use 15M-1H charts for entries with 4H/Daily for bias.

---

## Intraday Trading Framework

### Pre-Session Routine (Before 12:00 PM BD)
1. Check Daily candle — bias for today (bullish/bearish PO3?)
2. Mark 4H key levels (S/R, OBs, FVGs)
3. Mark Asian range (high/low)
4. Mark PDH/PDL
5. Determine bias and target

### During Session (12:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD)
1. **London (12-3 PM):** Watch for Asian range sweep + reversal setup
2. **NY AM (5-8 PM):** Continue London setup or enter fresh
3. **NY PM (after 10 PM):** Close any remaining positions or trail tight

### End of Day
- Close all positions by 11:00 PM BD (before NY Lunch)
- Journal the day's trades
- Review performance

---

## Intraday Timeframe Usage

| Timeframe | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Daily** | Bias determination |
| **4H** | Key levels, S/R zones |
| **1H** | Session structure, swing direction |
| **15M** | Entry timeframe, confirmation |
| **5M** | Precision entry (optional) |

## Real Example: Full Intraday Trade

- **Daily Bias:** Bullish (yesterday's bullish PO3 candle)
- **4H Level:** Support at 1.0855
- **Asian Range:** 1.0860-1.0878
- **London (12:20 PM BD):** Sweeps Asian Low at 1.0856
- **15M Confirmation:** Bullish engulfing at 1.0860
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0862
- **SL:** 1.0848 | **TP:** 1.0910
- **R:R:** 1:3.4
- **Close:** Hit TP at 7:30 PM BD during NY AM
