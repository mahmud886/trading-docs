## What is Multi-Timeframe Analysis?

Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTA) is the practice of analyzing the same instrument across **multiple timeframes** to get a complete picture. The higher timeframe gives you the **direction** (bias), and the lower timeframe gives you the **entry** (precision).

<Callout type="info" title="The Top-Down Approach">
Professional traders NEVER trade from a single timeframe. They always start from the highest relevant timeframe for direction, then drill down for entry. This single habit separates profitable traders from struggling ones.
</Callout>

---

## The Three-Timeframe Framework

| Role | Timeframe Examples | Purpose |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| **HTF (Bias)** | Weekly / Daily | Overall trend direction |
| **MTF (Structure)** | 4H / 1H | Setup identification, key levels |
| **LTF (Entry)** | 15M / 5M | Precise entry and stop placement |

### Common Timeframe Combinations:

| Trading Style | HTF | MTF | LTF |
|--------------|-----|-----|-----|
| **Position** | Monthly | Weekly | Daily |
| **Swing** | Weekly | Daily | 4H |
| **Day Trade** | Daily | 4H/1H | 15M/5M |
| **Scalp** | 4H | 1H | 5M/1M |

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: HTF — Determine the Bias
- Is the market in an uptrend, downtrend, or range?
- Where are the major S/R levels?
- Is price in premium or discount?

### Step 2: MTF — Identify the Setup
- Where is the nearest key level within the HTF structure?
- Is there a pullback to a key zone?
- Is internal structure aligning with the HTF bias?

### Step 3: LTF — Execute the Entry
- Wait for a CHOCH or BOS in the direction of the HTF bias
- Identify the order block or FVG for precise entry
- Place your stop and target

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD Day Trade
- **Daily (HTF)**: Uptrend, HH/HL structure. Price pulling back toward HL
- **4H (MTF)**: Price at 1.0880 — a 4H demand zone inside the Daily HL area
- **15M (LTF)**: Internal CHOCH above 1.0888. 15M bullish OB at 1.0882
- **Entry**: Buy 1.0883 | **Stop**: 1.0868 | **Target**: 1.0945
- **R:R**: 4.1:1 | **Result**: +62 pips ✅

### Example 2: Gold Swing Trade
- **Weekly**: Clear uptrend. Price in discount zone near $2,350
- **Daily**: Bullish engulfing candle at $2,355 demand zone
- **4H**: CHOCH bullish, OB at $2,360
- **Entry**: $2,362 | **Stop**: $2,340 | **Target**: $2,430
- **R:R**: 3.1:1 | **Result**: +$68 ✅

### Example 3: NAS100 Scalp (BD Time)
- **4H (HTF)**: Bullish structure since London session
- **1H (MTF)**: Price pulling back to 1H demand at 18,280
- **5M (LTF)**: At 9:00 PM BD, 5M CHOCH bullish at 18,285
- **Entry**: 18,288 | **Stop**: 18,270 | **Target**: 18,330
- **Result**: +42 points, 2.3R ✅

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="MTA Errors">

**❌ Trading against the HTF** — If Daily is bearish, don't take 15M longs. You're fighting the bigger force.

**❌ Too many timeframes** — 3 timeframes maximum. More creates confusion and conflicting signals.

**❌ Not starting from HTF** — Always begin analysis from the highest timeframe. Never start from LTF.

**❌ LTF noise overriding HTF** — A 5M signal doesn't override a Daily structure break. HTF always wins.

</Callout>
