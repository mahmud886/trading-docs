## What is Price Action Scalping?

Scalping is taking **quick trades on lower timeframes** (1M-5M) for small but frequent profits. Price Action scalping uses candlestick patterns and structure at micro levels without indicators.

---

## Scalping Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Timeframes** | Entry: 1M/5M, Bias: 15M/1H |
| **Session** | London-NY overlap only (5-8 PM BD) |
| **Spread** | Must be tight (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, Gold) |
| **R:R** | Minimum 1:2 per trade |
| **Max Trades/Day** | 3-5 quality setups |
| **Focus** | ONE pair per session |

---

## Scalping Strategies

### Strategy 1: Session Sweep Scalp
1. Mark Asian range on 15M
2. Wait for London to sweep one side
3. Enter on 1M reversal candle (pin bar, engulfing)
4. Target: Opposing side of Asian range
5. SL: Beyond the sweep | Typical R:R: 1:2-3

### Strategy 2: Key Level Bounce Scalp
1. Mark key 1H S/R levels
2. Wait for price to reach the level
3. Enter on 5M confirmation candle
4. Target: 10-15 pips | SL: 5-7 pips
5. Must be during active session

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Scalping Errors">

**❌ Overtrading** — 3-5 quality trades, not 30 random ones

**❌ Wide spreads** — Spreads eat scalp profits. Only trade tight-spread pairs

**❌ No HTF bias** — Even scalps need 15M/1H direction alignment

**❌ Emotional decisions** — Scalping requires MECHANICAL execution

</Callout>
