## What is AMD?

AMD stands for **Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution** — ICT's model for how price is delivered in every timeframe. Also known as **Power of 3 (PO3)**, this is the single most important concept in ICT methodology.

Every candle, every session, every trading day follows the same three-phase cycle.

<Callout type="info" title="Core Principle">
  "If you understand Power of 3, you understand why price does what it does. Every move starts with accumulation, uses
  manipulation to create liquidity, and ends with distribution." — ICT
</Callout>

---

## The Three Phases

### Phase 1: Accumulation

- Smart money **builds positions** during low-volatility consolidation
- Typically occurs during Asian session (6:00 AM - 12:00 PM BD)
- Price creates a tight range with clear highs and lows
- Retail traders see "nothing happening" — institutions are loading
- Creates the SSL and BSL that will be targeted

### Phase 2: Manipulation (Judas Swing)

- **False move** that traps retail traders on the wrong side
- Sweeps liquidity on ONE side of the accumulation range
- Happens at London Open (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM BD) typically
- Retail breakout traders enter → get stopped out
- This provides counterparty liquidity for institutional entries

### Phase 3: Distribution

- The **TRUE directional move** of the session/day
- Price moves aggressively in the opposite direction of manipulation
- Targets the liquidity on the OTHER side of the range
- Usually occurs during NY session (5:00 PM - 10:00 PM BD)
- Largest range expansion of the day

## AMD on Different Timeframes

### Daily Candle AMD

For a bullish day:

- **Open** → price moves DOWN first (manipulation — Judas Swing)
- **Low of Day** formed during London open
- **Rally** through the day (distribution)
- **Close** near the high

For a bearish day:

- **Open** → price moves UP first (manipulation)
- **High of Day** formed during London open
- **Sell-off** through the day (distribution)
- **Close** near the low

### Session AMD

- **Asian Session** = Accumulation (range forming)
- **London Session** = Manipulation (false breakout of Asian range)
- **New York Session** = Distribution (true move)

### Weekly AMD

- **Monday** = Accumulation (weekly range begins)
- **Tuesday** = Manipulation (weekly Judas Swing)
- **Wednesday-Thursday** = Distribution (weekly expansion)
- **Friday** = Profit-taking / reversal risk

## How to Trade AMD

### Step 1: Identify Accumulation

- Mark the Asian session range (high and low)
- Note equal highs/lows within the range
- This is your "launch pad"

### Step 2: Wait for Manipulation

- London open sweeps one side of the range
- Look for liquidity grab (wick beyond range)
- Wait for MSS on 5M/15M confirming the false move

### Step 3: Enter on Distribution

- After MSS confirms, enter at OB/FVG
- Target: opposing side's liquidity
- Stop: beyond the manipulation extreme

## PO3 Candle Analysis

Every candle has an open, high, low, and close that reveals the PO3:

### Bullish PO3 Candle

```
Close ←── Distribution (top)
  |
  |     Body = Distribution range
  |
Open  ←── Accumulation point
  |
Low   ←── Manipulation (wick down)
```

### Bearish PO3 Candle

```
High  ←── Manipulation (wick up)
  |
Open  ←── Accumulation point
  |
  |     Body = Distribution range
  |
Close ←── Distribution (bottom)
```

### Using Daily PO3 for Bias

- If today's Daily candle opens and goes DOWN first → expect bullish day
- If today's Daily candle opens and goes UP first → expect bearish day
- Check previous Daily candle's PO3 pattern for continuation clues

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="AMD Errors to Avoid">

**❌ Trading the manipulation** — Don't buy the breakout. The manipulation is the TRAP.

**❌ No patience for accumulation** — If there's no clear range, there's no AMD setup yet.

**❌ Entering before MSS** — Wait for structure shift confirmation after the sweep.

**❌ Ignoring session timing** — AMD works because of session transitions. Time is critical.

**❌ Fighting the distribution** — Once distribution begins, don't counter-trade it.

</Callout>
