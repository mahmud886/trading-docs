## What Is Pinning?

**Pinning** is when the price of a stock, ETF or index **settles very close to a strike with heavy open interest** at expiry. During the final day the ranges shrink and price keeps returning to that strike. After expiry, those options no longer exist, and price is free to move.

## Why It Happens

Pinning comes from **dealer hedging when dealers are long gamma** at that strike, usually because clients **sold** options there (covered calls, put-writing, short straddles):

- Price rises above the strike → dealer delta grows → they **sell** to rebalance.
- Price falls below it → they **buy**.
- Gamma at the at-the-money strike is largest on the last day, so the effect is strongest then.

If dealers are **short** gamma at that strike, their hedging pushes price **away** from it instead. That's why pinning doesn't happen at every big strike. See [Options Flow](/en/ict/options-flow-signals) and [Gamma Exposure](/en/ict/gamma-exposure).

## When It's Most Likely

| Condition                                   | Pinning more likely?               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Heavy open interest at one strike near spot | Yes                                |
| Estimated GEX positive                      | Yes                                |
| Quiet news calendar on expiry day           | Yes                                |
| Big US data or an earnings release that day | No                                 |
| Dealers short gamma (negative GEX)          | No: expect moves away from strikes |

## The Expiry Calendar (Bangladesh Time)

| Expiry                       | When                               | Stops trading (BD, US summer / winter) |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Daily (0DTE) SPX/SPY/QQQ** | Every trading day                  | 2:00 AM / 3:00 AM BD (next day)        |
| **Weekly**                   | Fridays                            | 2:00 AM / 3:00 AM BD Saturday          |
| **Monthly (OpEx)**           | Third Friday of the month          | 2:00 AM / 3:00 AM BD Saturday          |
| **Quarterly**                | Third Friday of Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec | Same; often the largest open interest  |

The US close is 4 PM New York: 2 AM BD in US summer, 3 AM BD in US winter.

## Trading Around Expiry

| Phase            | What to expect                     | Approach                                               |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Days before**  | Price may drift toward big strikes | Note the biggest strikes and GEX                       |
| **Expiry day**   | Narrow ranges near the strike      | Avoid breakout trades; fade small moves at range edges |
| **After expiry** | The "pin" is released              | Watch for a break; trade it with an [MSS](/en/ict/mss) |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: 'predictable price behavior'">
  The original Options Flow page said expiry dates "create predictable price behavior". Pinning is a tendency on some
  expiries, mostly in heavily traded US equity products. Many expiries show no pin at all, especially with news on the
  day. There's no comparable public effect for spot FX.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                           | Fix                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Expecting every big strike to pin | Only long-gamma strikes tend to            |
| Ignoring news on expiry day       | Data or earnings override pinning          |
| Using last week's open interest   | Check the current expiry's numbers         |
| Holding a breakout into the close | Pin risk: take profit before the last hour |

## Practice

1. For 8 Friday expiries on SPY, note the strike with the biggest open interest on Thursday.
2. Record Friday's close and its distance from that strike.
3. Note Monday's range compared with Friday's. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
