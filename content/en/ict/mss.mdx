## What is Market Structure Shift?

A Market Structure Shift (MSS) is a **confirmed change in market direction** evidenced by price breaking a key swing point with displacement. In ICT methodology, MSS is your green light to look for entries in the new direction.

<Callout type="info" title="MSS = Permission to Trade">
  MSS gives you "permission" to trade in the new direction. Without MSS, you're guessing. With MSS, you have
  institutional confirmation.
</Callout>

---

## MSS vs BOS vs CHOCH

| Concept   | Definition                            | Significance         |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **BOS**   | Break of structure in trend direction | Trend continuation   |
| **CHOCH** | First break against the trend         | Early warning signal |
| **MSS**   | Break against trend WITH displacement | Confirmed reversal   |

### Key Difference: Displacement

- CHOCH can happen with a weak break (just a wick)
- MSS requires **displacement** — strong, aggressive candle(s) closing beyond the level
- Displacement = large-bodied candle, minimal wicks, creating FVG

## How to Identify MSS

### Bullish MSS (Downtrend → Uptrend)

1. Price is making Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
2. Price takes out a key SSL (sweep)
3. Price then DISPLACES above the most recent Lower High
4. This break is with a strong bullish candle creating an FVG
5. ✅ MSS confirmed — look for buy setups

### Bearish MSS (Uptrend → Downtrend)

1. Price is making Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
2. Price takes out a key BSL (sweep)
3. Price then DISPLACES below the most recent Higher Low
4. This break is with a strong bearish candle creating an FVG
5. ✅ MSS confirmed — look for sell setups

## MSS Entry Model

### The Complete Setup

1. **HTF Bias** — Daily/4H direction determined
2. **Liquidity Sweep** — Price sweeps BSL or SSL
3. **MSS Forms** — Displacement through swing point on 5M/15M
4. **FVG Created** — The displacement creates a Fair Value Gap
5. **Enter at FVG** — Set limit order at the FVG (CE for precision)
6. **Stop Loss** — Beyond the liquidity sweep extreme
7. **Target** — Opposing liquidity pool

## Real Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD Bullish MSS

- **Context:** 4H bearish but Daily in discount (expecting reversal)
- **LTF:** 15M downtrend with LH at 1.0875
- **Sweep:** Price takes SSL at 1.0845 during London KZ
- **MSS:** Aggressive bullish candle breaks above 1.0875 LH
- **FVG created** at 1.0868-1.0878
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0873 (CE of FVG)
- **Stop:** 1.0842 (below sweep)
- **Target:** 1.0930
- **R:R:** 1:5.5

### Example 2: NAS100 Bearish MSS

- **Context:** Daily premium, expecting sell-off
- **LTF:** 5M uptrend with HL at 17,880
- **Sweep:** BSL taken at 17,950 during NY AM
- **MSS:** Strong bearish candle breaks below 17,880
- **FVG** at 17,890-17,870
- **Entry:** Sell at 17,880
- **Stop:** 17,955
- **Target:** 17,750
- **R:R:** 1:1.7

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="MSS Errors">

**❌ Calling every break an MSS** — MSS requires DISPLACEMENT, not just a wick beyond the level

**❌ No liquidity sweep first** — Valid MSS should follow a liquidity grab

**❌ MSS against HTF trend** — LTF MSS against Daily bias = low probability

**❌ Entering before MSS completes** — Wait for the candle to CLOSE beyond the swing point

**❌ Confusion with CHOCH** — CHOCH is the first sign, MSS is the confirmation with displacement

</Callout>
