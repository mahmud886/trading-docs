## What is the 2022 Mentorship?

The 2022 ICT Mentorship is considered the **most comprehensive and refined** version of ICT's teachings. Released throughout 2022, it introduced or refined several key concepts including IPDA theory, algorithmic price delivery, refined execution models, and advanced time/price relationships.

<Callout type="info" title="The Definitive Version">
Many ICT students consider the 2022 Mentorship the definitive version of the methodology. It corrects misinterpretations from earlier teachings and adds the time-based precision that was previously missing.
</Callout>

## Key Concepts Introduced/Refined

### 1. Algorithmic Price Delivery
- Price is NOT random — it's controlled by the IPDA
- The algorithm follows a specific sequence: liquidity → imbalance → liquidity
- Every move can be understood through this lens
- 20/40/60-day IPDA data ranges provide targets

### 2. Refined PO3 (Power of 3)
- Daily candle analysis: Open → Manipulation → Close
- PO3 applied to every timeframe, not just daily
- "What did the previous candle tell me about the NEXT candle?"

### 3. Institutional Order Entry Drill (IOED)
- Step-by-step institutional entry framework
- HTF → LTF → Kill Zone → Sweep → MSS → Entry
- Designed as a repeatable "drill" for consistent execution

### 4. AMD at Every Scale
- AMD isn't just daily — it's fractal
- 1M candles have AMD, 5M candles, weekly candles
- Understanding AMD at the candle level improves execution

### 5. ICT Macros
Refined specific time windows for displacement:
- 9:50-10:10 AM EST (most important)
- 10:50-11:10 AM EST
- 1:50-2:10 PM EST
- 3:15-3:45 PM EST (last hour)

## The 2022 Trading Model

### The Complete Entry Model
1. **Top-Down Analysis** — Weekly → Daily → 4H bias
2. **Session Selection** — Choose London or NY AM Kill Zone
3. **Wait for Judas Swing** — Manipulation at session open
4. **Confirm MSS** — LTF structure shift with displacement on 5M
5. **Identify PD Array** — FVG or OB in OTE zone
6. **Enter** — Limit order at CE of FVG or OB
7. **Stop** — Beyond the manipulation extreme
8. **Target** — Opposing liquidity pool using IPDA ranges

### Example Application
- **Weekly:** EUR/USD in discount, bullish bias
- **Daily:** Bullish PO3 candle yesterday
- **4H:** Making higher lows
- **Session:** London Kill Zone (12:00 PM BD)
- **Judas Swing:** Asian low swept at 12:20 PM BD
- **MSS:** 5M bullish at 12:35 PM BD
- **PD Array:** 5M bullish FVG CE at 1.0855
- **Entry:** Buy 1.0855, stop 1.0840, target 1.0930
- **R:R:** 1:5
