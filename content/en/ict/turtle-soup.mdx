## What is Turtle Soup?

Turtle Soup is a **reversal strategy** that fades false breakouts beyond significant highs and lows. Named ironically after the Turtle Trading system (which traded breakouts), ICT's Turtle Soup does the OPPOSITE — it enters against the breakout after a liquidity sweep.

<Callout type="info" title="The Name">
  The Turtle Traders became famous for buying breakouts. ICT named this strategy "Turtle Soup" because it's designed to
  fade and profit from the very breakouts that trap Turtle-style traders.
</Callout>

---

## How Turtle Soup Works

### The Setup

1. Price approaches a **significant high or low** (PDH/PDL, equal H/L, old swing point)
2. Price **breaks beyond** the level — triggering breakout traders' entries and stop orders
3. Price quickly **reverses back** below/above the level
4. This reversal = your entry signal
5. The breakout was FALSE — it was a liquidity sweep

### Bullish Turtle Soup (Fading a false breakdown)

- Price breaks below a key low (sweeps SSL)
- Then reverses back above the low
- Enter LONG with stop below the sweep extreme

### Bearish Turtle Soup (Fading a false breakout)

- Price breaks above a key high (sweeps BSL)
- Then reverses back below the high
- Enter SHORT with stop above the sweep extreme

---

## Identification Rules

1. **Significant level** — Must be a notable high/low (not random swings)
2. **Clean sweep** — Price should trade at least a few pips beyond the level
3. **Quick reversal** — Ideally within 1-3 candles on entry TF
4. **Displacement** — Reversal should be aggressive (not gradual drift)
5. **Kill Zone timing** — Turtle Soup during Kill Zones = highest probability
6. **HTF alignment** — The false breakout should be against the HTF bias

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Bearish Turtle Soup — EUR/USD

- **Key Level:** Previous Week High at 1.0950
- **Setup:** During NY AM (6:30 PM BD), price spikes to 1.0958
- **Breakout traders** enter long, stops are triggered
- **Reversal:** Strong bearish candle closes back below 1.0945
- **Entry:** Sell at 1.0945
- **Stop:** 1.0960 (above the sweep)
- **Target:** 1.0880 (SSL below)
- **R:R:** 1:4.3

### Example 2: Bullish Turtle Soup — Gold

- **Key Level:** Equal lows at 2,020 (three touches)
- **Setup:** London KZ (1:00 PM BD), price drops to 2,015
- **Breakdown traders** sell, stops hit
- **Reversal:** Bullish displacement, MSS on 5M
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,022
- **Stop:** 2,013
- **Target:** 2,055
- **R:R:** 1:3.7

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Turtle Soup Errors">

**❌ Fading every breakout** — Only fade when HTF bias opposes the breakout direction

**❌ Entering before reversal confirms** — Wait for displacement back inside the range

**❌ Tiny stops** — Stop must be beyond the sweep extreme to survive

**❌ Ignoring time** — Turtle Soup outside Kill Zones is low probability

</Callout>
