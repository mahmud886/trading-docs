## Why London Open Matters

The London session open is the **most important time of the trading day** in ICT methodology. This is when:

- The daily manipulation (Judas Swing) typically occurs
- The daily HIGH or LOW is most often formed
- The Asian session range gets swept
- Maximum volatility in EUR, GBP, and Gold

<Callout type="info" title="Statistical Edge">
  Studies show that the daily high or low is formed during the London Kill Zone over 60% of the time. This makes London
  Open the highest-probability session for reversal setups.
</Callout>

## London Open Trading Model

### Step 1: Mark the Asian Range

Before London opens, mark:

- Asian Session High (ASH)
- Asian Session Low (ASL)
- Asian midpoint (50%)
- Any equal highs/lows within the range

### Step 2: Determine Daily Bias

- Is today expected to be bullish or bearish?
- Use previous day PO3, HTF structure, draw on liquidity

### Step 3: Wait for the Judas Swing

**If bullish bias:**

- Expect London to sweep BELOW the Asian Low first
- This is the manipulation — taking SSL
- Wait for price to sweep ASL, then reverse

**If bearish bias:**

- Expect London to sweep ABOVE the Asian High first
- Taking BSL above the range
- Wait for price to sweep ASH, then reverse

### Step 4: Confirm and Enter

- MSS on 5M/15M after the sweep
- Enter at OB/FVG created by the displacement
- Stop beyond the sweep extreme
- Target opposing side liquidity

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Bullish London Open — EUR/USD

- **Asian Range:** 1.0860 - 1.0878
- **Daily Bias:** Bullish
- **12:20 PM BD:** Price drops below ASL to 1.0854 (Judas Swing down)
- **12:35 PM BD:** 5M bullish MSS — displacement above recent LH
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0862 (bullish OB)
- **Stop:** 1.0850 | **Target:** 1.0920
- **R:R:** 1:4.8

### Example 2: Bearish London Open — Gold

- **Asian Range:** 2,032 - 2,040
- **Daily Bias:** Bearish (premium, weekly LH forming)
- **12:45 PM BD:** Spike above ASH to 2,044 (Judas Swing up/sweeps BSL)
- **1:00 PM BD:** Bearish MSS on 5M
- **Entry:** Sell at 2,039 (bearish FVG)
- **Stop:** 2,045 | **Target:** 2,010
- **R:R:** 1:4.8

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="London Open Errors">

**❌ Trading the Asian breakout** — The first move out of Asia is usually the TRAP

**❌ No bias before London** — You need to know direction BEFORE London opens

**❌ Entering during the Judas Swing** — Wait for it to complete and MSS to form

**❌ Trading after 5:00 AM EST** — London KZ ends at 5 AM. Late entries are lower probability

**❌ Ignoring Asian range** — The range boundaries are your key reference points

</Callout>
