## What is Daily Bias?

Daily Bias is determining the **most probable direction** price will move today. In ICT methodology, every trade begins with establishing bias — without it, you're gambling. Bias tells you to ONLY look for buys or ONLY look for sells.

<Callout type="info" title="Rule #1">
  "Never take a trade without a clear daily bias. If you can't determine bias, stand aside." — ICT
</Callout>

---

## How to Determine Daily Bias

### Method 1: Previous Day PO3 Candle

Read the previous Daily candle's Power of 3 structure:

**Bullish bias if yesterday:**

- Opened, went DOWN first (long lower wick), then closed near HIGH
- This shows manipulation down → distribution up
- Today's bias = BULLISH (continuation expected)

**Bearish bias if yesterday:**

- Opened, went UP first (long upper wick), then closed near LOW
- This shows manipulation up → distribution down
- Today's bias = BEARISH (continuation expected)

### Method 2: HTF Structure

- **Daily in discount** (below 50% of range) + making HL → Bullish bias
- **Daily in premium** (above 50% of range) + making LH → Bearish bias
- Use Weekly/Monthly for overall context

### Method 3: Draw on Liquidity

Ask: "What is the nearest untouched liquidity pool?"

- If nearest BSL is the target → Bullish bias
- If nearest SSL is the target → Bearish bias
- HTF FVGs and OBs also act as "draws"

### Method 4: Day of Week Context

| Day           | Tendency                                |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Monday**    | Range-building, accumulation            |
| **Tuesday**   | Judas day — often manipulation/reversal |
| **Wednesday** | Highest probability expansion           |
| **Thursday**  | Continuation or reversal setup          |
| **Friday**    | Profit-taking, reduced probability      |

## Daily Bias Checklist

Before trading each day, answer these 5 questions:

1. ✅ **What is the Weekly bias?** (premium or discount?)
2. ✅ **What did yesterday's candle say?** (PO3 reading)
3. ✅ **Where is the draw on liquidity?** (BSL or SSL target?)
4. ✅ **Are there HTF FVGs/OBs to target?** (magnetic levels)
5. ✅ **What day is it?** (Tuesday/Wednesday = best days)

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Bullish Bias Day on EUR/USD

- **Weekly:** In discount, making HL
- **Previous Daily:** Opened at 1.0870, dipped to 1.0845, closed at 1.0895 (bullish PO3)
- **Draw:** BSL at 1.0950 (previous week high untouched)
- **Day:** Wednesday (expansion day)
- **Bias:** ✅ BULLISH — only look for buys today
- **Result:** London sweeps Asian low → buy setup → price reaches 1.0940

### Example 2: Bearish Bias Day on Gold

- **Weekly:** In premium, LH forming
- **Previous Daily:** Opened 2,040, spiked to 2,055, closed 2,028 (bearish PO3)
- **Draw:** SSL at 2,000 (round number + previous swing low)
- **Day:** Thursday
- **Bias:** ✅ BEARISH — only look for sells today
- **Result:** NY AM sweep of London high → sell → drops to 2,008

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Bias Errors">

**❌ Changing bias mid-day** — Once set, stick with it unless extreme invalidation occurs

**❌ No bias = still trading** — If you can't determine bias, DON'T TRADE

**❌ LTF overrides HTF** — A 5M setup against Daily bias is a losing trade

**❌ Ignoring day of week** — Monday and Friday have different character than Tuesday-Thursday

**❌ Chasing after the move** — Bias is set BEFORE the session, not after price has already moved

</Callout>
