## What Is Daily Bias?

**Daily bias** is your answer, before the session opens, to one question: _is price more likely to trade up to buy-side liquidity or down to sell-side liquidity today?_

It doesn't predict the day. It filters what you look for: with a bullish bias you only take buy setups, and a bearish [Judas swing](/en/ict/judas-swing) becomes your entry rather than a reason to sell.

<Callout type="info" title="No bias, no trade">
  The original lesson put this in quotation marks as an ICT quote, without a source. The idea stands on its own: if the
  checks below disagree with each other, there is no bias, and the best trade is none.
</Callout>

---

## The Four Inputs

### 1. Higher-timeframe structure and premium/discount

Mark the daily or weekly [dealing range](/en/ict/dealing-range) and its 50% level.

| Price is…                  | Structure           | Leans   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- | ------- |
| In **discount** (< 50%)    | Higher lows forming | Bullish |
| In **premium** (> 50%)     | Lower highs forming | Bearish |
| Either, but no clear swing | Ranging             | No bias |

### 2. Yesterday's candle

| Yesterday                                  | Reading                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Went down first, then closed near the high | Bullish PO3 → leans bullish |
| Went up first, then closed near the low    | Bearish PO3 → leans bearish |
| Small body, wicks both sides               | Indecision → weak input     |

This is one input, not a verdict. See [Power of 3](/en/ict/power-of-3).

### 3. Draw on liquidity

Ask _where is the nearest obvious untouched liquidity?_ A previous day's or week's high or low, equal highs or lows, or an unfilled higher-timeframe [FVG](/en/ict/fvg). If the nearest draw is above, lean bullish; below, lean bearish. See [Liquidity](/en/ict/liquidity).

### 4. Day-of-week context

| Day           | Tendency                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Monday**    | Often range-building                       |
| **Tuesday**   | Often makes the week's high or low         |
| **Wednesday** | Often expansion, or the week's reversal    |
| **Thursday**  | Continuation, or a reversal around US news |
| **Friday**    | Profit-taking; quieter after NY morning    |

These are tendencies only. See [Weekly Profiles](/en/ict/weekly-profiles).

### A bearish example (no chart)

Gold: weekly in premium with a lower high forming. Yesterday opened 2,040, spiked to 2,055 and closed 2,028 (bearish PO3). The draw is sell-side at 2,000, a round number under a previous swing low. Bias: bearish, sells only. The plan: sell a NY sweep of the London high.

## The Pre-Session Checklist

1. Where is price in the weekly and daily dealing range: premium or discount?
2. What did yesterday's candle do?
3. Where is the nearest untouched liquidity?
4. Is there high-impact news today? (See the [economic calendar](/en/tools/economic-calendar).)
5. Do at least three answers point the same way? If not, no bias.

---

## When to Drop the Bias

The original said "stick with it unless extreme invalidation occurs" without saying what that is. Use concrete rules:

| Bias    | It's invalid when…                                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bullish | Price closes a 1H candle below the manipulation low, or the draw is taken out the other way first   |
| Bearish | Price closes a 1H candle above the manipulation high, or sell-side is skipped and buy-side is taken |

Once invalid, stop trading that direction for the day. Don't flip and chase.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                | Fix                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Setting the bias after the move        | Set it before London opens                      |
| Trading with no bias                   | No agreement → no trade                         |
| Taking 5m setups against the bias      | Low-timeframe setups only in the bias direction |
| Treating day-of-week as a rule         | It's the weakest input                          |
| Holding a bias that's been invalidated | Use the invalidation rules above                |

## Practice

1. For 20 days, write your bias and the reasons before London opens.
2. At the end of each day, note whether the draw was reached and which input was wrong when it wasn't.
3. After 20 days, see which input was most useful on your pair. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

| HTF structure + PD | Discount with higher lows, or premium with lower highs? | Strongest |
