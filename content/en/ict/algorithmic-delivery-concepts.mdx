## What is Algorithmic Price Delivery?

ICT teaches that financial markets are NOT driven by random supply and demand but by an **Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA)** that systematically delivers price between liquidity pools and imbalances.

<Callout type="info" title="The Algorithm">
"The market is a price delivery algorithm. It doesn't 'go up' or 'go down.' It delivers price from one liquidity pool to another, filling imbalances along the way." — ICT
</Callout>

---

## How the Algorithm Works

### The Algorithm's Objectives (in order)
1. **Seek liquidity** — BSL or SSL (stop orders, pending orders)
2. **Fill imbalances** — FVGs, liquidity voids, volume imbalances
3. **Deliver price efficiently** — Move between premium and discount
4. **Create new liquidity** — Form new swing points that attract new orders

### The Price Delivery Sequence
```
Liquidity Pool A → Imbalance Fill → Liquidity Pool B → Imbalance Fill → ...
```

Price bounces between liquidity pools, filling FVGs along the way. This is NOT random — it follows the IPDA data ranges.

---

## IPDA Data Ranges

### The Lookback Periods
- **20 trading days** — Short-term institutional reference
- **40 trading days** — Medium-term reference  
- **60 trading days** — Quarterly institutional reference

### How to Use
1. Count back 20/40/60 candles on the Daily chart
2. Mark the HIGH and LOW of each lookback period
3. Any UNTOUCHED high or low = algorithm target
4. The nearest untouched level = most likely next target

### Example
- 20-day range: 1.0800 - 1.0950
- Current price: 1.0870
- 20-day low (1.0800) = untouched SSL → potential target
- 20-day high (1.0950) = untouched BSL → potential target
- Daily bias determines which one price seeks first

## Algorithmic Patterns

### Pattern 1: Liquidity → FVG → Liquidity
- Algorithm sweeps SSL → fills overhead FVG → continues to BSL

### Pattern 2: FVG Mitigation
- Algorithm returns to previously created FVGs to fill them
- This is why "price always comes back to the gap"

### Pattern 3: Time-Scheduled Delivery
- Algorithm activates at specific Kill Zone times
- Macro windows (:50-:10) show algorithmic displacement
- Sessions transition = algorithmic recalibration

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Algorithm Errors">

**❌ Thinking markets are random** — The algorithm is predictable once you understand its objectives

**❌ Ignoring IPDA ranges** — These are the algorithm's reference levels

**❌ Fighting the algorithm** — Align with its objectives, don't counter-trade them

**❌ Overcomplicating** — The algorithm does TWO things: seek liquidity and fill imbalances

</Callout>
