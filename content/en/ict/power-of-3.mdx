## What Is Power of 3?

**Power of 3 (PO3)** describes a price swing as three phases:

1. **Accumulation**: price ranges quietly while positions are built.
2. **Manipulation**: a false move out of the range takes liquidity on one side. This is the [Judas swing](/en/ict/judas-swing).
3. **Distribution**: the real move, in the opposite direction, toward the liquidity on the other side.

The same shape can be read on a session, a day, a week or a month. ICT also calls it **AMD**. See [AMD](/en/ict/amd).

<Callout type="info" title="A model, not a law">
  The original lesson said "every significant price move" follows PO3. It doesn't. Ranging and news-driven days often
  show no clean three phases. Use PO3 to frame days that do show it, and stand aside on days that don't.
</Callout>

## How the Day Prints as One Candle

![Power of 3 on a bullish and a bearish day: intraday candles and the daily candle they make](/images/ict/power-of-3/po3-daily-candle-dark.webp)

| Day         | Order of events                               | The daily candle                            |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Bullish** | Open → **low first** → high → close near high | Long lower wick below the open, closes high |
| **Bearish** | Open → **high first** → low → close near low  | Long upper wick above the open, closes low  |

<Callout type="tip" title="The practical rule">
  On a bullish day, look for buys **below the daily open**: that's where the manipulation usually prints. On a bearish
  day, look for sells **above the daily open**.
</Callout>

## The Session Template (Bangladesh Time)

| Phase            | Session          | BD — US summer | BD — US winter | What to do                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Accumulation** | Asian            | 6 AM – 12 PM   | 7 AM – 1 PM    | Mark the range high and low             |
| **Manipulation** | London kill zone | 12 PM – 3 PM   | 1 PM – 4 PM    | Wait for the false move; don't chase it |
| **Distribution** | NY AM kill zone  | 5 PM – 8 PM    | 6 PM – 9 PM    | Manage the trade; NY often extends it   |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected times">
  The original gave the Asian session as "12 AM – 9 AM BD", London as "2 – 5 PM BD" and NY as "7:30 PM – 12 AM BD".
  These don't match the [Kill Zones](/en/ict/ict-kill-zones) lesson. The table above uses the same windows as the rest
  of the site. Manipulation can also come at the NY open instead of London, in which case NY is both phases two and
  three.
</Callout>

## How to Trade It

1. **Daily bias.** Use the higher timeframe structure, the [dealing range](/en/ict/dealing-range) and the [draw on liquidity](/en/ict/liquidity). See [Daily Bias](/en/ict/daily-bias).
2. **Mark the Asian range.** This is the accumulation. Both of its edges hold stops.
3. **Wait for the manipulation.** With a bullish bias, expect a run below the Asian low first. With a bearish bias, expect a run above the Asian high first.
4. **Confirm.** A close back inside the range, then an [MSS](/en/ict/mss) or CHOCH with displacement on 5m–15m.
5. **Enter** at the [order block](/en/smc/order-blocks) or [FVG](/en/ict/fvg) the reversal leaves. Put the stop beyond the manipulation extreme.
6. **Target** the opposite side of the range and the liquidity beyond it.

1. **Accumulation:** the Asian range is **1.0850 – 1.0870**. Bias is bullish (daily uptrend, price in discount).
2. **Manipulation:** London opens at 12 PM BD and by **12:30 PM BD** runs below the Asian low to **1.0838**.
3. **CHOCH:** at **1:30 PM BD** a 21-pip displacement closes above the 1.0856 lower high.
4. **Entry:** the last down-close candle before it is the bullish OB. Price returns at 2:30 PM BD. Buy **1.0845**.
5. **Distribution:** New York takes price through the Asian high toward 1.0920.

| Level   | Price       | Note                         |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0845      | Bullish OB                   |
| Stop    | 1.0830      | Below the 1.0838 sweep low   |
| Target  | 1.0920      | Buy-side liquidity           |
| **R:R** | **1 : 5.0** | Risk 15 pips, reward 75 pips |

## PO3 on Higher Timeframes

The same three phases appear on weekly and monthly candles, but the timing is a tendency, not a schedule:

| Timeframe   | Accumulation            | Manipulation                                       | Distribution      |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **Weekly**  | Monday                  | Often Monday–Wednesday sets the week's high or low | Rest of the week  |
| **Monthly** | First days of the month | Often within the first one or two weeks            | Rest of the month |

See [Weekly Profiles](/en/ict/weekly-profiles) and [Monthly Profiles](/en/ict/monthly-profiles).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Trading the first break of the Asian range | It's usually the manipulation; wait for the reversal |
| No daily bias                              | Without one you can't tell which side is the trap    |
| Entering before the MSS                    | Wait for the close back inside and the shift         |
| Forcing PO3 on a range day                 | No clean manipulation → no trade                     |
| Summer times in winter                     | Add one hour from early November to mid-March        |

## FAQ

**How do I know which way the manipulation will go?** From the bias. Bullish bias → manipulation down first. Bearish bias → manipulation up first.

**Does it work every day?** No. Many days range or are driven by news. Count how often it appears on your pair (see Practice).

**Which timeframe?** Read the phases on 15m–1H; refine the entry on 5m–15m.
