## What are ICT Kill Zones?

Kill Zones are **specific time windows** during the trading day when the highest-probability institutional setups occur. ICT teaches that TIME determines WHEN price will move — Kill Zones are the framework for this time-based approach.

<Callout type="info" title="Time > Price">
  "You can have the perfect setup in the wrong time window and it will fail. Kill Zones tell you WHEN the algorithm is
  most active." — ICT
</Callout>

## The Four Kill Zones

### 1. London Kill Zone

| Detail        | Info                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **EST Time**  | 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM                     |
| **BD Time**   | 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM                    |
| **Character** | Manipulation phase — Judas Swing      |
| **Best For**  | Reversal setups, sweeping Asian range |
| **Pairs**     | EUR/USD, GBP/USD, EUR/GBP, Gold       |

London Kill Zone is where the **manipulation** phase of AMD occurs. The Judas Swing typically forms here, sweeping one side of the Asian range to create the daily high or low.

### 2. New York AM Kill Zone

| Detail        | Info                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **EST Time**  | 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM                 |
| **BD Time**   | 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM                  |
| **Character** | Distribution phase — true move     |
| **Best For**  | Continuation after London reversal |
| **Pairs**     | All major pairs, Gold, Indices     |

NY AM is where the **distribution** move accelerates. If London created the manipulation, NY AM delivers the expansion.

### 3. NY Lunch Kill Zone (AVOID)

| Detail        | Info                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **EST Time**  | 12:00 PM - 1:30 PM                       |
| **BD Time**   | 10:00 PM - 11:30 PM                      |
| **Character** | Low probability — choppy                 |
| **Best For**  | Nothing — step away                      |
| **Warning**   | False signals, low volume, erratic moves |

### 4. NY PM Kill Zone

| Detail        | Info                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **EST Time**  | 1:30 PM - 4:00 PM                     |
| **BD Time**   | 11:30 PM - 2:00 AM                    |
| **Character** | Continuation or reversal              |
| **Best For**  | Trend continuation, closing positions |
| **Pairs**     | USD pairs, indices                    |

## Kill Zone Trading Strategy

### London Kill Zone Setup

1. **Mark Asian Range** — High and Low between 6 AM - 12 PM BD
2. **Wait for sweep** — Price takes Asian High or Low
3. **Confirm MSS** — 5M/15M structure shift after sweep
4. **Enter** — At OB/FVG in the displacement
5. **Target** — Opposing liquidity (if Asian Low swept → target BSL)

### NY AM Kill Zone Setup

1. **Assess London result** — Did London create the daily high/low?
2. **Look for continuation** — If London reversed, NY AM continues that direction
3. **Enter on pullback** — Use FVG/OB from the London displacement
4. **Target** — Major liquidity pools (PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL)

## Real Examples

### Example 1: London KZ — EUR/USD

- **Asian Range:** 1.0870 - 1.0885 (6 AM - 12 PM BD)
- **12:15 PM BD:** Price sweeps Asian low at 1.0867
- **12:30 PM BD:** 5M bullish MSS forms
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0872 (bullish OB)
- **SL:** 1.0864 | **TP:** 1.0910
- **R:R:** 1:4.75

### Example 2: NY AM KZ — Gold

- **London created** daily HOD at 2,048 (manipulation up, then reversed)
- **5:30 PM BD:** Price pulls back to 15M FVG
- **Entry:** Sell at 2,040 (bearish FVG CE)
- **SL:** 2,049 | **TP:** 2,010
- **R:R:** 1:3.3

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Kill Zone Errors">

**❌ Trading outside Kill Zones** — Random entries = random results

**❌ Trading NY Lunch** — This is a low-probability trap zone

**❌ Not converting to your timezone** — Know YOUR local Kill Zone times

**❌ Trading all Kill Zones** — Master ONE Kill Zone before adding more

**❌ Ignoring macros** — The specific :50-:10 windows are where displacement occurs

</Callout>
