## What is the Unicorn Model?

The Unicorn Model is one of ICT's **advanced execution models** — a specific pattern where a Breaker Block and a Fair Value Gap overlap at the same price level, creating an extremely high-probability entry zone.

<Callout type="info" title="Why 'Unicorn'?">
It's called "Unicorn" because this confluence is rare — like spotting a unicorn. But when it appears, it's one of the highest-probability setups in ICT methodology.
</Callout>

---

## Unicorn Model Structure

### Bullish Unicorn Model
1. Price is in an uptrend making HH/HL
2. A bearish Order Block forms (potential resistance)
3. Price breaks DOWN through this OB — it FAILS → becomes a **Breaker Block**
4. The displacement through the OB creates a **FVG**
5. Price continues down, then reverses
6. When price returns UP to the Breaker Block zone, the FVG overlaps with the Breaker
7. This overlap = **Unicorn Model entry zone**
8. Enter LONG at this confluence

### Bearish Unicorn Model
1. Price is in a downtrend making LH/LL
2. A bullish Order Block forms (potential support)
3. Price breaks UP through this OB — it FAILS → becomes a **Breaker Block**
4. The displacement creates a **FVG**
5. Price continues up, then reverses
6. When price returns DOWN to the Breaker Block zone, the FVG overlaps
7. Overlap = Unicorn entry zone
8. Enter SHORT at this confluence

---

## Why Unicorn Model Is Powerful

1. **Double institutional confluence** — Breaker + FVG at same level
2. **Failed OB = trapped traders** — Previous OB traders are wrong-sided
3. **FVG = imbalance** — Algorithm needs to rebalance
4. **Low risk** — Tight stop beyond the FVG/Breaker zone
5. **High reward** — Often produces 1:5+ R:R

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Bullish Unicorn — EUR/USD
- **Context:** Daily bullish, price pulled back to discount
- **4H bearish OB** at 1.0870-1.0880 was acting as resistance
- **Price breaks through** 1.0880 with displacement → OB becomes Breaker
- **FVG created** at 1.0875-1.0885 during the displacement
- Price rallies to 1.0930, then retraces
- **Unicorn Zone:** 1.0875-1.0885 (Breaker + FVG overlap)
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0880
- **Stop:** 1.0868
- **Target:** 1.0960
- **R:R:** 1:6.7

### Example 2: Bearish Unicorn — NAS100
- **4H bullish OB** at 17,900-17,930 acting as support
- **Price crashes through** with displacement → Breaker Block formed
- **FVG** at 17,890-17,920
- Price drops to 17,750, then retraces
- **Unicorn Zone:** 17,890-17,920
- **Entry:** Sell at 17,905
- **Stop:** 17,935
- **Target:** 17,700
- **R:R:** 1:6.8

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Unicorn Model Errors">

**❌ Calling non-overlapping zones a Unicorn** — Breaker and FVG MUST overlap

**❌ Using on low timeframes only** — HTF Unicorn Models are far more reliable

**❌ No HTF bias alignment** — Even Unicorn Models need directional context

**❌ Forcing the pattern** — It's rare for a reason. Don't see Unicorns everywhere

</Callout>
