## What is OTE?

The Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) is a **retracement zone between the 62% and 79% Fibonacci levels** of an expansion leg. ICT teaches that after a displacement move, price will retrace into this zone before continuing — this is where institutions add to their positions.

<Callout type="info" title="Sweet Spot">
The OTE zone represents the "sweet spot" where risk is lowest and reward is highest. The 70.5% level (midpoint of 62-79%) is considered the ideal entry point.
</Callout>

---

## How to Draw OTE

### For Bullish OTE
1. Identify the **swing low** (start of expansion)
2. Identify the **swing high** (end of expansion / displacement high)
3. Draw Fibonacci from swing LOW to swing HIGH
4. OTE zone = 62% to 79% retracement
5. Wait for price to retrace INTO this zone

### For Bearish OTE
1. Identify the **swing high** (start of expansion)
2. Identify the **swing low** (end of expansion / displacement low)
3. Draw Fibonacci from swing HIGH to swing LOW
4. OTE zone = 62% to 79% retracement
5. Wait for price to retrace INTO this zone

### Key Fibonacci Levels
| Level | Significance |
|-------|-------------|
| 62% | OTE zone begins |
| 70.5% | Optimal entry (midpoint) |
| 79% | OTE zone ends — last chance entry |
| 100% | Full retracement — setup invalid |

## OTE + PD Array Confluence

The highest probability entries occur when OTE aligns with a PD Array:

### OTE + FVG
- Price retraces into OTE zone AND hits an FVG = highest probability
- The CE of the FVG within OTE = sniper entry

### OTE + Order Block
- An OB sitting within the OTE zone = institutional confluence
- Enter at the OB within OTE for the best risk:reward

### OTE + Breaker Block
- Failed OB that now acts as support/resistance in OTE zone
- Strong reversal point

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD Bullish OTE
- **Expansion:** Price rallies from 1.0820 (swing low) to 1.0880 (swing high)
- **OTE Zone:** 1.0843 (62%) to 1.0833 (79%)
- **Sweet Spot:** 1.0838 (70.5%)
- **What happened:** Price retraces to 1.0836, hits a 15M bullish OB
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0836
- **Stop:** 1.0818 (below swing low)
- **Target:** 1.0920 (BSL above)
- **R:R:** 1:4.7
- **Time (BD):** Setup at 6:15 PM (NY AM Kill Zone)

### Example 2: Gold Bearish OTE
- **Expansion:** Price drops from 2,048 to 2,020
- **OTE Zone:** 2,037 (62%) to 2,042 (79%)
- **Sweet Spot:** 2,040 (70.5%)
- **Confluence:** Bearish FVG sits at 2,038 - 2,042
- **Entry:** Sell at 2,040 (OTE + FVG CE)
- **Stop:** 2,049
- **Target:** 2,005
- **R:R:** 1:3.9

## OTE Rules

1. **Only after displacement** — OTE works after a strong impulsive move
2. **Must have MSS/CHOCH** — Structure must confirm direction before looking for OTE
3. **Kill Zone timing** — OTE retracements during Kill Zones are highest probability
4. **HTF alignment** — OTE must agree with Daily/4H bias
5. **One chance** — If price blows through the 79% level, the setup is invalid

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="OTE Errors">

**❌ Using OTE without displacement** — No strong move = no valid OTE zone

**❌ Entering at 50%** — The 50% level is NOT OTE. Wait for 62-79%

**❌ No PD Array confluence** — OTE alone is a zone, not an exact entry. Need OB/FVG within it

**❌ Ignoring invalidation** — If price closes beyond 100%, the OTE is dead

**❌ Wrong swing points** — Use the correct expansion leg, not random highs/lows

</Callout>
