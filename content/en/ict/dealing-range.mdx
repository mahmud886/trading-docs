## What is a Dealing Range?

A Dealing Range is the **price range between the most recent significant swing high and swing low**. It defines the "playing field" where price is currently operating. The equilibrium (50%) of this range divides price into premium (sell zone) and discount (buy zone).

<Callout type="info" title="Framework">
The Dealing Range gives you context for EVERY trade. Are you buying in discount? Selling in premium? Without knowing the dealing range, you can't determine premium vs discount.
</Callout>

---

## How to Identify the Dealing Range

### Steps
1. Find the most recent **significant swing high** (BSL target above)
2. Find the most recent **significant swing low** (SSL target below)
3. The range between these = your Dealing Range
4. Mark the **50% equilibrium** — this divides premium and discount
5. Update when either extreme is broken

### Timeframe Hierarchy
- **Monthly Dealing Range** — Macro context (position trades)
- **Weekly Dealing Range** — Swing trade context
- **Daily Dealing Range** — Day trade context (most used)
- **4H Dealing Range** — Intraday refinement

## Premium vs Discount Zones

### Premium Zone (Above 50%)
- Area above equilibrium
- **Sell setups only** — institutions sell in premium
- Look for bearish PD Arrays (OBs, SIBI FVGs)
- Retail traders buy here (wrong side)

### Discount Zone (Below 50%)
- Area below equilibrium
- **Buy setups only** — institutions buy in discount
- Look for bullish PD Arrays (OBs, BISI FVGs)
- Retail traders sell here (wrong side)

### Equilibrium (50%)
- The midpoint = "fair value"
- CE of FVGs often aligns with equilibrium
- Price tends to gravitate toward equilibrium
- Not ideal for entries — it's the neutral zone

## Trading the Dealing Range

### Bullish Setup (Buy in Discount)
1. Price is below 50% of dealing range → DISCOUNT
2. SSL sits below → potential sweep target
3. Wait for SSL sweep during Kill Zone
4. MSS confirms bullish reversal
5. Enter at bullish OB/FVG in discount
6. Target: BSL at the top of the dealing range

### Bearish Setup (Sell in Premium)
1. Price is above 50% of dealing range → PREMIUM
2. BSL sits above → potential sweep target
3. Wait for BSL sweep during Kill Zone
4. MSS confirms bearish reversal
5. Enter at bearish OB/FVG in premium
6. Target: SSL at the bottom of the dealing range

## Real Example: EUR/USD

- **Dealing Range:** Swing Low 1.0800 — Swing High 1.0950
- **Equilibrium:** 1.0875 (50%)
- **Current Price:** 1.0830 → DISCOUNT zone
- **Action:** Only look for BUYS
- **Setup:** SSL sweep at 1.0810 during London KZ → MSS → buy at OB 1.0820
- **Target:** 1.0950 (BSL at top of dealing range)
- **R:R:** 1:13

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Dealing Range Errors">

**❌ Buying in premium** — Always check premium/discount before entering

**❌ Wrong swing points** — Use SIGNIFICANT swings, not minor fluctuations

**❌ Not updating** — When price breaks the range extreme, reset the dealing range

**❌ Single timeframe** — Always know the HTF dealing range context first

</Callout>
