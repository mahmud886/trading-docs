## What are Market Maker Models?

Market Maker Models describe the **complete cycle of price delivery** by the interbank algorithm. They map out how price moves from accumulation through manipulation to distribution — giving you a roadmap of where you are in the cycle and what comes next.

<Callout type="info" title="The Algorithm's Pattern">
The algorithm repeats the same pattern over and over: sweep one side, displace to the other side, retrace, then expand. Market Maker Models help you identify which stage you're in.
</Callout>

---

## Market Maker Buy Model (MMBM)

The blueprint for a bullish cycle:

### Stages
1. **Original Consolidation** — Price ranges at the low (accumulation)
2. **Smart Money Reversal** — SSL sweep + MSS (manipulation)
3. **First displacement leg up** — Creates OBs and FVGs
4. **Re-accumulation** — Pullback to OB/FVG (this is your entry)
5. **Second leg up** — Expansion toward BSL targets
6. **Distribution** — Price reaches premium, starts topping out

### Entry Point
The ideal entry is during **Stage 4 (re-accumulation)**:
- Price pulls back to the FVG/OB from Stage 3
- This pullback often reaches OTE (62-79% retracement)
- Enter with stop below the Stage 2 manipulation low

---

## Market Maker Sell Model (MMSM)

The blueprint for a bearish cycle:

### Stages
1. **Original Consolidation** — Price ranges at the high (distribution)
2. **Smart Money Reversal** — BSL sweep + MSS (manipulation)
3. **First displacement leg down** — Creates bearish OBs and FVGs
4. **Re-distribution** — Pullback into bearish OB/FVG (entry)
5. **Second leg down** — Expansion toward SSL targets
6. **Accumulation** — Price reaches discount, starts bottoming

### Entry Point
Enter during **Stage 4 (re-distribution)**:
- Price retraces into the bearish FVG/OB from Stage 3
- Often reaches OTE zone
- Stop above the Stage 2 manipulation high

## Real Examples

### Example 1: MMBM on EUR/USD (Daily)
- **Stage 1:** 1.0800-1.0830 consolidation for 5 days
- **Stage 2:** SSL sweep to 1.0785, then MSS bullish
- **Stage 3:** Displacement to 1.0880, creating FVG at 1.0840-1.0855
- **Stage 4:** Pullback to 1.0848 (FVG CE + OTE zone) — **ENTRY BUY**
- **Stage 5:** Expansion to 1.0970
- **R:R from entry:** 1:8+

### Example 2: MMSM on NAS100 (4H)
- **Stage 1:** Consolidation at 18,000-18,100
- **Stage 2:** BSL sweep to 18,150, bearish MSS
- **Stage 3:** Displacement to 17,900 creating OBs
- **Stage 4:** Retracement to 18,020 (bearish OB) — **ENTRY SELL**
- **Stage 5:** Expansion to 17,500
- **R:R:** 1:7

## Identifying Your Position in the Model

| If Price Is... | You're in Stage... | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Ranging at support | 1 (Consolidation) | Wait for sweep |
| Sweeping lows with displacement up | 2-3 (Reversal) | Prepare for buy |
| Pulling back to FVG/OB after up move | 4 (Re-accumulation) | ENTER BUY |
| Expanding toward highs | 5 (Expansion) | Hold / take partials |
| Ranging at resistance | 6 (Distribution) | Prepare for MMSM |

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Market Maker Model Errors">

**❌ Entering at Stage 1** — Consolidation can last days/weeks. Wait for the sweep

**❌ Chasing Stage 3** — The first displacement is not the entry. Wait for Stage 4 pullback

**❌ Wrong timeframe** — Use Daily/4H for the model structure, LTF for execution

**❌ Forgetting it's a cycle** — MMBM ends → MMSM begins. It's continuous

</Callout>
