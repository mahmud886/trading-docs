## What is the Venom Model?

The Venom Model is an **advanced ICT execution model** focused on entering immediately after a liquidity sweep when price creates aggressive displacement. The "venom" refers to the deadly precision of the entry — strike fast, one shot.

<Callout type="info" title="Strike Once">
The Venom Model is about catching the exact moment smart money deploys capital after sweeping liquidity. It's a surgical entry with very tight stops and explosive R:R potential.
</Callout>

---

## Venom Model Setup

### The Sequence
1. **Liquidity Target** — Identify BSL or SSL that will be swept
2. **Sweep** — Price takes out the liquidity during a Kill Zone
3. **Displacement** — Aggressive reversal candle(s) immediately after the sweep
4. **FVG + OB** — The displacement creates both an FVG and an OB
5. **Entry** — Enter at the FVG (CE) or OB on the FIRST retracement
6. **Stop** — Tight, beyond the sweep extreme
7. **Target** — Opposing liquidity pool

### What Makes It "Venom"
- The entry is on the FIRST candle that retraces into the FVG/OB
- No waiting for multiple retracements
- The speed and aggression of the displacement = the "venom"
- One decisive entry, no scaling in

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Bullish Venom — Gold
- **SSL Target:** Equal lows at 2,020
- **Kill Zone:** London (1:15 PM BD)
- **Sweep:** Price drops to 2,016 (sweeps SSL)
- **Displacement:** Massive bullish candle 2,016 → 2,032 (FVG at 2,022-2,028, OB at 2,020)
- **1M Entry:** Price dips to 2,025 (FVG CE) — enter buy
- **Stop:** 2,015 (below sweep)
- **Target:** 2,060 (BSL above)
- **R:R:** 1:3.5
- **Key:** First touch of the FVG = Venom entry

### Example 2: Bearish Venom — EUR/USD
- **BSL Target:** PDH at 1.0940
- **Kill Zone:** NY AM (6:00 PM BD)
- **Sweep:** Price spikes to 1.0948 (sweeps BSL)
- **Displacement:** 3 bearish candles create FVG at 1.0935-1.0928
- **Entry:** Sell at 1.0932 (CE of FVG)
- **Stop:** 1.0950
- **Target:** 1.0870
- **R:R:** 1:3.4

## Venom vs Other Models

| Model | Key Feature | Timing |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Venom** | Immediate post-sweep entry | First retracement |
| **Unicorn** | Breaker + FVG overlap | Delayed retracement |
| **Silver Bullet** | Time-specific FVG | Within SB window |
| **Standard OB** | Any OB entry | Multiple retracement OK |

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Venom Model Errors">

**❌ Entering during the sweep** — Wait for displacement AFTER the sweep, not during

**❌ No Kill Zone timing** — Venom without session context = unreliable

**❌ Chasing the displacement** — Enter on the RETRACEMENT to FVG, not mid-displacement

**❌ Too wide stops** — Venom is about TIGHT stops. If your stop needs to be huge, it's not Venom

</Callout>
