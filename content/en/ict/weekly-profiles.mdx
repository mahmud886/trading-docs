## What are Weekly Profiles?

Weekly Profiles describe the **typical behavior pattern** of each day within a trading week. ICT teaches that the algorithm follows predictable weekly cycles — certain days tend to create manipulation while others create expansion.

<Callout type="info" title="Weekly Roadmap">
  Understanding weekly profiles gives you a roadmap before the week starts. You know WHICH days to trade aggressively
  and which to sit out.
</Callout>

---

## Day-by-Day Profiles

### Monday — The Accumulation Day

- Creates the week's initial range
- Often low-volatility, consolidation
- Price may probe one direction's liquidity
- **Action:** Mark Monday's range (high/low) — this is the week's "Asian range"

### Tuesday — The Judas Day

- The weekly manipulation day
- Often creates the week's high OR low (the false move)
- Retail traders get trapped on the wrong side
- **Action:** Watch for reversal setups, especially if Monday was range-bound

### Wednesday — The Expansion Day

- Highest probability expansion day of the week
- If Tuesday created the manipulation, Wednesday distributes
- Largest daily range often occurs on Wednesday
- **Action:** Trade aggressively with bias — best day for R:R

### Thursday — The Continuation/Reversal Day

- Can continue Wednesday's direction or start reversal
- If weekly target hit on Wednesday, Thursday may reverse
- If not hit yet, Thursday continues
- **Action:** Manage open positions or take continuation trades

### Friday — The Profit-Taking Day

- Institutions close positions before weekend
- Reduced reliability on setups
- Can see reversals as profits are taken
- **Action:** Reduce risk, close positions, avoid new entries after 12 PM EST

## Weekly Profile Templates

### Template 1: Classic AMD Week

| Day | Role         | Example                       |
| --- | ------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Mon | Accumulation | Range: 1.0850-1.0870          |
| Tue | Manipulation | Sweeps above to 1.0885 (trap) |
| Wed | Distribution | Drops to 1.0790 (expansion)   |
| Thu | Continuation | Reaches 1.0760 (target)       |
| Fri | Profit-take  | Bounces to 1.0790             |

### Template 2: Tuesday Reversal Week

- Monday ranges
- Tuesday creates weekly LOW (Judas swing down)
- Wednesday-Thursday rally to weekly HIGH
- Friday consolidates

### Template 3: Wednesday Reversal Week

- Monday-Tuesday trend in one direction
- Wednesday creates the extreme and reverses
- Thursday-Friday deliver the opposite direction

## Real Example: EUR/USD Week

- **Monday:** Opens 1.0900, ranges 1.0890-1.0915 (accumulation)
- **Tuesday:** Drops to 1.0870 sweeping SSL (manipulation down)
- **Tuesday PM:** MSS bullish on 4H at 1.0880
- **Wednesday:** Expansion — rallies to 1.0960 (weekly target BSL)
- **Thursday:** Retraces to 1.0930, holds
- **Friday:** Closes at 1.0945

**Key Learning:** Tuesday's low WAS the entry. Wednesday delivered the reward.

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Weekly Profile Errors">

**❌ Trading Monday aggressively** — Monday is for observation, not execution

**❌ Following Tuesday's direction** — Tuesday CREATES the trap. Don't fall for it

**❌ Ignoring Friday risk** — Reduced liquidity + profit-taking = unreliable signals

**❌ Not marking weekly levels** — PWH/PWL, Monday range are critical references

</Callout>
