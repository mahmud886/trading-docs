## What Are Weekly Profiles?

A **weekly profile** is a template for how a trading week tends to unfold: which day usually builds the range, which day makes the week's high or low, and which days carry the expansion.

It's the [AMD](/en/ict/amd) cycle on a weekly scale. The useful question isn't "what does Wednesday do?" but **"has the week's high or low already formed?"**. Once you think it has, you only look for trades away from it.

<Callout type="info" title="Tendencies, not rules">
  The original said "the algorithm follows predictable weekly cycles". Weekly tendencies are real enough to plan with,
  but they change with news weeks, holidays and month-end. Plan with them; don't bet on them.
</Callout>

## Day by Day

| Day           | Usual character                                                                    | What to do                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Monday**    | Builds the week's opening range; often quiet                                       | Mark Monday's high and low; observe            |
| **Tuesday**   | Often runs one side of Monday's range and makes the week's high or low             | Look for the reversal after an MSS             |
| **Wednesday** | Often the expansion day, or the mid-week reversal                                  | Trade with the week's direction                |
| **Thursday**  | Continues, or reverses if the weekly target is already hit; US data often moves it | Manage trades; new entries only with structure |
| **Friday**    | Profit-taking; can retrace the week's move                                         | Reduce risk; avoid new entries after NY lunch  |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: Friday cut-off time">
  The original said "avoid new entries after 12 PM EST". In Bangladesh that is **10 PM BD** in US summer and **11 PM
  BD** in US winter (New York noon).
</Callout>

## Three Weekly Templates

![Three weekly templates: classic AMD week, Tuesday-low week, Wednesday-reversal week](/images/ict/weekly-profiles/weekly-profile-templates-dark.png)

| Template                    | The trap                                          | The rest of the week                           |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Classic AMD week**        | Tuesday runs above Monday and fails               | Wednesday–Thursday expand down; Friday bounces |
| **Tuesday-low week**        | Tuesday runs below Monday and fails               | Wednesday–Thursday rally; Friday consolidates  |
| **Wednesday-reversal week** | Monday–Tuesday trend, Wednesday makes the extreme | Thursday–Friday deliver the other way          |

The original's classic AMD example in numbers: Monday ranges 1.0850 – 1.0870, Tuesday sweeps to 1.0885, Wednesday drops to 1.0790, Thursday reaches 1.0760, Friday bounces to 1.0790.

## Planning the Week

1. **Sunday/Monday:** set the weekly bias from the weekly and daily dealing range and the draw on liquidity (previous week's high or low, monthly levels).
2. **Mark** the previous week's high and low and Monday's range.
3. **Tuesday–Wednesday:** wait for a run of Monday's range against your bias and an MSS on 1H–4H.
4. **Once the week's extreme looks in,** trade only away from it, toward the draw.
5. **Thursday–Friday:** manage, take profit near the draw, and keep high-impact US data in mind.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                          | Fix                                                          |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trading Monday aggressively      | Monday is mostly for marking the range                       |
| Following Tuesday's break        | Against your bias, it's often the trap                       |
| Ignoring Friday risk             | Reduce size; profit-taking can retrace the week              |
| Treating weekdays as fixed rules | News weeks and holidays change the profile                   |
| Not marking weekly levels        | Previous week high/low and Monday's range are the references |

## Practice

1. For 10 weeks, record which day made the week's high and which made the low.
2. Note which template each week fitted, or "none".
3. Compare with your weekly bias at the start of each week. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Day       | Usual role                    | Your job            |
| --------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Monday    | Opening range                 | Mark it             |
| Tuesday   | Week's high or low (the trap) | Wait for the MSS    |
| Wednesday | Expansion or reversal         | Trade with the week |
| Thursday  | Continuation or reversal      | Manage              |
| Friday    | Profit-taking                 | Reduce risk         |
