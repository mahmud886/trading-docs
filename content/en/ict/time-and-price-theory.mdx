## What is Time & Price Theory?

ICT's Time & Price Theory establishes that **TIME is the senior element** in trading. While most traders obsess over price levels, ICT teaches that understanding WHEN the algorithm will move is more valuable than knowing WHERE it will go.

<Callout type="info" title="Time > Price">
"If you know the time, you can figure out the price. But if you only know the price, you can't figure out the time. Time is senior to price." — ICT
</Callout>

## The IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm)

### What is IPDA?
The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm is the system that controls price delivery in financial markets. It operates on:

- **20-day cycle** — The IPDA looks back 20 trading days for liquidity targets
- **40-day cycle** — Medium-term institutional reference
- **60-day cycle** — Quarterly institutional perspective

### IPDA Data Ranges
To find where price is heading, look back 20/40/60 days:
- Mark the highs and lows of these lookback periods
- These become the algorithm's liquidity targets
- The algorithm delivers price to these levels within the current cycle

## Key Time Concepts

### Macro Times
Specific minutes where displacement occurs:
| Macro | EST | BD Time |
|-------|-----|---------|
| London Macro | 1:33-4:00 AM | 11:33 AM - 2:00 PM |
| AM Macro 1 | 9:50-10:10 AM | 7:50-8:10 PM |
| AM Macro 2 | 10:50-11:10 AM | 8:50-9:10 PM |
| PM Macro | 1:50-2:10 PM | 11:50 PM-12:10 AM |
| Last Hour | 3:15-3:45 PM | 1:15-1:45 AM |

### Quarterly Shifts
Markets shift character at quarterly boundaries:
- **Q1** (Jan-Mar): Often sets the yearly low
- **Q2** (Apr-Jun): Expansion from Q1's range
- **Q3** (Jul-Sep): Reversal or continuation
- **Q4** (Oct-Dec): Year-end institutional positioning

### Day of Week Profiles
| Day | Profile |
|-----|---------|
| Monday | Accumulation / range setting |
| Tuesday | Manipulation / Judas day |
| Wednesday | Distribution / maximum expansion |
| Thursday | Continuation or reversal |
| Friday | Profit-taking / reduced reliability |

## Applying Time & Price Together

### The Complete Framework
1. **Monthly/Weekly** → Which liquidity pool is the target? (IPDA cycle)
2. **Daily** → What is today's bias? (PO3 candle + day of week)
3. **Session** → Which Kill Zone? (time of day)
4. **Macro** → Which :50-:10 window? (exact entry timing)
5. **Entry** → PD Array within the macro window

### Example: Full Time-Price Analysis
- **IPDA 20-day range:** Low at 1.0780, High at 1.0960 (untouched BSL)
- **Day:** Wednesday (expansion day)
- **Bias:** Bullish (yesterday was bullish PO3)
- **Session:** NY AM Kill Zone (5:00-8:00 PM BD)
- **Macro:** 9:50-10:10 AM EST (7:50-8:10 PM BD)
- **Action:** At 7:55 PM BD, displacement forms bullish FVG → ENTER BUY
- **Target:** BSL at 1.0960

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Time & Price Errors">

**❌ Ignoring time completely** — Most retail traders only look at price levels

**❌ Not knowing macro times** — These are the algorithm's "scheduled" moves

**❌ Trading Friday like Wednesday** — Each day has different character

**❌ Ignoring IPDA cycles** — The 20/40/60 day lookback shows you the algorithm's targets

</Callout>
