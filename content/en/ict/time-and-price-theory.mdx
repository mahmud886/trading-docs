## What Is Time and Price?

ICT's central claim is that **time matters as much as price**. A good level is only half a plan: price reacts at a level far more often when it gets there **during an active window**, such as a kill zone, than in a quiet hour.

So the framework pairs two questions:

- **Where?** The liquidity targets from the IPDA look-back ranges and the higher-timeframe PD arrays.
- **When?** The kill zones, Silver Bullet hours and macros.

<Callout type="info" title="Removed: the 'time is senior' quote">
  The original opened with an unsourced ICT quote. The idea it expresses, that timing filters out many bad setups, is
  testable on your own charts; you don't need to take it on authority.
</Callout>

## Where: IPDA Look-Back Ranges

ICT describes price delivery through what he calls the **IPDA** (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm). Whatever you think of the label, the practical tool is simple: the highs and lows of the last **20, 40 and 60 trading days** are the obvious liquidity for the coming weeks. See [Monthly Profiles](/en/ict/monthly-profiles) for a chart and how to use them.

### Macros

**Macros** are short windows, mostly 20–30 minutes, in which ICT says a displacement often starts. The commonly cited list:

| Macro     | New York time       | BD — US summer   | BD — US winter   |
| --------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| London 1  | 2:33 – 3:00 AM      | 12:33 – 1:00 PM  | 1:33 – 2:00 PM   |
| London 2  | 4:03 – 4:30 AM      | 2:03 – 2:30 PM   | 3:03 – 3:30 PM   |
| NY AM 1   | 8:50 – 9:10 AM      | 6:50 – 7:10 PM   | 7:50 – 8:10 PM   |
| NY AM 2   | 9:50 – 10:10 AM     | 7:50 – 8:10 PM   | 8:50 – 9:10 PM   |
| NY AM 3   | 10:50 – 11:10 AM    | 8:50 – 9:10 PM   | 9:50 – 10:10 PM  |
| NY lunch  | 11:50 AM – 12:10 PM | 9:50 – 10:10 PM  | 10:50 – 11:10 PM |
| NY PM     | 1:10 – 1:40 PM      | 11:10 – 11:40 PM | 12:10 – 12:40 AM |
| Last hour | 3:15 – 3:45 PM      | 1:15 – 1:45 AM   | 2:15 – 2:45 AM   |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected macro list">
  The original gave a "London macro" of 1:33 – 4:00 AM and a PM macro of 1:50 – 2:10 PM, labelled EST, with BD times
  that only fit US summer. The table above uses the commonly cited ICT list and gives BD times for both seasons. Sources
  differ by a few minutes; treat the windows as approximate.

## The Top-Down Framework

| Step | Timeframe        | Question                                       | Tool                                                            |
| ---- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Monthly / weekly | Which liquidity is the target?                 | 20/40/60-day ranges, [Weekly Profiles](/en/ict/weekly-profiles) |
| 2    | Daily            | Bullish or bearish today?                      | [Daily Bias](/en/ict/daily-bias)                                |
| 3    | Session          | Which kill zone?                               | Kill zones                                                      |
| 4    | Minutes          | Is there a displacement in a macro or SB hour? | Macros, Silver Bullet                                           |
| 5    | Entry            | Which PD array did it leave?                   | [FVG](/en/ict/fvg), [order block](/en/smc/order-blocks)         |

### Example (from the original)

| Step        | Reading                                                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| IPDA 20-day | Low 1.0780, high **1.0960** (untouched buy-side)                   |
| Day         | Wednesday                                                          |
| Bias        | Bullish (yesterday was a bullish PO3 candle)                       |
| Session     | NY AM kill zone, 5 – 8 PM BD (summer)                              |
| Macro       | 7:50 – 8:10 PM BD (summer)                                         |
| Trigger     | 7:55 PM BD: a bullish displacement leaves an FVG → buy the retrace |
| Target      | 1.0960                                                             |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                      | Fix                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Trading levels at any hour   | Wait for the level and the window to line up    |
| Treating macros as signals   | A macro is when to look; you still need a setup |
| Using summer times in winter | Add one hour from early November to mid-March   |
| Ignoring the bigger picture  | Time without a target is just a clock           |

## Practice

1. Print the time map in your local time and keep it next to your screen.
2. For 20 sessions, note when the session's main displacement started and whether it fell in a macro.
3. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
