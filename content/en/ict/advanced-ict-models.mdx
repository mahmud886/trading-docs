## Overview

The advanced ICT models are **entry recipes**. They all use the same ingredients: a bias, a liquidity sweep, a displacement, and a PD array to enter from. What differs is the **trigger** and **where you get in**.

You don't need all of them. Pick one, test it on your pair and session, and trade only that until your journal says it works for you.

## Model Comparison

| Model                                                  | Trigger                                      | Entry                        | Stop                     | Timeframe |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------- |
| **[Silver Bullet](/en/ict/silver-bullet)**             | Displacement inside a fixed hour             | First FVG of the window (CE) | Beyond the swing it left | 1m–5m     |
| **[Unicorn](/en/ict/unicorn-model)**                   | Breaker forms after a sweep, with an FVG     | Breaker ∩ FVG overlap        | Beyond the breaker       | 5m–1H     |
| **[Venom](/en/ict/venom-model)**                       | Kill-zone sweep + immediate displacement     | First touch of the FVG CE    | Beyond the sweep         | 1m–5m     |
| **[Market Maker Models](/en/ict/market-maker-models)** | Smart-money reversal on the HTF              | Pullback after the first leg | Beyond the reversal      | 4H–daily  |
| **[Turtle Soup](/en/ict/turtle-soup)**                 | False break of an obvious high/low           | After the close back inside  | Beyond the false break   | 5m–1H     |
| **[2022 model](/en/ict/2022-mentorship-concepts)**     | Sweep + MSS with displacement in a kill zone | FVG CE, ideally in OTE       | Beyond the sweep         | 5m–15m    |

## Building a Simple Plan

1. **One session.** London (12 – 3 PM BD, US summer) or NY AM (5 – 8 PM BD). Add one hour in US winter.
2. **One model.** The Silver Bullet and the 2022 model have the clearest rules, so they're the easiest to test.
3. **Backtest.** Replay at least 50 sessions. Mark every setup, taken or not.
4. **Measure.** Win rate, average R, and the worst losing streak. The original's "target 60%+ win rate with 1:3+" is not a realistic starting goal; a positive average R across a large sample is what matters.
5. **Trade small.** Forward-test on a demo or with minimal size for at least a month.
6. **Then add.** Only after the results hold, add one filter (e.g. [Weekly Profiles](/en/ict/weekly-profiles)) or a second model.

## Combining Models

| Combination                        | How it fits                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Market Maker Model + Unicorn**   | The MMBM gives the swing direction; the Unicorn gives the entry on the pullback |
| **Silver Bullet + Weekly Profile** | Take Silver Bullets only once the week's high or low looks in                   |
| **Turtle Soup + Kill Zone**        | Fade false breaks only during kill zones                                        |
| **Venom + London Open**            | Use the London open's Judas swing as the Venom sweep                            |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                     | Fix                                                |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Model hopping after losses  | Judge a model on 50+ trades, not three             |
| Trading every model at once | One model, one session                             |
| No backtest                 | Test on your pair and session first                |
| Skipping the basics         | Liquidity, MSS, FVG and bias come before any model |
