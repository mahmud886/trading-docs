## Overview of Advanced ICT Models

Advanced ICT models are **specific execution frameworks** that combine core concepts into repeatable trading patterns. Each model has a specific setup, entry trigger, and management approach.

<Callout type="info" title="Choose Your Model">
You don't need to trade ALL models. Master ONE model first, then expand. Most profitable ICT traders use only 1-2 models consistently.
</Callout>

## Building Your Trading System

### Step 1: Choose ONE Kill Zone
- London (12:00-3:00 PM BD) OR NY AM (5:00-8:00 PM BD)

### Step 2: Choose ONE Model
Start with Silver Bullet or 2022 IOED — they're the most structured.

### Step 3: Master the Model
- Backtest 3 months of data in replay mode
- Journal every setup (taken or not)
- Target 60%+ win rate with 1:3+ R:R

### Step 4: Add Confluence
Once the base model is consistent, add time confluence (Macros, day of week profiles).

### Step 5: Scale or Add
After 3 months of consistency, either scale position size or add a second model.

## Combining Models

### Powerful Combinations
- **Silver Bullet + Weekly Profile** — Trade SB only on Wednesday/Thursday
- **Turtle Soup + Kill Zone** — Fade breakouts only during KZ timing
- **MMBM/MMSM + Unicorn** — Identify cycle → enter at Unicorn zone within cycle
- **Venom + London Open** — Post-sweep Venom entries at London open

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Advanced Model Errors">

**❌ Model hopping** — Switching models after losses destroys consistency

**❌ Trading all models simultaneously** — Focus beats breadth

**❌ No backtesting** — Models must be validated on YOUR preferred pairs/sessions

**❌ Ignoring base concepts** — Advanced models REQUIRE mastery of basic ICT concepts first

</Callout>
