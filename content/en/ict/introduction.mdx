## What is ICT Methodology?

ICT (Inner Circle Trader) methodology is a trading framework developed by **Michael J. Huddleston** that reveals how the interbank market-making algorithm delivers price. Unlike retail trading approaches that rely on lagging indicators, ICT focuses on understanding the **institutional logic** behind every market move.

<Callout type="info" title="Core Philosophy">
  "The market is not random. It is an algorithm designed to seek liquidity and deliver price between premium and
  discount arrays." — ICT
</Callout>

## The History of ICT

Michael Huddleston began sharing his methodology on YouTube in 2010. Over the years, he released:

- **2016-2017 Mentorship** — First structured paid mentorship covering PD arrays, Kill Zones, OTE
- **2020 Pandemic Content** — Free YouTube teachings during COVID-19
- **2022 Mentorship** — Most comprehensive revision with algorithmic Price Delivery theory
- **2023-2024 Updates** — Unicorn Model, Venom Model, refined execution concepts

ICT's framework differs from SMC (Smart Money Concepts) — which is a simplified interpretation of ICT ideas by other educators. ICT methodology is more nuanced and time-centric.

## The 5 Pillars of ICT

### Pillar 1: Algorithmic Price Delivery

The market is controlled by an algorithm (IPDA — Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm). This algorithm:

- Seeks liquidity above highs and below lows
- Delivers price to imbalances (FVGs)
- Operates on predictable time schedules
- Rebalances inefficiencies in price

### Pillar 2: Time-Based Trading

ICT is unique in emphasizing TIME over price. Key time concepts:

- **Kill Zones** — Specific hours with highest probability setups
- **Session Opens** — Midnight, London, New York create setups
- **ICT Macro Times** — :50-:10 of specific hours show displacement
- **Seasonal Tendencies** — Monthly and quarterly bias patterns

### Pillar 3: Liquidity Theory

Everything in markets revolves around liquidity:

- **Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)** — Stop losses above swing highs
- **Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)** — Stop losses below swing lows
- **Engineered Liquidity** — Equal highs/lows create obvious targets
- The algorithm ALWAYS seeks the next liquidity pool

### Pillar 4: Premium & Discount Framework

Price operates in two zones relative to equilibrium:

- **Premium (above 50%)** — Ideal for selling
- **Discount (below 50%)** — Ideal for buying
- **Equilibrium (50%)** — CE of FVG, midpoint of ranges
- PD Arrays in premium = sell setups; PD Arrays in discount = buy setups

### Pillar 5: Power of 3 (AMD Cycle)

Every move occurs in three phases:

1. **Accumulation** — Smart money builds position in a range
2. **Manipulation** — False move (Judas Swing) traps retail
3. **Distribution** — True directional move after manipulation

## Key ICT Concepts Overview

### PD Arrays (Premium/Discount Arrays)

Institutional footprints on the chart:

- **Order Blocks (OB)** — Last opposing candle before displacement
- **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)** — 3-candle imbalance
- **Breaker Blocks** — Failed OBs that become support/resistance
- **Mitigation Blocks** — Partially filled OBs
- **Liquidity Voids** — Large single-candle inefficiencies

### Time Concepts

- **Kill Zones** — London (2:00-5:00 AM EST), NY (7:00-10:00 AM EST)
- **Silver Bullet** — 10:00-11:00 AM, 2:00-3:00 PM, 3:00-4:00 AM EST windows
- **ICT Macros** — :50 to :10 of specific hours
- **Power of 3 Candle** — Daily/4H candle structure (open → low → high → close)

### Market Structure

- **Market Structure Shift (MSS)** — Confirmed trend change
- **Change of Character (CHOCH)** — First sign of reversal
- **Displacement** — Strong momentum confirming direction
- **Break of Structure (BOS)** — Continuation signal

## Getting Started with ICT — The Learning Path

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

1. Learn market structure (swing highs/lows, BOS, CHOCH)
2. Understand liquidity (BSL, SSL, equal highs/lows)
3. Master PO3 candle identification on Daily chart
4. Study Kill Zone timing (convert to your timezone)

### Phase 2: Core Concepts (Weeks 5-8)

1. Identify FVGs and Order Blocks
2. Practice PD Array identification in premium/discount
3. Learn daily bias using HTF PO3 analysis
4. Paper trade London and NY Kill Zones only

### Phase 3: Execution (Weeks 9-12)

1. Build a specific model (Silver Bullet, 2022 Model, etc.)
2. Trade one setup per day maximum
3. Journal every setup (win or loss) with screenshots
4. Refine entry timing using 1M/5M confirmation

### Phase 4: Mastery (Months 4+)

1. Combine multiple timeframe confluence
2. Add session-specific models
3. Understand seasonal tendencies
4. Develop your personal trading plan

## Common Beginner Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid These ICT Errors">

**❌ Trying to learn everything at once** — ICT has 50+ concepts. Pick ONE model and master it first.

**❌ Trading all sessions** — Stick to ONE Kill Zone until consistently profitable.

**❌ Ignoring Daily Bias** — Every trade needs HTF directional conviction.

**❌ No patience** — The best ICT setups come 1-2x per day. Wait for proper timing.

**❌ Confusing ICT with SMC** — SMC simplifies ICT. Learn the original source material.

**❌ Backtesting without time** — You must backtest in replay mode to see time-based setups develop.

</Callout>
