## What Is ICT?

**ICT** (Inner Circle Trader) is a trading approach taught by **Michael J. Huddleston**. It reads price through **liquidity** (where stop orders sit), **time** (when big moves usually start) and the **footprints** strong moves leave behind, rather than through indicators.

Smart Money Concepts (**SMC**) grew out of ICT's ideas, taught by other educators with simpler names and rules. This site covers both; the ICT section uses ICT's terms.

<Callout type="info" title="How to read ICT">
  ICT describes markets as an "algorithm" delivering price between liquidity pools. You don't have to accept that
  description to use the tools. Treat every concept here as something to test on your own charts.
</Callout>

## How ICT Differs From Indicator Trading

| Aspect      | Indicator-based approach   | ICT approach                                         |
| ----------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Signals** | RSI, MACD, moving averages | Price, liquidity and time                            |
| **Entries** | Breakouts, crossovers      | Retracements into OBs/FVGs after a sweep             |
| **Stop**    | Below a recent swing       | Beyond the sweep (manipulation) extreme              |
| **Target**  | Fixed pips or R multiple   | The opposite liquidity pool                          |
| **Timing**  | Any time                   | Kill zones                                           |
| **Bias**    | Indicator direction        | Higher-timeframe structure and the draw on liquidity |

## Session Times in Bangladesh

ICT quotes New York time. Bangladesh is **10 hours ahead in US summer** (mid-March to early November) and **11 hours ahead in US winter**.

| Window               | New York time   | BD — US summer   | BD — US winter   |
| -------------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Asian range (ICT)    | 8 PM – 12 AM    | 6 AM – 10 AM     | 7 AM – 11 AM     |
| **London kill zone** | 2 AM – 5 AM     | **12 PM – 3 PM** | **1 PM – 4 PM**  |
| **NY AM kill zone**  | 7 AM – 10 AM    | **5 PM – 8 PM**  | **6 PM – 9 PM**  |
| NY AM Silver Bullet  | 10 AM – 11 AM   | 8 PM – 9 PM      | 9 PM – 10 PM     |
| NY lunch (avoid)     | 12 PM – 1:30 PM | 10 PM – 11:30 PM | 11 PM – 12:30 AM |
| NY PM                | 1:30 PM – 4 PM  | 11:30 PM – 2 AM  | 12:30 AM – 3 AM  |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: EST labels">
  The original labelled these times "EST" but its BD times only match US **summer** time (EDT). Use the winter column
  from early November to mid-March. Full detail: [Kill Zones](/en/ict/ict-kill-zones) and [Time and
  Price](/en/ict/time-and-price-theory).
</Callout>

| Weeks    | Focus                   | Lessons                                                                                                            |

## Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake                        | Fix                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Learning every concept at once | Follow the path; one model first                |
| Trading every session          | One kill zone until your results are consistent |
| No daily bias                  | Every trade needs a higher-timeframe direction  |
| Over-trading                   | Many days have no clean setup                   |
| Backtesting on static charts   | Use bar replay so you see setups form in time   |
| Treating ICT claims as facts   | Test them; keep what works on your pair         |
