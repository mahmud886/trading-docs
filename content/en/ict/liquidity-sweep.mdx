## What is a Liquidity Sweep?

A liquidity sweep occurs when price **momentarily trades beyond a key level** (swing high/low, equal H/L, session level) to trigger resting stop-loss orders and pending orders, then quickly reverses. It's the "manipulation" phase of AMD — the institutional fishing expedition for counterparty liquidity.

<Callout type="info" title="The Sweep is the Setup">
  "The sweep IS the setup. Once liquidity is taken, the smart money has their orders filled and the true move begins." —
  ICT
</Callout>

---

## Types of Liquidity Sweeps

### 1. Session Level Sweeps

- Sweep of Asian High/Low during London
- Sweep of London High/Low during NY
- Sweep of PDH/PDL (Previous Day High/Low)

### 2. Swing Point Sweeps

- Price takes out a recent swing high or swing low
- Quick wick beyond, then reversal
- Must be followed by displacement in the opposite direction

### 3. Equal High/Low Sweeps

- Multiple touches at the same level create "magnets"
- Sweep is pronounced — usually trades 5-15 pips beyond
- Very high probability of reversal after sweep

### 4. Trendline Sweeps

- Stop losses placed along trendlines
- "Breakout" takes the stops, then reverses
- Classic retail trap

## Sweep Confirmation

A sweep alone is NOT enough to trade. You need confirmation:

### Confirmation Checklist

1. ✅ Price trades beyond the liquidity level
2. ✅ Rejection candle forms (long wick, small body)
3. ✅ MSS on LTF (5M/15M) — displacement in opposite direction
4. ✅ FVG and/or OB created during the displacement
5. ✅ Kill Zone timing aligns

### What Invalidates a Sweep

- Price closes strongly beyond the level (not just a wick)
- No MSS follows — price continues in the sweep direction
- Multiple candles closing beyond = genuine breakout, not a sweep

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: PDH Sweep — EUR/USD Sell

- **PDH:** 1.0920
- **Setup:** NY AM opens, price spikes to 1.0928 (sweeps PDH)
- **Confirmation:** 5M bearish MSS at 1.0918, FVG at 1.0915-1.0920
- **Entry:** Sell at 1.0918 (FVG CE)
- **Stop:** 1.0930 (above sweep)
- **Target:** PDL at 1.0870
- **R:R:** 1:4
- **BD Time:** 5:45 PM

### Example 2: Equal Lows Sweep — Gold Buy

- **Equal Lows:** Three touches at 2,025
- **Setup:** London KZ, price drops to 2,022 (sweeps equal lows)
- **Confirmation:** 15M bullish MSS, bullish OB at 2,027
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,027
- **Stop:** 2,020
- **Target:** BSL at 2,060
- **R:R:** 1:4.7
- **BD Time:** 1:30 PM

## Sweep Trading Model

### The 4-Step Process

1. **Anticipate** — Mark all liquidity levels before the session
2. **Wait** — Let price come to the liquidity (don't chase)
3. **Confirm** — MSS + displacement after the sweep
4. **Execute** — Enter at OB/FVG with stop beyond sweep extreme

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Sweep Errors">

**❌ Fading every touch of a level** — Wait for the SWEEP (trade beyond), not just a touch

**❌ Entering during the sweep** — Wait for MSS confirmation AFTER the sweep

**❌ No HTF alignment** — A sweep against HTF bias = continuation, not reversal

**❌ Too tight stops** — Place stop beyond the sweep extreme, not at the level itself

**❌ Ignoring session context** — Sweeps during Kill Zones are far more reliable

</Callout>
