## What are Monthly Profiles?

Monthly Profiles describe the **seasonal and quarterly tendencies** that institutional traders follow. Just as weeks have daily patterns, months and quarters have predictable characteristics that help determine long-term bias.

<Callout type="info" title="Macro Perspective">
Monthly profiles give you the "big picture" context that day traders often miss. Understanding quarterly flows helps you avoid trading against massive institutional positioning.
</Callout>

---

## Quarterly Profiles

### Q1 (January - March)
- Often sets the **yearly low** or completes previous year's move
- January: New institutional positioning begins
- February: Continuation of January's direction
- March: Quarter-end rebalancing

### Q2 (April - June)
- Typically the **strongest trending quarter**
- April: Fresh institutional money enters
- May: "Sell in May" — equity markets may top
- June: Quarter-end adjustments

### Q3 (July - September)
- Summer doldrums (Jul-Aug lower volume)
- August: Can see flash crashes (thin liquidity)
- September: Institutions return, high volatility begins

### Q4 (October - December)
- Often sets the **yearly high** or yearly low
- October: Reversal month historically
- November: Pre-holiday positioning
- December: Year-end closing, low liquidity after Dec 15

## Monthly IPDA Mapping

Each month, use the IPDA data ranges to find targets:
1. **Look back 20 days** — Mark the range (high/low)
2. **Look back 40 days** — Wider context range
3. **Look back 60 days** — Quarterly perspective
4. The untouched liquidity at these levels = monthly targets

---

## Real Example: USD/JPY Q1 2024

- **December 2023:** Ended in discount (monthly low established)
- **January:** Bounced from Q4 low, began bullish structure
- **February:** Continuation — broke above December high
- **March:** Quarter-end → extended to Q1 high at 151.50

**Key:** Q1 set the low → Q2 continued the trend direction

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Monthly Profile Errors">

**❌ Ignoring quarterly flows** — Institutional rebalancing at quarter-end creates massive moves

**❌ Trading against seasonal tendencies** — Don't fight the macro current

**❌ Expecting daily patterns to override monthly** — Monthly bias > daily bias always

</Callout>
