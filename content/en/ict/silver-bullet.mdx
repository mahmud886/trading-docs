## What is the Silver Bullet?

The Silver Bullet is a **time-specific execution model** that identifies FVG entries within narrow time windows. ICT teaches that the algorithm creates displacement during these specific hours, leaving behind tradeable FVGs.

<Callout type="info" title="Precision Timing">
  The Silver Bullet reduces your screen time to just 1 hour per session. You only need to watch during the specific
  window and take the FVG that forms.
</Callout>

## How to Trade the Silver Bullet

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Determine Daily Bias** — You MUST have directional conviction first
2. **Wait for the Silver Bullet window** to open
3. **Look for displacement** — Strong candle creating an FVG during the window
4. **Mark the FVG** created during displacement
5. **Enter** — Limit order at the FVG (CE for precision)
6. **Stop Loss** — Beyond the swing point that created the displacement
7. **Target** — Next liquidity pool in the direction of bias

### Rules

- The FVG MUST form within the Silver Bullet time window
- Displacement must be in the direction of your Daily Bias
- Only take ONE Silver Bullet per session (the first valid one)
- If no FVG forms during the window → no trade (stand aside)

## Why Silver Bullet Works

1. **Algorithmic timing** — The IPDA creates moves at predictable times
2. **Volume confluence** — These windows align with institutional order flow
3. **Reduced noise** — Narrow time window = fewer false signals
4. **Mechanical approach** — Clear rules reduce emotional decisions

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Silver Bullet Errors">

**❌ No daily bias** — Silver Bullet without bias = random FVG trading

**❌ Trading FVGs outside the window** — Timing is the edge. FVGs at 9:30 AM EST are NOT Silver Bullet

**❌ Taking multiple Silver Bullets** — One per session maximum

**❌ Forcing trades** — If no FVG forms in the window, accept No Trade

**❌ Ignoring displacement quality** — The FVG must form from strong displacement, not a slow drift

</Callout>
