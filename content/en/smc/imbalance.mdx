## What is Imbalance?

An imbalance is an area on the chart where price moved **too aggressively in one direction**, creating an inefficiency. The market tends to revisit these areas to "rebalance" — filling the gap before continuing. In SMC, imbalances are key entry zones.

<Callout type="info" title="Market Principle">
"Price always seeks efficiency. Every imbalance is a debt the market must eventually repay." This is why gaps get filled.
</Callout>

---

## Types of Imbalance

### 1. Fair Value Gap (FVG)
- 3-candle formation where C1 and C3 wicks don't overlap
- Most common and most traded imbalance type
- See detailed guide: [FVG](/en/smc/fair-value-gap)

### 2. Liquidity Void
- A single large candle with minimal wicks
- Price moved so fast there's almost no trading activity in that range
- Larger imbalance than FVG — stronger magnetic pull

### 3. Volume Imbalance
- Gap between one candle's close and the next candle's open
- Often seen on session opens or news events
- Acts as support/resistance going forward

### 4. Opening Gap
- Gap between Friday's close and Monday's open
- Or gap between sessions
- Typically gets filled within the week

---

## How Imbalance is Used in SMC

### As Entry Zones
1. After an impulsive move, price creates imbalances
2. Price retraces to fill (partially or fully) the imbalance
3. Traders enter at the imbalance zone (buy at bullish imbalance, sell at bearish)
4. Stop beyond the imbalance

### As Targets
- Unfilled imbalances above/below current price act as targets
- The algorithm delivers price to fill these gaps over time
- HTF imbalances are major directional targets

## Real Example: Gold — Trading the Imbalance

- **Setup:** 4H shows a large bullish candle creating FVG at 2,025-2,035
- **Context:** Daily bullish, price was in discount
- **Retracement:** Price pulls back into the FVG
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,030 (50% of FVG)
- **SL:** 2,022 | **TP:** 2,065
- **R:R:** 1:4.4

## Imbalance Rules

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **HTF > LTF** | Daily imbalances override 15M imbalances |
| **Fresh > Old** | Recently created imbalances are more reliable |
| **With trend** | Imbalances aligned with HTF direction work best |
| **Context matters** | Imbalance in premium/discount zone adds probability |
| **Partial fill OK** | Price doesn't always fill the entire gap |

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Imbalance Errors">

**❌ Trading every imbalance** — Only trade imbalances aligned with HTF bias

**❌ Expecting 100% fill** — Many imbalances only partially fill before continuing

**❌ Ignoring timeframe** — A 1M imbalance means nothing against a 4H trend

**❌ No confluence** — Best entries: imbalance + OB + OTE zone overlapping

</Callout>
