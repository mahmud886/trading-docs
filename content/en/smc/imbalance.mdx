## What is Price Imbalance?

Price imbalance (also called inefficiency) occurs when price moves so aggressively in one direction that it creates **gaps between candle wicks** — areas where there was no two-sided trading. These gaps represent institutional urgency and price typically returns to fill them.

<Callout type="info" title="Imbalance = Magnet">
Price is attracted to imbalances like a magnet. Unfilled imbalances act as targets — price will almost always return to fill them before continuing. Use this knowledge for entries and targets.
</Callout>

---

## Types of Imbalance

### Fair Value Gap (FVG) — Most Common
- 3-candle pattern where middle candle creates a gap
- Candle 1 high doesn't overlap Candle 3 low (bullish)
- Or Candle 1 low doesn't overlap Candle 3 high (bearish)

### Volume Imbalance
- Gap between consecutive candle bodies
- One candle closes and next opens at different price
- Common at session opens

### Liquidity Void
- Large single candle with barely any wicks
- Represents one-sided movement
- Price returns to fill the body

---

## Why Price Returns to Imbalance

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Unfilled orders | Limit orders sitting in the gap |
| Fair value seeking | Market seeks equilibrium |
| Institutional reentry | Smart money re-enters at better price |
| Efficiency | Markets hate inefficiency |

## Trading Imbalance

### Entry at FVG Fill:
```
1. Identify FVG (gap between C1 and C3)
2. Wait for price to return to the FVG zone
3. Enter in the direction of the original impulse
4. Stop: Beyond the FVG (full candle range)
5. Target: Next structural level or liquidity

Example (Bullish FVG):
- Strong bullish move creates FVG at 1.1000-1.1020
- Price returns to 1.1010 (filling the gap)
- Enter long at 1.1010
- Stop: Below 1.0990 (below the FVG)
- Target: Next swing high
```

### Not All Imbalances Fill:
- HTF imbalances in strong trends may not fill for days/weeks
- Counter-trend FVGs in strong trends often DON'T fill
- Use imbalances aligned with HTF trend for highest probability
