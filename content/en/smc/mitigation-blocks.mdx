## What is a Mitigation Block?

A Mitigation Block is an **Order Block that has already been tested/touched by price** but didn't fully break. When price returns to an OB and reacts (partially fills), the OB becomes "mitigated" — its remaining power is reduced.

<Callout type="warning" title="Mitigated = Weaker">
Fresh, unmitigated Order Blocks are STRONGEST. Each time price returns and reacts, the OB loses significance. After 2-3 mitigations, the level is likely exhausted.
</Callout>

---

## Fresh vs. Mitigated

| Status | Description | Trade Quality |
|--------|------------|---------------|
| **Fresh (Unmitigated)** | Never been retested | Highest quality |
| **1x Mitigated** | Tested once, held | Still tradeable |
| **2x Mitigated** | Tested twice | Weak — caution |
| **3x+ Mitigated** | Tested 3+ times | Avoid — likely to break |

---

## How Mitigation Works

```
1. Bullish OB forms at 1.1000 (fresh)
2. Price rallies, then pulls back to OB
3. Price touches OB, bounces = 1st mitigation
4. Some institutional orders filled, but not all
5. Price rallies again, pulls back to OB again
6. 2nd touch = 2nd mitigation (weaker now)
7. Eventually: OB breaks (all orders filled/exhausted)

Rule: Trade the FIRST touch. Avoid 3rd+ touch.
```

---

## Mitigation vs. Breaker

| Feature | Mitigation Block | Breaker Block |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| OB status | Tested but holding | Completely broken |
| Direction | Same as original | Flipped (opposite) |
| Trade | Same direction (weakening) | New direction |
| What happened | Price touched but respected | Price violated completely |

---

## Trading Rules

1. **Always prefer fresh OBs** over mitigated ones
2. **1st mitigation** = still valid if reaction was strong
3. **2nd mitigation** = only trade with strong confluence
4. **3+ mitigations** = avoid entirely
5. **If price barely reacts** at mitigation = OB is dying
