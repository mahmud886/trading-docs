## What is Session Liquidity?

Each trading session creates liquidity (highs/lows) that subsequent sessions target. Understanding which session creates the target and which session sweeps it is fundamental to SMC day trading.

---

## Session Liquidity Map

### Asian Session → Creates Liquidity
- Forms a range (high and low)
- Stop losses accumulate above Asian High and below Asian Low
- This is the "bait" for London

### London Session → Sweeps Asian Liquidity
- London open typically sweeps one side of the Asian range
- This sweep forms the daily high OR low
- Provides the manipulation phase

### New York Session → Targets Long-Range Liquidity
- After London sweep, NY continues the true direction
- Targets PDH/PDL, equal H/L, or HTF levels
- Delivers the distribution/expansion

---

## Session Liquidity Levels to Mark

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Asian High (AH)** | BSL target for London bears |
| **Asian Low (AL)** | SSL target for London bulls |
| **Previous Day High (PDH)** | Major BSL target |
| **Previous Day Low (PDL)** | Major SSL target |
| **London High** | Potential NY sweep target |
| **London Low** | Potential NY sweep target |
| **Previous Week H/L** | Major swing targets |

## Real Example: Session Liquidity Play — Gold

- **Asian Range (6-12 PM BD):** 2,038 - 2,044
- **London (12:15 PM BD):** Sweeps Asian Low at 2,035 (SSL taken)
- **Confirmation:** 15M bullish CHOCH after sweep
- **Entry:** Buy at 2,039 (bullish OB within Asian range)
- **Target 1:** Asian High 2,044 (quick target)
- **Target 2:** PDH 2,055 (extended target)
- **SL:** 2,033 | **R:R:** 1:3.7

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Session Errors">

**❌ Not marking session levels** — Mark AH/AL and PDH/PDL before each session

**❌ Trading the Asian breakout** — Asian "breakout" is usually London manipulation

**❌ Ignoring the session order** — Asian → London → NY. Trade the sequence, don't skip

</Callout>
