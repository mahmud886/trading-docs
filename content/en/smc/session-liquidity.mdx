## What is Session Liquidity?

Session Liquidity refers to the **previous session's highs and lows** acting as liquidity targets for the next session. In SMC, each new session seeks to take the liquidity left by the previous one.

---

## Key Session Liquidity Levels

| Level              | Abbreviation | Significance      |
| ------------------ | ------------ | ----------------- |
| Previous Day High  | PDH          | Major BSL target  |
| Previous Day Low   | PDL          | Major SSL target  |
| Previous Week High | PWH          | Weekly BSL target |
| Previous Week Low  | PWL          | Weekly SSL target |
| Asian Session High | ASH          | London target     |
| Asian Session Low  | ASL          | London target     |

---

## How Sessions Target Liquidity

```
The Liquidity Chain:
1. Asian session creates a range (ASH/ASL)
2. London sweeps Asian H or L (takes liquidity)
3. London creates its own H/L
4. NY may continue or sweep London's H/L
5. Next day: PDH/PDL become new targets

Daily Cycle:
PDL/PDH → Today's target
ASH/ASL → London's target
London H/L → NY's target
```

---

## Trading Session Liquidity

### Strategy: PDH/PDL as Draw on Liquidity

```
1. Identify daily bias (bullish or bearish)
2. If bullish: PDH is the draw (target above)
3. If bearish: PDL is the draw (target below)
4. Look for entries at discount (bullish) or premium (bearish)
5. Target: PDH/PDL as take profit

Example (Bullish Day):
- Yesterday's high = 1.1050 (PDH)
- Price currently at 1.1000
- Daily bias: Bullish
- Entry: OB pullback at 1.0980
- Target: PDH at 1.1050
```
