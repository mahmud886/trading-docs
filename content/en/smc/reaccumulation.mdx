## What is Re-accumulation?

Re-accumulation is a **consolidation phase within an uptrend** where smart money adds to their existing long positions before pushing price higher. It looks like accumulation but occurs MID-TREND, not at the bottom.

<Callout type="info" title="Adding to Winners">
Re-accumulation is how institutions add to winning positions. They pause the markup, build more longs at a higher level, then push price to the next target.
</Callout>

---

## How to Identify Re-accumulation

### Characteristics
1. Price is in an established **uptrend** (HH/HL on HTF)
2. Price creates a **consolidation range** mid-trend
3. Minor liquidity sweeps within the range (stop hunts)
4. Range eventually breaks to the UPSIDE (continuation)
5. Often creates a bullish OB at the bottom of the range

### Re-accumulation vs Distribution
| Feature | Re-accumulation | Distribution |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| **HTF Trend** | Bullish (HH/HL intact) | Topping (HH failing) |
| **Break Direction** | Upside | Downside |
| **Volume** | Increasing on upside breaks | Increasing on downside |
| **Purpose** | Add longs | Exit longs |

---

## Trading Re-accumulation

### Entry Method
1. Identify uptrend on HTF (Daily/4H making HH/HL)
2. Spot consolidation on LTF (1H/4H range)
3. Wait for SSL sweep at the bottom of the range
4. Bullish CHOCH/BOS confirms range breakout
5. Enter at the bullish OB/FVG at the bottom of the range
6. Target: Next BSL above

### Real Example: EUR/USD
- **Daily:** Clear uptrend since 1.0750
- **4H:** Range forms between 1.0880 - 1.0920 for 3 days
- **Sweep:** SSL at 1.0870 taken during London KZ
- **CHOCH:** Bullish on 15M after sweep
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0880 (bullish OB at range bottom)
- **SL:** 1.0865 | **TP:** 1.0980 (next BSL)
- **R:R:** 1:6.7
