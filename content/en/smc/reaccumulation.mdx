## What Is Re-accumulation?

**Re-accumulation** is a **range in the middle of an uptrend**. The trend pauses, price goes sideways, often sweeps the range low, and then breaks up to continue. On a chart of a strong trend it looks like a **staircase**: push, range, push, range.

| Feature      | Accumulation            | Re-accumulation       | Distribution           |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Where        | After a decline         | **Inside an uptrend** | After a rally          |
| False break  | Below the lows (spring) | **Below the lows**    | Above the highs (UTAD) |
| What follows | Markup                  | **Continuation up**   | Markdown               |

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: 'adding to winners'">
  The original said institutions use these ranges to "add to winning positions". That may be true for some of the
  volume, but it can't be seen. What can be seen: a pause, a sweep of the range low, and a resumption with a BOS.
</Callout>

1. **Markup:** the trend breaks structure up.
2. **Range:** it stalls at **1.0880 – 1.0910**. Stops build under the range low.
3. **Sweep:** price trades to **1.0872**, under the low, and closes back inside.
4. **Entry:** the next pullback reaches **1.0890**. Buy.
5. **Confirmation:** a **BOS** above 1.0910 shows the trend has resumed.

| Target  | 1.0990      | Next buy-side above           |
| **R:R** | **1 : 4.5** | Risk 22 pips, reward 100 pips |

## Signs to Look For

| Sign                                                           | Why it matters                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Clear uptrend with BOS before the range                        | The range is a pause, not a top        |
| Range in the **lower half** of the last leg, or at an HTF zone | Buying at a discount within the trend  |
| Sweep of the range low that closes back inside                 | Sell-side taken without follow-through |
| Lower-timeframe CHOCH up after the sweep                       | Buyers are back                        |
| BOS above the range                                            | Confirmation                           |

---

## Trading It

1. Confirm the higher-timeframe uptrend.
2. Mark the range; wait for a sweep of its low.
3. Enter after a lower-timeframe CHOCH, at the OB/FVG or on the retest.
4. **Stop** below the sweep.
5. **Target** the next buy-side liquidity or HTF level.

Or wait for the **BOS above the range** and buy its retest: later, but more confirmed.

## Re-accumulation or a Top?

| Clue                    | Re-accumulation                      | Distribution (a top)            |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| False break             | Below the range                      | Above the range                 |
| Pushes inside the range | Dips weaken                          | Rallies weaken                  |
| Higher timeframe        | Trend intact, room to the next level | At a major HTF level or premium |
| Break                   | Up, and holds                        | Down, and holds                 |

The original called this setup "high probability". Its odds are better than a counter-trend trade because it's with the trend, but no pattern is high probability by itself. The break decides.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                              | Fix                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Buying the range high                | Buy after a sweep of the low, or the BOS retest |
| Ignoring the HTF level overhead      | A range under a major level may be distribution |
| Stop at the range low                | Below the sweep                                 |
| Holding after a breakdown that holds | The read was wrong; exit                        |

## Practice

1. On 4H charts of trending pairs, mark 10 mid-trend ranges.
2. Record whether each swept its low first, and which way it broke.
3. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
