## What Are Premium and Discount?

Take a meaningful range, from a swing low to a swing high, and split it at **50%**, the **equilibrium (EQ)**:

| Zone            | Where     | Use                    |
| --------------- | --------- | ---------------------- |
| **Premium**     | Above 50% | Look to **sell**       |
| **Equilibrium** | 50%       | No edge on price alone |
| **Discount**    | Below 50% | Look to **buy**        |

**EQ = (swing high + swing low) ÷ 2.** For 1.0800 – 1.1000, EQ is **1.0900**: premium 1.0900 – 1.1000, discount 1.0800 – 1.0900.

The idea is simple: in an uptrend, buy pullbacks while they're cheap relative to the leg; in a downtrend, sell rallies while they're expensive. You get a better price and more room to the target.

1. The leg runs from **1.0800** to **1.1000**. EQ is **1.0900**; OTE (62 – 79%) is **1.0842 – 1.0876**.
2. It left two bullish order blocks: one at **1.0946 – 1.0955**, in **premium**, and one at **1.0866 – 1.0876**, in **discount**.
3. On the pullback, the premium OB gives a weak bounce, then price **closes straight through it**.
4. The discount OB, at the top of OTE, **holds**. Buy **1.0874**.

| Level   | Price       | Note                          |
| ------- | ----------- | ----------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0874      | Bullish OB in discount        |
| Stop    | 1.0858      | Below the OB                  |
| Target  | 1.1010      | Above the 1.1000 high         |
| **R:R** | **1 : 8.5** | Risk 16 pips, reward 136 pips |

The point isn't that premium OBs always fail. It's that a pullback **usually** goes at least to equilibrium before the trend resumes, so a bullish zone above 50% is more likely to be run through.

## Grading Zones by Location

| Zone                           | With a bullish bias     | With a bearish bias  |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | -------------------- |
| OB / FVG in **discount**       | Buy setup               | Skip (selling cheap) |
| OB / FVG in **premium**        | Skip (buying expensive) | Sell setup           |
| OB / FVG in **discount + OTE** | Best buy location       | —                    |
| OB / FVG in **premium + OTE**  | —                       | Best sell location   |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: 'never' and the star ratings">
  The original said "never buy in premium, never sell in discount" and rated OBs with stars. Strong trends often retrace
  only to the premium side of the leg; a rigid rule will keep you out of those. And the stars had no data behind them.
  Use location as a **filter**, not an absolute rule.
</Callout>

## Which Range?

| Choice                    | Range to use                                               |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trading with the 4H trend | The last 4H impulse leg (the one that broke structure)     |
| Swing trading             | The daily or weekly [dealing range](/en/ict/dealing-range) |
| After a CHOCH/MSS         | The new leg, from the sweep extreme to the break           |

Redraw the range when a new high or low (and BOS) forms.

## Multi-Timeframe Alignment

| Timeframe | Check                              |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Weekly    | In discount (for buys)?            |
| Daily     | Pulling back into its discount?    |
| 4H        | Bullish OB/FVG in the 4H discount? |
| 15m       | Entry confirmation at the zone     |

When all agree, the trade has location on its side on every timeframe.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                            | Fix                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Using a random range               | Use the leg that broke structure                      |
| Buying any discount level          | Location filters; you still need a zone and a trigger |
| Treating "never in premium" as law | Strong trends retrace shallowly                       |
| Forgetting to update the range     | Redraw after each new BOS                             |

## Practice

1. On 4H charts, draw the last impulse leg and its 50% for 10 pullbacks.
2. Record where each pullback turned: premium, EQ, discount, or OTE.
3. Compare how OBs in premium vs discount held. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Element       | Rule                         |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **EQ**        | (High + low) ÷ 2             |
| **Buy**       | In discount, ideally in OTE  |
| **Sell**      | In premium, ideally in OTE   |
| **Range**     | The leg that broke structure |
| **Rule type** | A filter, not a law          |
