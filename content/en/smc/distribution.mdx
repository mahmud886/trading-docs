## What is Distribution?

Distribution is the **third phase** of the institutional cycle where smart money offloads their accumulated positions at premium prices. It's the mirror image of accumulation — the market looks bullish, but institutions are secretly selling.

<Callout type="info" title="Smart Money Exit">
After marking up price, institutions can't sell everything at once — it would crash the market. They gradually distribute (sell) at premium prices while retail traders are still buying.
</Callout>

---

## Distribution Process

### The 5 Steps
1. **Range at Top** — After markup, price consolidates at a high level
2. **Test Highs** — Price tests/slightly exceeds the range high (BSL sweep bait)
3. **Absorption** — Institutions sell into every buy order
4. **Upthrust (Final Sweep)** — One last spike above the range (sweeps BSL)
5. **Markdown Begins** — Price breaks below the range with displacement

---

## How to Identify Distribution

### Visual Clues
- Price ranges for days/weeks at a HIGH level (Daily premium)
- Multiple touches of the range high (looks like "resistance testing")
- Wicks above the range that quickly reject
- Decreasing momentum on each push up
- Volume decreases on rallies, increases on selloffs

---

## Real Example: EUR/USD Distribution

- **Markup:** EUR/USD rallies from 1.0800 to 1.0960 over 2 weeks
- **Range at Top:** 1.0930 - 1.0960 for 4 days
- **Upthrust:** Spike to 1.0975 during London (sweeps BSL)
- **BOS Down:** Bearish displacement below 1.0930
- **Entry:** Sell at bearish OB 1.0945 (pullback after BOS)
- **SL:** 1.0978 | **TP:** 1.0830
- **R:R:** 1:3.5

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Distribution Errors">

**❌ Buying the breakout above the range** — The upthrust IS the trap

**❌ Not recognizing premium context** — Distribution happens in premium, not discount

**❌ Expecting instant markdown** — Distribution takes time (days to weeks)

</Callout>
