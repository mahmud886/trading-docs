## What Is a Break of Structure?

A **break of structure (BOS)** happens when price **closes beyond the last swing point in the direction of the trend**:

- In an **uptrend** (higher highs, higher lows): a close **above the last swing high**.
- In a **downtrend** (lower highs, lower lows): a close **below the last swing low**.

A BOS doesn't predict a reversal. It **confirms the existing trend is still working**. For the first sign of a reversal, see [CHOCH](/en/smc/choch).

## BOS vs CHOCH

| Situation     | Break above the last swing high | Break below the last swing low |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Uptrend**   | **BOS** (continuation)          | **CHOCH** (possible reversal)  |
| **Downtrend** | **CHOCH** (possible reversal)   | **BOS** (continuation)         |

---

## Trading the BOS

A BOS isn't an entry by itself. Entering on the break means buying after the move. The usual plan is to **wait for the pullback**:

1. **Trend:** at least two higher highs and higher lows (or the bearish mirror).
2. **BOS:** a candle closes beyond the last swing.
3. **Mark the zone** the break left: the [FVG](/en/smc/fair-value-gap) in the breakout candle or the [order block](/en/smc/order-blocks) before it.
4. **Wait** for price to pull back into it, ideally in [discount](/en/smc/premium-discount) for a buy.
5. **Stop** below the higher low the break came from.
6. **Target** the next liquidity: an old high, equal highs, or a measured swing.

## Multi-Timeframe BOS

| Timeframe  | Use                                                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Daily / 4H | Direction: is the last BOS up or down?                |
| 1H         | Where the pullback zone is                            |
| 5m–15m     | Entry: a BOS in the same direction after the pullback |

When all three agree, the trade has the trend behind it. When the low timeframe breaks against the high one, that's a pullback, not a new trend.

## Reading the Trend's Age

| BOS count in one leg | Reading                                                         |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2                  | Trend establishing                                              |
| 3–4                  | Trend mature; pullbacks get deeper                              |
| 5+                   | Trend stretched; watch for a sweep and a [CHOCH](/en/smc/choch) |

These are rules of thumb, not statistics.

**A failed BOS:** if price closes beyond the high, then quickly closes back inside and breaks the last higher low, the BOS was a trap. Treat it as a sweep followed by a CHOCH.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                       | Fix                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Counting wicks as BOS         | Only closes count                                   |
| Buying the breakout candle    | Wait for the pullback into the zone                 |
| Marking tiny internal swings  | Use clear swings on your timeframe                  |
| Ignoring the higher timeframe | A 5m BOS against the 4H trend is usually a pullback |

## Practice

1. On a 1H chart, mark every swing high and low for one week.
2. Mark each BOS (close beyond) and each wick that didn't close beyond.
3. Note how often the wick-only breaks reversed. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
