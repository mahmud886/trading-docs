## What is Break of Structure (BOS)?

Break of Structure (BOS) is the **most fundamental concept** in Smart Money Concepts (SMC) trading. It occurs when price breaks beyond a previous **swing high** (in an uptrend) or **swing low** (in a downtrend), confirming the continuation of the current trend direction.

Think of it this way: the market moves in waves — higher highs and higher lows in an uptrend, lower highs and lower lows in a downtrend. Each time price makes a new high above a previous high, or a new low below a previous low **in the direction of the trend**, that's a Break of Structure.

<Callout type="info" title="BOS = Trend Confirmation">
  BOS does NOT signal a trend change — it confirms that the **existing trend is still intact**. If you see a bullish
  BOS, it means the uptrend continues. For trend changes, look at [Change of Character (CHOCH)](/en/smc/choch).
</Callout>

## Why BOS Matters

Understanding BOS is critical because it tells you:

1. **The trend is alive** — Smart money is still in control
2. **Where to look for entries** — After a BOS, look for pullbacks to enter with the trend
3. **Where institutional orders were placed** — The candle that caused the BOS often contains an Order Block
4. **When to hold trades** — Multiple BOS signals confirm your trade direction is correct
5. **When NOT to trade** — If BOS hasn't happened, the trend isn't confirmed

## Bullish Break of Structure

A **Bullish BOS** occurs when price breaks above a previous **swing high** in an uptrend:

```
                                    ★ New High (BOS!)
                                   /
                   Swing High ────/────
                  /              /
                 /    Swing Low /
                /         \    /
   Swing High  /           \/
       \      /        Higher Low
        \    /
         \/
     Swing Low
```

**How to identify:**

1. Price is making **higher highs** and **higher lows** (uptrend established)
2. Price pulls back to form a higher low
3. Price then rallies and **closes above** the previous swing high
4. This close above = **Bullish BOS confirmed**

**Key rules:**

- The candle must **close** above the previous high (not just wick above)
- A wick above without a close is a **liquidity grab**, not a BOS
- The BOS candle often contains a Fair Value Gap — mark it as your entry zone

### Bullish BOS Trading Process

1. **Identify** the uptrend (series of higher highs and higher lows)
2. **Wait for BOS** — price closes above the last swing high
3. **Mark the FVG** or Order Block created by the BOS candle
4. **Wait for pullback** to the FVG/OB zone
5. **Enter long** with confirmation at the zone
6. **Stop loss** below the most recent swing low
7. **Target** the next liquidity level or projected swing high

## Bearish Break of Structure

A **Bearish BOS** occurs when price breaks below a previous **swing low** in a downtrend:

```
     Swing High
        /\
       /  \
      /    \        Lower High
     /      \          /\
    /        \        /  \
   Swing Low  \      /    \
    ──────────────\──/──────\─────
                   \/        \
               Lower Low      ★ New Low (BOS!)
```

**How to identify:**

1. Price is making **lower highs** and **lower lows** (downtrend established)
2. Price rallies to form a lower high
3. Price then drops and **closes below** the previous swing low
4. This close below = **Bearish BOS confirmed**

## BOS vs. CHOCH — Critical Difference

This is one of the most important distinctions in SMC:

| Concept                         | What It Means                                                | Trend Status               |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| **BOS (Break of Structure)**    | Price breaks a swing point **in the direction** of the trend | Trend **continues**        |
| **CHOCH (Change of Character)** | Price breaks a swing point **against** the trend direction   | Trend may be **reversing** |

**Example:**

- In an **uptrend**: Breaking above a swing high = **BOS** (bullish continuation)
- In an **uptrend**: Breaking below a swing low = **CHOCH** (potential reversal to bearish)
- In a **downtrend**: Breaking below a swing low = **BOS** (bearish continuation)
- In a **downtrend**: Breaking above a swing high = **CHOCH** (potential reversal to bullish)

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't Confuse Them">
  Many beginners confuse BOS and CHOCH. Remember: **BOS = WITH the trend** (continuation), **CHOCH = AGAINST the trend**
  (reversal). Getting this wrong will put you on the wrong side of the market.
</Callout>

## How to Identify Valid Swing Points

Not every high/low is a valid swing point for BOS:

### Valid Swing High

- Has at least **one lower candle** on each side
- Represents a **clear structural point** where price reversed
- Is visible on your trading timeframe without zooming in

### Valid Swing Low

- Has at least **one higher candle** on each side
- Shows a clear point where selling stopped and buying began
- Aligns with other structural elements

### Internal vs. External BOS

**Internal Structure:** Minor swing points within a larger move. These create smaller BOS signals useful for entry timing on lower timeframes.

**External Structure:** Major swing points that define the overall trend. External BOS carries more weight and significance.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip: Timeframe Alignment">
  A BOS on the 1H chart within a Daily BOS direction gives you **confluence** — the highest probability trades come when
  multiple timeframes agree on direction.
</Callout>

## BOS Entry Strategies

### Strategy 1: FVG Entry After BOS

The most common and reliable approach:

1. Wait for a **confirmed BOS** (candle close beyond the swing point)
2. The BOS candle likely created a **Fair Value Gap**
3. Wait for price to **retrace** into the FVG
4. Enter with confirmation at the FVG
5. Stop loss beyond the opposing swing point
6. Target the next structural level

**Best for:** Intraday trading, 15min-1H timeframes

### Strategy 2: Order Block Entry After BOS

More conservative, higher-probability:

1. After BOS confirmation, identify the **last opposing candle** before the breakout
2. This is the **Order Block** — the zone where institutions placed their orders
3. Wait for price to return to the OB
4. Enter on a reaction at the OB
5. Tight stop loss below/above the OB
6. Target at least 1:3 R:R

**Best for:** Swing trading, 4H-Daily timeframes

### Strategy 3: Liquidity Sweep + BOS Combo

The highest-probability setup in all of SMC:

1. Price **sweeps liquidity** (takes out an obvious high/low)
2. Immediately after, a **BOS occurs** in the opposite direction of the sweep
3. This creates a displacement with an FVG
4. Enter on the pullback to the FVG
5. The sweep provides extra confirmation that smart money has entered

## Multi-Timeframe BOS Analysis

### Daily BOS

- Defines the **overall direction** for swing traders
- When Daily shows bullish BOS, only look for longs
- Each Daily BOS candle moves 50-200+ pips

### 4-Hour BOS

- Used for **intraday directional bias**
- Aligns with Daily structure for confluence
- Each 4H BOS moves 30-80 pips typically

### 1-Hour/15-Minute BOS

- Used for **entry timing** and precision
- Must align with higher TF direction
- Provides the actual entry signal for your trade

### The Process

1. **Daily**: Is there a BOS? What direction?
2. **4H**: Is the 4H BOS in the same direction as Daily?
3. **1H/15min**: Wait for BOS on LTF in the same direction
4. **Enter** on the pullback after LTF BOS during a Kill Zone

## Common Mistakes with BOS

### ❌ Mistake 1: Counting Wick Closes as BOS

A candle that wicks above/below a swing point but **closes back inside** is NOT a BOS. It's a **liquidity grab** or stop hunt. Wait for a proper candle body close beyond the level.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Trading BOS Against the HTF Trend

A 15-minute bullish BOS inside a Daily bearish trend is risky. Always ensure your BOS aligns with at least the 4H direction.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Entering Immediately After BOS

BOS is not an entry signal — it's a **directional confirmation**. Wait for a pullback to get a better entry price with lower risk.

### ❌ Mistake 4: Not Marking the BOS Candle

The BOS candle itself is valuable — it often contains an FVG or marks an Order Block. Always mark the BOS candle's range for future reference.

### ❌ Mistake 5: Ignoring Failed BOS

If a BOS is followed by a quick reversal (price closes back beyond the level), this is a **failed BOS** or a trap. It often indicates the opposite direction will be strong.

## Real Trading Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD — Bullish BOS During London

> **Context:** EUR/USD has been in an uptrend on the 4H chart, making higher highs and higher lows. The last swing high was at 1.0920.
>
> **BOS:** At 2:30 PM BD (London Kill Zone), a strong bullish candle closes at 1.0935, clearly above the 1.0920 swing high. This confirms a **Bullish BOS**.
>
> **Entry:** The BOS candle created an FVG between 1.0905 and 1.0915. Price pulls back to 1.0910 at 3:45 PM BD. A bullish pin bar forms on the 5min chart.
>
> **Execution:** Long at 1.0912, stop at 1.0895 (below the recent swing low), target at 1.0960 (next liquidity level).
>
> **Result:** Price rallied to 1.0965. Risk: 17 pips, Reward: 48 pips = **1:2.8 R:R**.

### Example 2: XAU/USD (Gold) — Bearish BOS

> **Context:** Gold has been trending down on the 1H chart. Last swing low at $2,310.
>
> **BOS:** During NY Kill Zone (8:00 PM BD), a strong red candle closes at $2,305, below the $2,310 swing low. **Bearish BOS confirmed**.
>
> **Entry:** The BOS candle created an FVG between $2,318 and $2,325. Price retraces to $2,322 at 9:15 PM BD.
>
> **Execution:** Short at $2,321, stop at $2,330, target at $2,290.
>
> **Result:** Gold dropped to $2,285 by the next day. Risk: $9, Reward: $31 = **1:3.4 R:R**.

### Example 3: BTC/USD — Multi-TF BOS

> **Context:** Bitcoin shows bullish BOS on the Daily chart (above $68,000). The 4H also shows bullish BOS (above $69,500).
>
> **Entry:** On the 1H chart, wait for a pullback and then a 1H bullish BOS that aligns with the higher TFs. This occurs at $69,800.
>
> **Execution:** Long at $69,900 (after 1H BOS pullback), stop at $69,200, target at $72,000.
>
> **Result:** BTC pushed to $73,000 over 3 days. Risk: $700, Reward: $2,100 = **1:3 R:R**.

## Advanced BOS Concepts

### BOS with Volume Confirmation

A BOS accompanied by **above-average volume** or a large-body candle indicates genuine institutional commitment. Low-volume BOS signals are more likely to fail.

### Multiple BOS (Trend Strength)

Counting consecutive BOS in the same direction tells you about trend strength:

- **1-2 BOS**: Trend is establishing
- **3-4 BOS**: Trend is mature
- **5+ BOS**: Trend may be exhausting — watch for CHOCH

### BOS Failure (Trap)

When a BOS immediately reverses:

1. This is a **trap pattern** / false breakout
2. The reversal is often more powerful than the original BOS
3. Smart money used the BOS to grab liquidity before reversing
4. Look for a CHOCH signal after a failed BOS

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the difference between BOS and a breakout?

Traditional breakouts focus on price breaking above resistance or below support. BOS is specifically about **market structure** — swing highs and lows in context of trend direction. BOS considers whether the break is WITH or AGAINST the trend.

### Can BOS happen on any timeframe?

Yes. BOS occurs on all timeframes from 1-minute to Monthly. Higher timeframe BOS carries more weight. Always trade LTF BOS that aligns with HTF BOS.

### How many BOS should I see before entering a trade?

At minimum, you want to see **one confirmed BOS** in your trading direction on your execution timeframe, with the higher timeframe also showing the same bias.

### What happens after a BOS?

After a bullish BOS, price typically **pulls back** to create a higher low before continuing up. This pullback is where you enter. After a bearish BOS, price rallies to form a lower high before dropping.
