## Understanding Institutional Order Flow

Institutional Order Flow is the study of **HOW large players (banks, hedge funds, central banks) execute orders** and how their activity creates predictable patterns in price delivery.

---

## How Institutions Execute

| Factor | Retail | Institutional |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Size | Small (0.01-1 lot) | Massive (100-10,000+ lots) |
| Fill | Instant | Requires liquidity over time |
| Impact | None | Moves markets |
| Strategy | Chase price | Engineer liquidity, then fill |
| Timeframe | Minutes | Days to weeks |

---

## Time and Price Delivery

```
Institutions deliver price in a predictable manner:

1. WEEKLY candle shows overall intention
   - Bullish weekly = price targets highs
   - Bearish weekly = price targets lows

2. DAILY candle refines the delivery
   - Which days does price push?
   - Which days does it retrace?
   - Tuesday/Wednesday = highest probability moves

3. SESSION timing determines entry
   - London initiates, NY continues
   - Kill Zones = institutional activity
```

---

## Identifying Institutional Footprints

- **Displacement:** Large body candles = institutional commitment
- **Fair Value Gaps:** Created by institutional urgency
- **Order Blocks:** Last opposing candle before institutional move
- **Liquidity Sweeps:** Institutional order filling method
- **Time alignment:** Moves during Kill Zones

---

## Weekly Bias Determination

```
1. Where did last week close relative to its range?
   - Upper 25% = bullish continuation likely
   - Lower 25% = bearish continuation likely
   - Middle 50% = look for other cues

2. Where is draw on liquidity?
   - Unswept weekly highs/lows = targets
   - Previous week H/L = immediate targets

3. Is there HTF imbalance to fill?
   - Monthly/weekly FVGs = directional bias
```
