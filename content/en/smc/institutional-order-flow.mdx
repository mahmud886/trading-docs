## What is Institutional Order Flow?

Institutional order flow is the **aggregate buying and selling activity** of banks, hedge funds, and large financial institutions. In SMC, we read this flow through price action — every candle tells a story about what institutions are doing.

<Callout type="info" title="Reading the Tape">
You don't need a Bloomberg terminal to see institutional order flow. SMC teaches you to read it directly from the chart through Order Blocks, displacement, and volume patterns.
</Callout>

---

## Signs of Institutional Activity

### 1. Displacement
- Large-bodied candles with minimal wicks
- Multiple candles in the same direction
- Creates FVGs and Order Blocks
- This is institutions COMMITTING capital

### 2. Absorption
- Price tests a level multiple times without breaking
- Each test has strong rejection (long wicks)
- Institutions are absorbing opposing orders
- Precedes a displacement move

### 3. Accumulation Pattern
- Tight range with decreasing volatility
- Stop hunts on both sides of the range
- Increasing volume on the eventual breakout
- Institutions are building positions

### 4. Distribution Pattern
- Price makes new highs but momentum decreases
- Decreasing volume on upswings
- Increasing volume on downswings
- Institutions are offloading positions

---

## How to Align with Institutional Flow

### Step 1: Identify the HTF Direction
- What are institutions doing on the Daily/Weekly? (accumulation or distribution?)
- Are they building longs (bullish OBs, bullish FVGs) or shorts?

### Step 2: Look for Displacement
- Where is the most recent strong move?
- This displacement tells you where institutions just entered

### Step 3: Wait for Retracement
- After displacement, price retraces to fill FVGs
- This is where you enter WITH institutional flow

### Step 4: Target Institutional Targets
- Where is the next liquidity pool?
- Where are unfilled HTF imbalances?

## Real Example: GBP/USD Institutional Order Flow

- **Weekly:** Displacement up from 1.2500 to 1.2700 (institutional buying confirmed)
- **Daily:** Pullback to 4H bullish OB at 1.2580 (retracement)
- **Institutional Flow:** Clearly bullish — buy the pullback
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.2580 (4H OB + FVG confluence)
- **SL:** 1.2540 | **TP:** 1.2750
- **R:R:** 1:4.25

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Order Flow Errors">

**❌ Fighting displacement** — Strong displacement = institutional commitment. Don't counter-trade it

**❌ Confusing consolidation with trend change** — Consolidation after displacement = continuation likely

**❌ Ignoring volume context** — High-volume displacement > low-volume breakout

</Callout>
