## Internal vs. External Liquidity

In SMC, liquidity exists in two forms:
- **External Liquidity:** Resting orders beyond swing highs/lows (the TARGET)
- **Internal Liquidity:** FVGs, Order Blocks within the range (the ENTRY)

<Callout type="info" title="The Flow">
Price moves FROM internal liquidity TO external liquidity, then back. Understanding this flow tells you where price is going next.
</Callout>

---

## Definitions

| Type | Location | Examples | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|----------|
| **External** | Beyond swing H/L | EQH, EQL, Swing points | TARGET (where price is going) |
| **Internal** | Within the range | FVGs, OBs, Breakers | ENTRY (where price enters from) |

---

## The Price Cycle

```
Price follows this cycle:

1. Takes EXTERNAL liquidity (sweeps high/low)
2. Returns to INTERNAL liquidity (FVG/OB retracement)
3. Reacts from internal → goes to next EXTERNAL
4. Repeat

Example:
1. Price sweeps EQH (external BSL taken)
2. Price drops to fill FVG below (internal)
3. Price reacts from FVG bullishly? No → reversal
4. Price heads to EQL (external SSL = next target)
5. Price reaches EQL, sweeps it
6. Price rallies to FVG above (internal)
7. Cycle continues...
```

---

## Trading Application

### Using External as TARGET:
- Draw on liquidity = next unfilled external level
- Use EQH/EQL, session H/L, swing points as TP

### Using Internal as ENTRY:
- After external is taken, look for internal entry
- Enter at FVG or OB retracement
- This gives you the best R:R
