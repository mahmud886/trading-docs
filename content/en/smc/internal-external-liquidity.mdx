## What is Internal vs External Liquidity?

In SMC, liquidity exists at two levels:
- **External Liquidity** — Major swing highs/lows, PDH/PDL, equal highs/lows (the BIG targets)
- **Internal Liquidity** — FVGs, Order Blocks, and micro swing points WITHIN a move (retracement targets)

<Callout type="info" title="The Cycle">
Price alternates between sweeping external liquidity and filling internal liquidity. External sweep → internal fill (retracement) → next external sweep.
</Callout>

---

## External Liquidity

### What It Is
- Resting orders at major swing highs (BSL) and swing lows (SSL)
- Equal highs/lows, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL
- Trendline liquidity

### How It's Used
- External liquidity is the DESTINATION — where price is heading
- Smart money drives price to external liquidity to fill orders
- Once external liquidity is swept, price retraces to internal liquidity

---

## Internal Liquidity

### What It Is
- Fair Value Gaps within a move
- Order Blocks left behind by displacement
- Minor swing points within a larger move

### How It's Used
- Internal liquidity is where price RETRACES to after sweeping external
- This is your ENTRY zone — buy/sell at FVG/OB during retracement
- After filling internal liquidity, price heads to the next external target

---

## The Internal/External Cycle

```
External SSL swept → Price retraces to internal FVG/OB → Continues to external BSL
                         (YOUR ENTRY)
```

### Real Example: EUR/USD
1. **External:** SSL at 1.0830 swept during London
2. **Internal:** Price retraces UP to 15M bullish FVG at 1.0855 (your entry)
3. **External:** Price continues UP to BSL at 1.0920
4. **Entry:** Buy at 1.0855 (internal), Target 1.0920 (external), R:R 1:4+

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Liquidity Errors">

**❌ Confusing internal and external** — FVGs are INTERNAL (entry zones), swing H/L are EXTERNAL (targets)

**❌ Entering at external levels** — External is where the sweep happens, not your entry zone

**❌ Ignoring the cycle** — Price alternates: external → internal → external. Trade the rhythm

</Callout>
