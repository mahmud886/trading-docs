## What is Manipulation in SMC?

Manipulation is the **intentional false move** created by smart money to trap retail traders on the wrong side of the market. It's the mechanism by which institutions generate the counterparty liquidity they need to fill large positions.

<Callout type="info" title="Why Manipulation Exists">
Banks can't just buy 500 million EUR/USD at once — there's no liquidity for that. They need someone to SELL to them. By pushing price below support (triggering sell stops), they get the sell orders they need to buy.
</Callout>

---

## Types of Manipulation

### 1. Stop Hunt (Liquidity Sweep)
- Price moves beyond a key level to trigger stops
- Quick wick beyond, then reversal
- Most common form of manipulation

### 2. False Breakout
- Price breaks above resistance or below support
- Breakout traders enter → get trapped
- Price reverses aggressively

### 3. Inducement
- Small moves that bait traders into bad positions
- Creates "obvious" patterns (trendlines, channels)
- Then breaks the pattern to stop out early entries

### 4. News Manipulation
- High-impact news creates both-ways spike
- Stops on both sides get triggered
- Then the true move begins after volatility settles

## How to Identify Manipulation

### Signs of Manipulation
1. **Long wicks** — Rejection candles at key levels
2. **Quick reversal** — Price doesn't hold beyond the level
3. **Kill Zone timing** — Manipulation happens during London/NY open
4. **Preceding range** — Usually follows a consolidation (accumulation)
5. **Volume spike** — Increased volume during the sweep

### What Comes AFTER Manipulation
1. **CHOCH/MSS** — Structure shifts in the true direction
2. **Displacement** — Strong candles creating FVGs
3. **OB/FVG formed** — Entry opportunities appear
4. **Expansion** — The real move toward opposing liquidity

## Real Examples

### Example 1: EUR/USD Stop Hunt Below Support
- **Support:** 1.0850 (equal lows — 3 touches)
- **Manipulation:** London opens, price drops to 1.0842 (sweeps SSL)
- **What happened:** Sell stops triggered below 1.0850
- **Smart Money:** Absorbed the sell orders to build LONG positions
- **Result:** Price reverses to 1.0930 (80+ pips)
- **BD Time:** 12:15 PM (London Kill Zone)

### Example 2: Gold False Breakout Above Resistance
- **Resistance:** 2,050 (previous day high)
- **Manipulation:** NY AM, price spikes to 2,056
- **Breakout traders:** Enter long expecting continuation
- **Smart Money:** Sell into the breakout buyers
- **Result:** Price collapses to 2,010 (400+ pips)
- **BD Time:** 6:00 PM (NY AM Kill Zone)

## How to Trade Manipulation

### The Framework
1. **Identify the target** — Where are the obvious stops? (equal H/L, PDH/PDL)
2. **Wait for the sweep** — Don't enter before manipulation happens
3. **Confirm reversal** — CHOCH on LTF after the sweep
4. **Enter** — At OB/FVG in the direction of the true move
5. **Stop** — Beyond the manipulation extreme
6. **Target** — Opposing liquidity pool

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Manipulation Errors">

**❌ Trading the breakout** — The breakout IS the manipulation. Don't buy/sell the break

**❌ Getting stopped out by manipulation** — Place stops beyond where manipulation typically reaches

**❌ No patience** — Wait for the FULL manipulation to complete before entering

**❌ Trading against HTF** — Manipulation on LTF can look like a trend change, but HTF direction still holds

</Callout>
