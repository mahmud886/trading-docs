## What are Kill Zones in SMC?

Kill Zones are **specific time windows** during the trading day when smart money is most active. SMC setups (OB entries, FVG fills, liquidity sweeps) have the highest success rate during these windows.

---

## The Three Key Sessions

### Asian Session (Accumulation)
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **BD Time** | 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM |
| **Character** | Low volatility, range-building |
| **SMC Role** | Creates the range that London will sweep |
| **Action** | Mark the range (high & low) — do NOT trade |

### London Session (Manipulation)
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **BD Time** | 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM |
| **Character** | High volatility, false breakouts |
| **SMC Role** | Sweeps Asian range, creates daily H/L |
| **Action** | Wait for sweep → CHOCH → enter at OB/FVG |

### New York Session (Distribution)
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **BD Time** | 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM |
| **Character** | Highest volatility, true move |
| **SMC Role** | Delivers the expansion after London manipulation |
| **Action** | Continue London setup or enter fresh after NY sweep |

---

## Kill Zone Trading Strategy

### Step-by-Step
1. **Pre-London:** Mark Asian session high/low
2. **London Open (12:00 PM BD):** Watch for sweep of Asian H/L
3. **Confirmation:** CHOCH on 5M/15M after the sweep
4. **Entry:** At OB or FVG created by the displacement
5. **Target:** Opposing liquidity pool
6. **Avoid:** NY Lunch (10:00-11:30 PM BD) — choppy, low probability

---

## Real Example: London Kill Zone — Gold

- **Asian Range (6-12 PM BD):** 2,035 - 2,042
- **12:20 PM BD:** Price sweeps above Asian High to 2,046 (BSL sweep)
- **12:35 PM BD:** Bearish CHOCH on 15M
- **Entry:** Sell at bearish OB 2,043
- **SL:** 2,047 | **TP:** 2,020
- **R:R:** 1:5.75

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Session Errors">

**❌ Trading during Asian session** — It's accumulation, not a trading window

**❌ Entering during manipulation** — Wait for CHOCH confirmation, don't chase the sweep

**❌ Trading NY Lunch** — 10:00-11:30 PM BD is a no-trade zone

**❌ Not knowing your local times** — Convert Kill Zone times to BD before trading

</Callout>
