## What are Kill Zones?

Kill Zones are **specific time windows** during the trading day when smart money is most active. SMC setups (OB entries, FVG fills, liquidity sweeps) have significantly higher success rates during these windows.

---

## The Three Kill Zones

| Kill Zone        | Time (EST)       | Characteristics                    |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **London**       | 2:00 - 5:00 AM   | Trend initiation, sweeps Asian     |
| **New York**     | 7:00 - 10:00 AM  | Continuation or reversal of London |
| **London Close** | 10:00 - 12:00 PM | Profit-taking, reversals           |

---

## Why Kill Zones Matter for SMC

```
Institutional participation peaks during Kill Zones:
- Orders are placed/executed
- Liquidity sweeps happen
- OB/FVG setups get activated
- Real directional moves start

Outside Kill Zones:
- Low volume, choppy price action
- SMC setups less reliable
- More false signals
- Avoid trading
```

---

## Kill Zone Strategy

### London Kill Zone:

- Asian range = reference (mark H/L)
- London often sweeps Asian H or L first
- Then real direction begins
- Best for: Trend initiation, OB entries

### New York Kill Zone:

- London direction = reference
- NY either continues London or reverses it
- Look for OB/FVG entries aligned with bias
- Best for: Continuation setups, high-volume moves

### London Close Kill Zone:

- Day's move often peaks here
- Profit-taking creates reversals
- Best for: Counter-trend scalps (advanced)
- Caution: Less reliable than London/NY open

---

## Rules

1. **Only take SMC setups during Kill Zones**
2. **Asian session = preparation** (mark levels, don't trade)
3. **Best entries:** First 2 hours of London or NY
4. **Avoid:** 12 PM - 2 PM EST (dead zone)
5. **Close all before end of NY session**
