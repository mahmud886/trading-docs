## What is Accumulation?

Accumulation is the **first phase** of the institutional market cycle where smart money quietly builds large long positions at discounted prices. The market appears to be ranging or even slightly bearish, but beneath the surface, institutions are absorbing every sell order.

<Callout type="info" title="The Quiet Phase">
Accumulation is boring by design. Smart money WANTS you to think nothing is happening so they can buy your sell orders at cheap prices.
</Callout>

---

## Accumulation Process

### The 5 Steps
1. **Range Formation** — Price creates a visible range with clear support/resistance
2. **Stop Hunts** — Minor sweeps below support to trigger sell stops
3. **Absorption** — Institutions buy every triggered sell order
4. **Spring (Final Sweep)** — One last aggressive sweep below the range
5. **Markup Begins** — Price breaks above the range with displacement (BOS)

---

## How to Identify Accumulation

### Visual Clues
- Price ranges for days/weeks at a low level (Daily discount)
- Multiple touches of the range low (looks like "support")
- Decreasing volatility over time
- Wicks below the range that quickly recover (stop hunts)
- Volume increases on the final spring

### Where Accumulation Happens
- At the end of a downtrend (Daily/Weekly discount)
- Above a major support level or demand zone
- Near round psychological numbers (1.0800, 2,000 Gold)

## Real Example: Gold Accumulation (Weekly)

- **Downtrend:** Gold drops from 2,050 to 1,980 over 3 weeks
- **Range Forms:** 1,980 - 2,000 for 2 weeks (accumulation)
- **Stop Hunt 1:** Dip to 1,975 — recovers quickly
- **Stop Hunt 2:** Dip to 1,972 — recovers even faster
- **Spring:** Final dip to 1,968 during London — massive bullish candle
- **BOS:** Price breaks above 2,000 with displacement
- **Entry:** Buy at 4H bullish OB at 1,985 (pullback after BOS)
- **Target:** 2,060 (BSL above)
- **Move:** 800+ pips from spring to target

## Accumulation vs Re-accumulation

| Feature | Accumulation | Re-accumulation |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Location** | At the bottom (trend reversal) | Mid-trend (continuation) |
| **HTF Context** | After downtrend | Within uptrend |
| **Purpose** | Build new positions | Add to existing positions |
| **Duration** | Longer (weeks/months) | Shorter (days/weeks) |

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Accumulation Errors">

**❌ Selling the range bottom** — Accumulation ranges are buying zones, not selling

**❌ Not waiting for the spring** — The spring is the manipulation. Enter AFTER it

**❌ Calling every range accumulation** — HTF must be in discount for true accumulation

**❌ Ignoring volume** — Low volume during range + high volume at breakout = accumulation confirmed

</Callout>
