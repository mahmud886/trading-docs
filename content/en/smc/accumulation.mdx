## What Is Accumulation?

**Accumulation** is a **range at the end of a decline** where buying gradually absorbs selling. Price goes sideways, often looks weak, and then breaks up into a new uptrend (**markup**).

| Phase            | What happens                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Accumulation** | Range at a low; buying absorbs selling  |
| **Markup**       | Price trends up                         |
| **Distribution** | Range at a high; selling absorbs buying |
| **Markdown**     | Price trends down                       |

SMC's ideas here come from **Richard Wyckoff**. SMC calls the key moment a liquidity sweep; Wyckoff calls it a **spring**. See [Wyckoff Relation](/en/smc/wyckoff-relation).

## What Volume Does

| Stage            | Volume                                        |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Selling climax   | **Highest**: panic selling meets heavy buying |
| Tests and spring | **Lower** each time: sellers are running out  |
| Sign of strength | **Rising** on the up-moves                    |

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected: volume">
  The original said volume is "increasing on pushes down (absorption)". In Wyckoff's model it's the opposite after the
  climax: volume **falls** on each push down (that's what shows sellers are exhausted) and **rises** on rallies. For
  spot forex, use tick volume or the futures contract's volume.
</Callout>

## Trading Accumulation

1. Find a **range after a decline**, at a higher-timeframe support or [discount](/en/smc/premium-discount) zone.
2. **Don't buy inside the range blindly.** Wait for the spring: a sweep of the range low that closes back inside.
3. Look for a **bullish CHOCH** on a lower timeframe after the spring.
4. Enter on the **test** or at the OB/FVG from the reversal. Or wait for the SOS and buy the **LPS**.
5. **Stop** below the spring low.
6. **Target** the range top first, then the next higher-timeframe level.

| Level   | Price       | Note                          |
| ------- | ----------- | ----------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0818      | On the test after the spring  |
| Stop    | 1.0788      | Below the 1.0792 spring       |
| Target  | 1.0960      | Next higher-timeframe level   |
| **R:R** | **1 : 4.7** | Risk 30 pips, reward 142 pips |

Buying the **LPS** (around 1.0875) instead gives more confirmation but a worse price.

## Accumulation vs a Plain Range

| Factor          | Accumulation                          | Plain range                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Location        | After a decline, at support/discount  | Anywhere                          |
| Low end         | A spring: sweep and close back inside | Clean bounces or a real breakdown |
| Volume          | Climax, then drying up on dips        | No pattern                        |
| Lower timeframe | Shifts bullish inside the range       | No shift                          |
| Outcome         | More likely to break up               | Either way                        |

Not every range that looks like accumulation is one. If price closes below the range and **stays** there, it was a pause in the downtrend (re-distribution).

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                    | Fix                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Buying the middle of the range             | Buy the spring/test or the LPS                   |
| Calling every dip below the range a spring | It must close back inside, ideally on low volume |
| Stop just under the range low              | Below the spring low                             |
| Ignoring a breakdown that holds            | That's not accumulation                          |

## Practice

1. Find five ranges after declines on daily charts.
2. Label SC, AR, ST, spring (if any), SOS and LPS.
3. Note which broke up and which broke down. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Element    | Key point                                      |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Range**  | Built by SC (low) and AR (high)                |
| **Spring** | Sell-side sweep that closes back inside        |
| **Volume** | Climax, then drying up, then rising on the SOS |
| **Entry**  | Test after the spring, or the LPS              |
| **Stop**   | Below the spring                               |
