## What is SMT Divergence?

SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence occurs when **correlated pairs show divergent price structure** — one makes a new high/low while the other doesn't. This reveals which direction smart money is actually positioned.

---

## How SMT Works

```
Correlated pairs should move together.
When they diverge, one is showing the TRUE direction.

Example (EUR/USD vs GBP/USD):
- Both typically move in same direction
- EUR/USD makes new low → GBP/USD does NOT make new low
- This divergence = smart money NOT bearish
- Expect bullish reversal

Example (DXY vs EUR/USD):
- DXY and EUR/USD are inversely correlated
- DXY makes new high → EUR/USD does NOT make new low
- Divergence = DXY strength is false
- Expect EUR/USD bullish move
```

## Trading SMT Divergence

```
1. Monitor correlated pairs on same timeframe
2. Look for structural divergence (new H/L on one, not other)
3. The pair that FAILS to make new extreme = true direction
4. Enter in the direction of the non-confirming pair
5. Use standard SMC entry (OB, FVG, CHOCH)
6. Target: Opposite liquidity

SMT adds CONFLUENCE — don't trade it alone.
Use it as additional confirmation for your SMC setup.
```
