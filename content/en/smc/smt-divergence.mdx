## What is SMT Divergence?

SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence occurs when **correlated assets fail to make the same high or low together**. When EUR/USD makes a new high but GBP/USD doesn't (or vice versa), it signals that smart money is positioning in one direction — the divergence reveals the true institutional intention.

<Callout type="info" title="The Hidden Signal">
SMT Divergence is one of the most powerful confirmation tools in SMC because it reveals what smart money is doing across multiple instruments simultaneously.
</Callout>

---

## How SMT Works

### Correlated Pairs to Watch
| Pair 1 | Pair 2 | Correlation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| EUR/USD | GBP/USD | Positive — both move with USD weakness |
| DXY | EUR/USD | Negative — opposite movements |
| ES (S&P) | NQ (Nasdaq) | Positive — both equity indices |
| Gold | Silver | Positive — both precious metals |
| USD/JPY | USD/CHF | Positive — both USD strength pairs |

### Bullish SMT Divergence
- Pair A makes a LOWER LOW
- Pair B (correlated) makes a HIGHER LOW or fails to make new low
- Signal: Smart money is accumulating — expect bullish reversal

### Bearish SMT Divergence
- Pair A makes a HIGHER HIGH
- Pair B (correlated) makes a LOWER HIGH or fails to make new high
- Signal: Smart money is distributing — expect bearish reversal

## Real Example: EUR/USD & GBP/USD

### Bullish SMT at Lows
- **EUR/USD:** Makes new low at 1.0820 during London KZ
- **GBP/USD:** Only reaches 1.2540 (previous low was 1.2530 — doesn't make new low)
- **Divergence:** GBP/USD is STRONGER (higher low while EUR makes lower low)
- **Signal:** USD weakness is ending → both pairs should rally
- **Trade:** Buy EUR/USD at the new low with OB confirmation
- **Result:** EUR/USD rallies from 1.0820 to 1.0900

### Bearish SMT at Highs
- **ES (S&P 500):** Makes new high at 5,250
- **NQ (Nasdaq):** Only reaches 18,400 (previous high was 18,450 — fails)
- **Signal:** Tech is weakening while broad market pushes → expect sell-off
- **Trade:** Sell NQ at the bearish OB after MSS on 15M

## SMT Rules

1. **Use correlated pairs** — Must be positively or negatively correlated
2. **Same timeframe** — Compare the same timeframe on both charts
3. **Kill Zone timing** — SMT during KZ is highest probability
4. **Combine with SMC** — Use SMT as CONFIRMATION, not sole entry signal
5. **HTF alignment** — SMT divergence should agree with HTF bias

---

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="SMT Errors">

**❌ Using uncorrelated pairs** — AUD/USD and EUR/GBP are NOT strongly correlated

**❌ Different timeframes** — Compare 15M to 15M, not 15M to 4H

**❌ SMT alone** — It's a confirmation tool, not a standalone entry signal

**❌ Forcing divergence** — Minor differences in swing depth are normal, not always SMT

</Callout>
