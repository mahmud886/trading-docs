## What is Entry Confirmation?

Entry confirmation is the **final trigger** that validates entering a trade after reaching your Point of Interest (POI). In SMC, you don't enter just because price touches an OB — you wait for confirmation that smart money is reacting.

---

## Confirmation Methods

### 1. LTF CHOCH (Best)
```
1. Price reaches your HTF POI (OB, FVG, etc.)
2. Drop to lower timeframe (M5/M15)
3. Wait for CHOCH in your trade direction
4. Enter after the CHOCH
5. Stop: Beyond the CHOCH swing point

This is the HIGHEST CONFIDENCE confirmation.
```

### 2. Displacement + FVG Creation
```
1. Price reaches POI
2. Strong displacement candle appears (Marubozu-like)
3. Creates a new FVG in your direction
4. Enter on retracement to the new FVG
5. Shows institutional commitment
```

### 3. Candle Confirmation
- Engulfing pattern at POI
- Pin bar/rejection at POI
- Strong close in your direction
- Weakest form of confirmation (use with confluence)

## Confirmation Hierarchy

| Rank | Method | Confidence | R:R |
|------|--------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | LTF CHOCH | Highest | Moderate |
| 2 | Displacement + FVG | High | High |
| 3 | Engulfing at POI | Moderate | Moderate |

## Grading Setup Quality

```
A+ Setup (take full size):
- HTF trend aligned ✓
- At unmitigated OB/FVG ✓
- In discount/premium zone ✓
- LTF CHOCH confirmed ✓
- Kill Zone timing ✓

B Setup (take reduced size):
- 3-4 of the above criteria met

C Setup (skip):
- Fewer than 3 criteria met
```
