## What is Entry Confirmation?

Entry confirmation is the set of **signals that validate your trade before you click the button**. In SMC, entering without confirmation leads to random results. The confirmation process filters out false setups.

---

## The SMC Confirmation Checklist

### Before Entry — 5 Checks
1. ✅ **HTF Bias** — Daily/4H direction clear?
2. ✅ **Kill Zone Timing** — Within London or NY AM window?
3. ✅ **Liquidity Swept** — Has BSL/SSL been taken?
4. ✅ **Structure Shift** — CHOCH or MSS on LTF (5M/15M)?
5. ✅ **PD Array Entry** — Entering at OB, FVG, or Breaker?

### Entry Model Flow
```
HTF Bias → Kill Zone → Liquidity Sweep → CHOCH/MSS → OB/FVG Entry
```

---

## Types of Confirmation

### 1. CHOCH (Change of Character)
- First break of structure against the current LTF trend
- Signals potential reversal after sweep
- Look on 5M/15M

### 2. Displacement
- Strong candles with full bodies (little to no wicks)
- Creates FVGs — these are your entry zones
- Shows institutional COMMITMENT

### 3. Engulfing Candle
- Single candle that completely engulfs the previous candle
- Shows strong momentum shift
- Especially powerful at OB/FVG levels

### 4. SMT Divergence
- Correlated pair fails to confirm the move
- Extra confluence for your entry
- See [SMT Divergence](/en/smc/smt-divergence)

## Real Example: Full Confirmation Trade

### EUR/USD Long
1. ✅ **HTF Bias:** Daily bullish (making HH/HL, price in discount)
2. ✅ **Kill Zone:** London session (12:30 PM BD)
3. ✅ **Liquidity Swept:** Asian Low at 1.0855 swept to 1.0848
4. ✅ **CHOCH:** 5M bullish CHOCH at 1.0858 — displacement candle
5. ✅ **Entry:** Bullish OB at 1.0855, FVG at 1.0852-1.0858
6. **Entry:** Buy at 1.0855 (OB + FVG confluence)
7. **SL:** 1.0845 | **TP:** 1.0910
8. **R:R:** 1:5.5

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Confirmation Errors">

**❌ Entering without all 5 checks** — Skip a check = skip the trade

**❌ CHOCH without displacement** — Weak break is not confirmation. Need strong candle

**❌ Entering on LTF against HTF** — 5M CHOCH against Daily trend = losing trade

**❌ No liquidity sweep** — If liquidity hasn't been swept, the manipulation isn't done

</Callout>
