## What Is Inducement?

**Inducement (IDM)** is an **obvious minor swing** that sits **between price and the real zone**. It looks like a reason to trade: a "break lower" or a "breakout". Price takes it, triggers the stops and breakout orders behind it, and only then reaches the real [order block](/en/smc/order-blocks) or [FVG](/en/smc/fair-value-gap) and turns.

In short: **the first level price hits on a pullback is often not the one it turns from.**

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: 'engineered by institutions'">
  The original described inducement as deliberately engineered to trap retail traders, with "the algorithm" exploiting
  them. Nobody can see that intent. What you can see is that obvious minor swings often get taken before price reaches a
  stronger zone. Trade that observation, not a story about who did it.
</Callout>

1. **Real zone:** a bullish OB at **1.0850 – 1.0856** started the rally to 1.0940.
2. **Inducement:** on the pullback, a minor low forms at **1.0870**, above the OB. Longs put stops under it; breakdown sellers wait below it.
3. **IDM taken:** price breaks 1.0870. It looks bearish. Those stops and sell orders are filled.
4. **Entry:** price reaches the **real OB** and holds. Buy **1.0855**.
5. **Confirmation:** a CHOCH above the 1.0893 internal high.

| Level   | Price       | Note                         |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Entry   | 1.0855      | Inside the real OB           |
| Stop    | 1.0842      | Below the OB                 |
| Target  | 1.0950      | Above the 1.0940 high        |
| **R:R** | **1 : 7.3** | Risk 13 pips, reward 95 pips |

The original example gave no stop, only a result ("+95 pips"). The stop here is the natural one, under the OB.

## Where Inducement Forms

| Location                | What happens                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Above a bullish OB      | A minor low is broken; price dips into the OB and reverses up    |
| Below a bearish OB      | A minor high is broken; price pops into the OB and reverses down |
| At support / resistance | A small break traps breakout traders, then reverses              |
| At trendlines           | A trendline break triggers entries, then reverses                |
| Around news             | A first spike one way before the real move                       |

## Using It Instead of Falling for It

| Rule                                     | Why                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mark the IDM before price gets there** | The first obvious internal swing between price and your zone |
| **Don't enter at the IDM**               | That's where the trap is                                     |
| **Wait for the IDM to be taken**         | Then the real zone is in play                                |
| **Enter at the real zone, with a shift** | OB/FVG beyond the IDM, plus a lower-timeframe CHOCH          |
| **If the IDM isn't taken**               | Price may not reach your zone; no trade                      |

## Inducement vs a Real Break

| Question                    | Inducement                       | Real break                           |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Higher-timeframe structure? | Still intact                     | Broken                               |
| What's just beyond?         | A stronger zone (OB/FVG)         | Open space to the next pool          |
| After the break?            | Price reaches the zone and turns | Price keeps going, with displacement |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                      | Fix                                                |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Buying the first minor low on a pullback     | That's often the IDM                               |
| Entering at the zone before the IDM is taken | Wait for it                                        |
| Calling every break inducement               | Check the higher timeframe: real breaks happen too |
| Not marking internal swings                  | You can't spot the IDM if you didn't mark it       |
