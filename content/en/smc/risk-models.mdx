## SMC Risk Management

In SMC, risk management uses **structural invalidation** rather than arbitrary pip distances. Your stop goes where your thesis is WRONG — beyond the structural level that would invalidate the setup.

---

## Stop Placement in SMC

| Setup Type    | Stop Location                  | Why                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| OB entry      | Beyond the OB (body, not wick) | OB violated = thesis wrong     |
| FVG entry     | Beyond the FVG range           | FVG broken = imbalance filled  |
| Sweep entry   | Beyond the sweep wick          | New low/high = not a sweep     |
| CHOCH entry   | Beyond CHOCH swing point       | Structure not actually shifted |
| Breaker entry | Beyond the Breaker zone        | Breaker failed                 |

---

## Position Sizing Formula

```
Lot Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop pips × Pip value)

Example:
- Account: $10,000
- Risk: 1% = $100
- Stop: 25 pips (OB width + buffer)
- Pip value: $10/lot
- Size: $100 ÷ (25 × $10) = 0.40 lots
```

## Risk Per Setup Grade

| Grade | Criteria Met                                | Risk              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| A+    | 5/5 confluences, Kill Zone, CHOCH confirmed | 1-2%              |
| B     | 3-4 confluences, good but not perfect       | 0.5-1%            |
| C     | 2-3 confluences, uncertain                  | 0.25-0.5% or SKIP |

---

## Rules

1. **Max 1-2% per trade** — non-negotiable
2. **Max 3% daily loss** — stop for the day
3. **Max 5% weekly loss** — reduce size next week
4. **Correlated pairs** count as ONE risk unit
5. **Wider stop = smaller size** (never remove stop)
6. **Break-even after 1R** gained
