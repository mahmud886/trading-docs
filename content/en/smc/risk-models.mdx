## Why Risk Management is #1

Even with the best SMC analysis, you will have LOSING trades. Risk management determines whether those losses are small bumps or account-ending blows.

<Callout type="info" title="The Truth">
"Risk management is not a concept — it's the ONLY thing that keeps you in the game." Professional traders risk 1-2% maximum per trade.
</Callout>

## SMC Risk Framework

### Position Sizing Rules
| Account Size | Max Risk/Trade | Max Risk/Day |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| $1,000 | $10-20 (1-2%) | $30 (3%) |
| $5,000 | $50-100 (1-2%) | $150 (3%) |
| $10,000 | $100-200 (1-2%) | $300 (3%) |
| $50,000 | $500-1,000 (1-2%) | $1,500 (3%) |
| $100,000+ | $1,000-2,000 (1-2%) | $3,000 (3%) |

### R:R Minimums
| Setup Type | Minimum R:R | Typical R:R |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Scalp (5M entry) | 1:2 | 1:3-5 |
| Day Trade (15M entry) | 1:3 | 1:4-7 |
| Swing Trade (4H entry) | 1:3 | 1:5-10+ |

## Stop Loss Placement in SMC

### Where to Place Stops
1. **After liquidity sweep** — Beyond the sweep extreme (safest)
2. **Below/above OB** — Beyond the Order Block range
3. **Beyond FVG** — Beyond the FVG with some buffer (5-10 pips for Forex, 50-100 for Gold)

### Where NOT to Place Stops
- At the obvious swing low/high (that's where liquidity rests!)
- At round numbers without buffer
- Too tight (needs room for manipulation)

## Trade Management

### Partials Strategy
1. **First target (1:2 R:R)** — Move stop to breakeven, take 30-50% off
2. **Second target (1:4 R:R)** — Take 30% more
3. **Runner (let it ride)** — Leave 20% to reach full target

### When to Cut Early
- Price closes through your OB/FVG entirely (invalidation)
- CHOCH forms against your trade on entry timeframe
- News event changes the fundamental picture

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Risk Management Errors">

**❌ Risking more than 2%** — One bad day can take weeks to recover from

**❌ No stop loss** — ALWAYS have a predetermined exit

**❌ Moving stop to breakeven too early** — Give the trade room to breathe

**❌ Revenge trading** — After a loss, take a break. Don't double size

**❌ Not journaling** — Track every trade to improve your edge

</Callout>
