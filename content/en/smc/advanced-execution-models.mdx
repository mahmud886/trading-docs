## What are Execution Models?

Execution models are **complete trading systems** that combine multiple SMC concepts into a step-by-step process. Instead of randomly applying concepts, you follow a specific model for consistent results.

---

## Model 1: The London Sweep Model

### Setup Requirements
1. Clear HTF bias (Daily/4H direction)
2. Asian session range marked
3. London Kill Zone timing (12:00-3:00 PM BD)

### Execution Steps
1. Wait for London to sweep Asian H/L
2. Confirm CHOCH on 15M after sweep
3. Identify OB/FVG created by the displacement
4. Enter at the OB/FVG
5. SL beyond the sweep extreme
6. TP at opposing liquidity pool

### Win Rate: 55-65% | Average R:R: 1:3-5

---

## Model 2: The HTF-LTF Alignment Model

### Setup Requirements
1. 4H OB in premium (sells) or discount (buys)
2. Price reaches the 4H OB
3. LTF (15M) shows CHOCH at the OB level

### Execution Steps
1. Mark 4H OBs at premium/discount extremes
2. Set alerts at these OBs
3. When price reaches 4H OB, drop to 15M
4. Wait for 15M CHOCH at the OB
5. Enter at the 15M OB/FVG within the 4H OB
6. SL beyond the 4H OB | TP at 4H liquidity target

### Win Rate: 60-70% | Average R:R: 1:4-8

## Model 3: The POI Stacking Model

### Concept
Stack multiple Points of Interest (POI) for maximum confluence:
- 4H OB + 15M FVG + OTE zone = extremely high probability

### Execution
1. Identify 4H OB at discount/premium
2. Within that OB, find 15M FVG
3. Check if this zone aligns with OTE (62-79% Fib)
4. Enter when ALL three align
5. Tightest stop possible (behind the FVG)

### Win Rate: 65-75% | Average R:R: 1:5-10

## Building Your Own Model

### Requirements
1. **Define the bias method** — How do you determine direction?
2. **Define the entry trigger** — What confirms your entry?
3. **Define risk** — Where's the stop? What R:R do you need?
4. **Backtest 100 trades** — Minimum sample size for statistical validity
5. **Track results** — Journal everything, adjust as needed

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Execution Errors">

**❌ Trading without a model** — "I see a setup" is not a model. Have specific rules

**❌ Model hopping** — Give each model 3 months before evaluating

**❌ No backtesting** — Backtest in replay mode with time, not hindsight analysis

**❌ Too many confluences** — 3-4 factors max. More = zero trades

</Callout>
