## What Is an Execution Model?

Once you've found a point of interest (POI), the **execution model** decides exactly how you get in: where the entry is, where the stop is, and what has to happen first. Two traders can share the same POI and the same target and still get very different R:R, depending on the model.

This lesson builds on [Entry Confirmation](/en/smc/entry-confirmation), which compared confirmation levels with a fixed stop. Here the **stop moves with the model**, which is what makes the refined entry worth learning.

| Model              | Entry                                         | Stop                                | Risk    | Reward  | **R:R**     |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------- | ------- | ----------- |
| **1 Risk**         | Sell limit at the OB, 1.0950                  | 1.0963, above the OB                | 13 pips | 70 pips | **1 : 5.4** |
| **2 Confirmation** | Sell at the close of the CHOCH candle, 1.0928 | 1.0963, above the OB                | 35 pips | 48 pips | **1 : 1.4** |
| **3 Refined**      | Sell the FVG the CHOCH left, 1.0938           | 1.0954, above the 1.0951 lower high | 16 pips | 58 pips | **1 : 3.6** |

The refined entry gets confirmation **and** a good R:R because its stop no longer has to sit above the whole OB. Once the CHOCH has formed, the lower high that made it is the new invalidation point.

## The Three Models

### 1. Risk entry

- **How:** a limit order at the edge of the OB or FVG; stop beyond the zone.
- **For:** the best price and R:R; you don't miss fast moves.
- **Against:** no evidence the zone will hold, and it's filled on every setup that fails.
- **Use when:** the higher-timeframe context is very strong (an A+ setup), and the zone is fresh.

### 2. Confirmation entry

- **How:** wait for a lower-timeframe CHOCH at the POI, then enter at market on the CHOCH close; stop beyond the POI.
- **For:** structure has shifted before you're in.
- **Against:** the worst price, and a wide stop if it stays beyond the POI. R:R is often poor.
- **Use when:** you can't wait for a retrace, or the move is likely to run without one.

### 3. Refined entry

- **How:** wait for the CHOCH, then a limit order at the FVG or OB the CHOCH created; stop beyond the swing that made the CHOCH.
- **For:** confirmation plus a tight, logical stop.
- **Against:** price sometimes doesn't retrace to the FVG, so you miss the trade.
- **Use when:** the default for most setups.

## When to Use Each

| Situation                | Model                   | Why                                       |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| A+ setup, fresh HTF zone | Risk (small) or refined | Context carries the trade                 |
| Standard setup           | Refined                 | Confirmation at a sensible R:R            |
| Counter-trend            | Refined                 | You need proof that structure turned      |
| Fast news-driven move    | Confirmation            | A retrace may never come                  |
| Learning                 | Refined                 | Builds the habit of waiting for structure |

## Stacking Entries

You can split one trade across models **without raising total risk**: a small risk entry at the POI, and the rest on the refined entry.

Using the chart, a $10,000 account and 1% total risk ($100):

| Leg            | Risk        | Entry  | Stop   | Size                         |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------ | ------ | ---------------------------- |
| 1 — Risk entry | 0.25% ($25) | 1.0950 | 1.0963 | $25 ÷ (13 × $10) = 0.19 lots |
| 2 — Refined    | 0.75% ($75) | 1.0938 | 1.0954 | $75 ÷ (16 × $10) = 0.46 lots |

The average entry is about **1.0941**, still in the premium half of the move. If the zone fails before a CHOCH forms, only leg 1 is lost: 0.25% instead of 1%.

<Callout type="info" title="Corrected: premium or discount">
  The original said stacking gives an "average entry price in premium zone" for every trade. That's right for a
  **short**. For a long, the goal is an average entry in **discount**.
</Callout>

### Second example (no chart) — Gold long, refined entry

Gold taps a 4H bullish OB at **2,380 – 2,388**. On 5m, a CHOCH above the **2,394** lower high leaves an FVG at **2,389 – 2,392**. The swing low that made the CHOCH is **2,384**. Buy **2,390**, stop **2,382**, target **2,420**: risk $8, reward $30, **R:R 1 : 3.8** (planned). A confirmation entry at the CHOCH close (2,396) with the stop under the OB (2,377) would have been 1 : 1.3.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                       | Fix                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mixing models mid-trade                       | Decide the model before price reaches the POI               |
| Confirmation entry with a POI stop by default | Consider the refined stop at the CHOCH swing                |
| Chasing when the FVG retrace doesn't come     | No retrace, no refined trade                                |
| Stacking and adding risk with each leg        | Split the planned risk; don't add to it                     |
| Moving the refined stop back to the POI       | If the CHOCH swing breaks, the reason for the trade is gone |

## Practice

1. Take 20 past POI touches.
2. For each, record the risk, confirmation and refined entries, their stops, and the R:R to the same target.
3. Note which ones would have hit the refined stop but not the POI stop.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).

## Summary

| Model        | Entry                       | Stop                   | Trade-off                    |
| ------------ | --------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Risk         | Limit at the POI            | Beyond the POI         | Best R:R, no proof           |
| Confirmation | Market at the CHOCH         | Beyond the POI         | Proof, poor R:R              |
| Refined      | Limit at the CHOCH's FVG/OB | Beyond the CHOCH swing | Proof and good R:R; may miss |
