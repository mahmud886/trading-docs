## The Wyckoff-SMC Connection

Smart Money Concepts are built on the foundation of **Richard Wyckoff's methodology** from the early 1900s. Wyckoff studied how large operators (the "Composite Man") manipulated markets — the same principles SMC uses today.

---

## Wyckoff's Three Laws

### Law 1: Supply and Demand
- Price moves up when demand exceeds supply
- Price moves down when supply exceeds demand
- In SMC: Order flow determines direction

### Law 2: Cause and Effect
- The "cause" (accumulation/distribution) determines the "effect" (markup/markdown)
- Larger accumulation = larger markup
- In SMC: Longer consolidation = bigger breakout

### Law 3: Effort vs Result
- Volume (effort) should match price movement (result)
- High volume + small move = absorption (smart money vs retail)
- In SMC: Displacement with volume = institutional commitment

## Wyckoff Accumulation → SMC Translation

| Wyckoff Phase | SMC Equivalent |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Preliminary Support (PS)** | First demand zone / OB appears |
| **Selling Climax (SC)** | Major SSL sweep — panic selling |
| **Automatic Rally (AR)** | First bullish reaction — creates range top |
| **Secondary Test (ST)** | Re-test of SC area — builds OB |
| **Spring** | Final manipulation — sweeps SSL below range |
| **Sign of Strength (SOS)** | BOS above range — displacement |
| **Last Point of Support (LPS)** | Pullback to OB/FVG — YOUR ENTRY |
| **Markup** | Distribution phase — price rallies to target BSL |

## Wyckoff Distribution → SMC Translation

| Wyckoff Phase | SMC Equivalent |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Preliminary Supply (PSY)** | First supply zone / bearish OB |
| **Buying Climax (BC)** | Major BSL sweep — euphoria |
| **Automatic Reaction (AR)** | First bearish reaction |
| **Upthrust (UT)** | Final manipulation — sweeps BSL above range |
| **Sign of Weakness (SOW)** | BOS below range — displacement |
| **Last Point of Supply (LPSY)** | Pullback to bearish OB — ENTRY |
| **Markdown** | Price drops to target SSL |

## Why Wyckoff Matters for SMC

1. **Context** — Wyckoff phases tell you WHERE you are in the cycle
2. **Patience** — Accumulation can last days/weeks. Don't rush
3. **Confirmation** — Spring/Upthrust = the sweep. SOS/SOW = the BOS. LPS/LPSY = the entry
4. **Target** — Cause (range size) predicts Effect (move size)
