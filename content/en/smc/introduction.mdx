## What is Smart Money?

**Smart Money** refers to institutional traders — banks, hedge funds, pension funds, and market makers — who have the capital, information, and infrastructure to move markets. They represent over 90% of daily trading volume. Smart Money Concepts (SMC) is a framework for understanding HOW these institutions trade.

<Callout type="info" title="The Core Idea">
  "Retail traders lose because they trade against institutions. SMC teaches you to identify institutional footprints and
  trade WITH smart money, not against it."
</Callout>

## Smart Money vs Retail Money

| Aspect             | Retail Traders               | Smart Money                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Volume**         | 5-10% of market              | 90-95% of market                |
| **Information**    | Price charts, indicators     | Order flow, dark pools          |
| **Entries**        | Breakouts, indicator signals | Liquidity sweeps, OBs           |
| **Stop Placement** | Below support/resistance     | Creates the stop hunts          |
| **Timeframe**      | Minutes to hours             | Hours to weeks                  |
| **Goal**           | Quick profits                | Accumulate/distribute positions |

## The SMC Framework

### Core Concepts

1. **Market Structure** — Understanding trend direction (HH, HL, LH, LL)
2. **Order Blocks (OB)** — Institutional entry zones
3. **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)** — Price imbalances
4. **Liquidity** — Where stops rest (BSL/SSL)
5. **Break of Structure (BOS)** — Trend continuation signal
6. **Change of Character (CHOCH)** — First reversal signal
7. **Premium & Discount** — Zones for selling and buying

### The Wyckoff Foundation

SMC is heavily influenced by Richard Wyckoff's methodology:

- **Accumulation** — Smart money buys at the bottom
- **Markup** — Price rises as positions are built
- **Distribution** — Smart money sells at the top
- **Markdown** — Price falls after distribution

## How Markets Really Move

### The Institutional Cycle

1. **Accumulation Phase** — Banks build positions silently in a range
2. **Manipulation Phase** — False breakout to trap retail traders and generate liquidity
3. **Expansion Phase** — The true directional move begins
4. **Distribution Phase** — Banks unload positions at premium prices

### Why Retail Loses

- Retail buys breakouts → Smart money SELLS into breakout buyers
- Retail places stops at obvious levels → Smart money HUNTS those stops
- Retail uses lagging indicators → Smart money creates the price action indicators lag behind

| Feature           | SMC                                 | ICT                           |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Origin**        | Community-derived from ICT concepts | Michael Huddleston's original |
| **Time Focus**    | Less emphasis on time               | Time is critical (Kill Zones) |

| **Accessibility** | Easier to learn                     | Steeper learning curve        |

## Getting Started with SMC

### Phase 1: Market Structure (Week 1-2)

- Learn swing highs and swing lows
- Identify bullish structure (HH/HL) and bearish structure (LH/LL)
- Practice marking BOS and CHOCH

### Phase 2: Supply & Demand Zones (Week 3-4)

- Identify Order Blocks
- Mark Fair Value Gaps
- Understand premium vs discount zones

### Phase 3: Liquidity (Week 5-6)

- Mark BSL and SSL on charts
- Understand liquidity sweeps
- Learn inducement and stop hunts

### Phase 4: Live Trading (Week 7+)

- Combine all concepts
- Trade ONE pair, ONE session
- Journal every setup

## Real Trading Example

### EUR/USD — Complete SMC Analysis

1. **Daily:** Bullish structure (Higher Highs, Higher Lows)
2. **4H:** Price in discount zone, near a bullish Order Block
3. **1H:** Liquidity sweep below equal lows (SSL taken)
4. **15M:** CHOCH bullish → BOS confirms
5. **Entry:** Buy at 15M bullish OB within the 4H OB
6. **Stop:** Below the liquidity sweep
7. **Target:** Next BSL above (1:4 R:R)
8. **Time (BD):** London Kill Zone (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM)

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="SMC Beginner Errors">

**❌ Trading every Order Block** — Not all OBs are valid. Need context (structure, premium/discount)

**❌ Ignoring higher timeframes** — HTF direction is everything. Don't go against the Daily trend

**❌ No patience** — Wait for the full setup: sweep → CHOCH → OB/FVG entry

**❌ Over-marking charts** — Mark only the RELEVANT levels on your trading timeframe

**❌ Confusing BOS and CHOCH** — BOS = continuation; CHOCH = reversal. Know the difference

</Callout>
