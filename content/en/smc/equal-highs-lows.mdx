## What are Equal Highs & Equal Lows?

Equal highs/lows are **multiple swing points at approximately the same price level**, creating a highly visible "support" or "resistance" line. In SMC, these are NOT support/resistance — they are **liquidity magnets** that smart money will target.

<Callout type="info" title="SMC Perspective">
When retail traders see equal highs, they think "strong resistance." When smart money sees equal highs, they think "BSL target — lots of stops above that level."
</Callout>

---

## Why Equal Highs/Lows Form

1. **Institutional engineering** — Smart money deliberately creates these levels
2. **Retail behavior** — Traders anchor stops above/below these obvious levels
3. **Algorithmic logic** — More touches = more stops = more liquidity
4. **The trap** — The more "confirmed" the level looks, the more stops accumulate

---

## How to Trade Equal Highs/Lows

### Scenario 1: Fading the Sweep (Most Common)
1. Mark equal highs/lows on your chart
2. Determine HTF bias (are they targets or traps?)
3. Wait for price to SWEEP the equal level
4. Confirm with CHOCH/MSS on LTF
5. Enter opposite direction after the sweep
6. Target the opposing liquidity pool

### Scenario 2: Trading Toward the Target
1. If HTF bias points toward the equal levels
2. Equal highs = BSL target (bullish bias → price will reach them)
3. Enter pullbacks toward the target using OB/FVG
4. Take profit AT or just beyond the equal level (where stops rest)

## Real Examples

### Example 1: Equal Lows Sweep — EUR/USD
- **Equal Lows:** Three touches at 1.0830
- **Daily Bias:** Bullish (price in discount)
- **Sweep:** London KZ, price drops to 1.0823 (sweeps SSL)
- **Confirmation:** 15M bullish CHOCH
- **Entry:** Buy at 1.0835 (bullish OB)
- **SL:** 1.0820 | **TP:** 1.0900 (BSL target above)
- **R:R:** 1:4.3

### Example 2: Equal Highs as Target — NAS100
- **Equal Highs:** Two touches at 17,950
- **Daily Bias:** Bullish (ascending structure)
- **Action:** Buy the pullback to OB at 17,820
- **Target:** 17,960 (just beyond equal highs where stops rest)
- **R:R:** 1:3.5

## Common Mistakes

<Callout type="warning" title="Equal H/L Errors">

**❌ Treating equal highs as resistance to sell** — They're liquidity targets, not resistance

**❌ Placing YOUR stops at equal levels** — Your stops will be the liquidity that gets taken

**❌ Entering before the sweep** — Wait for the equal level to be TAKEN before trading

</Callout>
