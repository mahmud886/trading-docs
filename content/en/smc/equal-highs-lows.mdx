## What are Equal Highs & Lows?

Equal Highs (EQH) and Equal Lows (EQL) are **two or more swing points at approximately the same price level**. In SMC, they represent engineered liquidity pools — retail traders place stops just beyond them, making them prime targets for institutional sweeps.

<Callout type="warning" title="Equal = Target">
When you see equal highs or equal lows on ANY timeframe, expect price to eventually sweep them. The more obvious the equal level, the more liquidity sits there, and the more likely institutions will target it.
</Callout>

---

## Why Equal Levels Form

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Retail stop placement | Traders put SL above EQH / below EQL |
| Breakout orders | Buy stops above EQH, sell stops below EQL |
| Obvious level | Creates massive order cluster |
| Institutional target | Perfect liquidity source for fills |

## Trading Equal Levels

### Strategy: Wait for Sweep
```
1. Identify EQH or EQL (2+ tests at same level)
2. DON'T trade the breakout — it's likely a sweep
3. Wait for price to pierce the level (wick beyond)
4. Wait for reversal candle / LTF CHOCH
5. Enter OPPOSITE to the sweep direction
6. Stop: Beyond the sweep wick
7. Target: Opposite liquidity (EQH → target EQL, vice versa)
```

### Rules:
- More touches at equal level = more liquidity = better sweep
- NEVER place stops just above EQH or below EQL
- Expect the sweep — don't be surprised by it
- After sweep: high-probability reversal zone
