## What Is Delta?

**Delta** is the difference between **volume traded at the ask** (aggressive buying) and **volume traded at the bid** (aggressive selling):

> Delta = volume at ask − volume at bid

Positive delta means aggressive buyers outweighed aggressive sellers; negative means the reverse. Delta says who was **more aggressive**, not who was right or who won.

<Callout type="warning" title="Corrected">
  The original said "a green candle can have negative delta … institutions are buying the dip" and called delta "the
  truth behind the candle". It's a different measure of the same trades, not a truer one. A green candle with negative
  delta means more volume traded at the bid than at the ask while price still rose — which can mean passive buyers
  absorbed the selling, **or** that classification and thin liquidity are distorting the number. Treat it as a clue.
</Callout>

---

## Three Kinds of Delta

| Kind                       | What it is                                         | Use                                                                                               |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bar delta**              | Delta of one candle                                | Who dominated this bar                                                                            |
| **Cumulative delta (CVD)** | The running total of bar delta from a chosen start | The direction of aggressive pressure over time                                                    |
| **Delta at price**         | Delta at each price in a bar (the footprint)       | Where in the bar the imbalance happened — see [Footprint Charts](/en/order-flow/footprint-charts) |

CVD depends on **where you start counting** (usually the session open) and resets or drifts with the choice. Compare **shapes** (highs, lows, slopes), not absolute values.

| Stage         | Price                              | Delta                                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First leg up  | Rises 22 pips in 12 bars           | +4,080 (every bar positive); CVD reaches 4,080                      |
| Pullback      | Falls 10 pips                      | −1,230; CVD falls to 2,850                                          |
| Second leg up | **Higher high** (1.0879 vs 1.0873) | Only **+750** in 10 bars; CVD peaks at **3,600** — a **lower high** |
| Drop          | Falls 21 pips                      | −2,440; CVD falls to 1,160                                          |

At the second high, price said "new high" while aggressive buying had **weakened** — a **bearish divergence**. In this synthetic session the divergence was followed by a fall; in real markets divergences often fail, especially in strong trends.

## Reading Delta Signals

| Signal                                   | Reading                                       | Caution                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Price up + CVD up                        | Confirmation                                  | Doesn't mean it can't reverse                                         |
| Price up + CVD down/flat                 | Bearish divergence: advance on weaker buying  | Can persist for a long time in trends                                 |
| Price down + CVD down                    | Confirmation                                  |                                                                       |
| Price down + CVD up/flat                 | Bullish divergence: decline on weaker selling | Same caution                                                          |
| Large positive delta, price doesn't rise | Possible absorption by passive sellers        | See [Absorption and Exhaustion](/en/order-flow/absorption-exhaustion) |
| Large negative delta, price doesn't fall | Possible absorption by passive buyers         | Same                                                                  |

### Example (no chart) — acting on a divergence

On a 6E chart price makes a high at **1.0879**, CVD is below its earlier peak, and the next candle closes below the previous candle's low at **1.0875**. Plan: sell **1.0874**, stop **1.0882** (above the high), target **1.0858** (the start of the move): risk 8, reward 16, **1 : 2.0** planned. The trigger is the **price confirmation** (a close below the prior low), not the divergence alone. If price closes above **1.0882**, the divergence has failed.

## Limits

| Limit                  | Why it matters                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Classification         | Delta depends on labelling each trade a buy or sell      |
| One venue              | In spot forex, a broker's delta covers only its own flow |
| Where CVD starts       | Different starts give different lines                    |
| Divergences fail often | Strong trends can run on weakening delta for a long time |
| Needs real volume      | Tick-volume "delta" is a weaker approximation            |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                   | Fix                                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Trading a divergence without confirmation | Wait for a close beyond the prior swing         |
| Comparing CVD values across sessions      | Compare shapes within one session               |
| Treating delta as "institutional buying"  | It's aggressive volume; it can come from anyone |
| Ignoring price context                    | Mark levels and structure first                 |
| No stop                                   | Place it beyond the divergence extreme          |

## Practice

1. On a futures chart, plot bar delta and cumulative delta from the session open for five sessions.
2. Mark each swing high and low in price and compare with the CVD at those points.
3. List divergences and whether they led to a reversal, and whether a price confirmation preceded it.
4. Log it in your [trading journal](/en/tools/journal-template).
